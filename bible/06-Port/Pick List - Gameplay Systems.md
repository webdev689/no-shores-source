# Pick List - Gameplay Systems

Part of the [[Pick List]]. How to read the verdicts and the "Lands as" column: [[Port]].

Scope: the tip (`ccr-aff93cb4-gw1nto-audit`), 1,016 `.js` files and 118.8k lines at the top level (359 files). Git also
holds 379 `.txt`/`.json` quest and sheet files that the checkout leaves out. Every row below comes from file headers and
import lists I read (`sysscan/*.txt`), plus a direct-import seam scan (`sysagent/seams.tsv`). No row is marked
(unread).

Verdicts follow the brief. **Cut** names the seam. Where a row splits, the deciding half lands as a Rust rebuild and the
presenting half as a TS port, and the Lands-as cell says so. "Seam-free" means the files import no `src/formats`, no
DFU item template, no DFU entity model, no Daggerfall character table, no `combat/formulas`, no `gameDate` and no mod
settings.

## Notes

- **A few shared seams decide most rows.** These are DFU's item templates (100 systems files import `item/itemTemplates`),
  DFU's entity model (`skills`/`statMods`/`races`/`armorMaterials`, 124 files), Daggerfall's enemy and townsperson
  tables (`characters/enemy`, `mobile`, name banks: 90), `src/formats` (150, including `dfRandom` and `mapsFile`),
  `combat/formulas`, `gameDate` and `textManager` (181). Give the new game its own item table, stat and skill model,
  bestiary, calendar and map grid. Most Cut rows then move to Clean with only small edits.
- **157 `src/systems` files are already statically reachable from `server-account/` and `server/`.** They include the
  item law, itemTemplates, inventory, enchanting, loot, survival, the wild laws, the legacy law and sigil. The Rust
  judge must carry DFU's template table as data or replace it.
- **The deciding laws mostly live in `src/net/*Law.js`,** not here: marketLaw, battlePassLaw, townEconLaw,
  professionLaw, recipeLaw, decorLaw, homeLaw. So market, battle pass, town economy and professions appear here only
  as client halves (`tradePack`, `pass/`, `town/townEcon`, `professions/`), and those are thin TS ports.
- **Clean gems:**
  - Naval combat core: 16 files, seam-free; `navalAI` alone is 2,220 lines.
  - The living-world social simulation core, which is pure and seeded.
  - The profession and gathering acts.
  - Our music stack: `gmSynth`, `song/songPlayer`, and the written scores `gateScore`, `sdScore`, `egScore`,
    `seaScore`, `arenaScore` and `introScore`.
  - `soundscape/`, `instruments/`, `wind/` and `sea/`.
  - The realm save client (`realmSaves`, `cloudSaves`, `online/`), `party/` laws, `lootLevelGate` and `powerCeiling`.
- **The quest machine is DFU's.** `quest/` translates QuestMachine.cs 1:1 (about 17k lines). Our 264 own-words quests
  keep DFU's QBN byte for byte, and the 43 questline scripts and 9 start scripts are written in DFU's script language.
  Our own quest layer (`questlines/`, `questShare`, `questStamps`, `questGuidance`) only works with a QBN machine
  underneath, or a rewrite of the scripts.
- **Misfiled: looks ours, but another author's design or words remain.**

    the author's dish names.
  - `naval/navalShips` gun positions were measured off Come Sail Away's prefabs.
