- **Most valuable Clean pieces:** the forge's core (`rig.py`, `body.py`, `anatomy.py`, `model.py`, `shape.py`,
  `stage.py`, `rendercache.py`, `bulk.py`/`fanout.py`, `clipcheck.py`, `mocap.py`), `player/collider.js`, the HUD and
  DOM helpers, `ui/touch|plus|menu`, `characters/pieces/`, `testChanged`/`testShards`, `ownSounds/build.py`.
- **Forge, generic vs Daggerfall-fitted:** the body, rig, skinning, shells, depth layers, render cache and bulk render
  are ours and generic. Fitted to Daggerfall: `fit.py`/`colour.py`/`skingrade.py` and all 210 `specs/*.json` (keyed by
  archive: 399, 481-488, WEAPON01-10), `reference*.mjs` reading ARENA2, colour ramps (`raceskins`, `haircolours`), and
  the output layout (records, canvases, `place`, `keys`) in `forgeLooks.json`.
- **How the game wears strips:** the studio listing (`art/studio/index.json`) names PNG strips; `forgeLayers.js`
  composes a record on demand, nearest depth per pixel, snaps to the Daggerfall palette (`dfLook`), dyes with DFU's
  tables, and stands it in place of TEXTURE.488 etc. A production build swaps each PNG for lossless WebP
  (`scripts/forgeWebp.mjs` + `tools/studioWebp.py`, flag `FORGE_WEBP`, ~62% of the bytes).
- **Generated files in git:** 117,567 forge files (~2.27 GB, nearly all layer strips) are committed, and 257 `forge-*` shard branches each
  commit their slice of PNGs. `forgeViewer/pack.py`'s WebP packs with a byte-offset index are a ready format for strips
  kept outside git.
- **Hardest seams:** the four hosts (one closure each; `world.js` 1,217 imports) carry no code over, only their wiring
  order. The Morrowind layer (`fpArm.js` 7.4k, `npcBodies`, `bodyMotion`, `mwCamera`, `ownModels`) needs the user's
  Morrowind files. `bodyMotion`'s gait and solve and `climbRig`'s reach solve are the salvage.
- **Combat split:** almost every deciding rule here is a DFU translation or a mod port (`formulas.js`,
  `playerWeapon.js`, `enemyMotor.js`, `pcaao.js`, `hostCombat.js`, `hostMagic.js`). The Rust combat has no
  DFO-original spec here beyond PvP, techniques, duels and the `infra-to-server` puppet protocol.
- **Misfiled:** `player/eyeOfTheBeholder/` is a mod port at its core, but its `layered*`/`eotb*` add-ons are ours.
  `scenes/naval/` is ours but drives Come Sail Away's runtime. `player/classSkins.js` sits on a pack's layout, its
  pictures ours. `ui/grimoire*.js` is our code dressing a third-party art pack.
- **Gates that paid:** `testChanged`/`testShards` (fast loop, balanced CI), module-load and TDZ smoke, HOST-SPLIT's
  move tool, the provenance scan. **Gates that churned:** committed mutant records (~416 re-aims), short-name spell-outs
  (~400), cite re-resolves (~241), host ceilings (whose test carries kilobytes of history on one row), and ~1,444
  source-text pins of `world.js`.
- **Typing cost:** DFO is non-strict with opt-in `@ts-check`; only about half the files here have it (characters
  25/108, src/tools 5/27). Strict TS means typing most of this area from scratch.
- Rows marked `(unread)` were placed by name and the coupling scan, without opening the file.
