# Pick - NPC Marks

DFO's marks over the people who do something: a small pixel picture bobbing over the head of each person whose click
does more than talk, so a merchant or a quest's person is found among people who look alike. The owner asked for it
to come over, over what a player can use, each wearing its keeper's own mark
([[What a Player Can Use Wears Its Mark Over It]]); this is its approval.

## What comes over

- The mark's form: a pixel picture standing on a point over the crown, turned to the eye, hidden by what stands before
  it, never narrower on the screen than a set angle of the view, faded out over the last fifth of its reach.
- Its numbers: 0.2 m over the crown, a reach of 30 m, a bob of 6 cm once a second on a phase of each mark's own,
  holding still under reduced motion, the nearest few laid.

## What stays behind

DFO's three pictures (a quest's "!", a merchant's coin, a guild's gem), as each keeper wears their own; the route a
click takes, which Overlook's counters answer themselves; its own GL pass, as Overlook lays the marks as it lays its
drops' pictures; and the switch on DFO's Features page.

Ported in [[Game]] ("Over what a player can use").
