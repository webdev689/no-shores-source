# Pick - Boss Rings

DFO's Ghostfence stood round Red Mountain: a ring of stone pylons, each holding a glowing orb in four horns, with a
pale curtain of ghost-light between them that nothing walked through. The owner chose its look and its wall for the
rings that hold a boss's fight in the world ([[Bosses in the World Fight Inside a Ring Like DFO's Ghostfence]]).

## What comes over

- The pylon's shape: a sunk plinth and its cap, an eight-sided shaft banded at its middle, a flaring collar, four
  horns curling in, and the orb they hold.
- The curtain between neighbouring pylons, from under the ground to under the collars, and solid.
- The ghost-light: the curtain shimmering, strands climbing it, a halo round each orb, brighter by night.
- Its two pictures, painted in DFO's own code: dark basalt laid in courses, and the orb's pale green.

## What stays behind

The name, the lore and Red Mountain; its 14 km ring wandered by noise, and Ghostgate, as a boss's ring has no gate;
the countryside, lairs and roads kept off its line; the far views and the pixel build that stood it, which No Shores
has no use for.
