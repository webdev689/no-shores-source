# Pick - Website

DFO Online's website: plain pages built by Vite, each page's words named rather than spelled in its markup, and every
picture but the logo drawn by code at build time in a pixel style, the same at every build
([[The Website Is at noshores.online, After DFO's]]).

## What comes over

- The way a page is made: a frame of a bar and a foot round each page, words kept apart from the markup, links by
  name, no script on a page but the bot's panel, and nothing loaded from another site.
- The kind of picture: pixel scenes drawn by our own code, of No Shores' own places.
- The look: night colours, a pixel face, framed cards.
- The Discord bot's page, from the `bot-panel` branch ([[Pick - Discord Bot]]).

## What stays behind

- The live timers ("The world right now") and the staff panel, as the owner asked.
- The Public Test Realm's gate, the account area, the halls, the status and news pages, the downloads, the guide and
  the languages: pages the first version does not have.
- Every word, picture and place of DFO's and Daggerfall's: Tamriel, the logos, the map.
