# Pick - Guilds and Heraldry

DFO's guild arms (a field, a division in a second colour, a device in its own), drawn once for every face from a
banner on a hall's door to a name plate, its guilds' powers read inside each write, and its banners hung as cloth that
sways in the wind. The owner asked for the guild system to be researched and "see what can be adapted from DFO"
([[Guilds Gain Banners, Levels, Bounties and Outposts]]); the rows this note covers wait for the owner to mark them
approved in the [[Pick List]].

## What comes over, as a specification only

- A banner of parts from fixed lists, checked by one law before it is kept, with every pair of parts that would lose
  an edge refused (DFO's border the colour of its second field lost the cloth's edge).
- One drawing for every face, from the guild's page to its tag's frame.
- A rank's right read inside the write it guards, so one demoted a moment before cannot act.
- A tag carried with the player into the room, so a change of guild reaches the plate.

## What stays behind

- Every Daggerfall and Elder Scrolls part: realm characters, Renown as a gate, siege weeks, the Iliac Bay's seats.
- DFO's 16 tinctures and 40 heraldic devices, its heraldic words, and its uploaded logos and their review.
- Editable ranks and powers: the owner's ranks are Founder, Admin and Member
  ([[The Roster and Emblems as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- No DFO code or data; the banner is drawn by our own pixel law ([[Own Work]]).

## Our version

- A banner after Destiny 2's: cut, cloth, device, sign and its ground, and a staff the guild's level opens.
- The emblems' signs and eight drawn for guilds, at whole multiples of their cells.
- System note: [[Guilds]].
