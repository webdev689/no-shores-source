# Open Questions

Decisions waiting on the owner. None is in force until the owner decides it and it moves to [[Decisions]].

## Hidden from whom?

Static isles are "hidden until discovered" ([[The World Map]]). Either:

- **Each player's own map**: an isle stays hidden for every player until they find it themselves, and shows the first
  discoverer's name once they do; or
- **Everyone's map**: an isle is hidden from all players until anyone finds it, then shows on every map with the
  discoverer's name.

## Which nine climates?

The continents start from World 2.0's ten climate profiles: the Iliac Bay and High Rock (temperate, wet), Hammerfell
(hot desert), Skyrim (cold), Morrowind (volcanic), Sumurset (mild), Valenwood (jungle), Elsweyr (dry), Black Marsh
(swamp) and the Imperial heartland (mixed). Which nine, one per continent? The names are DFO's; Overlook's continents
take their own.
