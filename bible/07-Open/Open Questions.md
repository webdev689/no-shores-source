# Open Questions

Decisions waiting on the owner. None is in force until the owner decides it and it moves to [[Decisions]].

- **Enemy factions.** Whether presence shifts over time, what sets one faction apart, and where each is present
  ([[Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe]]).
- **Being the pirate.** Whether a player may attack merchantmen for their cargo, and what notoriety it brings
  ([[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]]).
- **The opening voyage.** The prologue's first attackers at sea are the Grey Tithe's
  ([[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]), as the lore has them, unknown under grey sails. The chart that disagrees
  with the coast is not in it yet ([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]).
- **Friendly ships' port factions.** Which port faction each friendly ship belongs to
  ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]]).
- **Ship materials.** Which materials there are, what each upgrade costs, and the small islands' wreckage: built as
  Claude chose them, to change ([[Ships Have Their Own Upgrade Materials, From Ships and Island Wreckage]],
  [[Ship Power]]).
- **Crew as companions.** Which of DFO's companion systems the crew comes from: its naval `crewCompanions`, its
  fleet's `companionSlots`, or another.
- **The Grey Tithe.** Its roster, its boss on land and where its garrisons stand are built as Claude chose them, to
  rename or change ([[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]]). How deep the Covenant under them goes, and when the
  player learns it. Whether its sails are unmarked, as the lore has them, or fly its colours and flag, as its ships at
  sea do ([[The Grey Tithe Take a Tenth, and Overlook Does Not Pay]]).
- **The Lattice in the game.** Whether it comes to sea; where it stands beside the Grey Tithe's garrisons; its
  emblem; its foes' numbers on their roles, without the parries and guards the roles give, and with their own blows;
  the Heart's fight, its phases and the Husks it tears out. Its looks and moves are
  Claude's to change ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]], [[The Lattice as Claude Shapes It]]).
- **The Lattice's armour and weapons.** How its red and purple share the set, and its shapes; whether the Atheric
  gear keeps it where Daedric stood; what the horse's Daedric barding becomes, since the set leaves it out and its
  name is Bethesda's ([[The Lattice's Armour and Weapons Take Daedric's Place]], [[Gear]]).
- **The Lattice's places and events**, being talked through with the owner
  ([[The Lattice Comes Into the Game Meaningfully, Talked Through First]]): the nine story missions' shape (three acts
  of three, one to a port, or one chain through the islands), when the player first meets the Lattice, where the Heart
  falls and how near the ninth comes to the Cube; who gives the side quests between them; where its corrupted areas lie
  and where its props stand ([[The Lattice Has Props of Its Own, Made in Blender]]).
- **The public events**, built on Claude's calls ([[Public Events as Claude Builds Them]]), each the owner's to change:
  the Heart not yet rendered; the events at the top of Saltmere's band; the Grey Tithe's garrison joining the
  Fissure's fight; other players' numbers crowding an event; the Tithe-Taker without a ledger; a leaver's vault past
  its 20; the end chest and the heroic task, which the offers and the owner called Destiny 1's, are Destiny 2's.
- **Revives, respawns and relogs**, built on Claude's calls ([[Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them]]),
  each the owner's to change: whether an Elite Dungeon's Court is a darkness zone below Abyss, as Destiny's dungeons
  make their bosses'; the 2 s hold and the 2.5 m reach; whether any player in a room may revive, or only the company;
  whether a mission's and a shared dungeon's won groups should hold their ground as a run's bands do, where now they
  stand again and the checkpoint falls back; and whether logging back in at sea keeps the voyage's tier, Normal now.
- **The one mystery.** What the Cube is called, how long it stays unseen, and what calls in the deep
  ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]).
- **The islands.** The owner's "Islands dont come and go" beside the discoveries that fade when nobody is near
  ([[The World Map]]) and the small islands of the sea that fade once nothing holds them ([[Islands]], "Held and
  faded").
- **The factions' people.** The Stormcallers' and the Veiled's keepers in Overlook have no names of their own yet, as
  the Lamplighters' Elder Maren Hale has; their speakers are "Stormcaller" and "The Veiled"
  ([[Factions and Vendors as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- **A faction's rank 25.** Destiny 1's rank 25 opened a quest for an exotic class item; whether a faction's high ranks
  give anything beyond packages ([[Factions]]).
- **Reputation's sources.** Whether bounties and activities should give reputation ([[Factions]]). Until the owner
  says, Claude's calls stand: every deed's XP gives it with the cape worn
  ([[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]]), and a faction's own bounties pay it besides
  ([[The Bounty Board as Claude Shapes It]]).
- **The faction bounties.** Built on "take autonomy": three a day from each faction, taken only pledged to it,
  counting only with its cape worn, paying XP and its reputation, and their twelve names
  ([[The Bounty Board as Claude Shapes It]]). Whether that is the owner's design, and whether "their own standard icon
  design" means one icon for every faction's bounties or, as built, each faction's emblem in one framed design.
- **The bounties' cards.** Destiny's card carries a line of its bounty's tale under its task; none is written, as the
  owner's "Not text bloat" stood, so whether each bounty gains one ([[The Bounty Board as Claude Shapes It]]).
- **Shape's hours.** Shape is in Overlook from Friday 09:00 to Sunday 21:00 UTC; whether the evening he leaves on is
  another time or another zone's ([[Gear]], "Vendors").
- **The Tack's wares.** The horse has the four bardings the forge draws and six coats laid on its colours; more
  looks need the forge to draw them ([[Gear]], "Cosmetics").
- **The welcome's mark.** The story, and the stranger's welcome at its end, are marked played in the browser, so a
  second account on one browser never sees them (her gift is given it all the same, as it arrives) and one account on
  a new browser sees them again; whether they should be kept with the character in its room ([[Prologue]]).
- **What others see.** The room puts the cosmetics and faction colours a player has earned into the dress others see,
  and keeps a worn set or metal only of its piece's own content
  ([[The Room Keeps Only Looks of a Piece's Own Content]]); the garments drawn are still each page's own word ([[Server]]).
- **The Abyss Dungeons' crossbows.** The forge draws no adamantium or ebony crossbow, so they show the nearest metal it
  draws (mithril); their own art waits (the owner: "Not now").
- **The balance pass's open point** ([[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]]): Conduit (Deepwatch) brings
  a Tenet back about every 27 s against 105.
- **Signing the desktop app.** Unsigned, its Windows installer meets "Windows protected your PC" and its Mac app opens
  only from Privacy & Security, and cannot update itself. Signing takes the owner's accounts and keys: Apple's
  Developer Program for a Mac (a Developer ID and notarizing), and for Windows a code-signing certificate or Azure's
  Artifact Signing ([[Desktop App]], "Releases and downloads").
