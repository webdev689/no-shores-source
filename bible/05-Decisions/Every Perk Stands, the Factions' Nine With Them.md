# Every Perk Stands, the Factions' Nine With Them

Shown every perk in the game, the factions' nine as Claude drafted them among them, the owner said:

> "Lets go through the entire perk list and lll approve, disapprove or suggest a change"

and, having gone through it:

> "All approved"

## What it settles

- **Every perk in the game stands as it is**: the weapons' and armour's shared pool, each Atheric's intrinsic and
  signature, the ship's modules' and the crew's ([[Gear]]).
- **The factions' nine perks are approved as drafted**, three a faction, two for weapons and one for armour, each tied
  to its Art's element ([[Set Names and Faction Perks as Claude Drafts Them]]), and built for a faction's pieces to roll
  beside the shared pool ([[Faction Weapons Wear Their Colour and Roll Their Faction's Perks]]).
- **The set names are not part of it**: they wait on the owner ("Not yet").

How the rules carry the nine: [[Gear]] ("Verbs") and [[Factions]], with Claude's calls in
[[Gear as Claude Shapes It]] ("The factions' perks").
