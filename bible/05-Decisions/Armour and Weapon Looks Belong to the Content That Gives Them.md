# Armour and Weapon Looks Belong to the Content That Gives Them

> "Going through and organizing each armor style/weapon style.
>
> - World Specific
> - Faction Specific
> - Dungeon Specific
> - Elite Dungeon Specific
> - Abyss Dungeon Specific
> - Sea Dungeon Specific
> - Breach Specific (Defered)
>
> So basically the models of armor and weapons should be strictly attached to their respective content. Armor for
> factions is already settled."

> "Please go above and beyond with this, take autonomy, and return when everything is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- **Every armour set and weapon metal belongs to one kind of content**: the world, a faction, a dungeon, an Elite
  Dungeon, an Abyss Dungeon, a sea dungeon or a Breach. A look seen on a player says where it was earned.
- **The factions' armour stays as it is**: each faction's guild set and metal in its own colour
  ([[Each Faction Sells Gear of Its Own, in Its Own Colour]]).
- **Breaches' looks wait** with the Breaches ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]]).
- **Which look goes to which content is Claude's call**, on the owner's autonomy, recorded in
  [[The Playlist and Its Dungeons as Claude Shapes Them]] and open to the owner's change.

How it works: [[Gear]], "Looks".
