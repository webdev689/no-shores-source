# The Hub's Plants Are the World's Low-Poly Ones

The owner, shown the hub's courts with their trees and beds ([[The Hub's Paths Are Smooth and Rounded]]): "One more
note: any flora like bushes or trees needs to use the low poly versions we use in the world"

## What it settles

- **Every bush and tree in the hub is one of the world's own low-poly plants**, as the land's nature draws them, not
  a model of the hub's own.

How it is done is Claude's: [[Overlook's Hub as Claude Shapes It]].
