# A Held Aim Keeps the Camera on Its Side

> "When aiming on the sides of your ship, holding aiming shouldnt allow you to rotate your camera out of aiming."

## What it settles

- While the aim is held in a sea fight, the camera cannot be turned out of it: the look stays where the side's guns
  bear.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The side held (replaced in part: the nearer side as the aim is taken, [[Aiming a Ship Looks Square Off Its Side, Clear of Its Rig]]) | The side the look first lays on after the aim is taken, until it is let go | Aiming taken over the bow is not snapped round; once on a side, it stays |
| The arc (replaced: the look stands square off the side, [[Aiming a Ship Looks Square Off Its Side, Clear of Its Rig]]) | The guns' own, past 35° off the bow and short of 40° off the stern (the rules' `BOW_ARC_DEG`, `STERN_ARC_DEG`), held a degree inside its ends | Out of it the guns lay nothing, and the aim's marks are gone |
| The ship turning (replaced: the look stands square off the side, [[Aiming a Ship Looks Square Off Its Side, Clear of Its Rig]]) | Holds the look in the arc too | Turning under a held aim must not take it out either |
