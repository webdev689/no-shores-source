# The Sea Fight's Camera Keeps Its Zoom, and a Hostile Ship Seen by Night Is One to Click

Asked whether the mouse wheel should stop zooming the sea fight's camera when the player is not aiming, as it stopped
in the Oversea view, the owner chose:

> "Keep it"

Told that at night a dark Tithe ship can be seen farther than 220 m away but clicked or chased only within 220 m, and
asked which it should be, the owner chose:

> "Clickable if seen"

## What it settles

- **Out of the aim, the wheel still zooms the sea fight's camera.** Only the Oversea view lost its zoom
  ([[A Held Aim Swings Between the Sides, the Oversea View Keeps Its Height, and No Ship Is Sailed Through]]).
- **A hostile ship that can be seen can be clicked and chased.** By night a dark ship drawn past its night sight is
  still one to click, and a chase of it holds while it is drawn. What it is sighted and marked within is unchanged
  ([[Night Sight Reaches Every Sighting at Sea, Stern Windows Burn, and Fight Effects Improve]]).
