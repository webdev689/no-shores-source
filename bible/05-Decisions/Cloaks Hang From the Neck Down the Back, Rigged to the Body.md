# Cloaks Hang From the Neck Down the Back, Rigged to the Body

The owner, on the cloaks the forge draws:

> "I noticed when running with the cape equipped. It disappears in one of the frames"

> "Honestly capes as they stand are way too complicated. Can we just redesign this properly from the ground up"

> Offered ways to redesign it, the owner chose: "Rigged cape"

> "I think the cloak design itself could be slimmer. Where it doesnt interact with the shoulders", then "Slimmer",
> then "Slim 0.30"

> "Can you also make it where the top of the cloak doesn't stretch to the shoulders", with a picture of a hooded
> cloak hanging from the neck down the back, as Destiny's hunters wear theirs: "like this"

> On the pilot: "I love the look but it needs a clipping pass", then "I think the top should spread out a little
> more", then "Its perfect"

## What it settles

- A cloak is hung once on the body standing and worn on its bones after, its cloth swung on chains, so no frame
  loses it ([[Sprite Forge]]).
- Only its middle is worn, from the nape down the back between the shoulder blades, never over the shoulders' tops:
  0.38 of its width round the body across its top, drawing in to 0.30 of it below.
- As the pilot the owner approved shows: a hood on a cloak ends in a short cowl round the neck, the cloak hanging
  from under it, and no clasp crosses the throat.
- Its top rides the shoulders as they move, so the body does not show through it.
