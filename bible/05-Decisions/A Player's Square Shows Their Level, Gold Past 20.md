# A Player's Square Shows Their Level, Gold Past 20

Where the square over another player's head showed their Prestige from level 20, the owner wrote:

> "Instead of ptestige showing on players icon after level 20. Instead show their following levels in gold on the
> player icon"

Asked whether level 20 itself should be gold, or only the levels past it, the owner chose "21 and up (Recommended)",
offered as "1 to 20 white as now; 21 to 30, the levels Prestige earns, in gold."

## What it settles

- **The square shows the player's level always**, never their Prestige.
- **Levels 21 to 30 show in gold**; 1 to 20 as before.
