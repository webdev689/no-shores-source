# The Lattice's Armour and Weapons Take Daedric's Place

> Asked during the Lattice's audit pass, to follow it: "When this is all finished. I want to replace the deadric armor
> with this factions new armor set and weapons across the board"

> Asked which of the Lattice's two crystals it is made of: "A mix of purple and red". Asked how much of the Lattice's
> horror a player's armour shows, offered crystal only, crystal on sinew, or full gore: "Crystal only". Asked where it
> replaces Daedric, offered armour in third person, first person, weapons and shields, and the horse's armour:
> "Armour, third person", "First person", "Weapons and shields". Asked its name in the game: "Lattice".

> Shown a first look, Daedric's set in the two crystals with crystal grown out of it: "This aint good, chief". Asked
> what was off: "You need to completely redesign the armor sets/gear", "Still Daedric underneath". Asked which way to
> take it, offered crystal grown into armour ("No metal at all: faceted crystal plates grown in the shape of armour,
> dark at the roots and lit at the edges, clusters only at the shoulders and crown"), shard lamellar, crystal through
> iron, or the owner's own: "Crystal grown into armour".

> Shown it drawn as faceted shells and plates: "This is so ass". Asked what to build from: "Use blender and create
> this from scratch". Then, with a picture of crystal plate armour: "This is the reference. Daggerfall style, proper
> shading."

> Shown the set built from it, standing still from three sides: "I like it"

## What it settles

- The forge's Daedric armour set and its Daedric weapons are replaced by the Lattice's own
  ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]): in the armour seen in third person, in first person, and
  in the weapons and shields. The horse's armour is not among them.
- It is called Lattice.
- It is made of both of the Lattice's crystals, red and purple.
- It is crystal only: no flesh or blood on it.
- It is designed anew in Blender, nothing of Daedric's set under it: crystal grown into armour, no metal.
- It follows the owner's reference picture: a full suit of crystal plate, a tall closed helm with crest blades swept
  back, large flared pauldrons with jagged points, a sculpted breastplate with a high collar, a studded belt with a
  long pointed plate hanging in front, jagged cuisses, greaves and sabatons, spiked vambraces, the plates strapped
  on with leather over a dark suit. The picture is the owner's to keep; it is not in this repository.
- It is drawn in Daggerfall's style, properly shaded: built from the forge's own armour kit, its crystal graded red
  with purple in its shadows, every plate's edge lit purple, strapped with leather over a dark suit
  (`tools/lattice/armoury.py`, [[Lattice]]). The owner likes it.
- It follows the Lattice's audit and detailed pass ([[The Lattice Is Drawn Whole Before It Comes Into the Game]]).

How the two crystals share the set is not settled ([[Open Questions]]). Daedric's place among the gear is in
[[Gear]] and [[Gear as Claude Shapes It]].
