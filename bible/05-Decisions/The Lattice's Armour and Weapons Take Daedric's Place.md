# The Lattice's Armour and Weapons Take Daedric's Place

> Asked during the Lattice's audit pass, to follow it: "When this is all finished. I want to replace the deadric armor
> with this factions new armor set and weapons across the board"

## What it settles

- The forge's Daedric armour set and its Daedric weapons are replaced by a new armour set and weapons of the Lattice's
  ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]), across the board.
- It follows the Lattice's audit and detailed pass ([[The Lattice Is Drawn Whole Before It Comes Into the Game]]).

How the set looks, what it is called, and which pieces and views it covers are not settled ([[Open Questions]]).
Daedric's place among the gear is in [[Gear]] and [[Gear as Claude Shapes It]].
