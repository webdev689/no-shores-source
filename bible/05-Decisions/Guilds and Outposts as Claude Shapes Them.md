# Guilds and Outposts as Claude Shapes Them

The owner handed the guild system over: "This is your baby to curate and I want it to be AAA grade"
([[Guilds Gain Banners, Levels, Bounties and Outposts]]). What the owner decided is in that note and in
[[The Roster and Emblems as Claude Shapes Them]] (ranks, the hundred, names and tags). Everything below is Claude's,
made under that word, and the owner may change any of it. The numbers live in `content/guilds/`, which the rules, the
accounts and the page all read.

## What came from where

Destiny 2's clans (Bungie's Clans Guide; the clan screen the owner showed): a clan has a name, a tag of up to four, a
motto and a banner of a cloth shape and its colour, a detail and its colour, and an emblem with its own colour and
ground, hung from a staff. Members earn clan XP, each at most 5,000 a week, and the clan rises in level. Each week a
clan may earn up to four engrams for activities played together, claimed once a week. DFO had no guild levels, perks or
bounties; it had arms of a field, a division and a device, cheap hanging cloth, a treasury kept on a ledger, halls,
fortification works built in tiers from silver and delivered materials, and a decor placer
([[Pick - Guilds and Heraldry]], [[Pick - Halls, Works and Decor]]). Guild Wars 2's halls are funded by favour from
missions and by a mine that fills over time; Albion's guild islands are bought land with buildings on them.

## Levels and contribution

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contribution | A tenth of the XP a member earns from kills, clears and bounties, at most 5,000 a week a player | Destiny 2's 5,000 a week; a tenth makes it a few evenings' play |
| Guild XP | Every contribution, and 2,500 for each guild bounty the guild completes | Destiny's clan XP came from play and from milestones |
| Levels | Ten: 15,000 for level 2, then 40,000, 80,000, 140,000, 220,000, 320,000, 450,000, 600,000 and 800,000 | Destiny 2's clans rose through six; ten gives a guild a longer road. Ten players at their weekly most reach level 10 in about three months |
| Kept | A level reached is never lost; a member who leaves leaves their contribution behind | Destiny's clan levels were kept; nothing a guild earned is taken back |
| What a level brings | Staffs, outposts, structures and their tiers, as the table under Outposts says | Each level is something to build or show |

## Guild bounties

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How many | Four a week, drawn by the week from twelve, the same four for every guild | Destiny 2's four weekly clan engrams |
| What they ask | Felling, gathering, sinking and clearing, as the game's bounties count them ([[Activities]]); some ask for two of the guild in one run | Destiny's engrams needed the clan to play together |
| Progress | Shared: what each member does counts toward the guild's total | Engrams were earned together |
| Done | The guild gains 2,500 XP; each member may claim it once that week from the Guild tab | Destiny's engrams, claimed once a week |
| The claim | A guild package: 1,000 Aurem and a Vestige at the player's level, as a faction's package | One kind of package in the game ([[Factions]]) |
| Who claims | A member who was in the guild before the week turned | So no one hops from guild to guild collecting the week's claims |

## The treasury

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Aurem the guild keeps, up to 1,000,000 | DFO's treasury |
| In | Any member puts in their own Aurem, standing at one of the guild's outposts; the outposts' income | A guild funds its own works, and a reason to sail there |
| Out | Only onto the guild's outposts, by the founder and admins; never back to a player | DFO found a treasury paid out to players could be made to mint; a one-way treasury cannot |
| Kept | On a ledger: each line names who, what and the balance after, and a request id (the room's errand id, or the page's `rid`) so a retried ask is one line | DFO's ledger and its idempotent asks |

## Outposts

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where | One outpost site on each of the nine islands: a cove on its island's coast, a node of a kind of its own reached from the sea as any node is | The owner's islands are the nine continents ([[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]), whose land beyond the nodes is kept for later |
| Whose | Each guild raises its own outpost at a site, apart from every other guild's: the site is a room for each guild | No guild can take another's ground or wait for it; the owner's "almost sharded shared instances" ([[No Shores]]) |
| How many | One from level 1, a second at level 4, a third at level 7, each on a different island | "outposts on any island": more islands, more of their resources |
| The cost | Raising one: 5,000 Aurem from the treasury | "for a cost" |
| Who acts | The founder and admins raise, build and decorate; every member may visit, deliver, collect and take from the stores | Destiny's founders and admins run the clan |
| Visitors | Members, and anyone in a company with a member there | The Roster's join ([[The Roster and Emblems as Claude Shapes Them]]) |
| The plot | 64 m square of level ground, cut into 4 m cells, with its hall raised in the middle at its founding | A grid keeps structures apart and every check plain |
| Building | A structure placed costs its Aurem from the treasury at once; members then deliver its materials at the outpost; it stands the moment the last arrives | DFO's works were built from silver and delivered materials |
| Tiers | Three a structure; the hall's tier is the highest any other may reach | DFO's fortification tiers |
| Removing | Half its Aurem back to the treasury; materials are spent | DFO's half back |
| Upkeep | None | Each guild has its own ground, so none is hoarded; upkeep would tax play away |
| Abandoning | The founder may, for half the Aurem spent on it back | A guild may move to another island |
| Effects | Each counts once for the guild: the highest tier standing at any of its outposts, for every member wherever they play | A buff never stacks by building it twice |

### The structures

| Structure | Cells | Opens at | What it does, by tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hall: Command Tent, Longhouse, Stronghold | 4 × 4 | Founding | The outpost's heart; its tier caps the rest; it holds 40, 80 or 150 pieces of decoration |
| Banner Standard | 1 × 1 | Founding | The guild's banner on its staff, in the wind |
| Counting House | 2 × 2 | Level 1 | Its strongbox fills with 30, 60 or 120 Aurem an hour and holds two days; a member empties it into the treasury |
| War Table | 2 × 2 | Level 2 | Members earn 5, 10 or 15% more XP from kills and clears |
| Island Camp | 2 × 2 | Level 2 | Gathers 15, 30 or 60 of its island's resource a day into the outpost's stores |
| Dockyard | 2 × 3 | Level 3 | Brings in 8, 16 or 32 each of Timber, Canvas and Iron a day into the stores |
| Delve Gate | 3 × 3 | Level 4 | Opens the guild dungeon, laid afresh each week in its site's continent, at Normal, Elite or Abyss |
| Captain's Lodge | 2 × 2 | Level 5 | Members earn 5, 10 or 15% more faction reputation |
| War Horn | 2 × 2 | Level 5 | Sounded once a week, calls the guild boss to the muster ground |
| Assay House | 2 × 2 | Level 8 | Members earn 5, 10 or 15% more Aurem from kills and clears |

Tier 2 opens at level 3 and tier 3 at level 6, each needing the hall at that tier. What each costs is in
`content/guilds/outposts.json`: Aurem, and Timber, Canvas, Iron, Heartwood and the island's resource, more at each
tier, so a tier 3 asks for many members' stores.

Claude's call on the materials: across one outpost with every structure at tier 3,
Timber, Canvas and Iron are asked about evenly (1,940 : 1,600 : 1,730), within 1.3 to 1 of each other, because flotsam
and the Dockyard give them evenly, so a guild holds none it cannot spend. Each structure keeps its own lean (the
Dockyard Canvas, the Assay House and the Delve Gate Iron). Heartwood, rare in flotsam and given by no Dockyard, is asked
only at tier 3 of the Hall (30), the Delve Gate (25), the Assay House (20) and the War Horn (15): 90 an outpost, about
45 Breaches. The Aurem and the island's resource are as they were.
