# The World Is Claude's to Mold

> "I want you to take autonomy and do everything yourself without my input to 100%. Be as detailed as possible, take
> your time, use destiny as a reference and ensure this is perfection. This is your baby to mold.
>
> Only return to me when this is 100%, no exceptions"

## What it settles

- How the world works, feels and looks ([[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]) is Claude's to design
  and build, with Destiny 1 as the reference, without asking the owner along the way.
- Claude records each choice it makes in [[The World as Claude Shapes It]], as [[The Continents' Biomes]] records
  Claude's choice of biomes. The owner can change any of them.
- The [[Rules]] still hold, and so does [[Ask Before Deploying]]: the work comes back to the owner finished, and
  merging and deploying wait for the owner's yes.
