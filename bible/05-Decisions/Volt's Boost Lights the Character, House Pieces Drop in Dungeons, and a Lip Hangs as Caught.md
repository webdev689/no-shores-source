# Volt's Boost Lights the Character, House Pieces Drop in Dungeons, and a Lip Hangs as Caught

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on fighting and gear.

Asked whether Conduit (Deepwatch) bringing a Tenet back about every 27 s stands, the owner answered:

> "Keep. One note is the volt's boost charge effect needs to be redesigned. Have it highlight the character instead of
> the trail"

Asked whether the healing drink is a heal that Exposure halves, the owner chose:

> "Yes, halve it"

Asked whether a lip caught off square should be hung square, or as caught, the owner chose:

> "Hang as caught"

Asked whether an old save reaching "Put them on" or "Arm yourself" should buy the Ranged weapon or be given it, the
owner chose:

> "Give it free"

Asked whether "the world's" Vestiges, from which a house piece may drop, leave out the dungeons, the owner answered:

> "Dungeons too"

Asked which, the owner chose:

> "Dungeons,Elite/Abyss,Sea dungeons,Breaches"

## What it settles

- **Conduit stands** ([[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]]).
- **Volt's boost charge is redesigned to light the character**, not a trail ([[Combat]]).
- **Exposure halves the healing drink** as it halves every heal ([[Activities]]).
- **A lip is hung as it was caught**, not turned square ([[Movement]]).
- **An old save is given the Ranged weapon** at "Put them on" or "Arm yourself"
  ([[The Welcome's Gifts, the Hordes, the Ride and the Lessons as Claude Builds Them]]).
- **A house piece may drop in the dungeons too**: the patrols' dungeons, the Elite and Abyss Dungeons, the sea's
  dungeons and the Breaches ([[Magic and Rare House Pieces Drop in the World, for the Pledged House]]).

Not built yet: all of it but Conduit, which stands.
