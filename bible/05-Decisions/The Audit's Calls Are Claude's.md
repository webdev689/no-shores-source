# The Audit's Calls Are Claude's

> Handed the calls the gear loop's audit left open: "Do another detailed pass. All decisions are yours"

## What it settles

The calls the first audit of the gear loop left to the owner are Claude's. Each is Claude's choice under these
words, with its reason; the owner can change any of them. The rules ([[Rules]]), keys, security and deploys are not
among them: those still need the owner ([[Ask Before Deploying]]).

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hardy and a blocked telegraphed blow | Hardy softens every telegraphed blow, the share a guard lets through as well | Its words say telegraphed blows strike softer, and a guard's share is one of them |
| Executioner and ripostes | A riposte is not counted | Combat does not count the stagger in a riposte, which is the stagger's own answer; so Executioner rewards a stagger earned by poise |
| Opportunist with Slipstream | They multiply, to 1.875 times on a counter | Slipstream's words: half again as hard as a counter does, Opportunist's counter among them; an Atheric's own is meant to stack |
| Sessions | One with a username or Discord lapses after 30 days unseen; a guest's never does; any account with a way back in may sign out everywhere | A guest's token is its only way back, so it cannot lapse; the rest can sign in again |
| The Workers plan | A sign-in keeps PBKDF2 at 100,000 iterations | The most a Worker derives; fewer would weaken every password to save a plan's CPU |
| Held presses | Dismantle, buying and a node's purchase are held; so are signing out and a guest signing in | Destiny 1 held what could not be undone, and an upgrade |
| Storm seas | Three levels higher, not four | Four is the gap at which a player's blows do nothing: a player at the top of an island's band must still strike its foes |
| Rarity colours | The owner's: Normal white, Magic blue, Rare yellow, Legendary gold, Atheric cyan blue | [[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]] names them; Normal's frame is the kit's plain stone, so it never outshines a finer piece |
