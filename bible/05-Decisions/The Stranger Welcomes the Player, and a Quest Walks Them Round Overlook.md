# The Stranger Welcomes the Player, and a Quest Walks Them Round Overlook

> "NEW TUTORIAL:
>
> - After being lead into town, the noc who found you should begin talking to you as soon as you arrive.
>
> - You start with no armor, no weapon, no mount, absolutely nothing. The NPC tells you a little history pertaining to
>   lore, and about the places to visit in overlook. As they speak, the camera should navigate to each of the talked
>   about locations until finally coming back to the player, the NPC wishing you the best, handing you some aurem to
>   get started, and bidding you farewell.
>
> - The quest now guides you to each vendor, explains their functions, leads to buy simple start gear, and eventually
>   ends with introducing you to each faction.
>
> From this point you pledge as usual."

## What it settles

- **The stranger who found the player on the shore speaks to them as they arrive in Overlook**: a little of the
  world's history and the places to visit there, the camera going to each place she names and back to the player;
  then she wishes them well, gives them some Aurem to start with, and says farewell.
- **The player arrives with nothing**: no armour, no weapon, no mount.
- **The quest then guides them to each vendor**, says what each is for, has them buy simple starting gear, and ends by
  introducing each faction. The pledge follows as before ([[Quests]]).
