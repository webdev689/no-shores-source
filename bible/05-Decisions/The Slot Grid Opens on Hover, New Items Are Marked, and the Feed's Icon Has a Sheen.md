# The Slot Grid Opens on Hover, New Items Are Marked, and the Feed's Icon Has a Sheen

A slot's grid on the character page opened with a click and closed once the pointer left it
([[The Slot Grid Closes Off Hover, and the Inventory Shows Only Held Stacks]]). The owner wrote:

> "Can we also improve the pickup icon visual, give it a sheen and in the inventory a notifcation display over the
> slot that has new items in it? Also the grid should show up and close just by hovering, no clicks required"

The owner then chose from Claude's options:

| Asked | The owner's pick | The option as offered |
|---|---|---|
| Which pickup icon gets the sheen | "The loot feed's icon" | The item picture that slides in at the side of the screen when you pick something up: a light glint sweeps across it |
| How a slot with new items is marked | "Dot" | A small glowing dot in the corner, no number; clears once seen |

## What it settles

- **The loot feed's item picture has a sheen**: a light glint sweeps across it as it shows ([[Interface]]).
- **A slot holding items not yet seen shows a small glowing dot** in its corner on the character page, and the dot
  clears once its items have been seen in that slot's grid.
- **A slot's grid opens as the pointer comes onto the slot and closes as it leaves**, no click needed, as it closed
  before.

It replaces in part [[The Slot Grid Closes Off Hover, and the Inventory Shows Only Held Stacks]]: the grid now opens on
hover too.
