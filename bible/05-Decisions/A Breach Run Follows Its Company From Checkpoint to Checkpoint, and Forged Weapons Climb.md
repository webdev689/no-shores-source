# A Breach Run Follows Its Company From Checkpoint to Checkpoint, and Forged Weapons Climb

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on the first Breach, the Drowned Vale.

Asked whether a run's company is fixed at launch, or follows the accounts' company as it changes, the owner answered:

> "#2 + add checkpoints to each encounter start and a way to reset checkpoints in the playlist ui"

(its second choice being that the run follows the company). Asked whether launching again within the gate's day
returns the run whose gate stands open, or starts a new one, the owner answered:

> "See #2"

Asked whether that means launching again returns to the last checkpoint, and the Playlist's reset starts the run
fresh, the owner answered:

> "Yes, that's it"

Asked whether the gate's state should outlive an eviction or a deploy, the owner answered:

> "Yes, keep it"

Asked whether the Unlit Ways wait on every living player of the company, or only those in the Ways, the owner chose:

> "Every living player"

Asked whether a Forged weapon should differ from its Normal one in more than its name, element and perk grades, the
owner answered:

> "Not true"

Shown the code (a Forged weapon has its name, always an element, and its stronger perks, but only Hard's armour climbs
to Prestige 30), the owner chose:

> "Weapons climb too"

Asked, as the work was audited, whether a Forged weapon should out-hit a Normal one at level 30 too, where a weapon's
reach is held to the foe's level and the extra Attack did nothing, the owner chose:

> "Yes, make it count"

## What it settles

- **A run follows the company** as it changes, not the company it was launched for ([[Breaches]]).
- **Each encounter's start is a checkpoint.** Launching again returns to the last checkpoint reached; the Playlist
  has a way to reset the checkpoints, which starts the run fresh.
- **The gate's state outlives an eviction, a restart or a deploy.**
- **The Unlit Ways wait on every living player of the company**; the fallen are not counted
  ([[The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It]]).
- **Forged weapons climb further**, as Hard's armour does: weapons carry no Prestige, so theirs is Attack, 263 to 315
  against Normal's 248 to 300, and it counts at level 30 against Hard's foes too
  ([[Breach Weapons Come Normal and Forged]]).

Built ([[Breaches]], [[Gear]]; Claude's calls in [[The Open Questions' Answers as Claude Builds Them]]). Already so: the
Unlit Ways waited on every living player, and a run let in its company's members as the company stood; only its relaunch
window, 30 minutes, is now the gate's day.
