# The Sloop's Trim

> Asked whether the Sloop sits right, its waterline taken 0.4 m above its model's z = 0, and what the small cube at
> its bow is: "Your choice"

## What it settles

The owner left both to Claude, who chose:

- Its waterline stays 0.4 m above its model's z = 0, where its beam is 1.86 m of its widest 2.0 m: its keel reaches
  0.6 m below it and its deck stands about 0.6 m above it ([[Sailing]]).
- The small cube on its foredeck stays as modelled: a bitt for its anchor line, drawn with it.
