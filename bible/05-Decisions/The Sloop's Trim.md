# The Sloop's Trim

> Asked whether the Sloop sits right, her waterline taken 0.4 m above her model's z = 0, and what the small cube at
> her bow is: "Your choice"

## What it settles

The owner left both to Claude, who chose:

- Her waterline stays 0.4 m above her model's z = 0, where her beam is 1.86 m of its widest 2.0 m: her keel reaches
  0.6 m below it and her deck stands about 0.6 m above it ([[Sailing]]).
- The small cube on her foredeck stays as modelled: a bitt for her anchor line, drawn with her.
