# The Key Strip Follows the Moment, and K Opens Every Control

The key strip at the bottom right listed every key of a mode at once, the mouse's buttons in words ("Right button",
"Wheel"). The owner asked:

> "rework the bottom right control keybinds to be something more user friendly/detailed"

Asked how, the owner chose from Claude's options:

| Asked | The owner's pick | The option as offered |
|---|---|---|
| How the keys are shown | "Context strip + full sheet (Recommended)" | The strip shows only what applies right now (near a keeper: E and their name; aiming: release to fire), with drawn mouse-button and keycap icons and plain verbs. A key (K) opens a full controls sheet grouped Move, Fight, Arts, Camera, Menus, each control with a sentence on what it does. |

## What it settles

- The key strip shows only the keys that apply at the moment, in plain verbs, each key on its keycap and the mouse's
  buttons drawn ([[Interface]], "The key strip").
- Near something E uses, E and what it does show.
- K opens a sheet of every control, grouped Move, Fight, Arts, Camera and Menus, each control with a sentence on
  what it does ([[Interface]], "The sheet of every control").

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| E | The prompt for E stays as it is and the strip leaves E out on foot | The prompt already shows E and the deed by name |
| On foot | Weapon put away: Z, Shift, Space, C, the menu. Melee drawn: swing, guard or parry, dodge, drink, the Ranged loadout. Ranged drawn: aim, fire, dodge, drink, the Melee loadout. Aiming: fire, let go to stop aiming, dodge. Dusk Daggers aimed: let go of Q to cast. Riding: get down, gallop, the menu. R, Q and G while an Art is worn and the hands are free. The click to look round first while the mouse is free. Dead: Enter once death is told | What each moment can do, the rest left to the sheet |
| At sea | Sailing: the click, A and D, W, S and the menu (the sea bar names its own keys). Manage: drag, wheel, B. Deck: Shift, Space, E. Map and landing: their clicks, Enter, Escape. A fight: the helm, the sails, the burst, the aim and the brace; aiming: let go to fire, hold fire, the shot, the drag | The owner's "aiming: release to fire" is the sea fight's own aim |
| The strip's length | Eight entries at most, K All controls always last | "Only what applies right now", and the way to the rest from any moment |
| Shadow's Dusk Daggers | No longer named while Shadow is only worn: once aimed, the strip says to let go of Q, and the sheet says how they aim | It keeps the strip short and shows the key when it applies |
| The sea's groups | Two more groups, Sea and Sea fight, after the owner's five | The sea has keys of its own |
| The drawn mouse | 7 by 11 texels, the named button, wheel, side button or drag arrow lit in brass, each texel a pixel larger than the words' step, edged dark over the world; the arrow keys drawn on their caps | Read at a glance beside 11 px words; the face has no arrows |
| Where K works | Wherever the strip shows, but not over the menu's gear screen; K, Escape or Close shuts it | The story's tap, its windows and its cutscenes show no strip, and the gear screen has the keys |
| While the sheet is open | No key reaches the game and its scrim takes the pointer; the world goes on | As under the menu |
| The sheet's layout | 400 px columns, as many as the view holds, each near an even share of the rows; the list scrolls; the focused control lit in brass, read aloud with its keys | Fits a phone and 1440p alike |
| A phone's strip | 240 px wide in a view under 720 px | Such a view left it no room at all |

These calls take the place of the key strip's calls in [[Quests Get a Task Tracker, and the Placeholder Panel Goes]]
(the click to look round always shown) and [[Three Healing Charges, Refilled by Blows and Kills]] ("3 Heal" after
"1 / 2 Loadout").
