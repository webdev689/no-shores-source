# Foes Leave a Player Arriving Through a Passage Gate Be, and No Band Stands Near the Arrival

> "going through a gate has issues where you immediately die to enemies"

> Asked how a player arriving through a passage gate is kept from that (a band of foes stood 11 to 14 m from the
> arrival, and the room counted the player a target while the page was still bringing them in), the owner chose
> "Grace + bands further out": "Foes leave you be from joining until 10 s after you arrive (striking first ends it, as
> after a revive), and no band stands within 25 m of the arrival."

## What it settles

- **A player joining a passage zone's room** ([[Passages]]) is left be by its foes from joining until 10 s after
  they arrive; striking first ends it, as after a revive ([[Revives]]).
- **No band of foes stands within 25 m of a zone's arrival.**
