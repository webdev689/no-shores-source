# The Seas Chosen Bring Their Weather, and a Storm Is the Mystery's

Told that the weather's sea states and the seas a voyage chooses share the names calm, rough and storm, and asked
whether choosing Storm brings a storm:

> "Your thoughts?"

Offered the seas chosen as a floor on the weather: Calm the natural weather, Rough never calmer than rough seas,
Storm always a storm, on the voyage and over the island landed on:

> "I like it, but storm should stick with the lore and have an odd feel. Like red lighting, a swirling sky, etc"

Offered the Storm as the one mystery's weather, shown and never explained: from afar only dark cloud and heavy seas;
under it red lightning, a sky turning slowly round a point as if on an axis it should not have, clouds lit from
inside, and now and then something out of step, as rain hanging a moment or swells against the wind; no entity, no
rift, nothing in the sky to point at; Overlook's people calling it a red storm and no more:

> "I love it"

## What it settles

- **The seas chosen set the least weather**: at Calm the world's own weather; at Rough never calmer than rough seas;
  at Storm always a storm. It holds at sea and over the island landed on.
- **A Storm is not an ordinary storm.** From afar it is dark cloud and heavy seas. Under it: red lightning, a sky
  turning slowly round a point, clouds lit from inside, and now and then something out of step, as rain hanging a
  moment or swells against the wind.
- **Nothing in the sky to point at**: no entity and no rift. Nobody explains it; Overlook's people call it a red
  storm.
- **Not built yet.**

It builds on [[Where You Go and the Seas You Choose Set the Difficulty]], and is part of
[[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
