# A Player's Melee Weapon Reaches a Tenth Further

> "So, I also want to slightly increase the reach/range of melee weapons"

Asked how much further, the owner chose "+10% each". Asked whose weapons, the owner first chose everyone's, then said:

> "Wait. Just do the reach change for the player"

## What it settles

- A player's melee weapon reaches a tenth further than its class's reach in the [[Combat]] table: a sword 1.98 m,
  a greatsword 2.53 m.
- A foe's weapon keeps its class's reach, so foes close and hold their rings as before ([[Enemies]]).
- A foe guards or dodges a player's blow by its true reach, and the swing's arc is drawn to it.
