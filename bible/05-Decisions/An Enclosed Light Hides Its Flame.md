# An Enclosed Light Hides Its Flame

The owner's words, on a picture of the passages' lamp under ground with its flame showing through its glass:

> "Thr cage should hide the flame"

> Asked which lanterns hide their flame (the glass glowing and flickering, no flame seen): "Anything caged should
> have"

> Told which lanterns were not caged (the layouts' post lanterns, the city's glass blocks, the mine entrance's
> lantern): "Hide its flame"; asked which: "Any enclosed light"

## What it settles

- Any enclosed light (a lantern, caged or not, a glass block, a lantern room) shows no flame: its glass glows and
  flickers with its light, and it casts its light as before
  ([[Every Light Is Redesigned and Animated, and Each Casts Light]]).
- An open flame (a torch, a brazier, a camp fire, a candle, a hearth) keeps its fire ([[Lights as Claude Builds Them]]).
