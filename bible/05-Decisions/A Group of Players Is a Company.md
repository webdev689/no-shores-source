# A Group of Players Is a Company

> "Also change the fireteam name to something more fitting. Im not trying to copy destiny's lingo"

Asked what Overlook should call a group of up to three players who play together, and offered Company, Crew, Party
and Band, the owner chose "Company (Recommended)", offered as "A ship's company is everyone sailing together:
seafaring, and not taken by anything else in the game."

## What it settles

- **A group of players is a company**, wherever the game, the server and the notes name it.
- **Its name is Overlook's own**, not Destiny's word.
