# Faction Weapons Wear Their Colour and Roll Their Faction's Perks

> "The faction weapons should get their unique coloring and have their own faction specific perk pool along with the
> general perk roll (relating to their art element)"

Asked who writes each faction's perks, the owner chose:

> "Claude drafts, you approve (Recommended)"

Asked how the Veiled's weapons should read, their colour being four neutral greys, the owner chose:

> "Faint cold violet (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **A faction's weapon wears its faction's colour** on its hilt, guard and trim
  ([[Faction Blades Wear Their Faction's Colour on Hilt and Trim]]).
- **It rolls from its faction's own perk pool beside the general perks**, as a faction's armour does too ([[Everything
  Carries a Set Name, and Perks Roll by Pool]]), the faction's tied to its Art's element:
  the Stormcallers' Storm, the Lamplighters' Divine, the Veiled's Shadow ([[Three Factions Keep the Arts, After Destiny
  1]]). Claude drafts each pool for the owner's yes before any is built.
- **The Veiled's colour leans to a faint cold violet**, so their trim reads on a black blade; their armour shifts with
  it.

Its pools are approved: [[Every Perk Stands, the Factions' Nine With Them]].
