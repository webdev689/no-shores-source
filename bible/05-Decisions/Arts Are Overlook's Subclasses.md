>

>
> We begin with a core of 3 Arts. Each art utilizes the magic cast animation with color corresponding to its art.
> Arts utilize our established elements.
>
> Storm (Electric)
> Divine (Fire)
> Shadow (Cosmic)
>
> Every art recieves 3 spells. 2 minor and 1 major. Cooldowns should be referenced from Destiny.
>
> Please see the attached image for UI reference.
>
> The core is
> Anima (Main Spell)
> Tenants (Minor Spells)
> Core (Fragments to customize your class)
>
> Storm is agile, and pumps out dps.
> Main: Cast to send out tracking orbs that seek enemies and deal damage.

> Main: Cast to spawn an orb that links with you and your players in a radius. Healing them and increasing damage for

The UI reference was a set of Destiny 2 subclass screens (Anathema, Feardrinker, Swarmsinger, Dawnblade): the
subclass's diamond at the left, a row of abilities, aspects and fragments. Asked where a player chooses their Art, the
owner chose: "From Character (Recommended)", offered as "As Destiny does: the Art's diamond sits on the Character page
above the weapons; pressing it opens the Art's screen laid out like your reference (Anima, Tenets, Core)." Asked to
order the round's parts, the owner answered: "Build it all to 100%. Take full autonomy and ensure this is 100%
developed. You got this. Dont come back until finished".

## What it settles

- **Arts are Overlook's subclasses.** Three to begin: Storm (electric, the Volt element), Divine (fire, Blaze) and
  Shadow (cosmic, Cosmic).
- **Each Art casts with the magic cast animation, in its own colour.**
- **Each Art has three spells: one major, the Anima, and two minor, the Tenets**; and a **Core** of fragments that
  customise it.
- **Cooldowns take Destiny's as their reference.**
- **Storm** is agile and deals damage: its Anima sends out orbs that seek enemies; one Tenet speeds the player and
  their allies for a short time; the other shocks and damages enemies in front, paralysing them briefly.
- **Divine** supports with buffs: its Anima spawns an orb that links the player and the players around, healing them
  and raising their damage for a short time; one Tenet heals the player at once; the other casts a dawnflare, a small
  fire bird that makes a strafing run, damaging enemies near it.
- **Shadow** is the mystic: its Anima spawns a duskraven that follows the player for a short time, its shots making
  enemies take more damage and healing the player for the damage it deals; one Tenet, held then cast, makes an area of
  whirling cosmic daggers that damages enemies and makes them take more damage from everyone's weapons; the other is a
  dodge roll into a short invisibility, broken by attacking, in which a backstab always crits.
- **Three new verbs:** Crit (more damage), Expose (the enemy takes more damage), Charge (the enemy is paralysed).
- **Weapons' perk pools change where they need to.**
- **Enemies show debuff icons on their new health bars.**
- **The Art is chosen from the Character page**, its diamond opening its screen.

The numbers, names, fragments and the rest are Claude's, in [[Arts and Loadouts as Claude Shapes Them]].
