# Other Players Wear a Nameplate, Their Level or Prestige in a Square

Showing three pictures of Destiny's nameplates over players' heads, the owner wrote:

> "So for other players, above their head I want like the reference shows. And a sqaure instead of a circle, showing
> their player level and if maxed out on XP, their prestige. If in a menu show the three dots. Blue if not in your
> party, and green if in your party."

Asked what the square shows once a player reaches level 20, the most XP gives, the owner chose "Prestige total
(Recommended)", offered as "Below 20 the square shows the level; at 20 it shows their raw Prestige total (e.g. 84), as
Destiny's Light reads."

## What it settles

- **Every other player wears a nameplate over their head**: a square and their name, as the pictures show.
- **The square shows their level**, and from level 20, the most XP gives, the Prestige total of what they wear
  ([[Prestige Stays as Built, With a Costly Infusion]]).
- **A player in a menu shows three dots** in the square's place.
- **Blue for a player not in the viewer's company, green for one in it** ([[A Group of Players Is a Company]]).
