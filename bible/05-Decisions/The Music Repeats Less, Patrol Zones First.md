# The Music Repeats Less, Patrol Zones First

> "Do a detailed pass on all music, espcially for patrol zones to decreases repetition, and include more variety"

## What it settles

- All the music has a detailed pass, the patrol zones' first: it repeats less and has more variety ([[Music]]).

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What stays | The music keeps what it is: its keys, metres, tempi, themes, instruments, layers, its laws (nothing over B5, nothing past 6 kHz, nothing ringing) and the rests between calm pieces | A pass on what is there, not new music in its place |
| The order | The patrol zones first, then the social space and the sea where the measure showed repetition | "espcially for patrol zones" |
| The measure | Per place: the calm pieces and their minutes, the takes of each fight song, and how many minutes of one fight pass before a pairing of takes comes again (six seeded dice, then eighty) | Repetition counted, not judged by ear |
| What repeated soonest | An island's two calm pieces (under four minutes of music, heard between rests of five to nine), one cadence and one victory ending every fight of a family, green's and dry's fights hearing a pairing again after 7 and 8 minutes, the sea's one piece for each sky | The measure |
| The islands' calm pieces | Two more to each family, eight in all, each in its own key, tempo and lead within its family, closing on the world's leitmotif: green's Bracken (A Dorian, a slip jig in 9/8, 84, fiddle) and Barrow (D Aeolian, a sarabande in 3/2, 72, cello), so green's four are each in a metre of their own; dry's Mirage (D Phrygian dominant in 7/8, 80, flute) and Caravan (E Phrygian dominant in 3/4, 63, clarinet), each with the dry family's flat second and major third; wet's Mangrove (C Aeolian in 6/8, 70, flute) and Peat (A Aeolian in 5/4, 64, oboe); cold's Floe (F Aeolian in 3/4, 60, flute) and Polar Night (G Aeolian in 5/4, 66, fiddle) | The patrol zones' music is heard most |
| Every piece its own | No two calm pieces of the islands, the social space or the sea share their verse's chords or their opening, nor more bars of tune than the older pieces shared (three of the same degrees at the same places, four of the same degrees in any rhythm), nor holds a line a half step from the pad unless written so as an older piece's colour; six older pieces had a chord or two of their verse changed so none shares another's (Mistwrack, Fen, Rime, Lamplight, Still Water, Night Watch), Moorlight kept its own (`variety.test.ts`) | A piece heard is its own: its chords, its opening and its tune |
| The families' fights | Eight more takes to green, dry and wet, seven to cold: each tune's halves in every feel over its own chords and over the turned ones (a take sharing its feel voiced as another feel, its bed struck each bar), a third break and a third breakdown (cold has no third break: its takes are long, and ten minutes must still hear every one); the arranger, with no take unheard among its choices, takes one that leads to a take unheard | On eighty dice a pairing comes again only after 8.6 minutes or more in green, 10.4 in dry, 20.3 in wet, 22 in cold, and every take is heard in ten minutes; the tension, heard alone on a long stalk, has as many more takes |
| The fights' endings | A second cadence and a second victory to each family, each a figure of its own, heard in the family's own turn with the first; one defeat still | No longer the same close to every fight |
| The social space | A fourth piece, the Ropewalk (C Dorian, 80, oboe) | Three pieces came round within a quarter hour |
| The sea | A second piece to each sky, heard in turn with the first: the Long Swell (open, E Dorian, fiddle), Glass (still, B Aeolian, clarinet), the Lantern (night, C Aeolian, oboe) | One piece a sky played every four to eight minutes |
| A place's last piece | Each place keeps its own last piece through other places, deaths and the story, so none begins the piece it began last there | Else a death or a voyage between brings an island's piece twice running |
| How much is enough | As above, no more takes: a fight family's pairing comes again only after 8.6 minutes or more of its music, longer than a fight lasts. The owner left the call to Claude: "Make the best decisions and fix any issues" | The measure |
| Left as they were | The Breach's music (three calm pieces, twelve takes a fight, a pairing again only after 17 minutes or more), the bosses' (eleven or twelve takes a phase, a pairing again only after 9.4 minutes or more, longer than one fight) and the story's | The measure found no real repeat |
