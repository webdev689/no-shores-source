# The Music Repeats Less, Patrol Zones First

> "Do a detailed pass on all music, espcially for patrol zones to decreases repetition, and include more variety"

## What it settles

- Every place's music was measured for how soon it repeats, and the places that repeat soonest were given more:
  the patrol zones first, then the social space and the sea ([[Music]]).
- The music keeps what it is: its keys, metres, tempi, themes, instruments, layers, its laws (nothing over B5, nothing
  past 6 kHz, nothing ringing) and the rests between calm pieces.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The measure | Per place: the calm pieces and their minutes, the takes of each fight song, and how many minutes of one fight pass before a pairing of takes comes again (six seeded dice) | Repetition counted, not judged by ear |
| What repeated soonest | An island's two calm pieces (under four minutes of music, heard between rests of five to nine), one cadence and one victory ending every fight of a family, green's and dry's fights hearing a pairing again after 7 and 8½ minutes, the sea's one piece for each sky | The measure |
| The islands' calm pieces | Two more to each family, eight in all, each in its own key, metre, tempo and lead within its family, closing on the world's leitmotif: green's Bracken (A Dorian, 72, fiddle) and Barrow (D Aeolian in 3/4, 58, cello); dry's Mirage (D Phrygian dominant in 7/8, 80, flute) and Caravan (G harmonic minor in 3/4, 63, clarinet); wet's Mangrove (C Aeolian in 6/8, 70, flute) and Peat (A Aeolian in 5/4, 64, oboe); cold's Floe (E Aeolian in 3/4, 60, flute) and Polar Night (C sharp Aeolian in 5/4, 66, fiddle) | The patrol zones' music is heard most |
| The families' fights | Eight more takes to green, dry and wet, seven to cold: each tune's halves in every feel over its own chords and over the turned ones, a third break and a third breakdown (cold has no third break: its takes are long, and ten minutes must still hear every one) | A pairing comes again only after 10 to 17 minutes in green and dry, none in twenty in wet and cold; the tension, heard alone on a long stalk, has as many more takes |
| The fights' endings | A second cadence and a second victory to each family, heard in turn with the first; one defeat still | No longer the same close to every fight |
| The social space | A fourth piece, the Ropewalk (C Dorian, 80, oboe) | Three pieces came round within a quarter hour |
| The sea | A second piece to each sky, heard in turn with the first: the Long Swell (open), Glass (still), the Lantern (night) | One piece a sky played every four to eight minutes |
| Left as they were | The Breach's music (three calm pieces, twelve takes a fight, a pairing again only after 17 minutes or more), the bosses' (eleven or twelve takes a phase, a pairing again after 10 to 16 minutes, within one fight's length) and the story's | The measure found no real repeat |
