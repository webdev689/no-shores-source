# Each Patrol Zone Has a Built Landing Site, Its Guards Beyond It

> "The image shows my first experience loading into the patrol zone. Absolute chaos as I repeatedly die. Do patrol
> zones not have proper landing areas like the social space? What in the world is happening here?"

> Told the zone's own guards stand 33 to 47 m from where a player lands, crossbows reaching 51 m, with no grace on
> arriving, and offered a safe ring round the landing, the guards pushed back, or a built landing site with the
> guards placed beyond it: "C"

> Asked what a player should be able to do at the landing site besides arrive safely: "Its a fucking landing zone
> dude, what other purpose should it have?"

## What it settles

- **Each patrol zone has a proper arrival place**, a dock or a camp, where a player lands ([[Layouts]]).
- **It is a safe place to arrive**, and nothing more.
- **Its guards are placed beyond it** ([[Enemies]]).

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The site | The landing each layout already lays where a player arrives (its boardwalk, lanterns, stores and beached boat, [[Layouts]]), with no foe in reach of it | A landing zone is a safe place to arrive and nothing more, as the owner answered |
| Beyond it | No garrison foe stands within 61.2 m of a spawn, a shooter's reach and 10 m more, and the room's 70 m square of navmesh starts that far inland, so no foe walks or shoots into the landing | Foes had stood 33 to 47 m from the landing, shooters reaching 51.2 m, so a player standing there died again and again |
