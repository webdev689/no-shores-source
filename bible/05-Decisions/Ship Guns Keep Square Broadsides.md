# Ship Guns Keep Square Broadsides

> "Ship combat doesn't work in all directions just like DFO, and the aiming visuals arent exact"

Asked how the guns should fire (traverse to the look, fire all round, or keep square broadsides), the owner chose
"Keep square broadsides". Asked what was inexact in the aim: "research how dfo does it".

## What it settles

- A broadside still fires square to the hull and the look sets only its range; no battery bears near her bow or her
  stern ([[Naval Combat]]).
- The aim shows where the balls will go as DFO's does after its own aim audit: a look on a ship lays the guns on
  that point, each gun's arc ends where it stops, and the landing zone is as wide as the scatter.
