# Gear as Claude Shapes It

> "I want you to take this on in full with autonomy. Be extremely detailed, no half assed work or band aids, and take
> your time. Treat this as your baby and go above the limits to achieve something amazing."

## What it settles

Claude's choices for everything [[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]] leaves open. Each follows Destiny 1 at
launch where its numbers are known, from wikis and players' reports of 2014; where they are not known, or where
Overlook has no such thing, the choice is Claude's, and its reason says so. The owner can change any of them. How the
rules carry them out is [[Gear]].

### Level, XP and Prestige

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| XP for each level, 1 to 20 | 1,000 times the level reached from: 1,000 for level 2, 19,000 for level 20, 190,000 in all | Destiny 1's own curve was never published; a steady rise that missions will be paced against |
| XP past level 20 | Every 20,000 is a Fruit of Dagon | Destiny 1: past the cap, each level's worth of XP became a Mote of Light |
| XP for a kill | The kind's own times (5 + the foe's level) ÷ 10: a skirmisher 60, an archer 70, a swordsman 90, a brute 180, a captain 2,000 | Higher foes are worth more, as in Destiny 1; a captain is a boss |
| Who earns a kill's XP | Every player who landed a blow on the foe in its last 10 s, in full | A shared world: helping is never a loss |
| An item's XP | A kill's XP goes to the weapon whose blow last landed on the foe and to every armour piece worn | "The more the weapon itself is used"; armour earns as the player plays |
| Prestige for each level, 21 to 30 | 21, 32, 43, 54, 65, 76, 87, 98, 109, 120 | Destiny 1 at launch: 21, then 11 more for each level |
| Prestige a piece | Rare at level 20, 13 to 15; Legendary, 18 to 27; a Breach's Legendary, 18 to 30; Atheric, 21 to 30; Normal, Magic and Rare below 20, none | Destiny 1 at launch, its raid armour as the Breaches' (the owner: "Highest prestige drops") |
| What that reaches | Four Rares, level 24; four Legendaries, 28; three Legendaries and an Atheric, 29; level 30 only with Breach armour | Destiny 1 at launch: level 30 took raid armour |

Replaced in part by [[The Balance Pass Goes Halfway Back, and Loot Follows Destiny 1's Order]]: a kill's XP goes by its kind and its rank, and the captain's 2,000 is gone
([[Gear]], "A kill's pay").

### Attack, Defense and damage

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A Legendary's Attack | 248, to 300 fully upgraded | Destiny 1 at launch |
| An Atheric's Attack | 260 to 300 | Destiny 1 at launch |
| Normal, Magic and Rare | An item has a level, 1 to 20. Fully upgraded its Attack is 15 times its level times 0.80, 0.88 or 0.94; before, 0.85 of that. At level 20: Normal 204 to 240, Magic 224 to 264, Rare 240 to 282 | Destiny 1's lesser weapons rose with the level they dropped at and stayed under a Legendary; its rares sat near 224 to 264 |
| A blow's damage, by Attack | Times 1.07 for each of the foe's levels, up to the foe level Attack ÷ 15 (level 20 at 300), and one more for each level the player has past 20 | Destiny 1, as players measured it; past 20, Prestige carries the reach, so a matched player still fells a foe in the tuned blows |
| The level gap | A player level with or above the foe strikes in full; one level below, 0.67; two, 0.56; three, 0.47; four or more, not at all | Destiny 1, as players measured it. Being above earns no bonus |
| A foe's health | Its kind's, times 1.07 for each of its levels | So a player matched in level and Attack fells a foe in as many blows as combat is tuned for at every level |
| Defense | Each armour piece has a defense level: its item level times its rarity's share (as Attack's) without Prestige, or 20 + its Prestige ÷ 3 with it. Its Defense is 45 times that times its slot's share: head 0.275, torso 0.30, arms 0.225, legs 0.20, a shield 0.15 more | Destiny 1's raid armour at 30: helm 372, gauntlets 304, chest 406, legs 270, which those shares give within a point |
| What Defense does | The player's defense level is their Defense ÷ 45. A foe's blow strikes 1.07 times harder for each of its levels above that | The same rule as Attack's, turned round: Destiny 1's Defense scaled against the attacker's level |
| A foe above the player | Its blows strike a quarter harder for each level the player is below it, up to double at four | Destiny 1's higher foes struck harder; its numbers were never measured |
| Poise and stamina | Unchanged by gear's power | Power is about who wins the exchange of health, not how a body moves |

### Nodes

Columns run left to right; a column's nodes unlock when the item's XP reaches it, and one node of each column is
chosen, switched freely among those bought. An upgrade raises Attack, or Defense with Prestige, by equal steps to the
item's most. Handling is how a weapon is gripped or fired, Finish how any weapon is balanced: two pools, so no item
offers a trait twice.

| Rarity | Weapon's columns | Armour's columns | XP a column |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Upgrade | Upgrade | 1,000 |
| Magic | Handling (2), Upgrade, Perk, Upgrade | Perk, Upgrade, Upgrade | 1,500 |
| Rare | Handling (2), Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade, Finish (2), Upgrade | Perk (2), Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade | 3,000 |
| Legendary | Handling (3), Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade, Finish (2), Perk (2), Upgrade, Upgrade, Upgrade | Perk (2), Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade | 10,000 |
| Atheric | Handling (3), Upgrade, its own, Upgrade, Finish (2), Perk (2), Upgrade, Upgrade, Upgrade | Perk (2), Upgrade, its own, Upgrade, Perk (2), Upgrade | 15,000 |

| Node | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Normal's upgrade | 100 Aurem | Cheap: the first lesson in buying nodes |
| Magic's handling or perk; its upgrade | 200 Aurem; 300 Aurem and 1 fragment | Destiny 1: lesser items cost glimmer, and their damage nodes parts |
| Rare's handling or perk; its upgrade | 400 Aurem and 1 fragment; 600 Aurem and 3 fragments | As above, dearer |
| A Legendary's or Atheric's handling or perk | 800 Aurem, 2 fragments and 5 of its island's resource | Destiny 1: legendaries asked for a planet's material |
| A weapon's first upgrades | 1,000 Aurem, 5 fragments and 10 of the resource; an armour piece's upgrades are all among its last three | As above |
| Their last three upgrades | 1,500 Aurem and 6 Essence each, 18 an item | Destiny 1 at launch: the last three nodes, 6 Ascendant materials each |
| An Atheric's own node | 1,000 Aurem and 1 Essence | Destiny 1's exotics needed Ascendant materials at launch |

A Breach's Legendary armour has a Legendary's columns, its upgrades 4 Prestige each instead of 3.

Fragments and Essence are the item's own kind: a weapon's are Weapon Fragments and Weapon Essence, armour's Armor.
A Fruit of Dagon gives an item 5,000 XP.
