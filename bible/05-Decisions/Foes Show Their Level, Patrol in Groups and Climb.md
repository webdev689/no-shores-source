# Foes Show Their Level, Patrol in Groups and Climb

> "So enemies should have their level instead of the symbol. Keep the symbol for later use.
>
> Enemies should patrol in groups with proper pathfinding. Enemies should be able to climb. Melee unit's bunch
> together too much when in large numbers."

## What it settles

- A foe's plate shows its level in the square where its faction's emblem stood ([[Enemies]], [[Interface]]); the
  emblems are kept for later use.
- Foes patrol in groups, finding their way by the navmesh ([[Foes]], [[Navmesh]]).
- Foes can climb.
- Melee foes in large numbers spread out rather than bunch together.
