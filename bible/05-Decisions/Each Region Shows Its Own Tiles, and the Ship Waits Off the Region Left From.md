# Each Region Shows Its Own Tiles, and the Ship Waits Off the Region Left From

The island's tiles listed every place of the island wherever the ship lay, so docked at Overlook they stood together,
and leaving a patrol zone found the ship where it was left. The owner wrote:

> "So I wanted each patrol zone to have an enter to each other patrol space, which would also put you on that portion
> of the island, where if you leave, your ship would be on the correct side of the island. Each region of the island
> should still have it's own panels. Currently all the panels are stacked up at the port. I also want to reduce the
> transparency of the panels"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked whether the zones' entries are ways on foot from zone to zone, each zone's tile on the island view, or both:
> "On foot, zone to zone"

> Asked whether each place's tile should stand over its region on the island view or the grid be grouped by region:
> "The overworld view for each region"

> Asked how opaque the tiles should be, from 74%: "90%"

> Told that the server judged a landing from the sea by the whole island's coast, so a changed page could land in a
> region the ship does not lie off: "We need to fix that also"

## What it settles

- **The patrol zones are entered from one another on foot**, as the crossing gates on each zone's landing already
  lead ([[Each Island Has Three Regions, and Saltmere's Three Patrol Zones Stay, Linked]], [[Layouts]], "Gates").
- **Leaving a patrol zone finds the ship off that zone's side of the island**, in its region, not where it was left
  ([[Game]], "Landings").
- **Each region has its own view over the island**: docked or ashore in a region, the island's tiles are that
  region's places ([[Interface]], "The island's tiles"; [[World Map]], "Regions").
- **The tiles are 90% opaque**, from 74%.
- **The server lands a place from the sea only while the ship lies off its region**, as the tiles offer it
  ([[Server]]).
