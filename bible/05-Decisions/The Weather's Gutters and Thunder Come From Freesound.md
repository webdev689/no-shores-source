# The Weather's Gutters and Thunder Come From Freesound

The owner judged the weather's first sounds on the weather sound sheet: Keep for the rain's hiss, its near patter and
its drumming on the Sloop's deck; Redo for the gutters and for both near cracks and both far rolls of thunder. Then:
"Pass done. Dont be afraid to use freesound"

## What it settles

- The rain's hiss, patter and drumming on deck stay as the synthesiser makes them.
- The gutters and the thunder are made again from Freesound recordings, picked on the sheet
  ([[Sounds Come From Freesound, Judged on a Test Sheet]]).
