# The Menu Follows Destiny, the Vault and Vendors in the Social Space

> "So the vault itself will be present in the social space along with vendors. So they shouldnt be a tab in the
> inventory (see destiny).
>
> The menu tabs should be.
>
> Character
> Inventory
> Journal (For quests, defered until later)
> Ship
> Social (Friends list)
> Guild (Defered until later)
> Settings"

Asked whether Journal and Guild show before they are built, the owner chose: "Show them, marked Later".

## What it settles

- **The menu's tabs**, in this order: Character, Inventory, Journal, Ship, Social, Guild, Settings.
- **Journal** is for quests and **Guild** is for guilds. Both come later; until then each shows in the menu, marked
  Later.
- **Social** is the friends list.
- **The vault and the vendors are in the social space**, as Destiny's are in the Tower. Neither is a tab in the
  menu or a shelf in the inventory.
