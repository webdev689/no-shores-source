# The Sloop Is Sailed by Its Keys and Seen Close From Any View

> "Overworld view should now be wasd along with click. You should be able to always see your crew/you at the helm from
> any view."
>
> "Zooming in all the way should allow you to see your ship at full detail, along with the proper animations that the
> world editor receives. Hope this makes sense"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> How W, A, S and D sail the Sloop in the Overworld's view, alongside a click: "Helm, as in a fight (Recommended)": A
> and D turn her while held, W sets her sailing straight ahead at the pace dial, S heaves her to, and she holds the new
> heading until stopped or a click sets a voyage.
>
> Which of the World Editor's animations she has, zoomed all the way in, the camera about 25 m off her as she eases to
> her true size: "Ride the real swell", "Heel into turns", "Wake on the water" and "Sails by the wind".

## What it settles

- **The helm's keys at sea.** In the Overworld's view the Sloop is sailed by a click, as before, and by the fight's
  helm keys ([[Game]], "At sea"). The arrow keys still turn the view, and off the helm the same keys walk her deck.
- **Her crew and the player are seen from any view**: the player at her wheel and her hands on her deck, drawn as
  large as she is ([[Game]]).
- **The wheel comes all the way in** past the Overworld's lowest, to her at her true size ([[Game]]).
- **She moves as the World Editor's does**: riding the swell, heeling into a turn, her wake on the water and her sails
  by the wind ([[Sailing]]).
