# The Arts' Birds Are Forged in Blender

> "Use the blender pipeline to craft and curate the bird designs for arts"

Asked whether to render designs to pick from, the owner chose "Yes, you pick the designs". On publishing them: "The
key is in the repo", and asked to add a workflow that publishes with it, "Yes, add forge.yml". Of their views: "Needs
to be all 8 directions also".

## What it settles

- Dawnflare's bird of fire and Duskraven's raven are drawn by the [[Sprite Forge]] in Blender, not by the page's own
  pixels ([[Arts]]).
- Their designs are Claude's ([[Arts and Loadouts as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- Each is drawn in eight directions, turned to the eye as a figure is.
- A `forge` workflow renders them and publishes them to R2 with the repository's Cloudflare token.
