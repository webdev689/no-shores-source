# Walk-in Windows Are Seen Through and Larger, Public Events Show on the Map, and So Does Every Player

After walking a patrol zone's hamlet, the owner:

> "One thing I also noticed is that in the town layouts. Buildings walls can be see through the inside, windows should
> be see through, toen signs need to recieve a wood texture from our procedural system"

Asked how the glass should look, the owner chose "Clear panes", the option that read: "The panes are cut out of the
window picture: frame and leading stay, and you see straight in or out between them. The smallest change."

Then:

> "1. The windows have this weird indent around them. Windows should be larger
>
> 2. Remove the public event UI unless youre in the public event itself. It should utilize the map to show active
> public events
>
> 3. You should be able to see other players on the map anywhere, color coded like their tags"

Told the indent is the window picture itself, a storey's strip of wall squashed into the window's square, and asked
how wide a window should be laid as its frame alone, the owner chose "2 m wide", the option that read: "About 2 m by
1.7 m, sill 0.9 m up, top well under a storey's ceiling. Clearly larger, and the panes fill most of it." Asked which
buildings, the owner chose "Every building", the option that read: "One look everywhere: cities' and closed
buildings' windows also lose the squashed wall and grow, but stay painted (nothing inside to see)."

Asked what counts as being in a public event, the owner chose "On its ground", the option that read: "Within the
event's area, where its foes fight you. Elsewhere in the zone no panel shows, and the map (N) marks each event
running, with its name and time left."

Asked whether the map should also show public events running in Saltmere's other two patrol zones (only the player's
own zone's event was marked), the owner chose "Leave as is", the option that read: "Each zone's map (N) marks only its
own event. The other zones' events show nowhere until you travel there."

Asked how far "anywhere" is for other players on the map, coloured as their nameplates are, the owner answered:
"Anywhere in the zone whether in a zone, sea or social space".

## What it settles

- A building walked into shows its windows seen through, their panes clear, frame and leading kept
  ([[Forged Models]], [[Cities]]).
- Every building's window is its picture's window and frame alone, 2 m wide at the frame's proportions, painted where
  nothing is inside ([[Forged Models]]).
- The public event's panel shows only on the event's ground; the held map marks each event running, with its name
  and the time it has left ([[Public Events]], [[Held Map]]). A zone's map marks its own zone's event alone; the
  other zones' show nowhere until the player travels there.
- The held map shows every other player in the room the player is in, a patrol zone's, the sea's or a social
  space's, coloured as their nameplate is ([[Held Map]], [[Interface]]).
