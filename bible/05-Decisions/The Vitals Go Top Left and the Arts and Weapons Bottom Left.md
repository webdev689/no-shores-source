# The Vitals Go Top Left and the Arts and Weapons Bottom Left

> "1 more thing to add into this. Its a UI overall in out enganced plus.
>
> 1. Moving the health, stamina, and poise plus redesigning it with the reference in mind (top left screen)
>
> 2. Moving the art abilities and weapons to the bottom left with weapon sprites visualized in the bottom left.
> Reference shown.
>
> I really want this to be as detailed as possible"

The owner sent two pictures with it:
- **For the vitals:** a souls-like game's display, with long thin bars at the top left, health over stamina, each
  with pointed ends.
- **For the arts and weapons:** Destiny 2's display at the bottom left: its abilities as icons, the super in a
  diamond and the others in circles with their buttons under them. Beside them, the weapon in hand drawn as a
  picture with its ammunition, the magazine large over the reserve, and the next weapon below it, smaller, with its
  own.

## What it settles

- **The vitals** (health, stamina and poise) move to the top left and are redesigned after the first picture, in
  the Enhanced Plus kit ([[Interface]]). This replaces [[The Vitals Keep Their Panel Under DFO's Compass]]'s
  window at the bottom left. DFO's compass stays across the top's middle, with the boss bar and the target's plate
  under it.
- **The arts and weapons** move to the bottom left, after the second picture: the arts as icons with their keys,
  and the weapons drawn as their own pictures with their ammunition, in the Enhanced Plus kit ([[Interface]]).
- **It is as detailed as it can be.** How each piece is drawn, and where everything else on the display goes to make
  room, is Claude's, recorded in [[The Display as Claude Lays It Out]].
