# The Drawing Click Swings, and the Player's Own Hits Show at Once

> "In the tutorial when I attempt to hit an enemy, it doesnt register immediately"

> "Does this also fix the rest of the game? Because it happens outside of the tutorial also"

Asked three things, the owner answered:

1. Offered "One click draws the weapon and swings at once, as your rule that a click during a weapon swap swings when
   the swap ends.": "Draw and swing (Recommended)"
2. Offered "The flash, hit sound and hit-stop play the moment your screen's own rules say the blow lands. The server
   still decides the damage and the number. Rarely, a flash plays for a blow the server then calls a miss.": "Show it
   at once (Recommended)"
3. Asked whether the blow's wind-up stays (0.27 s with fists or a dagger, 0.30 s with a sword or mace, 0.33 s with an
   axe or greatsword): "Keep. Are you sure this is the issue?"

## What it settles

- **A click with the weapon put away draws it and swings it**, on the same tick; the first person and the figure
  swing with it ([[Combat]]).
- **The player's own blow shows its hit at once**: its flash, hit sound and hit-stop play on the tick the page's own
  rules land it. The room still decides the blow, and its damage and number come with the room's word.
- **The wind-up stays** as [[Melee Swings a Quarter Faster, Strikes All in Reach, and the Lock Lets Go Past 70°]] sets
  it.
