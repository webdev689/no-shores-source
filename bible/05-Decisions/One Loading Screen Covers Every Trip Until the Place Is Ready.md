# One Loading Screen Covers Every Trip Until the Place Is Ready

> "My point with the loading acreen was how can it be utilized to help the game everywhere. Not just the prolouge"

Asked how the cover over every trip should look, the owner chose "Logo, Loading, trip words" (the prologue's loading
screen everywhere: the logo, a breathing LOADING, and the trip's words under it; one screen for the whole game, the
story's hand-over to Overlook too). Asked what it waits for, "Everything nearby" (it holds until the place round the
player is fully ready, then draws a few frames unseen before lifting; never longer than 20 s). Asked about heavy
loading with no trip (a continent opening while sailing, islands coming into sight, the city rebuilt on foot), "No
screen, smoothed" (play goes on; that work is readied ahead, at start-up or behind the last loading screen, and spread
over frames). Asked whether respawns and a lost link take it, "Leave them as they are".

## What it settles

- Every trip's cover ([[Game]], "Travel") is the loading screen of
  [[The Prologue's Scenes Change Slower, Behind the Logo While They Load]]: the logo, LOADING, and the trip's words,
  the story's hand-over to Overlook's hub among them.
- The cover lifts once everything round the player is ready, after a few frames drawn under it; at most 20 s.
- Nothing puts it up mid-play: what loads then is readied ahead and spread over frames.
- A respawn keeps its arrival and a lost link its "Reconnecting…".
