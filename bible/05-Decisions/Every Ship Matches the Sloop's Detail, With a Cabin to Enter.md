# Every Ship Matches the Sloop's Detail, With a Cabin to Enter

> Offered the ships to build, the Schooner first, in the Sloop's style: "The mew models need to match the detail of
> the sloop we implemented. Each ship will have an interior cabin that the player can interact at the emtrance with to
> enter the interior"

## What it settles

- Every new hull is modelled to the Sloop's detail, not a simpler stand-in ([[The Ships as Claude Shapes Them]],
  [[Hulls]]).
- Every ship has a cabin inside her, the Sloop among them. The player interacts at its entrance to go in.
