# Every Ship Matches the Sloop's Detail, With a Cabin to Enter

> Offered the ships to build, the Schooner first, in the Sloop's style: "The mew models need to match the detail of
> the sloop we implemented. Each ship will have an interior cabin that the player can interact at the emtrance with to
> enter the interior"

> Shown the Schooner with a cabin shaped to her own hull: "One cabin interior design across all ships"

> "Make sure the interior is spacious. It'll be used for further customization and personlization in the future"

## What it settles

- Every new hull is modelled to the Sloop's detail, not a simpler stand-in ([[The Ships as Claude Shapes Them]],
  [[Hulls]]).
- Every ship has a cabin, the Sloop among them. The player interacts at its entrance to go in.
- The cabin is one design, the same room whichever ship it is entered from; each ship has her own entrance to it.
- The cabin is spacious, its floor kept open, since players will customise and personalise it later. Being entered
  as a space of its own, it need not fit inside any hull.
