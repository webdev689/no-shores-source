# Magic and Rare House Pieces Drop in the World, for the Pledged House

> "I think we should add magic and rare faction drops to the world loot pool"

Asked whose house's gear can drop for a player, the owner chose "Pledged house only (Recommended)", the option that
read: "A player pledged to a house finds only that house's pieces; one not yet pledged finds none. Keeps house gear
tied to belonging, as Destiny 1's faction gear was."

Asked how often a Magic or Rare world drop should come as a house piece instead of its area's own set look, the owner
chose "1 in 10 (Recommended)", the option that read: "A house piece now and then; the area's own set stays most of
what drops."

Asked which house pieces can drop at Magic and Rare, the owner chose "Armour and weapons (Recommended)", the option
that read: "The four armour pieces and the house's weapons, each in the house's look; the cape stays bought at the
counter."

Asked whether it goes into the pull request of the keepers' gifts, hordes and lessons or its own, the owner chose
"Into #223".

## What it settles

- **Magic and Rare house pieces join the world's loot**: one in ten of the Magic and Rare pieces that drop in the world
  is a piece of the player's house, in its look ([[Gear]], [[Factions]]).
- **Only the pledged house's**: a player not yet pledged finds none.
- **Its four armour pieces and its weapons**; the cape is only bought at the house's counter.
- It replaces in part [[Every Drop Wears Its Content's Set, With Random Perks]] (one in ten of the world's Magic and
  Rare drops wears the house's look, not the area's set) and [[Each Faction Sells Gear of Its Own, in Its Own Colour]]
  (house pieces also drop, at Magic and Rare; the Legendary ones are still sold alone).
