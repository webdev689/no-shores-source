# The Grey Tithe Take a Tenth, and Overlook Does Not Pay

> Asked who the Grey Tithe are, what they take and from whom: "Whats your suggestion?"

> Offered: a levy at sea, the Grey Tithe stopping ships "across the Pale Expanse" and taking a tenth of what each
> carries, a ship that refuses taken whole, under grey, unmarked sails, "which is why the opening's ships are
> 'unknown'"; Overlook does not pay, which is why it is "the most welcoming place in the region" and why a ship bound
> for it gets no mercy; underneath, project-final's Uranian Covenant adapted, what they collect not really for coin,
> the rewriting of the islands to them something restored, not broken; how deep that goes left open; the pirates their
> own faction, "the Tithe are the authority and pirates are the outlaws they also hunt". Asked whether it stands, with
> the horror's tone: "We can go with that. Although that sounds a little over explanatory"

> Offered it shorter, "they take a tenth, and Overlook doesn't pay", what lies underneath unwritten, and asked whether
> it was too much for the player or for us, with Claude's vote that the bible may know more than the game ever says:
> "Actually, i approve"

> Offered back as the whole suggestion, with the bible keeping more than the game says: "Approved"

## What it settles

- **A levy at sea.** The Grey Tithe ([[Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe]]) stop ships across the Pale Expanse
  and take a tenth of what each carries. A ship that refuses is taken whole.
- **Grey, unmarked sails**, which is why the ships of the opening are unknown
  ([[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]]).
- **Overlook does not pay.** It is the most welcoming place in the region for that, and a ship bound for it gets no
  mercy.
- **Underneath, the Covenant.** They are Raum's Uranian Covenant, adapted
  ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]). What they collect is not really for coin, and they see the rewriting
  of the islands as something restored, not broken. How deep this goes, and when the player learns it, is open; the
  game says little of it ([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]).
- **The pirates are a faction of their own**: the Tithe are the authority, the pirates the outlaws they also hunt
  ([[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]]).

Replaced in part by [[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]: there are no pirates for the Tithe to hunt.
