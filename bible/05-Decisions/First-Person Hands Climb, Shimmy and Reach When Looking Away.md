# First-Person Hands Climb, Shimmy and Reach When Looking Away

> "First person hands do not utilize climbing animations/ledge look away hand animation"

They follow DFO's climbing hands ([[Built From What DFO Taught Us]]), which the owner shaped there:

> "Definitely animate the arms for when you are climbing, or shifting left to right climbing/on a ledge"

> "whenever you look away from the wall your climbing or ledge you are hanging from"

> "the hands should stay on whatever your climbing and not rotate with the screen. The only one that should rotate
> with the screen is the single arm out"

## What it settles

- In first person the hands move as the climbing body does: they shimmy along a lip, climb hand over hand, and go down
  the view, press and let go over a top ([[First Person]]).
- Looked away from the wall, the hand on that side lets go and reaches out from its own edge; the other holds on.
- The fists stay on the wall as the view turns; the reaching arm stays on the screen.
