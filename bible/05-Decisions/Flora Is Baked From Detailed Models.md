# Flora Is Baked From Detailed Models

> "Creating detailed flora assets for each biome using the blender pipline, baking them as sprites and then turning
> them into low poly assets for each biome"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked which way the flora is made: (A) one detailed model per plant, baked two ways, a low-poly mesh for close up
> and sprites for far away, the sprites replacing today's far meshes and the painted woods; (B) the detailed model's
> leaves and bark baked into pictures worn by low-poly card meshes; or (C) sprites as the look up close, low-poly only
> for shadows, collision and distance: "A"
>
> Asked who makes the detailed sources, Blender scripts in the repository or the owner's own models: "You"

## What it settles

- Each biome's plants are made as detailed models with the Blender pipeline, and each is baked two ways: a low-poly
  mesh for close up and sprites for far away ([[Nature]]).
- The sprites replace the far meshes and the woods painted on the land.
- Claude makes the detailed sources, as Blender scripts in the repository ([[Own Work]]); what they bake is published
  to R2 ([[Generated Files on Cloudflare R2]]).
- It builds on [[Which Made-for-DFO Mods Come Over]]: Overlook builds its own trees.
