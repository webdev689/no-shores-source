## What it settles

- Overlook is a shared world, not an MMO. Destiny 1 vanilla is the main reference for its quests and gear.
- **The names:** Overlook is the port town of Saltmere, an island in the Pale Expanse. The ships that attack are the
  Grey Tithe's, and their captain is Captain Brannoc Thule.
- **Where:** continent 1 (`continent-1` in `content/world/layout.json`) is the main social space, with the port
  city.
- **The beginning:** the player is a captain sailing for the port town of Overlook. Unknown ships attack: a naval
  fight, then a ramming vessel and a fight on the deck in waves, then a boss fight with its captain, with lock-on,
  dodging and parrying. The player loses, is thrown overboard and drowns, then washes up on Overlook's beach. The
  boss fight needs new Blender animations.
- **Overlook** is the social hub: other players, buying goods, hiring crew, joining a faction, taking quests.
- **Power** comes from gear, earned through drops, activity completions and quests. XP comes from general play and
  affects drops.
- **Weapons** have types that set a melee weapon's weight, speed and damage and a ranged weapon's own stats. Weapons
  and armour are upgraded by nodes, with currency, materials from dismantling and rare or legendary resources. A
  weapon unlocks more nodes as it is used and gains weapon XP; armour has neither.
- **The world** is a vast region: nine continents, many smaller islands and unique random encounters. The player sails
  from Overlook on their own ship and sees other players on their own adventures. Islands, and Overlook itself, are
  not walked as an open world: the player lands when close enough. From the side of an island they get a bird's-eye
  view of the area, choose missions and are taken to them, and may find other social havens.
- [[Overlook Is Our Own Game]] and [[The World Map]] stay in force where they agree with this scope. Where they
  differ, this scope holds: a shared world rather than MMO scale, and islands landed on rather than walked as an open
  world.
- The systems already built and ported can still be used. `project-final` is a reference, and DFO stays the source
  of system imports ([[Built From What DFO Taught Us]]). Its pick list is [[Pick List - project-final]].
- Stargrim, `vestige-gps-source` and project-dagger are the owner's own, and so is project-final's music: what
  project-final takes from them is the owner's work ([[Own Work]]). Daggerfall's data and Destiny's own names in it
  are not.
