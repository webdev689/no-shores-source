# The Launcher Stands in a Window of Its Own

> "The launcher itself is placed inside a browser page. The launcher needs to stand on it's own"

> Asked which it should become, offered "A separate launcher window, sized to the launcher and filled by it edge to
> edge. Play closes it and opens the game's window, which goes straight to the title. The world starts loading after
> Play, so Play-to-title takes a little longer.": "Its own window (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **The [[Desktop App]]'s launcher opens in a window of its own**, sized to it and filled by it edge to edge, with
  nothing of the game's page or world behind it.
- **Play closes it and opens the game's window**, on the title; the world loads after Play.
- It narrows [[The Desktop App Opens on a Launcher in the Game's Look]], which stands in all else.
