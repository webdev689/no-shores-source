# The Downs' Landing Zones as Claude Builds Them

Claude's choices in building the Saltgrass Downs' three landing zones, their map and their fast travel, on the owner's
[[The Saltgrass Downs Plays as Destiny 2's EDZ, Its Three Landing Zones Spread Across It]] and
[[The Island's Tiles Are Painted Scenes, and Each Patrol Zone Has Three Landing Zones]]. Each is the owner's to change.

| Call | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where the north's and the south's stand | On a way, in the claim's north half and its south half; of the ways' points 40 m or more inside the claim and 30 m or more off every place's reach, the one that leaves the half's farthest walkable ground nearest to a zone | The owner's "so any part of the Downs is a short walk from one"; on a way so a way reaches it, off the places so no garrison stands on it |
