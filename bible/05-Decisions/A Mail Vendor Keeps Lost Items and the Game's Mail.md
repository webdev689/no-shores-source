# A Mail Vendor Keeps Lost Items and the Game's Mail

Shown that a first clear's Vestige could be lost (laid on the floor once the record was marked paid), the owner wrote:

> "Another session is working on the social space. Implement a mail vendor who acts as a mailbox for lost items,
> ingame mail, etc"

Asked whether players may also send mail to each other, the owner chose "Game mail only": only the game writes, of
lost items, rewards that did not fit and notices; players do not write to each other.

## What it settles

- A keeper in Overlook's social space keeps each player's mailbox.
- What a player loses goes there rather than vanishing: an item left lying, and a reward with no room for it.
- The mail is the game's alone: no player writes to another.
- Where the counter stands is the social space's ([[Activities]], [[Factions and Vendors as Claude Shapes Them]]).
