## What it settles

- **Passages lead out of the patrol zone**, from set pathways in it; for now only in the Saltgrass Downs.
- **Going through one** pans the camera out to an overworld-style view of the island, dots tracing the way, and into
  the next zone, where the character walks in.
- **Passage zones are procedural**, smaller than a patrol zone and of varying size, underground, above ground and
  built from Blender pieces. Each has passages on to others: a web, a traversable maze across the island.
- **They are not seen** from the sea or from the overworld over the island.
- **Deeper is harder and pays more**, and the dive fits each continent's levels, so early islands keep their worth.
- **Mission types of their own, dungeons and materials** are dealt into the zones.
- **A public passage or a company route**, as the player chooses.
- **The layout rotates weekly**, and the week sets its primary modifiers; each zone adds smaller ones of its own,
  growing with depth.
- **A return structure** that fits the lore stands about every 15 minutes of play, about five zones; at each the
  player banks the haul and returns to the patrol zone, or presses on. Death loses only what was found since the last
  structure.
- **The first Atheric bounty** is for one of the Atherics, after Destiny 1's exotic bounties, and drops only, rarely,
  in the Saltgrass Downs' passages. Claude finds the lore for why passages exist.
- **Daily and weekly rewards**, and **every activity's rewards an hour are measured and kept within one band**, so the
  dive is never the one place worth playing.
- **Built in full**, not as a slice first; the calls are Claude's, in [[Passages as Claude Builds Them]].
