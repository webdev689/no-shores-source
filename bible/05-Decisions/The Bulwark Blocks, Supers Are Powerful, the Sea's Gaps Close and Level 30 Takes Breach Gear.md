# The Bulwark Blocks, Supers Are Powerful, the Sea's Gaps Close and Level 30 Takes Breach Gear

The owner's answers to the open points [[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]] left.

> Asked whether the Bulwark, which carries a shield but as a brute never raises it, should block blows from the front:
> "Yes"

> Told that the Arts' supers do about a few seconds of a sword's damage to one foe: "The art itself is a super and
> should be powerful"

> Asked whether to change three gaps at sea (travelling between waters resets the hostile roll so a player who keeps
> travelling meets more ships; a cove's two or three wrecks hold more in all than a Minor ship; a fire burns a flat 21
> hull whatever the ships' levels): "Approved"

> Told that a ship tops out at level 29 and level 30 needs Breach modules, the way a player needs Breach armour:
> "Yes. Same for player gear. Thats always been the goal"

> Told that two scripted players stand near the line of holding the heroic raid: "Keep it for now"

## What it settles

- The Bulwark blocks blows from the front.
- Each Art's super (its Anima) is powerful.
- The three gaps at sea close: travelling brings no more ships, a whole cove holds less than a Minor ship, and a fire
  keeps its worth as the ships' levels rise.
- Level 30 takes Breach gear, for a player's armour and a ship's modules alike.
- The heroic raid stays as it is for now.

How each is built is Claude's, in [[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]].
