# Saltmere's Detail Pass Goes Deep

> "Let's do a detailed design pass and comprehensive audit"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked what the design pass covers: fix the five findings left on Saltmere's three zones, then picture each zone
> area by area and run beat by run beat and add detail where it reads thin, the other 24 zones taking the generator
> changes but no per-zone tuning or runs until the owner has looked; or all 27 zones tuned, with the other 24's runs;
> or the five findings only: "Saltmere deep (Recommended)"
>
> Asked how wide the audit is: the whole repository; a second, deeper pass over what this branch adds, after the
> fixes now in progress land; or only the systems just merged from main: "This PR's work again"
>
> Asked how the design pass is built: richer rules that shape the land from each area's kind and the climate; shapes
> written per zone in the briefs; or the current land dressed with more pieces: "Your decision"
>
> Asked to approve the design: "Approve (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **Saltmere's three zones get a deep detail pass**: the five open findings are fixed (outcrops that read as tents,
  one shape of basin everywhere, the Gullwing Vale's unreached middle and outside river, plain keep walls, a cavern
  roof afloat), and every area and run beat is pictured and given detail where it reads thin.
- **The other 24 zones** take the generator changes, without per-zone tuning or runs, until the owner has looked.
- **The audit is a second pass over this branch's own work** (layouts, land, runs, the editor, flora, ground), after
  the fixes now in progress.
- **How it is built is Claude's call**, recorded in [[The World as Claude Shapes It]].

It builds on [[Patrol Zones Stay as Built and Gain Linear Combat Areas]], whose limits stand: the pass reshapes
basins, canyons, outcrops and great works, not a zone's areas, paths and gates.
Keeping a zone's areas, paths and gates as built is replaced by
[[Zones Rise and Fall, Above and Below Ground]].
