# The Story's Mark Stays With the Character, the Page Plays Online, and a Join Reaches Any Landmass

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on the interface and the page.

Asked whether the story, and the stranger's welcome at its end, should be marked played in the browser or kept with
the character in its room, the owner chose:

> "With the character"

Asked whether the garments drawn on a player should stay each page's own word or be the room's, the owner chose:

> "Room decides"

Asked whether Graphics should gain settings of its own, a grass setting among them, the owner chose:

> "Yes, add some"

Asked whether the pad should gain buttons for the chat and for reporting a line, the owner chose:

> "Yes, add them"

Asked whether a join from the Roster should also carry a player to a friend on another landmass, the owner chose:

> "Any landmass"

Asked whether the page should play online by default, without `?room=`, the owner chose:

> "Online by default"

## What it settles

- **The story's and the welcome's played mark is kept with the character**, so a second account on one browser sees
  them and one account on a new browser does not see them again ([[Prologue]]).
- **The room decides the garments** others see on a player ([[The Room Keeps Only Looks of a Piece's Own Content]],
  [[Server]]).
- **Graphics gains settings of its own**, a grass setting among them; which others is Claude's call ([[Interface]]).
- **The pad gains buttons for the chat and for reporting a line.**
- **A join reaches a friend on any landmass** ([[The Roster and Emblems as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- **The page plays online by default**, with accounts, without `?room=`.

Built ([[Interface]], [[Prologue]], [[Server]]; Claude's calls in
[[The Open Questions' Answers as Claude Builds Them]]). Already so: the room kept the story played with the character;
signing in at the helm on a new browser now goes on past it. Not built yet: reporting a chat line, which exists on no
device yet, and so its button on the pad: what reporting a line does is the owner's to say ([[Open Questions]]).
