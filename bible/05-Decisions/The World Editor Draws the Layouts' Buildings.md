# The World Editor Draws the Layouts' Buildings

The owner's answer:

> Asked whether the World Editor may import the city layer, the only way it can draw the layouts' buildings, props
> and solids (it drew only their plans): "Yes"

## What it settles

- The World Editor's layer may import the city layer, and the editor draws each layout's buildings, props and solids
  as the game does ([[World Editor]], [[Layouts]]).
