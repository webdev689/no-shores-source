# Dungeons Go Deeper, With Storeys, Stairs, More Ways and DFO's 3D Map

> "So we're currently working on new passage content in another session, and here I want to work on deepening the
> dungein system.
>
> Currently. Dungeons are one floor and linear beginning to end.
>
> I want dungeons to utilize floor layers, actualy stair cases, more pathways, larger rooms, more varied layouts, and
> more of a reason to explore.
>
> I want to implement the 3D dungeon map from DFO into our system.
>
> I also want to increase chest rewards.
>
> I want you to research, be as detailed as possible, use blender when needed, and go insanely deep on improving this.
> Take your time and make this your own. Come back when this is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- **Dungeons have floor layers**, one over another, joined by real staircases.
- **More pathways, larger rooms, more varied layouts, and more reason to explore** a dungeon.
- **DFO's 3D dungeon map comes over** into the dungeons ([[Pick List - Presentation and Tooling]], `src/ui/automap/`).
- **Dungeon chests pay more.**
- **Blender is used where it is needed**, and the work is Claude's to shape ("make this your own"), researched in
  detail and built in full before it comes back to the owner. Claude's choices are in
  [[Dungeons as Claude Builds Them]], and the owner can change any of them.
- It deepens [[DFO's Dungeons 2.0 Comes Over, Within the Patrol Areas]], whose storeys and stairs had stayed behind
  ([[Pick - Dungeons 2.0]]), and the [[Dungeons]] they describe.
