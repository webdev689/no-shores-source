# A Click During a Weapon Swap Swings as It Ends

Asked three things in plain words, the owner answered:

> "1. A
> 2. Best decision
> 3. Stay"

1. Swapping weapons takes 0.4 s, and a click to attack during it was thrown away. The owner chose (a): remember the
   click and swing the moment the swap finishes.
2. An enemy attacking a player who stands right next to it, then struck by another player, keeps to the player beside
   it. That is Claude's call, under "Best decision", recorded in [[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]].
3. The gold parry ring stays where it is.

## What it settles

- A swing pressed during a weapon swap is kept, and the swing starts as the swap ends ([[Combat]]).
- A foe trading blows with a fighter beside it is not drawn off by another's blow ([[Foes]]).
- The parry ring keeps its height ([[Foes Show a Closing Parry Ring]]).
