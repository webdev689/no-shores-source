# The Inspect Panel Follows Destiny's Weapon Page, Its Stats Shown and Its Nodes Square

> "I want to focus on improving the weapon/armor inspect panel."

> "In our own vision, im really trying to push the design and fidelity to match the references, along with making
> weapon/armor stats themselves be more visible. We can also improve the hover inspect + add a compare."

> "I want you to take some autonomy and be extremely detailed. Make this yours and do everything you can to push the
> design further. (For nodes do not use circles and use squares)"

> "Come back when everything is 100%"

The owner sent two pictures with it: Destiny 1's page for No Time to Explain, its Attack large beside its stat bars,
its node grid and a node's card listing what it costs beside what the player holds ("Glimmer 24745 / 500"); and its
page for Pest Control Matrix, a node hovered ("Rifled Barrel: Increased Range, Slower Reload") lighting its gain on one
stat bar in green and its loss on another in red.

## What it settles

- **An item's page and its hover card push toward the two pictures**, as detailed as Claude can make them.
- **A weapon's and an armour piece's stats are made more visible.**
- **The hover card is improved, and a compare is added.**
- **Nodes are squares, never circles.** The growth inside a locked node that [[Text Is Plain]] set as a ring is a
  square.
- **Claude makes the calls** and writes each down with its reason
  ([[The Inspect Panel as Claude Shapes It]]), for the owner to change.
