## What it settles

- **A zone's playspaces include handcrafted modules**, made in Blender and placed by the layout generator where they
  fit the land and the ways.
- **Claude scripts each module in Blender**, as the [[Flora]] is grown: its mesh, its walkable floors and its
  collision, published by a workflow.
- **The first set is all four**: cavern chambers, cliff stairs and ledges, the keep's citadel, and ruins and bridges,
  in the Gullwing Vale.
- **They come after the terraced land**, then the Vale is pictured, then the Saltgrass Downs and the Driftwood Moors.

Replaced in part by [[Saltmere Keeps Only the Saltgrass Downs, and Everything Folds Into It]]: the Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood Moors are removed, so the playspaces come in
the Saltgrass Downs.

It builds on [[Zones Rise and Fall, Above and Below Ground]].
