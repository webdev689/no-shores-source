# A Caged Lantern Hides Its Flame

The owner's words, on a picture of the passages' lamp under ground with its flame showing through its glass:

> "Thr cage should hide the flame"

> Asked which lanterns hide their flame (the glass glowing and flickering, no flame seen): "Anything caged should
> have"

## What it settles

- A lantern with a cage round its glass shows no flame: its glass glows and flickers with its light, and it casts its
  light as before ([[Every Light Is Redesigned and Animated, and Each Casts Light]]).
- A lantern with no cage keeps its small flame behind its glass ([[Lights as Claude Builds Them]], which says which
  lanterns are caged).
