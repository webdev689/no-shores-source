# Public Events Are Built in Full, on Claude's Autonomy

> Asked whether to go on to the nine story missions once the public events were settled: "I want to develop this in
> full first. Take autonomy, just available props. Develop new using the blender pipeline and make yhis insanely
> detailed. I really want to wow people. Dont come back until this is 100%"

> Shown the public events built, with what was not yet pictured and what still looked wrong: "Do a comprehensive audit
> and deep detail pass. Make this perfect"

## What it settles

- **The public events are built in full before the story missions** are talked through
  ([[Public Events Run on a Clock, After Destiny 1]]).
- **Claude decides what the owner has not**, recording each call in [[Public Events as Claude Builds Them]].
- **Props**: the ones already made are used, and new ones are made with the Blender pipeline.
- **The bar is very high**: insanely detailed, to wow people; done means whole, not a first pass.
- **Before they are done**, a comprehensive audit of everything the public events touch and a deep detail pass on how
  they look and play, every beat seen and every fault found fixed.
