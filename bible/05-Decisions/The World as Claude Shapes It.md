# The World as Claude Shapes It

> Asked for the sizes in metres: "Your choice"

> "This is your baby to mold." ([[The World Is Claude's to Mold]])

## What it settles

Claude's choices for how the world works, feels and looks ([[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]). The
owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A port city's size | A walled city 6,912 m across, its wall 3,456 m from its middle | Half DFO's Imperial City, whose wall stands 6.9 km out (13.8 km across) |
| The social space | 260 × 120 m | The size of Destiny 1's Tower, from its walk end to end; no published measure exists |
| A patrol area | About 1.6 km across | A Destiny 1 patrol area joined with its neighbours |
| A mission's or dungeon's section | About 600 m across | Room for an encounter and its approach |
| A world boss's section | About 500 m across | An arena with room to circle |
| Past a section's edge | "Return to the area" and a 10 s count; back inside ends it; at 0, or 60 m past the edge, the player dies | Destiny 1's out-of-bounds warning |
| Respawning | Offered 4 s after death, at the section's nearest spawn | Destiny 1's patrol respawn |
| The steepest ground walked | 50° (1.19 m of rise a metre); steeper is a wall to slide along | Hills and banks walk; cliffs stop a body, as Destiny 1's do |
| The port cities | Overlook (Saltmere), Cairnwatch, Elmhaven, Sunspire, Goldreach, Mistfall, Windmere, Fenhallow, Rimeholt, continents 1 to 9 | Names of our own, each read from its biome |
| Their motifs | The Beacon, the Span, the Great Hall, the Sun Dome, the Granary Ring, the Stepped Temple, the Windwall, the Drowned Bell, the Whalebone Gate ([[Cities]]) | Each known from the sea by one shape, as DFO's Imperial City by its tower |
| Where each city stands | On the continent's coast about 700 m inland, where the land round it is gentle and the water off it deep | A harbour below the landmark, the city between |
| Their landforms | A headland raised under each landmark except on flat desert, rainforest and swamp coasts: Overlook's 75 m, Cairnwatch's 55 m, Rimeholt's 50 m, Windmere's 40 m, Elmhaven's 25 m, Goldreach's 15 m | The coasts are low; a social space should look down on its city and harbour, as the Tower does |
| A continent's nodes | Ten in three sides round its coast: three patrol areas, four missions, two dungeons and a world boss; with its city's social space | Destiny 1's destinations: landing zones round a patrol space, each side seen from the sea |
| Node names | Places of our own: a patrol area named for its biome's ground (The Saltgrass Downs), missions and dungeons for what stands there (Wrecker's Cove, Kraken's Grotto), bosses for their lair (The Maw of the Shallows) | Read on the Overworld's marks |
| Each biome's nature | Temperate coast: oaks, umbrella pines, gorse, boulders; highland: firs, birches, heather, granite; woodland: oaks, beeches, firs, ferns, hazel; desert: cacti, date palms by water, red rock, dry shrub; savanna: acacias, baobabs, tall grass, thorn bush; rainforest: buttressed giants, canopy trees, palms, tree ferns; steppe: sagebrush, dry grass, birches by water; swamp: cypresses, mangroves by water, reeds; tundra: spruce, dead trees, snowy rock, dwarf shrub ([[Nature]]) | Each continent reads as its biome from the sea and on foot |
| The player's ship | A two-masted trader 30 m long, of our own, in the player's paint; drawn 2.5 times its size at sea | It reads at a glance from the Overworld's raised camera, which is not to scale |
| Returning to the ship | The ship lies 600 m past deep water off its city's harbour, facing the island, the view 30 degrees down | The island stands in the first view, as Destiny's orbit opens on the planet |
| Arriving | On foot at the social space's first spawn, 4 m inside its seaward parapet, facing out from the landmark over the city to the sea, the camera 4.5 m behind and looking 17° down | The Tower's arrival looks out over the City |
| The social space's edge and courtyard | Its section is the plaza's own edge, walled all round, so the parapet is reached without a warning; a portico with a great door on the landward side, arcades with an alcove a bay either side, braziers round an obelisk 18 m high in the middle, lanterns and benches along the seaward parapet, stone bands in the paving | The Tower's railings are in bounds, and its courtyard is framed by halls, alcoves and marks, never an empty floor |
| The sun's shadows | One shadow map 300 m across ahead of the camera: the city's solids and near models and the near trees cast, the land, the city and the plants receive; none from more than 400 m up | Destiny's places are lit by a hard sun, their shapes read by their shadows |
| A landmark's stone | Every great stone mass dressed with pilasters a bay apart and a cornice of trim | A landmark reads as built from the plaza and the sea, as the Tower's walls do |
| Woods from afar | The land coloured with its biome's canopy where nature's grove field puts the trees, past their drawn reach | Destiny's islands read as wooded from orbit and from the sea |
| Death | The world drains to grey until respawn | Destiny's death greys the world |
