# Clothing Is Armour, With a Wardrobe Later

> "So ive been thinking. Instead of doing clothing sets. All clothing/armor should count as armor.
>
> Armor itself is the more prestigious sets. Some sets being tied to certain content"

> "Actually idk we can talk about this more"

> Asked whether every clothing piece counts as armour or stays a cosmetic set worn over it: "Im torn tbh"

> Offered Destiny 1's rule, with an appearance wardrobe later: "Armour, plus a wardrobe later (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **Every piece of clothing is armour**: a head, torso, arms or legs item like any other, with nodes, Defense and,
  from level 20, Prestige. Everyday clothes are the lower rarities.
- **Armour proper is the more prestigious sets**, some of them tied to certain content.
- **No armour toggle**: what a player wears is what protects them, as in Destiny 1.
- **An appearance wardrobe comes later**, showing a collected piece over what is worn.
- This replaces clothing as cosmetic sets with an armour toggle, in [[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]].

Replaced in part by [[The Tack Gives the First Horse, the Prologue Ends in Plain Clothes, and Each Speaker Is Named in a Colour]]: the plain clothes the prologue's end dresses a player in have no stats.
