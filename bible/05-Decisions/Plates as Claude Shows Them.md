# Plates as Claude Shows Them

> Shown Claude's recommendation for fewer plates, after Destiny's: "Yep do all that you recommended"

> Later the same day: "I wanna do a conprehensive audit on everything ensuring its perfect"

Claude's choices in building [[Fewer Plates Show, Sized by Rank, After Destiny]], and in fixing what the audit found,
recorded in [[Interface]] and [[Enemies]]. The owner can change any of them.

## What it settles

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "About 5 degrees" | Whole within 4 degrees of the crosshair, none past 6, easing between over 0.25 s | A plate fades at the cone's edge rather than blinking on and off |
| Nearest the crosshair | Measured to the nearest point of a foe's body, feet to head and 0.35 m round, the body the crosshair's own hits are judged by | A foe is at the crosshair wherever on its body the crosshair rests, near or far |
| Two foes at the crosshair | The one whose body the crosshair is on wins, the nearest of them if several | Destiny's bar is for the enemy under the reticle: a foe behind another on the line is not the one aimed at |
| The cone's hold | Another foe takes the cone only once the crosshair is on it, or it stands a degree nearer, or the cone would show a quarter more of it | Two foes side by side, or a view wobbling between them, trade nothing |
| The foe at the crosshair fought, locked or a Boss | The cone shows no other foe's plate; a Boss takes the cone whether its bar shows or not | One plate for the foe aimed at, never a second for its neighbour |
| A fought foe's plate | Whole, then fading over the last 1.5 s of the 6 s, as the target plate does | "The target plate's own time, so the two agree" |
| A Minor's bar | 6 px high, three fifths of the plate's width; the plate itself as wide as a Major's | "? Crossbowman", a Minor's longest name, fills the plate in the game's face; a 6 px bar keeps its colour far off |
| An Elite's and up | 176 px with a 10 px bar, marked in thirds by dark marks edged light, over figures only | The thirds read over a full bar and an empty trough alike; the target plate at the top keeps its own look |
| The land hiding a plate | The line to the head tried against the ground every 2 m | No rise in the ground near the eye is narrower than twice that |
| A plate fading out of the cone | Holds its place and lifts no other plate over it | The plate taking the cone stands where it will stay, rather than being lifted over the one going and let down |
| A plate's elements | Kept, hidden, while its foe stands, for its next showing | A view sweeping across a crowd built a plate for every foe it passed |
| The words of blows | Keep clear of the plates drawn, never of a foe with none; a word pushed down clear of a plate stays down as the plate goes | A word over a foe with no plate stays by its blow, and never jumps back up as a plate fades |
| Ships' plates at sea | As they were | The request was about foes on foot |
