# The Saltgrass Fold-In as Claude Builds It

The owner chose which places come into the Saltgrass Downs as Claude's call: "Grow with the best few"
([[Saltmere Keeps Only the Saltgrass Downs, and Everything Folds Into It]]). Everything below is Claude's, and the
owner may change any of it.

## The places

| Call | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What comes | Grey Point Light (a lighthouse), the Old Stones (a shrine), the Saltbreaker Hulks (hulks), Moorhold (a keep), Hollow Coven (a coven) and the Sunken Gate (the Breach's gate, at level 20, as it stood) | Each holds a house task's scene the Downs had nothing like (a lighthouse's lamps, a ring of stones, a ship's graveyard, a reckoner's hold, a coven under the chalk), and three stand the handcrafted modules: the Old Stones, the citadel and the gate |
| Moorhold | A keep, where the Moors had it as a ruin and a run | Its lore calls it a square stone keep, and a keep stands the citadel built for Valeguard, so the module stays in the game |
| What stays out | Gullwing, Weir Mill, Valeguard, Orchard Farm, the Water Meadows, Vale Mouth, Moorend, Driftwood Strand, Drowned Men's Graves, the Gorse Moor and the Moors' Tithe Camp | Each repeats a kind the Downs hold already (a hamlet, a farm, a landing, a graveyard, wilds, a camp), or needs a river the Downs do not have |
| Their runs | None come: the Downs keep the Chalk Adit, the Beacon Stair and the Tithe Barns | Every zone has three runs, and the passages' three mouths stand at their ends |
| The order | The six after the Downs' own eight | The Downs' own places are placed first, so they keep their ground |
| The theme | "Chalk downland over the sea: sheep walks, salt wind, an old beacon tower on the heights, a lighthouse on the point and hulks on the strand, old stones older than anyone remembers, a coven in the chalk, and the Grey Tithe camped in the chalk pits." | The brief's line names what stands there |

## The houses' tasks

| Task | Where it is now |
|---|---|
| Find Pell's trail | Grey Point Light |
| Run down the Tithe at their camp (`run-down-tithe-camp`) | Tithe Camp |
| Catch the tally riders (`race-downs-road`) | A race from the Old Beacon by Tithe Camp to the Old Stones in 270 s (some 1.1 km over the ground), the riders at the Old Stones |
| Ground the stolen storm | The rods round the Old Stones, staked 16 m out round the foot of its dais, where the ground is walked on every side |
| Carry the light to Saltgrass (`carry-light-saltgrass`), Hold Saltgrass (`hold-saltgrass`) | The hamlet Saltgrass |
| Find the lens's buyer | The Saltbreaker Hulks |
| Set Grey Point true | Grey Point Light |
| Follow the reckoner, Empty Moorhold, Open the reckoner's chest | Moorhold |
| Meet Wren and follow her | From Shingle Landing past the Barrow Field into Hollow Coven: her way runs by the barrows |

A renamed task keeps its old id in `was`, so every player's place on their road is kept. The lines and the lore that
named the Vale's or the Moors' places are reworded for the Downs; the chapters of those places' own history, and the
Drowned Vale's book below the gate, keep the Vale and the Moors as land far off on the island.

## The rest

| Call | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The passages' places | The five that came into the Downs are no longer names a passage zone takes; the Vale's and the Moors' other names stay | A zone under the Grey Reach named for Grey Point Light would point at a place that stands on the Downs |
| The public events | One zone's clock: an event every 15 minutes, the Fissure and the raid in turn | The clock as it was for each zone, with no other zone beside it |
| The dungeon of the day | One of the Downs' three dungeons | The island's one patrol zone |
| The hulks' greatest hull | 50 m off the middle, behind it from the way in | A way ends at a place's middle; a hull standing on it left the Saltbreaker Hulks with no way at all |
| The gate's model | Kept as built, its mill stones and mooring stones as they were | The Sunken Gate is a door into the Vale as it was, so the Vale's stone suits it |
