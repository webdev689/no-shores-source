## What it settles

- **One patrol zone on each island**, the one by its port city: the Saltgrass Downs, the Highwater Fells, the Elder
  Thicket, the Ochre Wastes, the Lion Grasslands, the Emerald Tangle, the Kurgan Plateau, the Bogmire Sloughs and
  the Whitewind Tundra. The other eighteen patrol zones go.
- **Each zone stays about 1.5 km across**, made of several connected combat spaces of about 500 × 500 m, joined by
  paths, each with its own ruins and points of interest.
- **Each zone is unique, distinct, varied and detailed**, each given its own care.
- **Each zone has a written plan**: its spaces and what each holds, written for it. The layout generator's rules
  build each space to its plan ([[Layouts]]).
- **The missions, dungeons and bosses beside the zones that go** are not decided: they stay as they are until the
  owner decides.
- **A comprehensive audit and a detailed design pass** of the patrol zones.
- **Saltmere's zone first**: the Saltgrass Downs is designed and built before the other eight, and what it needs is
  built so the others can use it later.

It builds on [[Patrol Areas Are Laid Out With RMB, and Missions Run Through Them]] and
[[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]].
