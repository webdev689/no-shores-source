# Allied Crew Fight on the Wreck and Can Fall

> "There should be allied crew mates fighting others on board with green health bars"

Asked how they should work (fighting and able to fall, fighting and never dying, or following the player), the owner
chose "Fight, and can fall", the option that read: "Two or three of your crew in each zone, already fighting the
boarders when you arrive. They can win or die, so helping them matters."

## What it settles

- On the wreck, the Brig's surviving crew fight the boarders, with green health bars.
- They can win their fights or fall.
  Those still standing regroup ahead as the player goes on: replaced in part by
  [[The Crew Still Standing Regroup Ahead]].
- The crew and the players cannot harm each other. Asked "Right now you cannot hurt your own crew on the wreck, and
  they cannot hurt you: your swings, shots and spells pass through them. Is that what you want?", the owner chose
  "No friendly fire", the option that read: "Keep it as built: crew and player can't harm each other, so swinging
  into a crowded fight never kills an ally."

## Claude's calls

- Each zone with boarders stands three crew at spots laid with the wreck, each about 2 m from a boarder on his deck,
  facing him, so the fight is on from its first tick; the export refuses a spot further than 4 m from a boarder. A
  zone laid without spots stands as many as its wave has foes, two at least and three at most, 1.6 m from a boarder
  toward the zone's checkpoint. At most 18 crew live at once, so the boarders always have room.
- The crew are Minors at the boarders' level, a swordsman and a skirmisher in turn, in the looks of the Brig's three
  plain hands. The named speakers are never stood there, so none can die on the wreck and speak after it.

- An ally's plate shows no alert mark: it would tell the player nothing of a threat to them.
