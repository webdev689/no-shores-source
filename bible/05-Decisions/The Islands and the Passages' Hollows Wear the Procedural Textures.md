# The Islands and the Passages' Hollows Wear the Procedural Textures

> "Outside maps need to recieve the procedrual texture system"

Asked what should wear them, as the passages' zones above ground are drawn by the same renderer as every island landed
on at sea, the owner chose both.

## What it settles

- **The passages' hollows and every island at sea** wear the procedural textures the mainland's ground wears, on their
  ground and their rock, where today they are coloured flat ([[Procedural Textures]], [[Passages]], [[Islands]]).
