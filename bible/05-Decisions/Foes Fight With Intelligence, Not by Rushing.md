# Foes Fight With Intelligence, Not by Rushing

Told what the audit of #68 measured, the owner answered each in turn:

> "1. Best decision
> 2. It's fine
> 3. Best decision
> 4. The goal is enemies have intelligence. Not simply all rushing the player
> 5.  best decision"

The five were these:
1. Foes barely answered the faster swings: Minors never, and no foe blocked a melee swing.
2. The soft lock lets go mid wind-up past 70 degrees.
3. Melee foes never struck a player walking away.
4. Three or more Majors felled a player in about 6 s.
5. Walking groups raise each player's snapshots from about 5 to 20 KB/s.

## What it settles

- **Answers:** foes read and answer a blow at the fight's pace. Their reaction and the lead a raised guard needs
  take the same three quarters as the swings, so they answer as often as before ([[Foes]]). This is Claude's call,
  under "Best decision".
- **The lock:** it stays as it is, letting go past 70 degrees even in a wind-up ([[Combat]]).
- **A player walking away:** a foe in its turn runs after its target, while the rest keep their places ([[Foes]]).
  This is Claude's call, under "Best decision".
- **The goal for foes in numbers:** they fight with intelligence and do not simply all rush the player ([[Foes]],
  [[Enemies]]).
- **Snapshots:** they stay as they are. 20 KB/s is under a tenth of a modest connection, and the rooms' messages are
  not billed ([[Server]]). This is Claude's call, under "Best decision".
