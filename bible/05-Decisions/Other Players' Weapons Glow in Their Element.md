# Other Players' Weapons Glow in Their Element

> "You can't see someone else's element when they have it equipped"

## What it settles

- **Another player's weapon glows in its element** on their figure, as the player's own does, while it is drawn.
- It changes [[Your Weapon Glows in Its Element]] in part: other players' figures no longer stay plain. Foes and the
  menu's pictures still do.

How it works: [[Weapon Glow]], "Other players".
