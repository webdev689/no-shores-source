# Organised and Tested Without Overburden

> "We need to establish proper file organization, proper testing and procedures without overburdening ourselves"

## What it settles

- A small set of [[Rules]], in force from the first commit, most checked by one command: `cargo xtask check`.
- No rule ships with a baseline or a ratchet: a rule that exists holds everywhere.
- A new rule or check needs the owner's agreement, so the list stays short.
- The procedures: [[Making a Change]], [[Branches and Merging]], [[Porting from DFO]].
