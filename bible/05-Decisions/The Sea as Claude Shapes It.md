## What it settles

Claude's calls in building the sea the owner decided ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]],
[[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]],
[[The Sea's Fights, Waves and Close Views Get a Detailed Pass]]). The owner can change any of them.

### The waters

- **Names**: The Lantern Sea, The Cairn Straits, The Elmwater, The Sunscorch Coves, The Gilded Reach, The Mistwrack,
  The Windward Levy, The Fen Deeps and The Rimeholt Deep, for the first island to the ninth.
- **The seam**: sea nearest the fourth or the eighth island is contested where the other's coast lies at most 200 km
  farther.
- **Odd points**: a point as near two coasts goes to the lower island; an isle owns no water; a point off the world
  reads as land.

- **No Boss by a roll.** Her level comes from her water's band. Her way is the Sloop's times 0.9 for a Minor, 1 for a

- **Sighted** half a minute of the two closing head-on, 432 m a step of the voyage's pace, never under 5 km. She is
  kept until 1.1 times that; no roll launches while three sail in sight.
- **Where she comes.** A bold ship comes within the cone from which she meets a voyage that holds its course: 0.8 of
  the arcsine of her way over the ship's. A wary one comes within 60 degrees of the course, and at x10 and under both
  do. A pirate takes the place nearest a coast; a Tithe ship the place nearest a lane, along it.
- **The chase**: a click on her mark sails to meet her, and within 750 m the fight begins.

### Friendly ships

- **Three lanes**, one between each pair of port cities, every leg more than 800 m clear of every coast.
- **A ship** leaves each end every 1,200 s of world time at 5 m/s and is gone on arriving. Her hull comes from a hash
  of her number: a Sloop four times in ten, a Schooner three, a Brig two, a Frigate one.
- **A roadstead** lies 3 km out from its port's coast, and farther, 500 m at a time, until it is 1 km clear of every
  coast.

### Hulls

- **One strength a rank.** Every hull's best way is the Sloop's. A side's broadside weighs the Sloop's, spread over
  her guns, each ball's chances times three over her guns a side, and harm's fractions carried. Her hull, canvas and
  men scale by how often a captain's gunners hit her, so every hull of a rank lasts as long under a captain's fire.
  Under a player's, a Frigate lasts about 5% longer.
- **Rigs and names.** Her handling gain is the matching rig's in DFO: the Large Boat V for the Schooner, the galleon
  for the Brig, the Carrack for the Frigate. Every sail is small. A captain is named by her side and hull, as "Tithe
  Brig".
- **Colours.** The Grey Tithe's slate hull, bone stripe and grey canvas; the pirates' black hull, red stripe and dark
  canvas; a friendly ship's own timber with a band of Overlook's blue, cream canvas and a blue flag.

### Close in

- **Her pace eases** down the voyage's steps to the world's own time while she is watched close (close in, the helm,
  Manage, the deck), a step each 0.15 s, and back up as the view rises. The dial keeps its step.
- **Her size grows** by the same ratio each notch of the wheel coming in, never under her true size ([[Game]], "Her
  size coming in").

### The ruins

- **Walls** breached at every gate and at one piece in three, their towers half as high; piers kept to 40 percent.
- **Buildings**: one in four fallen to a heap, the rest roofless and broken at 35 to 75 percent of their height,
  scorched toward the break. The landmark keeps a third of its height, and its light is out.
- **The streets**: four heaps of rubble to each cell of a way, none within 110 m of the plaza; a Sloop and a Schooner
  wrecked in the harbour, heeled 25 degrees.

### Weather, swell and wakes

- **Weather**: systems about 600 km across drift east at 10 m/s and change over six hours. The sea is calm 15
  percent of the time, moderate 50, rough 25 and storm 10, the wind from 2 m/s in a calm to 25 in a storm.
- **Shelter**: the swell is a quarter of the open sea's under 2 km of open water upwind, whole at 60 km. Within 3 km
  of a port's roadstead it is 0.3 of a moderate sea's.
- **A ship on it** rides through a lag of 0.02 s a metre of her length. She slams when the crest at her stem closes
  faster than 1 m/s and throws spray.
- **Her wake**: Kelvin arms at 19.5 degrees, 0.7 m wide at her stern and widening 0.03 m a metre aft; a bow wave 1.2 of
  her lengths long; her track's wash gone in 20 s. The water carries up to 48 ships' footprints, the player's first.

### The held map

- **Sizes**: words 13 px and a port's name 15 px on a 1280 x 720 screen, growing with the map; each word with a
  parchment halo.
- **What gives way**: words are placed by priority (the place chosen, ports, waters, ruins, nodes), clear of the
  gauntlets' thumbs, every mark and every other word. One with no clear place is dropped; the ship and the marks never
  are. A place's name stands right of its mark, else left.
- **The waters' names**: tried across her own sea in view, 10 px apart, nearest its middle first; wholly on her sea if
  any spot allows, else with her middle on it, never on land.
- **The washes**: each holder's colour at 0.26 over the parchment: home `#3f7f6f`, the Grey Tithe slate `#6b7785`,
  the pirates `#d95c5c`, the contested `#b8862f`. The contested and the Grey Tithe are hatched too, 7 px apart.
- **Difficulty**: green, gold, orange, red and deep purple at levels 1, 8, 15, 22 and 30, darkened to read at 5 : 1
  on the ring, taken at a band's middle.
- **The key**: one framed panel, "Held by" and "Levels", each swatch painted as the map shows that holder. It stands
  in a clear corner. With none clear, it takes the place that costs least: a water's name it covers counts 10, land
  under it 5, any other name 1.

### The fights' ends

- **Sinking**: any ship at no hull goes down over 20 s. She leaves each player who fought her 3, 4, 5, 6 or 8 pieces,
  Minor to Boss, each a kill's drop by the gear loop's law; from an Elite up, one piece is a chest a rank higher.
  They lie within 30 m, drift downwind at 0.3 m/s, are gone after 180 s, and are gathered within 25 m.
- **Striking**: a struck ship lies hove to 90 s, then is scuttled.
- **Boarding**: within 30 m of her, both ships under 2 m/s. Her crew are 3 hands at a Minor, 3 and a heavy at a
  Major, 4 and a heavy at an Elite, 5 and a mate at a Miniboss, 6 and her Boss at a Boss. Boarders come over her rail
  on their own ship's side, 0.5 m in, facing her bow, and a fallen one stands again at the nearest rail. A deck won is
  held 60 s, then she is scuttled.
- **On her deck**: the view cuts to the boarder, no pan. The third-person eye keeps clear of her masts (0.3 m), spars
  (0.2 m) and sides (0.3 m thick, 6 m up), for the camera alone. Her name heads the place line, with "Aboard her: her
  crew stands to meet you"; won, "Her deck is yours" and her hold's take.
- **Losing**: washed up at the port city nearest by the sea's way; a sunk ship comes back with 10% of her hull, 25%
  of her canvas and half her men.
- **Repair**: free at a port city, within 3 km of its roadstead, at rest: a second for each hundredth missing, 100 s
  at most.
- **Limits**: a player or a hostile ship within 750 m of a fight joins it. A wary captain falls on a Sloop by DFO's
  own rule: one she outguns 1.25 to one, or one holed under 0.35 of her hull.

### The fight's look

- **Effects**: 10 puffs of smoke a gun, lifting and thinning within a few seconds; 32 splinters to a hull hit, a hole a
  dark middle in a rim of raw timber; a ball's splash a column of water rising and falling back in a second, 0.6 to 2
  times a round shot's, leaving a ragged patch of foam.
- **Rig**: a third of each sail's 7 by 6 cells shot through at no canvas left; a torn sail and a fallen mast fall to
  leeward and float 40 s; a mast falls in 3 s, taking every piece whose top lies within 2.5 m along her and 3 m over
  its foot; colours come down in 4 s.
- **Going down**: trimmed 10 degrees by her end with more holes, listing to the side with more; 80 flecks of foam a
  second over her from halfway down.
- **What floats**: each piece under a gold mark 2.5 m over it, never under about ten pixels across.
