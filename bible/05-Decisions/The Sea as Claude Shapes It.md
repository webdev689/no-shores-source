## What it settles

Claude's calls in building the sea the owner decided ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]],
[[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]],
[[The Sea's Fights, Waves and Close Views Get a Detailed Pass]]). The owner can change any of them.

### The waters

- **Names**: The Lantern Sea, The Cairn Straits, The Elmwater, The Sunscorch Coves, The Gilded Reach, The Mistwrack,
  The Windward Levy, The Fen Deeps and The Rimeholt Deep, for the first island to the ninth.
- **The seam**: sea nearest the fourth or the eighth island is contested where the other's coast lies at most 200 km
  farther.
- **Odd points**: a point as near two coasts goes to the lower island; an isle owns no water; a point off the world
  reads as land.

### Friendly ships

- **Three lanes**, one between each pair of port cities, every leg more than 800 m clear of every coast.
- **A ship** leaves each end every 1,200 s of world time at 5 m/s and is gone on arriving. Its hull comes from a hash
  of its number: a Sloop four times in ten, a Schooner three, a Brig two, a Frigate one. Arrived, it lies at the
  roadstead 600 s before it is gone, so about one ship lies at each port at any moment.
- **A roadstead** lies 3 km out from its port's coast, and farther, 500 m at a time, until it is 1 km clear of every
  coast.

### Hulls

- **One strength a rank.** Every hull's best way is the Sloop's. A side's broadside weighs the Sloop's, spread over
  its guns, each ball's chances times three over its guns a side, and harm's fractions carried. Its hull, canvas and
  men scale by how often a captain's gunners hit it, so every hull of a rank lasts as long under a captain's fire.
  Under a player's, a Frigate lasts about 5% longer.
- **Rigs and names.** Its handling gain is the matching rig's in DFO: the Large Boat V for the Schooner, the galleon
  for the Brig, the Carrack for the Frigate. Every sail is small. A captain is named by its side and hull, as "Tithe
  Brig".
- **Colours.** The Grey Tithe's slate hull, bone stripe and grey canvas, and a flag `#8f9092` with its balance in
  `#2e3438`, to read apart from the pirates' at fight range; the pirates' black hull, red stripe and dark canvas; a
  friendly ship's own timber with a band of Overlook's blue, cream canvas and a blue flag.

### Close in

- **Its pace eases** down the voyage's steps to the world's own time while it is watched close (close in, the helm,
  Manage, the deck), a step each 0.15 s, and back up as the view rises. The dial keeps its step.
- **Its size grows** by the same ratio each notch of the wheel coming in, never under its true size ([[Game]], "Its
  size coming in").

### The ruins

- **Walls** breached at every gate and at one piece in three, their towers half as high; piers kept to 40 percent.
- **Buildings**: one in four fallen to a heap, the rest roofless and broken at 35 to 75 percent of their height,
  scorched toward the break. The landmark keeps a third of its height, and its light is out.
- **The streets**: four heaps of rubble to each cell of a way, none within 110 m of the plaza; a Sloop and a Schooner
  wrecked in the harbour, heeled 25 degrees.

### Weather, swell and wakes

- **Weather**: systems about 600 km across drift east at 10 m/s and change over six hours. The sea is calm 15
  percent of the time, moderate 50, rough 25 and storm 10, the wind from 2 m/s in a calm to 25 in a storm.
- **A fight's wind** holds from where and when the fight began, with its swell, and does not change with the weather
  while it lasts: the captains' aim and courses are laid by it, a fight is minutes long while the weather changes over
  hours, and every player in it sails the same wind.
- **Shelter**: the swell is a quarter of the open sea's under 2 km of open water upwind, whole at 60 km. Within 3 km
  of a port's roadstead it is 0.3 of a moderate sea's.
- **A ship on it** rides through a lag of 0.02 s a metre of its length. It slams when the crest at its stem closes
  faster than 1 m/s and throws spray.
- **Its wake**: Kelvin arms at 19.5 degrees, 0.7 m wide at its stern and widening 0.03 m a metre aft; a bow wave 1.2 of
  its lengths long; its track's wash gone in 20 s. The water carries up to 48 ships' footprints, the player's first.

### The held map

- **Sizes**: words 13 px and a port's name 15 px on a 1280 x 720 screen, growing with the map; each word with a
  parchment halo.
- **What gives way**: words are placed by priority (the place chosen, ports, waters, ruins, nodes), clear of the
  gauntlets' thumbs, every mark and every other word. One with no clear place is dropped; the ship and the marks never
  are. A place's name stands right of its mark, else left.
- **The waters' names**: tried across its own sea in view, 10 px apart, nearest its middle first; wholly on its sea if
  any spot allows, else with its middle on it, never on land.
- **The washes**: each holder's colour at 0.26 over the parchment: home `#3f7f6f`, the Grey Tithe slate `#6b7785`,
  the pirates `#d95c5c`, the contested `#b8862f`. The contested and the Grey Tithe are hatched too, 7 px apart.
- **Difficulty**: green, gold, orange, red and deep purple at levels 1, 8, 15, 22 and 30, darkened to read at 5 : 1
  on the ring, taken at a band's middle.
- **The key**: one framed panel, "Held by" and "Levels", each swatch painted as the map shows that holder. It stands
  in a clear corner. With none clear, it takes the place that costs least: a water's name it covers counts 10, land
  under it 5, any other name 1.

### The fights' ends

- **Sinking**: any ship at no hull goes down over 20 s. It leaves each player who fought it 3, 4, 5, 6 or 8 pieces,
  Minor to Boss, each a kill's drop by the gear loop's law; from an Elite up, one piece is a chest a rank higher.
  They lie within 30 m, drift downwind at 0.3 m/s, are gone after 180 s, and are gathered within 25 m.
- **Striking**: a struck ship lies hove to 90 s, then is scuttled.
- **Boarding**: within 30 m of it, both ships under 2 m/s, never a ship with no crew on its deck. Its crew are 3 hands at a Minor, 3 and a heavy at a
  Major, 4 and a heavy at an Elite, 5 and a mate at a Miniboss, 6 and its Boss at a Boss. Boarders come over its rail
  on their own ship's side, 0.5 m in, facing its bow, and a fallen one stands again at the nearest rail. A deck won is
  held 60 s, then it is scuttled.
- **On its deck**: the view cuts to the boarder, no pan. The third-person eye keeps clear of its masts (0.3 m), spars
  (0.2 m) and sides (0.3 m thick, 6 m up), for the camera alone. Its name heads the place line, with "Aboard: its
  crew stands to meet you"; won, "Its deck is yours" and its hold's take.
- **Losing**: washed up at the port city nearest by the sea's way; a sunk ship comes back with 10% of its hull, 25%
  of its canvas and half its men.
- **Repair**: free at a port city, within 3 km of its roadstead, at rest: a second for each hundredth missing, 100 s
  at most.
- **Limits**: a player or a hostile ship within 750 m of a fight joins it. A wary captain falls on a Sloop by DFO's
  own rule: one it outguns 1.25 to one, or one holed under 0.35 of its hull.

### The fight's look

- **Effects**: 10 puffs of smoke a gun, lifting and thinning within a few seconds; 32 splinters to a hull hit, a hole a
  dark middle in a rim of raw timber; a ball's splash a column of water rising and falling back in a second, 0.6 to 2
  times a round shot's, leaving a ragged patch of foam.
- **Rig**: a third of each sail's 7 by 6 cells shot through at no canvas left; a torn sail and a fallen mast fall to
  leeward and float 40 s; a mast falls in 3 s, taking every piece whose top lies within 2.5 m along it and 3 m over
  its foot; colours come down in 4 s.
- **Going down**: drawn in the pose the room sends, trimmed 10 degrees by its end with more holes, listing to the side with more; 80 flecks of foam a
  second over it from halfway down.
- **What floats**: each piece under a gold mark 2.5 m over it, never under about ten pixels across.
- **The aim** (in part replaced by [[Aiming a Ship Looks Square Off Its Side, Clear of Its Rig]]: the view looks
  square off the side at the lay, and a ship alongside blocks by its widest): the look runs from the eye as drawn,
  so the sight is where the guns lay. It reaches 0.15 rad over the horizon, past 153 m, and zooms from DFO's
  nearest, 11.4 m, out to the fight camera's 75 m. A ship alongside blocks the line by its waterline's length and
  beam, from its draught to 1 m over its guns.
- **The readout**: its plate at the top left, where the vitals stand on foot
  ([[The Sea Fight's Ship Plate Wears the Vitals' Thin Bars]]): Hull over Sails over Crew, the hull at health's height
  and the others at stamina's, each as long as the vitals' health, with no words inside, as the vitals have none; its
  fire and brace chips and its battery rose under them, as the buffs stand under the vitals, the laid side glowing
  gold (the brace chip and the rose replaced by the ship's bar at the bottom left,
  [[A Ship's Abilities Stand at the Bottom Left, as the Arts Do]]). In a fight the place's name gives way to them, as it does to the ships' plates. The card stands under
  the compass on the ship its laid guns fall on, else the nearest. The volley's tally stands under the sight for 4 s,
  the fight's status line at the top right. DFO's lights on nearby hulls are not drawn yet.
- **The sea's words are notices**, as DFO's toasts are: a ship sighted, other waters entered, who joins a fight,
  colours struck, boarders away, what floats gathered and washing up, each standing 6 s at the top right; the status
  line keeps the state alone.
- **A captain is "it"**, as its ship is, since a captain is the ship's mind in the rules.
- **The sea's clock**: the sea holds the Unix minute its host sets (`Sea::minute`), as a room on land does; the
  bounties count the ships sunk or taken in the sea room itself once the activities branch lands, so the sea tells
  its hosts no word of a ship gone down.
