# The Face Is Mended Whole, and the Gauge Counts Upgrades

After the third audit the owner was asked ten questions. Their answers to nine of them are below; the sixth is
[[The Roster Follows Destiny 1, With Blocking]].

| Asked | The owner's answer |
|---|---|
| "The 5 comes from another pixel font (Silkscreen) and sits slightly off Pixelify Sans's grid. Should I redraw it to match, like the 2, B and Z?" | "Yes" |
| "The Z's diagonal is one smooth stroke rather than pixel steps. Keep it?" | "Your decision" |
| "Text uses one weight (500). Would you prefer the lighter 400?" | "Your choice" |
| "A few symbols, like the anchor and the rarity dots, aren't in the font and fall back to the system's. Should I draw them?" | "Yes" |
| "Every element that doesn't match a shield does half damage. Should each pairing have its own value?" | "Its fine" |
| "The server doesn't check that you're actually standing at the counter you're using. Should it?" | "Should it? Whatever the best decision is" |
| "The hand-drawn letters are generated by a script and stored inside a checked-in stylesheet. Is that OK under the generated-files rule, or should a repo check cover it?" | "Whatever the best decision is" |
| "The gauge on each item icon fills with the item's XP. Should it count upgrades bought instead?" | "It should count weapon upgrade progress, not general xp" |
| "Should the Test Ground's panel and the editor use Pixelify Sans too?" | "Why not" |

## What it settles

- **The 5 is drawn on Pixelify's grid**, as the 2, B and Z are. Silkscreen's 5 is no longer used.
- **The symbols the face lacks are drawn on its grid**: the anchor, the rarity dots and the map's other marks.
- **A shield of another element takes half of every blow**, whatever the pair.
- **An icon's gauge counts upgrade progress**, not general XP.
- **Pixelify Sans is the face of the Test Ground and the editor** as well.
- **The Z, the weight, the counters and the drawn glyphs are Claude's to decide.**

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The gauge | The share of the item's nodes bought | "Upgrade progress" read as its upgrades bought; XP toward a column counts only once it is spent |
| The Z | Stepped in cells, a diagonal one cell wide | Pixelify's own diagonals step; the 2's two-cell steps and round top still tell it from the Z |
| The weight | 500, one weight | The mended glyphs and every size were drawn on its strokes; 400 thins them to less than a cell |
| The counters | The room serves each ask only at its own counter: a Vestige at the Reader's, the vault at a vault's, a ware at its vendor's | The server is the sole authority; a page could otherwise buy or open from anywhere in the plaza |
| The drawn glyphs | Drawn in code and made into a font in the page; nothing generated is kept in git | [[Generated Files Stay Out of Git]] holds as it is, and no new check is needed |
