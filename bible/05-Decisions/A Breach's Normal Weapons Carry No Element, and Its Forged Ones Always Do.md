# A Breach's Normal Weapons Carry No Element, and Its Forged Ones Always Do

> "So, the normal mode weapons should never role with an element while the hard mode always rolls with an element"

## What it settles

- **A Breach's weapon won at Normal never carries an element.**
- **A Breach's weapon won at Hard, its Forged one, always carries an element**
  ([[Breach Weapons Come Normal and Forged]]).

How it is built: [[Breaches]] ("Rewards"), [[Gear]] ("Elements").
