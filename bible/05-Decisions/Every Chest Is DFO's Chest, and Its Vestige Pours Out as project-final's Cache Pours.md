# Every Chest Is DFO's Chest, and Its Vestige Pours Out as project-final's Cache Pours

> "Also the chest should be ported from DFO, but utilize the way everything comes out from project-final"

> Asked which chests become DFO's chest (the chests to find and the dungeon's cache; only the chests to find; or every
> chest, as DFO's rule had it: the chests to find, the cache and the public events' plain and heroic chests): "Every
> chest"

> Asked whether the Aurem and the patrol materials also come out as drops to walk over beside the Vestige, and how they
> look: "Only the Vestige drops"

> Asked whether a searched container pours its finds out the same way, or only the chests: "Only chests pour"

## What it settles

- **Every chest is DFO's chest**, the owner's model DFO stood in every chest (a base, a lid, a hinge and a latch;
  [[Pick - Chest]]): a patrol's and a dungeon's chests to find, a dungeon's cache, and a won public event's plain and
  heroic chests. It wears the game's own wood, iron and gold, never Daggerfall's pictures ([[Own Work]]).
- **A chest's Vestige comes out as project-final's cache pours its loot**: thrown up out of the chest as its lid
  opens, bouncing and settling, and taken by walking over it ([[Pick - project-final Drops]]).
- **Aurem and patrol materials go straight to the player**, as before.
- **A searched container pours nothing**: what it gives goes to the player as before.

Replaces in part [[Chests and Searchable Containers as Claude Builds Them]] (the chests' looks, and the Vestige laid
before the chest) and [[Public Events as Claude Builds Them]] (the chest's lid and its Vestige put in the ten). How each
is built is in [[Chests and Searchable Containers as Claude Builds Them]].
