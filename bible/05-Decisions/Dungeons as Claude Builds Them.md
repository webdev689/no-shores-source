# Dungeons as Claude Builds Them

> Asked in what order Dungeons 2.0 comes over: "3. Take your time and do everything properly"
> ([[DFO's Dungeons 2.0 Comes Over, Within the Patrol Areas]])

> "Dont adapt things just to adapt them. It needs to fit into what we are building"

> "They need to be a slighty linear thing with a clear beginning, middle, end and an easy navigation path out of the
> dungeon, like a return portal"

> "Do a comprehensive and detailed pass on this. Ensure everything recieves our texture system, props are laid, and
> everything is perfection"

> "This is your baby to mold." ([[The World Is Claude's to Mold]])

## What it settles

Claude's choices for the dungeons in the patrol areas, built in full and recorded in [[Dungeons]]. The owner can
change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What of the Forge comes | Its block of 16 by 16 cells of 3.2 m; rooms carved by a seeded split, in its styles' sizes and shapes; corridors by the cheapest way through the rock; each room's doorways framed; pillars in great rooms; torches on room walls and every fourth corridor cell; the way in's exit door; the check that every block is one walk, with a failed seed taking the next; and its heart's way back to the start, as the return portal | The generator the owner's Patrol asks for, as DFO built it |
| One floor | A dungeon is one floor; the Forge's stacked storeys and their stairs stay behind | [[Movement]] and the [[Navmesh]] stand one floor at a point |
| A run, not a maze | A block's rooms joined one after another, from the doorway in to the doorway on, never by the Forge's spanning tree and loops; a block opens only onto the blocks before and after it in the chain, by one doorway. The end is the last room of the last block | The owner: "a slighty linear thing with a clear beginning, middle, end"; about one loop in a dungeon, where the Forge's law made five |
| The return portal | On the end room's wall farthest from the way in, a doorway of light as wide and high as the exit door, open while the Reeve lies fallen, as the cache is | The owner's "easy navigation path out of the dungeon, like a return portal", and DFO's heart, which carried the player back to the way in |
| Props | Each style's own forged props laid as the Forge laid its scenery, one for each ten cells of a room, against its walls: the Tithe's crates, cabinets, shelves, tables and racks in the Halls; beds, benches and a trough in the Cells; coffins, tomb markers and broken columns in the Crypt; statues, pedestals, a lectern and a throne in the Sanctum. Each is a solid. They keep clear of the doorways, the way in, the portal, the torches and where the foes and the Reeve stand | The owner asked that props be laid; the forged props come over with the rest of the branch's models |
| What stays behind | Doors and locks, traps, scenery sprites (forged props stand in their place), the blocks' people, riddle vaults, last fire, light hues, block names, the director, DELVE II's encounters, and the cave and mine styles | Each needs a system Overlook does not have (lockpicking, Luck, magicka, poison, rest, Daggerfall's light table and sprites), or nothing in Overlook asks for it (block names, the director, beside the garrison's own hearing and standing again); caves and mines need a rock picture the painter does not paint |
| Dungeons in a patrol area | Three, each behind its own entrance | A patrol is about 1.6 km across: three give a route between them and its resource spots |
| Where an entrance stands | Laid out from the patrol's id, the same every time: inside the ring and 60 m inside its edge, 60 m from every spawn, 40 m from every resource spot, 300 m from another entrance, on dry ground that rises no more than 1 in 4 across the 12 m round it | Found while crossing the patrol, never in the way at a spawn |
| An entrance | One of the four dungeon entrances Daggerfall's dungeon blocks stand, among the forged models (42000, 42001, 43601, 43602), drawn from the dungeon's seed, its door facing the middle of the patrol | Daggerfall's own dungeon doors, forged with the rest of its models |
| Its name | "The \<style\> of \<the patrol\>": The Crypt of the Saltgrass Downs, The Halls of the Gullwing Vale, The Cells of the Driftwood Moors, The Sanctum of the Pale Wastes. A patrol's three take three different styles | A place named for what it is and where it lies, as the nodes are |
| Its size | Three or four blocks of 51.2 m in a chain from the way in's block, each next one beside the one before | A few minutes' walk to the end, with fights on the way |
| Its style | Crypt, hall, cells or temple: the Forge's rooms for each, in its two dungeon pictures, in the island's climate | The four styles whose pictures the painter paints |
| Who shares one | One room for each dungeon at each sea, as each node has one at each | No fresh copy to loot again |
| Entering and leaving | E within 2.5 m of the entrance's door: "Enter" and its name; the veil says "Entering" it. Inside, E at the exit door by the way in, or at the return portal once the Reeve has fallen: "Leave", the veil, and the player stands before the entrance's door, facing out | Destiny's use prompt, and the Game's veil between places; the portal is the owner's easy way out |
| Its foes | The Grey Tithe's dungeon garrison ([[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]]) on the Forge's floor: a Reeve Miniboss alone in the end room; in the other rooms past the way in's, a band of Majors and Elites, one foe for each ten of the room's cells, two to four; spread evenly along the walk when they would pass the room's most | A dungeon's garrison, standing where the Forge put its foes |
| Their levels | The patrol's: its island's band, raised by the seas | One patrol, one band |
| The cache | A chest beside the Reeve's post, shut while the Reeve stands. When the Reeve falls it opens, and every player in the dungeon finds a Vestige before it, theirs alone, sure, at the Reeve's level and colours. It shuts when the Reeve stands again | Each activity gives its own Vestiges on completion ([[Gear as Claude Shapes It]]) |
| Dying inside | Back at the way in after the 4 s | The way in is the dungeon's one spawn, as a section's spawns are its own |
| The light | No sky and no sun: each torch a warm light reaching 8 m that flickers, and a faint cold light everywhere else, so a wall past a torch still reads | Underground |
