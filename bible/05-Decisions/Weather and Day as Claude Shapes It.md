# Weather and Day as Claude Shapes It

> Asked how dark the night should be, after the day's length was settled: "Make the most detailed decisions and
> finish this to 100%. Come back when you're done"

## What it settles

Claude's choices for the weather and the day and night
([[Weather and Day and Night Come From DFO, Much Improved]], [[A Day Lasts One Real Hour]]), built in full and recorded
in [[Sky]] and [[Weather]]. The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The hour | One hour across the whole world, read off the wall | Players meet in one social space; a time zone for each island would split them |
| The sun's path | A real sun's at each place's latitude, from 62 degrees north at the map's top to 8 at its foot | The owner's map already lays the cold islands north and the hot ones south |
| Seasons | None yet: a long summer, the sun 15 degrees north of the equator | Night lasts 18 to 27 minutes of the hour, near the third the owner was shown |
| The moon | One, new to new in eight days, unnamed | A name is the owner's |
| The weather's make | Drifting fields carried by westerlies in the north and trades in the south, each climate's thresholds set by its biome's warmth and rain | One field over the whole map, as [[The World Map]] settles; DFO's map of storm systems cost a fifth of a second a node and was each browser's own |
| The wind | Round the cloudy lows over the steering flow, 0.8 to 26 m/s, never a dead calm | A calm would leave a ship stopped with no way to make way |
