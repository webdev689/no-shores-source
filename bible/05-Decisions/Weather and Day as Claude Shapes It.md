# Weather and Day as Claude Shapes It

> Asked how dark the night should be, after the day's length was settled: "Make the most detailed decisions and
> finish this to 100%. Come back when you're done"

## What it settles

Claude's choices for the weather and the day and night
([[Weather and Day and Night Come From DFO, Much Improved]], [[A Day Lasts One Real Hour]]), built in full and recorded
in [[Sky]] and [[Weather]]. The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The hour | One hour across the whole world, read off the wall | Players meet in one social space; a time zone for each island would split them |
| The sun's path | A real sun's at each place's latitude, from 62 degrees north at the map's top to 8 at its foot | The owner's map already lays the cold islands north and the hot ones south |
| Seasons | None yet: a long summer, the sun 15 degrees north of the equator | Night lasts 18 to 27 minutes of the hour, near the third the owner was shown |
| The moon | One, new to new in eight days, unnamed | A name is the owner's |
| The weather's make | Drifting fields carried by westerlies in the north and trades in the south, each climate's thresholds set by its biome's warmth and rain | One field over the whole map, as [[The World Map]] settles; DFO's map of storm systems cost a fifth of a second a node and was each browser's own |
| The wind | Round the cloudy lows over the steering flow, 0.8 to 26 m/s, never a dead calm | A calm would leave a ship stopped with no way to make way |
| Ships and the wind | A ship sails in the weather's wind where she is, held a minute at a time, the usual breeze (6.5 m/s) her rated wind, held between 0.3 and 2 times it | [[The World Map]] settles that the server judges the wind; a minute's hold lets a browser predict a ship exactly |
| The swell | The long swell stays the world's one swell; the local wind drives the chop, the whitecaps and the spray | A swell comes from far storms; changing it under a ship would shake every fight's tested numbers for little |
| The key light | The sun by day, the moon once the sun has set, shadows cast along whichever lights | One light the whole scene agrees on, so a moonlit night has shadows |
| Night | In between: readable under the moon in the open, dark under trees and in alleys; the city's lanterns, the Beacon and a carried torch or lantern light the ground round them | Offered to the owner as Claude's pick; no lit windows, which have no places in the city's data |
| What falls | Rain, snow and sand drawn round the eye, standing in the world; lightning's bolts drawn from the strike itself | Every player sees the same storm, as the weather is shared |
| Lightning | Strikes on a 4 km lattice in 6 s slots, the same for everyone, thunder after each strike's distance at 343 m/s | DFO's overhead lightning was each browser's own and lit nothing at night |
| Music at sea | A sea song now and then while sailing out of danger, 4 to 8 minutes apart, never in rain, storm or fog: the sea at night after dusk, the still sea in light wind, the open sea otherwise; Landfall once at the landfall moment | Gives the four sea songs a home without breaking roaming's "ambience only until danger nears" |
| The homeless sounds | The ambience hears the hour, so the night's voices and beds play; rain, snow and sand have beds of their own; thunder follows each strike; the wooden step plays on the Sloop's deck | The owner's "attaching the sounds that dont have a home" |
| The World Editor and the Test Ground | Keep the clear afternoon | They are tools for shaping and trying, where a moving sun would only get in the way |
