# Patrol Areas Are Laid Out With RMB, and Missions Run Through Them

> "Using our RMB for town layouts to create detailed patrol spaces and utilizing the system for missions."

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked where RMB layouts go: (a) villages, ruins and outposts built inside patrol and mission sections, by World
> 2.0's steps at a village's scale; (b) parts of the port cities opened up as patrol space; or (c) both: "A"
>
> Asked which of Destiny 1's activities come, and whether missions are written by hand or generated: "Story missions
> come later. Patrol spaces should have public events, beacons, resource nodes, and more to come later. I really just
> want to gey the layout generator right"
>
> Asked whether a mission runs in its own room or inside a patrol area's shared room: "Missions are single player with
> the ability to invite other players. Patrol zones will be reused for mission layout. So much like destiny, a mission
> could lead through an open area with other players and then back into a closed instance"
>
> Asked whether players go inside RMB buildings or they stay solid: "Buildings are now open sp a cess instead of
> interact to enter."

> "I want you to take some autonomy, research online, get the layouts right and be as detailed as possible. These
> dont need to be simple square areas. These need to have proper detail, handcrafted.
>
> Take your time and only come back when this is 100% complete. AAA grade is the goal"

Then, with screenshots of Destiny's Cosmodrome (the Divide, the Cosmodrome Wall, the map of its areas) as
reference:

> "Also the big thing im going for is ensuring the land isn't completely flat and that patrol zones dont feel like
> they're on open land.
>
> Images attached as reference.
>
> Basically patrol zones have large regions, inter connectivity, detail. Walls, canyons, large structures that create
> pathways, bounds, etc
>
> Im really pushing for maximum detail, geometry and hand craftedness. We're pushing this beyond limits"

## What it settles

- Villages, ruins and outposts are built from Daggerfall's RMB blocks ([[RMB Blocks]]) inside the patrol areas and
  the mission sections, by World 2.0's steps at a village's scale; the port cities stay as they are ([[Cities]]).
- The work now is the layout generator. Patrol areas will hold public events, beacons, resource nodes and more, later;
  story missions come later.
- Missions are single player, and the player can invite other players. Patrol areas are reused in missions' layouts:
  as in Destiny, a mission can lead through an open area shared with other players and then into a closed instance.
- Buildings are open spaces walked into, not entered by interacting.
- The layouts are not simple square areas: they are detailed and handcrafted, AAA grade. How they are laid out is
  Claude's to research and decide ([[The World Is Claude's to Mold]]).
- A patrol area's land is not flat and does not feel like open land: it has large regions joined to one another, and
  walls, canyons and large structures that make its pathways and its bounds, in as much detail and geometry as can be
  made. Destiny's Cosmodrome is the reference for the feel; nothing of Destiny's comes over ([[Own Work]]).

It builds on [[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]] and [[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]],
and takes up the mission loop that [[Gameplay Starts With the Shared Rules]] left for when the work reached it.
