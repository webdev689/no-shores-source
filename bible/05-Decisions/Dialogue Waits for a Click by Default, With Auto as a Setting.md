# Dialogue Waits for a Click by Default, With Auto as a Setting

> "I want to add the default option where you can click to skip dialouge and must click to continue instead of it
> auto continuing."

Asked whether click to continue is a setting or how dialogue always works, the owner chose "Setting, click by
default": a Settings toggle, Dialogue: Click to continue or Auto, defaulting to click, kept with the account as the
other settings are.

Asked which lines wait for the click, the owner chose "All but mid-fight": every line of the dialogue box waits, but
lines said while a fight is on keep moving on by themselves; barks over heads stay timed.

Asked what else moves a line on besides the left click, the owner chose "Space / E" (rebindable as every control
is), "Pad A", and "Show a prompt": a small mark in the box once a line is typed, saying to click to continue.

## What it settles

- A click while a line is typing shows the whole line; a click once it is typed moves to the next ([[Speech]]).
- By default a line of the dialogue box waits for that click; Auto, chosen in Settings, moves on by itself as before.
- Lines said while a fight is on move on by themselves under either setting. Barks over heads stay timed.
- Space or E on the keys, and A on the pad, do what the click does; they are rebindable
  ([[Every Control Can Be Rebound, and the Pad Plays as Destiny's]]).
- Once a line is typed and waits, the box shows a small prompt to continue.
