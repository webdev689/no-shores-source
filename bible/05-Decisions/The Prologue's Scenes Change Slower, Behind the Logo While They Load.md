# The Prologue's Scenes Change Slower, Behind the Logo While They Load

> "2. There should be a longer transition between scenes, if needed utilize a loading screen when needed to improve
> performance"

Asked how much longer, the owner chose "About twice" (fades out over about 2 s, a second of black, and in over about
2.5 s); asked what the loading screen shows, "Logo and 'Loading'" (the No Shores logo over black, with the loading
line under it).

## What it settles

- A change of scene through black fades out over 2 s, holds black at least a second, and fades in over 2.5 s
  ([[Prologue]], "Between scenes").
- While the next scene is still coming, the logo and LOADING stand over the black, and its first frames are drawn
  unseen under it.
