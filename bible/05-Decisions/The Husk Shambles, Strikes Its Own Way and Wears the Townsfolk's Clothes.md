# The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes

> Shown the Husk's first prototype from the Sprite Forge: red crystal broken out of the skull, through the right eye,
> from both shoulders and down the spine, each shard tearing a wound. Asked whether the look is the direction, whether
> Husks wear the forge's townsfolk clothes, torn, rather than bare bodies, and whether the Lattice's build gets its own
> pull request:
>
> "1. Honestly love it, but more gore. Walk animation should be more zombie-like, with husk having its own unique
> attack plus the lunge attack
>
> 2. Yep, same clothing
>
> 3. Keep this on one PR"

## What it settles

- **The Husk's look** is the prototype's: the forge's own body, red crystal broken out of the skull, the right eye,
  both shoulders, the spine and the left forearm ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]).
- **More gore**: torn flesh round every wound, and blood run down the skin from each wound and from the mouth.
- **Its walk is its own**, more like the walking dead's than a person's.
- **Its blows**: an attack of its own, and the lunge.
- **It wears the townsfolk's clothes**, torn: the clothes of the ports' people it was.
- **The Lattice's build goes on the same pull request** as the lore.
