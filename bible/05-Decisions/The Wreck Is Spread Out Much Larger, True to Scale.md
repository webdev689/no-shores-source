# The Wreck Is Spread Out Much Larger, True to Scale

After playing the [[Prologue]] live:

> "The first combat playspace ambience and design is amazing, but it's way too small for combat"

> "After the cinematic and you wake, enemies are right on top of you, no time to breath or learn the game controls"

Asked how it should grow (spread out at true size, scaled up half again, or its decks cleared), the owner chose to
spread it out at true size, and wrote: "#1, the playspace needs to be much larger".

## What it settles

- The wreck is rebuilt in Blender much larger: its hulls pushed apart and joined by wide wreckage, so each fight has
  far more room. The ships stay true to scale.
- Its look and its ambience stay as they are: the owner called them amazing.
- Waking on it gives the player time to breathe and to learn the controls before the first foes reach them.
