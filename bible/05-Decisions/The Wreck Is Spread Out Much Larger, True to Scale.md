# The Wreck Is Spread Out Much Larger, True to Scale

After playing the [[Prologue]] live:

> "The first combat playspace ambience and design is amazing, but it's way too small for combat"

> "After the cinematic and you wake, enemies are right on top of you, no time to breath or learn the game controls"

Asked how it should grow (spread out at true size, scaled up half again, or its decks cleared), the owner chose
"Spread out, true size", the option that read: "Rebuild it in Blender with the hulls pushed apart and joined by wide
wreckage (fallen masts, decking, a floating debris field), each fight zone about three times the area. The ships stay
true to scale." They wrote: "#1, the playspace needs to be much larger".

## What it settles

- The wreck is rebuilt in Blender much larger: its hulls pushed apart and joined by wide wreckage, so each fight has
  far more room. The ships stay true to scale.
- Waking on it gives the player time to breathe and to learn the controls before the first foes reach them.

## Claude's calls

- The wreckage between the hulls is six wide rafts of torn decking on mast lengths and casks, ground a body can
  circle on, kept clear of the swell. Every fight zone has at least four times its old ground, more than the option's
  "about three times", for "much larger"; the export refuses a wreck where under 2,000 m² is walked.
- The player wakes on the Brig's quiet stern: no wave, no crew and nothing across the way on. A zone's wave stands
  only as the player enters it, well across from where they come in.
- To join the ships, the Frigate's port side is stove in abreast its gun deck and its port bulwark broken forward,
  where the way comes aboard from the wreckage; the Sloop floats higher, so its side stays out of the swell; the
  Frigate's quarterdeck is cleared but for its wheel, and the arena's heap stands below its front, piled 3.7 m. Her
  side stove in abreast her gun deck, and the arena's heap below her quarterdeck's front, are replaced by
  [[Thule Fights on a Quarterdeck Twice as Long]]: her quarterdeck runs forward over the gun deck, and the way up onto
  it is a ramp from the wreckage off her port quarter, its heap on the ramp.
- A rim round the wreck's blocks holds every body on it, and the sea is drawn round the hulls and rafts, not through
  them.
