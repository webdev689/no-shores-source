# Strikes Run Faster, Not Twice as Fast

> "Also do me a favor and speed up third person attack frames more."

That ran a blow's frames about twice as fast as DFO's. Then:

> "Also reduce the attack frames please. We went too far with them"

## What it settles

- **Third person.** A blow's frames run about 1.4 times as fast as DFO's: the wind-up through the last three quarters
  of the time up to the blow, the follow-through over a little over half that time after it. The blow still lands when
  the fight's rules land it ([[Test Ground]]).
