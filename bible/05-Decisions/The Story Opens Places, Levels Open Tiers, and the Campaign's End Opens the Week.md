# The Story Opens Places, Levels Open Tiers, and the Campaign's End Opens the Week

> "A lot of content also opens up as your progress in the story and post campaign."

Offered: the story along the way opens places and the hub's functions, Saltmere's bounty board and patrols after the
prologue and each further island and its waters as the story reaches it; levels open the daily patrol dungeon's
tiers; finishing the campaign opens the endgame week, the Elite Dungeon of the week, the Abyss Dungeon of the week and
Breaches, each also asking its level or Prestige; and until more of the campaign exists, the prologue stands in for
its end. Asked what the story should open along the way and what should wait for the campaign's end:

> "Approved and I'll take your suggestions"

## What it settles

- **The story opens places and the hub's functions**: Saltmere's bounty board and patrols after the prologue
  ([[The Game Opens With a Story Mission at Sea]]), and each further island and its waters as the story reaches it.
- **Levels open the daily patrol dungeon's tiers.**
- **Finishing the campaign opens the endgame week**: the Elite Dungeon of the week, the Abyss Dungeon of the week and
  Breaches, each also asking its level or Prestige.
- **Until more of the campaign exists, the prologue stands in for its end.**
- **What the story opens beyond this** is Claude's to suggest, and the owner's to change.

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]], with
[[Dailies and Weeklies Follow Destiny 1, With One Abyss Dungeon a Week]].
