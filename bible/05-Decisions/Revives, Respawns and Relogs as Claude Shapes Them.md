# Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them

> "Please take autonomy and be as detailed as possible. Come back when this is 100% complete"

## What it settles

Claude's choices in building [[Fallen Players Are Revived as in Destiny]],
[[Activities Keep Checkpoints, and Patrol Zones Respawn the Player Near Where They Fell]] and
[[A Player Logs Back In Where They Left the Overworld or a Social Space]]. The owner can change any of them. How each
works is in [[Revives]] and [[Accounts]].

### The revive

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who may revive | Any other player in the room, alive, on foot and not riding | Destiny's revive is a fireteam's; here everyone in a room fights on one side |
| Who may be revived | A player fallen in a fight; not one dead past the section's edge or by a fall | Destiny's Ghost stays where its Guardian fell; past the edge there is nowhere to stand them |
| How | Hold Use within 2.5 m across and 2 m up or down of the fallen body | Destiny's "hold interact in close proximity"; its range is unpublished, and 2.5 m is a node's gather's reach |
| How long | 2 s | Unpublished: the estimates found run from 1 to 5 s; a node's gather is held 2 s |
| What breaks it | Letting go of Use, leaving its reach, falling, mounting; a blow taken does not | No published rule makes damage break it; Destiny's one documented break was a bug, fixed in 7.3.0 |
| Two at once | The first to take it up holds it; a second hand adds nothing | No published rule; the simplest that cannot be raced |
| Revived | Where they lay, whole with every healing charge, the foes leaving them be 3 s | As a respawn stands a player ([[Three Healing Charges, Refilled by Blows and Kills]]); Destiny's health after a revive is unpublished |
| A revive's lockout | None: a fallen player may be revived at once | Destiny's normal raids wait 5 s, its patrol none |
| The fallen ally's mark | A faceted shell of light round a glowing eye, drawn new after Destiny's Ghost, 1.1 m over the body, lit so it beats and shows through walls | Destiny marks the fallen with their Ghost; a lit mark is the game's own for what is seen from everywhere ([[A Mark Over What a Player Can Use Is Seen Through Walls]]) |
| Told | "Hold to revive" and the name, with the hold's ring; over the view for 3 s: "Ada is down", "Ada revived you", "You revived Ada", "An ally" for a guest | Destiny's own words are unconfirmed; plain and short ([[Plain Text]]) |

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|

## Destiny's rules found

Researched for [[Fallen Players Are Revived as in Destiny]] from search results (destinypedia.com and pcgamer.com could
not be fetched from the cloud session):

- **Revive**: hold interact by the fallen player's Ghost, in close proximity (destinypedia.com/Ghost). Raid revives wait
  5 s in Destiny 2 and 30 s in Destiny 1's normal raids; Destiny 2's Restricted Zones revive at once
  (destinypedia.com/Raid, destinypedia.com/Darkness_Zone).
- **Self-respawn**: Destiny 1 after 3 s where no darkness holds (destinypedia.com/Darkness_Zone); Destiny 2's dungeons
  45 s (sportskeeda.com, 2023). Patrol has no darkness zones (destinypedia.com/Patrol).
- **Darkness zones**: no self-revive; a fireteam all down returns to the last checkpoint (destinypedia.com/Fireteam).
  Destiny 2's raids add a 2:30 shared timer (gamerant.com, d2.destinygamewiki.com/wiki/Raid).
- **Checkpoints**: one for each completed encounter in raids and dungeons, kept until the weekly reset
  (help.bungie.net/hc/articles/360049199911).
- **Limited revives**: Destiny 2's Nightfalls, "Players have a limited amount of revives. Defeat Champions to gain more
  revives." (destinypedia.com/Nightfall); Destiny 1's Nightfall sent a wiped team to orbit.
- **Unpublished**: the revive's range and hold time, whether a blow breaks it, health after it, and how a patrol's
  respawn point is chosen.
