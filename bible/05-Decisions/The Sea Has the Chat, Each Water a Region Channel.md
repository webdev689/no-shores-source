# The Sea Has the Chat, Each Water a Region Channel

Told that the chat opened on foot alone and that Local at sea reached no one else, the owner wrote:

> "The sea should have the chat. Each sea region is its own region channel + world chat"

Asked which key should open the chat now that it works at sea, where Enter sets sail, lands and docks at the helm, the
owner chose "T everywhere (Recommended)", offered as "T opens the chat on foot, at the helm and in a sea fight; Enter
keeps setting sail at the helm and stops opening chat."

## What it settles

- **The chat works at sea**: at the helm, in a sea fight and on deck.
- **Each sea region has a channel of its own**, beside World.
- **T opens the chat everywhere**; Enter no longer opens it, and keeps setting sail, landing and docking at the helm.
