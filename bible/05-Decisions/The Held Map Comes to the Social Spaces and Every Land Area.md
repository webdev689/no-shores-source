# The Held Map Comes to the Social Spaces and Every Land Area

The owner, once Overlook's hub was merged and deployed: "Our new task will be starting on integrating the held map
into social spaces and all land based areas"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> How the map is raised on land: "In hands, anywhere" (N anywhere on land: the view goes to first person where the
> player stands and their gauntlets raise the parchment, as at the helm)
>
> What it shows on land: "The island or social space mapping. Public event spawns, vendors, etc"
>
> What a click on it does on land: "Nothing, only to look"
>
> Whether the world goes on while it is up: "Yes, as at the helm" (the player stands still holding it; others,
> townsfolk and foes carry on, and a foe can reach them)

## What it settles

- **The held map** ([[Held Map]]), which today is raised only at the ship's helm
  ([[The Sea Is Navigated From the Helm With the Held Map]]), **is brought into the social spaces and every land
  area** as well.
- **On land it is raised in the player's own hands, anywhere**, as at the helm: first person where they stand, the
  gauntlets raising the parchment.
- **It shows the island, or the social space, where the player is**: its mapping, with its public events' spawns,
  its vendors and the like.
- **A click on it does nothing**: on land it is only looked at.
- **The world goes on while it is up**, as at the helm.
