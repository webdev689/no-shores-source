# The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It

Asking for the first Breach ([[The First Breach Follows the Vault of Glass, at Normal and Hard]]), the owner wrote:

> "I want you to really utilize vault of glass as reference on building the traversal, encounters, bosses with our
> own unique mechanics and following our own lore."

> "I want you to take autonomy and build our first breach to AAA grade perfection."
> ([[The First Breach Is Built in Full, on Claude's Autonomy]])

## What it settles

Claude's choices for the first Breach, built in full and recorded in [[Breaches]]. The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The Drowned Vale: the Gullwing Vale as it was, drowned under the sea's underside and written over by the Lattice; its red Lattice the overtaken | The vale's own words ("its old stones older than anyone remembers") and the Lattice's ([[Lattice]]); a place, as the Vault is, with no Cube shown |
| Where it opens | The Sunken Gate, an area of its own in the Gullwing Vale: a hollow before a cliff and its sealed doorway | The owner's "dedicated designed section in the patrol zone"; a vale with a keep and old stones fits a door into its past |
| The opening | The Waking: three moorings round the Spire held at once fill it in 150 s; fewer let it ebb (a quarter at Normal, a half at Hard); a Bulwark claims an empty mooring | The Vault's Spire, on our moorings and our Bulwark |
| Its parts, in order | The Waking; the Descent; the Mending; the Choir; the Unlit Ways; the Broken Stair; the Two Shores; the Steward | The Vault's: the Spire, the jump, the Confluxes, the Oracles and the Templar, the Gorgons, the jumping puzzle, the Gatekeepers, Atheon |
| Each encounter's own turn | Seams the overtaken fuse at; bells broken in their waking order; desks the Scrivener is blotted from; Watchers' cones and sound; rifts held open from moorings, a Keeper beyond each; the Steward's takings to a shore of bells | The Vault's shape, each mechanic our own |
| The relic | The Lodestone: carried bare handed; Strike, Hold True (cleanses, halves foes' blows), Ground (breaks a ward); left lying it unwrites the company | The Aegis' place in the fights, our own powers and its own risk |
| The mark | Rewritten, unwritten when its time comes unless cleansed (the font, Hold True) | Marked by Time, in our lore's words |
| The tiers | Normal: foes at 26, opens at 25, the fallen stand again after 10 s. Hard: foes at 30, opens at 29 once Normal is won, the fallen raised only, 5 revives an attempt. At either tier the fallen outside a running encounter stand again after 6 s | The Vault's Normal and Hard, one level under its foes: two under, a company strikes at about half its match and could not fell the Steward in the time its rules give however it played; the owner's limited revives and no respawn at Hard, read as within an encounter, as the Vault let the fallen stand again between encounters: the owner may want it everywhere |
| Raising and following | Any company member raises a fallen one by holding the interact key 3 s within 2.5 m; the fallen follow a living company member's view. Replaced, for raising, by the game's revive ([[Fallen Players Are Revived as in Destiny]]): the Breach keeps only its own rules on top ([[Breaches]]) | The owner's player follow, for the company alone |
| Wipes | Every living player down 3 s at once ends the attempt: the company stands whole at its stage | The Vault's wipe, with no wait |
| The company | Up to eight in a company and a Breach's run; every other run four. Launched by its company from the Playlist, never matched | A Breach is for up to eight ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]]); the Vault had no matchmaking |
| Into the run | The zone's room opens the gate to the company that woke the Spire; the run lets in that company's members once its gate opened, wherever they come from, its first one being one it was launched for | The opening is fought outside and the rest is the run's ([[The Breach Opens in the Patrol Zone, and the Rest Is Its Own Instance]]) |
| Seen by the zone | Everyone in the Gullwing Vale sees the Waking, and which companies are inside, on what encounter | The owner's "other players in the zone can see that your team is taking on the raid" |
| Loot | Each encounter's first clear a week at each tier pays a Breach Vestige of that tier, the Steward two; Normal's armour climbs from 19 to 28 Prestige, Hard's from 21 to 30, 3 an upgrade; the banner (an emblem) at the first clear ever; the dye and the horse by chance, likelier at Hard; hidden chests pay Essence and a lore chapter | The owner's own loot for each tier and max Prestige from Hard alone; the Vault's weekly lockout |
| Its cosmetics | The banner The Drowned Vale; the dye Drowned Vale as its shader; the horse barding Drowned (rust, barnacles, weed, crystal up the mane); the book *Soundings*, ten chapters | The owner's own horse, banner, shader and lore book |
| On the Playlist | Its crest between the playlists and the week's dungeons, Normal over Hard; Launch only | One place for every activity the Playlist starts |
| The gate on screen | Its banner within 60 m of the gate, a line further off; a compass mark in violet | Seen by the whole zone, loud only near it |
| The run on screen | Drawn over the dark, lit as below (Its light); the Lodestone turning over its bearer; the way out at the Descent's entry or the court's portal | The run's spaces hang in the dark; a dungeon's door and portal |
| Its great foes' figures | The Scrivener in the Idol's body, the Steward in the Heart's, the bells as crystal with no figure | The Lattice's bodies until their own are made |
| Following | F (RB) chooses which living company member the fallen follow | A free key, next to the others |
| Its look | Split-stone cliffs, the old vale's dressed stone faced and ruined, the Lattice's red crystal grown through it, every piece over the dark hung with the rock it was torn from | One drowned place, read at a glance |
| Its great foes' weight | Matched to everyone who has played in the run, living, fallen or away, and matched again as more come in (keeping its share of health); then the Scrivener six times, the Steward six and a half, the Mender and the Warden one and a half; a Keeper matched to half the company | A great foe must last until its rules come round: the Scrivener past a minute and a half against eight, the Steward past its third taking, and never beaten before its last toll without being laid bare, as Atheon needed Time's Vengeance; and within reach at the tier's opening level for eight who lay it bare in every window |
| The great foes' blows | The Steward sweeps and lunges, slams at two thirds with two Husks called beside it, rings at one third; the Scrivener shoots aimed, at half quicker with two Flickers called behind it | Bosses with telegraphed blows, as every Elite Dungeon's has, of kinds the page already draws |
| The Steward's clock | Takings on a fixed measure whether or not some are away, those still away unwritten first; taken from the whole run; laid bare once between takings | No company can stall it, step out of its reach or keep it bare |
| The Unlit Ways | Won when every living member stands on the Stair; Watchers cannot be harmed | The Gorgons' maze is walked by the company, not one sneak, and the Gorgons could not be killed |
| The Choir's stakes | The font rests 25 s; bells break in a few blows; a wave stands as each round wakes; a ring Rewrites the whole run | Failure costs something, the order is a test of memory not damage, and eight have work |
| Hard's weight | Every blow on a player weighs 1.25 times at Hard, in the run and the company's Waking | Hard above an Abyss Dungeon at the same level, as the hardest content should be |
| The gate | Open to its company until the Breach is cleared or a day passes; a Waking lost when the Spire stands empty a minute; its waves sized to the company; one refused told why | A company can always come back through its gate, and one or two players cannot hold it from everyone else |
| Leaving and coming back | Down if they left down, their mark and its time, and their shore kept by account | Leaving and rejoining undoes nothing the Breach laid on a player |
| Teaching and telling | A teaching line as each encounter begins and a won line as it ends; a wipe named by its cause; each bell its own note; warning rings dashed; the Spire's fill a light the zone sees | Every mechanic taught before it kills, readable without colour, loud for the company and seen by onlookers |

## Its rules at their edges

Each a call of Claude's, made where an audit of the whole raid found a rule that could be slipped or a mechanic that
punished what play could not prevent.

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who a great foe is matched to | Everyone who has played in the run, by account, still here, away or fallen; a great foe standing as more come in is matched again, keeping its share of health | Seven could leave before the Steward stood and come back to a quarter of its health; one could stand it alone for the rest to join |
| The company unwritten | Loses the attempt 3 s on, whoever stands again meanwhile | At Normal one fallen a few seconds earlier stood again inside the wipe's 3 s, the attempt went on with the Lodestone's rule off, and the Steward's last unwritings could be outlasted |
| Hold True's rest | From whenever it ends: guard let go, its most held, a set-down, a pass, its bearer fallen | A set-down and a take-up raised it again at once, near always up |
| The Lodestone in the dark | Fallen into the dark with its bearer, it goes home to its plinth; it is set down only standing | Lying metres under any floor, out of reach, it unwrote the company with no counterplay |
| The Scrivener's ward | Holds where a blow is judged, as an Elite Dungeon's Boss's ward does | Re-warded at a sliver, one heavy blow felled it in the tick before its health was put back |
| The Arts and the bells | No spell seeks or strikes a bell or a Watcher | A Tempest's orbs and the Duskraven's bolts seek the nearest foe, so an Art could break a bell out of its order |
| The font | Stays lit 3 s after its first cleanse, then rests 25 s from it | A ring marks the whole chapel; the first to reach the font spent it for everyone |
| The Scrivener's lone bell | Once a writing, 22 s before it: a miss rings 14 s (Hard 16 s) before the writing | Every 20 s against writings every 30 s, a miss rang 2, 12 or 22 s before one in turn: the clocks decided, not the company |
| The Mending's Penitents | Each walks to the nearest open seam, turning as one shuts and falling apart with none open; one every 5 s for four, as much more often for more | They fused into seams just shut, and into the vale with the Mender up; at six to eight the fusions never came near |
| The Watchers' hearing | From 3 s into the Ways | The first Watcher posts within its hearing of where the Ways begin, so a company running in was heard before it could read the teaching line |
| A sighting at Hard | Its unwriting comes whoever reaches the Stair in its 3 s; the Heart's chest shuts once anyone is seen | Stepping onto the Stair cancelled it; the chest stayed open to an attempt someone had been seen in |
| A Bulwark at a rift's mooring | Shuts the rift only from a mooring the company has left, as at the gate | It shut a held rift at once, stranding those inside, against what the Waking teaches |
| The Steward's weight and the tiers' doors | 6.5 times its match; each tier opens one level under its foes, 25 and 29 | Two under, a company strikes at about half its match and could not fell an eight-times Steward in the time its takings give, however it played; never laid bare, a matched company still cannot fell it before its last toll |

## Its music

Asked for "proper thematic music", and whether the Drowned Vale's music should be played on recorded instruments, the
synthesiser or Freesound's own pieces, the owner answered "Plenty of sounds come from freesound": read as scores
written for the Drowned Vale and played on instruments recorded on Freesound, picked on the sound sheet. Claude's
musical calls, built in [[Music]]:

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The theme | One for the whole raid: D Phrygian, low, D rising through E flat to G and home, then to B flat and down through the mode; a second strain answering it | A drowned chapel's chant, not happy; the Phrygian flat second is the Vale's pull, heard in every song |
| Who carries it | The low choir and the organ; the cello or the horn its strain; the Steward's horn doubling the choir | The chapel and its bells; the climax at its fullest |
| Each encounter's colour | The Waking and the Mending on the Lattice's glass, the Choir and the Two Shores on the bells, the Ways on the waterphone | The Lattice glows as glass, the chapel's bells are the Choir's sound, the Ways are wet and dark |
| Keys and tempi | The Waking D at 92, the Mending C at 100, the Choir E at 84, the Ways B flat at 66, the Two Shores E flat at 108; the Scrivener G at 112 to 134, the Steward D at 120 to 140 | Each fight its own, the Steward home in the theme's key |
| The modern layer | A drone under every fight, booms on the downbeats, struck metal and a noise riser into the bar line, never a pitched sweep | The owner's modern spin, inside the music's rule against ringing and whining |
| The fights' form | Twelve takes of eight bars a fight: a vamp, the theme, the strain and a breakdown, each in three feels, the third over turned chords; nine in a last stand | As every fight song varies ([[The Fight Music Varies, Recombined From Takes]]) |
| The Unlit Ways | No drums: a drone, the waterphone, the taiko's heartbeat and a low pulse; the peak (the high choir, a boom) only once a Watcher has seen | Stealth, as asked; the seen moment is its danger |
| The Two Shores | The tonic struck against its flat second | Two shores a half step apart |
| The great foes | The Scrivener from its waking; the Steward's score from its court's start, whether or not the Steward is engaged; the Steward's fall the theme in D major, the Breach cleared | The Steward's court is the climax from its first taking |
| Between encounters | Three calm pieces (the Descent's, the Broken Stair's, After the Bells), sparse, on felt piano, harp, cello, a singing bowl and the choir; the first 20 s into a quiet stretch, then 45 to 120 s of silence after one, never the one begun last while there is another | A run is short: an island's two to nine minutes would never let one play |
| A fight outside an encounter | The song of the encounter fought where the player stands, else the Waking's | The run never hears the island family's fight |
| The drums' recordings | Each lasts no longer than its kit drum; struck metal, the bowed cymbal and the riser cut a tenth of a second after their peak, a riser's recording ending on its bar line | No drum rings, recorded or synthesised, and a swell lands where it is written |
| Until a pick comes | Each track plays its FM program, each drum the kit's own | The Breach has music now and gains its instruments as they are picked |

## Its light

Claude's calls for how the run is lit and drawn ([[Breaches]], "Its art"). The owner can change any of them.

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The run's sky | Its own, written every frame: no sun or moon, a cool light from above about three quarters of a clear noon's, darker from below, the dark as its fog | Underground; never the zone's day or night, which the run cannot see. A raid's floor must read where to stand, the foes and the edges: a floor median of at least 0.12 in sRGB where no lamp reaches |
| Distance | Everything fades into the dark from 40 m and is black by 120 m | The shores no longer float in view, and the dark has depth |
| Where the light comes from | The pieces' own crystal (a hot red, as bright as the square of its glow), flames (warm) and glass (coloured), read from the built meshes, the 16 lamps nearest the eye, falling off steeply | The seals, bells, Seam, candles, rose window, hearth and lantern light what is round them, wherever Blender puts them, in pools at least twice as bright as the floor, so the dark keeps its depth |
| The figures | Lit by the same sky and lamps as the stone | A body by a fire is warm, one in the dark is dim |
| The shores | Drawn only while a player is in one or being taken there | Rooms 230 m off across the dark were drawn every frame in plain view |
| Stained glass | Leaded panes of ruby, cobalt, amber, green and pale, lit from behind | A rose window, not a flat orange disc |
| Water | A slow ripple, the sky's glare at a grazing eye, and glints of the lamps | The drowned floors read as water |
| Bells outside their rounds | A dim bell asleep at every socket no bell foe holds | Empty frames read as missing art; the woken bell is the bright one |
| A hidden chest that will pay | The heroic chest, its crystal alight; the plain chest dim while it will not | Told apart at a glance, with what the game already has |
| The gate opened to a company | Its doorway the dark of the way in, filling the doorway's opening exactly, a faint violet low in it | The recess's back wall read as a wall where the company is meant to go through; the seal's own shape stood proud of the jambs |
