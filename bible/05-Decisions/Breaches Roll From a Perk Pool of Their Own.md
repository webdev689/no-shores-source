# Breaches Roll From a Perk Pool of Their Own

> "The future breach content will be the same but have its own unique perk pool."

## What it settles

- **A Breach's drops are as every content's**: its own set look, its perks rolled at random
  ([[Every Drop Wears Its Content's Set, With Random Perks]]).
- **Its perks roll from a pool of the Breaches' own**, made with the Breaches
  ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]]).

## How it works

- The pool is thirteen perks marked `breach` in `content/gear/traits.json`; a Breach's armour or weapon made by a
  Vestige rolls every node of its perk columns from them, never one twice on the piece, and its other columns from the
  shared pools, and keeps what it rolled ([[Gear]], "Verbs", "The Breaches' perks"; the calls in
  [[Gear as Claude Shapes It]], "The Breaches' perks"). The Drowned Vale's Vestiges make them ([[Breaches]]).
