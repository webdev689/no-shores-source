# The Lattice as Claude Shapes It

> Shown the Husk's second round, its shambling walk and lunge in a townsman's torn and soaked clothes: "I love this"

> Asked which attack of its own the Husk makes, the Gore (a head-down charge with its great crystal), the Rake (both
> arms raking down) or the Burst (a ring of shards cracking off its back): "you decide"

> Shown the Gore: "Go even deeper detail with the animations"

## What it settles

Claude's calls inside the owner's decisions on the Lattice
([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]],
[[The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes]],
[[The Heart Is the Port's People Fused Into One Giant]]). The owner can change any of them. How each is drawn:
[[Lattice]].

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Every foe's crystal | Clusters through the skin, each shard dark at its root and bright toward its point; a torn wound round each; blood run and spattered; veins creeping under the skin from every wound | The crystal grows and overtakes, flesh up close ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]) |
| Where the gore lies | Over the clothes, so they look torn and soaked; the veins under them | "more gore", which clothes would hide |
| The Husk | Crystal through the back of its skull, set back so its face shows, and through its right eye, jaw, chest, shoulders, spine, left forearm and right knee; a townsman's long shirt, belt, breeches and boots | The ports' people were townsfolk |
| The Husk's attack | The Gore: bowed until its great crystal points ahead, pawing the ground, held as its tell, then a short rush; the fight's charge shape ([[Combat]]) | Only the Husk can make it, and a lowered head reads from across a fight |
| The Husk's walk and lunge | A shamble of two unlike steps under a constant tremor, the head cocking on its own; a lunge that finds its prey, coils, grabs and buckles | After the owner's own Husk in project-final: snap-then-plant, uneven strides, the odd shudder |
| The Flicker | After project-final's: gaunt, tall and purple; headless, a spray of shards from its neck; its arms crystal limbs ending in long blades, each a layer the game takes away when it is shot off; in breeches. It blinks, slashes left or right and strikes overhead | Both blades never "swiping the same arc at once", which "reads as a toy" |
| The Idol | The Flicker's body, its head behind a flat crystal mask with one eye and a crown of spikes; its arms welded into one emitter, a barrel to a lens, the eye and lens glowing cyan; no weak spot, as in project-final. At rest it holds the emitter low at its right side; it aims, holds a charge as its tell, and fires | After project-final's, its visor and sensor made a mask and an eye |
| The Ravager | The Husk's body, its head held higher, under a ragged crystal hood closed over its skull, two eyes glowing in its shadow; a mantle of dark shards hanging from its shoulders; a crystal blade pointed forward and splayed out from each fist, with the shards it throws growing on them, a layer of their own; a townsman's vest. It hurls in a burst, the hands in turn, and mauls when cornered | project-final's gun-blades reshaped: no firearms |
| The Bulwark | At 1.2 times a man, flesh slabs on its shoulders, back and belly, a crystal boulder for a head; a slab of crystal on its forearm for a shield, its core glowing in it; a maul that forms when the shield breaks, a hammer of crystal held out to its right so it never crosses its head; bare to the waist in breeches, a belt and boots. It discharges from the shield's core, sweeps and slams | project-final's first shield, since being flanked needs a front; its rifle reshaped as a maul |
| The Heart's look | Three times a man, hunched; eight people half out of it, faces with open mouths; dark crystal ribs over its glowing heart; the anchor held by its ring, long enough to rest its crown on the ground, rusted and grown with crystal, barnacles on its arms. The fused people, the heart, the anchor and the person it tears out are layers of their own | The owner's pick ([[The Heart Is the Port's People Fused Into One Giant]]) |
| The Heart's moves | A heaving idle, a lumbering walk dragging the anchor's crown along the ground, the anchor swept and slammed, a stomp, and the tear: a person clawed out of its side, held up and flung, to rise a Husk | "it drags the anchor, stomps and sweeps", and tears out a Husk when hurt |
| The Lattice's armour | From the owner's reference, built with the forge's armour kit: red crystal plate, purple in its shadows, every edge lit purple; a fanned crest; points broken out of the pauldrons, vambraces, cuisses and greaves; leather straps and a studded belt over a dark suit | The owner's "A mix of purple and red" on the reference's shapes; the forge's own grading for Daggerfall's shading |
| Struck and killed | Each foe flinches and staggers a step back; the Heart staggers down onto one knee, its chest before whoever struck it. Killed, every foe disperses in the one wave: a burst of light, its figure gone to crystal and flung up and out, a ring of shards rushing out on a band of light | The owner's "one wave for all"; the Heart's kneel is its opening |
| The Lattice's props | Sixteen to start: blighted ground, a spire, a cluster, a vein; a crusted rock, a crate, a barrel, a cart, a wall, a lamppost, a boat, crystal grown through each; a nest, a pylon, a shrine; a fissure and a crater.
