## What it settles

Claude's calls inside the owner's decisions on the Lattice
([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]],
[[The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes]]). The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Husk's own attack | The Gore, fourteen frames: the head dropped with a shudder until the great crystal points ahead, the right foot pawing the ground back twice as a bull does, aimed and trembling two frames as its tell, then the rush, the jolt of what it strikes, a stagger and the head shaken as it rights itself; the fight's charge shape ([[Combat]]) | Only the Husk can make it, it turns the Husk's mark into its weapon, and a lowered head reads from across a fight |
| The Husk's clothes | A townsman's plainest from the forge's wardrobe: a long shirt, a belt, breeches and boots | The ports' people were townsfolk |
| Its walk | Sixteen frames, two steps unlike each other: the right leg dragged, its toe scraping; the left swung slow, snapped down and stamped flat, the knee giving. Hunched under a constant tremor, the head lagging the lurch and once snapping sideways, one deep shudder, the crystal's shoulder hitching, the dead left arm swung by the lurch alone, the right hand clenching and opening | More like the walking dead's than a person's, after the owner's own Husk in project-final: snap-then-plant footfalls, uneven strides, a tremor, the odd shudder, the head cocking on its own |
| Its lunge | Twelve frames: the head snapping up to find its prey; coiled with both arms cocked behind, trembling three frames as its tell; launched; fingers splayed at full reach; the hands clenched shut on the grab; overreached on a buckling knee; stumbling back; the head shaken | A lunge a player can read and step from |
| Where its gore lies | Over its clothes: the shirt torn where the crystal broke through, and soaked | "more gore", and the clothes would hide gore under them |

The owner's word on its look, walk and clothes as built: "I love this".
