# The Lattice as Claude Shapes It

> Shown the Husk's second round, its shambling walk and lunge in a townsman's torn and soaked clothes: "I love this"

> Asked which attack of its own the Husk makes, the Gore (a head-down charge with its great crystal), the Rake (both
> arms raking down) or the Burst (a ring of shards cracking off its back): "you decide"

> Shown the Gore: "Go even deeper detail with the animations"

## What it settles

Claude's calls inside the owner's decisions on the Lattice
([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]],
[[The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes]]). The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Husk's own attack | The Gore, fourteen frames: the head dropped with a shudder until the great crystal points ahead, the right foot pawing the ground back twice as a bull does, aimed and trembling two frames as its tell, then the rush, the jolt of what it strikes, a stagger and the head shaken as it rights itself; the fight's charge shape ([[Combat]]) | Only the Husk can make it, it turns the Husk's mark into its weapon, and a lowered head reads from across a fight |

Built on the forge's own body at a quarter of its size and drawn four times as large; its shell, core, cradle and tendrils layers of their own, shown by its phase. The owner's word on its look, walk and clothes as built: "I love this".
