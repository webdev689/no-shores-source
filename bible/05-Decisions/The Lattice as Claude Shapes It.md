# The Lattice as Claude Shapes It

> Shown the Husk's second round, its shambling walk and lunge in a townsman's torn and soaked clothes: "I love this"

> Asked which attack of its own the Husk makes, the Gore (a head-down charge with its great crystal), the Rake (both
> arms raking down) or the Burst (a ring of shards cracking off its back): "you decide"

## What it settles

Claude's calls inside the owner's decisions on the Lattice
([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]],
[[The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes]]). The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Husk's own attack | The Gore: the head dropped until the great crystal points ahead, held as its tell, then driven into whoever stands there in a short rush; the fight's charge shape ([[Combat]]) | Only the Husk can make it, it turns the Husk's mark into its weapon, and a lowered head reads from across a fight |
| The Husk's clothes | A townsman's plainest from the forge's wardrobe: a long shirt, a belt, breeches and boots | The ports' people were townsfolk |
| Its walk | Eight frames: hunched, the head lolled toward the great crystal, the left shoulder dropped under its crystal and that arm hanging dead, the right half raised, the right leg dragged short with its toe down | More like the walking dead's than a person's |
| Its lunge | Eight frames: crouched with both arms cocked behind, held two frames as its tell, thrown forward clawing at the chest, overreached, stumbling back | A lunge a player can read and step from |
| Where its gore lies | Over its clothes: the shirt torn where the crystal broke through, and soaked | "more gore", and the clothes would hide gore under them |

The owner's word on its look, walk and clothes as built: "I love this".
