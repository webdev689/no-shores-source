# Set Names and Faction Perks as Claude Drafts Them

Claude's drafts for [[Everything Carries a Set Name, and Perks Roll by Pool]] and [[Faction Weapons Wear Their Colour
and Roll Their Faction's Perks]], each the owner's to change. The faction perks are approved; the set names are not in
force until the owner says yes.

## Set names

Every piece is named by its set and its kind ("Portwatch Helm", "Portwatch Sword"). An armour set and the weapon metal
of the same content and look share a name. No name is a material's.

| Content | The forge's look | Set name |
|---|---|---|
| The world | clothes (a Normal piece) | Homespun |
| The world | leather | Deckhand |
| The world | the thieves' | Smuggler |
| The world | chain | Shoreguard |
| The world | iron | Portwatch |
| The world | steel | Wayfarer |
| A dungeon | the knights' | Barrowknight |
| A dungeon | silver | Gravewarden |
| A sea dungeon | elven | Reefborn |
| A sea dungeon | dwarven | Brassbound |
| An Elite Dungeon | orcish | Wreckborn |
| An Elite Dungeon | mithril | Brightwater |
| An Abyss Dungeon | adamantium | Deepwarden |
| An Abyss Dungeon | ebony | Blackwater |
| A faction | its guild's set and metal | its faction's: Stormcaller, Lamplighter, Veiled |
| A Breach | daedric | named with the Breaches |

## Faction perks

Approved by the owner, as drafted: [[Every Perk Stands, the Factions' Nine With Them]].

Each faction's pieces roll its own perks beside the shared pool, tied to its Art's element and the statuses the fight
already has. Three a faction: two for weapons, one for armour.

| Faction | Perk | Fits | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stormcallers (Storm) | Gathering Storm | weapon | Every fourth blow in a row on one foe Charges it. |
| Stormcallers (Storm) | Thunderhead | weapon | Blows on a Charged foe strike 15% harder. |
| Stormcallers (Storm) | Squall Step | armour | A perfect dodge gives you Haste for 3 seconds. |
| Lamplighters (Divine) | Kindling | weapon | A kill with it Links you to your nearest ally for 6 seconds. |
| Lamplighters (Divine) | Shared Flame | weapon | While Linked, your blows strike 10% harder. |
| Lamplighters (Divine) | Lightbearer | armour | Your healing charges restore 15% more health. |
| The Veiled (Shadow) | Nightfall | weapon | A riposte Veils you for 3 seconds. |
| The Veiled (Shadow) | From the Dark | weapon | Your first blow while Veiled crits. |
| The Veiled (Shadow) | Shade Step | armour | While Veiled, a dodge costs 20% less stamina. |
