# Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed

> "Lets only do 3 port cities."

> Asked what that covers, the friendly ships' routes alone or the whole world: "3 main port cities. The others are
> desolate and destroyed"

> Asked which three: one per holder, Overlook (home), Sunspire (the pirates' first island) and Rimeholt (the Grey
> Tithe's seat): "One per holder"

> Asked how ships run between the three, between every pair or through Overlook: "Best detailed decision"

## What it settles

- Three port cities remain: Overlook on the first island, Sunspire on the fourth and Rimeholt on the ninth, one for
  each holder of the waters ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]]).
- The other six cities (Cairnwatch, Elmhaven, Goldreach, Mistfall, Windmere and Fenhallow) are desolate and destroyed:
  no longer ports.
- Friendly ships sail only between the three port cities. How they run between them is Claude's call
  ([[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]]).

Replaced in part by [[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]: Sunspire's waters are the Grey Tithe's, as the pirates'
were.
