# Foe Arrivals as Claude Builds Them

> "I really want to be as detailed as possible and do this right. Take some autonomy. Be as detailed as possible and
> make this your own"

## What it settles

Claude's choices in building [[Some Foes Stand Already, Others Arrive by the Tithe's Airships and the Lattice's Crystal]].
The owner can change any of them. How it works is in [[Enemies]] ("Arrivals"), [[Lattice]] and [[Modules]].

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who stands already | A garrison as its room opens; a site's or a quest's group as a player comes within 150 m; an escort's ambush and a step's watchers; a dungeon's bands; the prologue's boarders; companions; a foe a page asks for | Each is there before the player sees it, as Destiny's foes on the ground are; an ambush is sprung, not flown in |
| Who comes by airship | Under the open sky (a node's land or an island's): a garrison group standing again, a half-fallen patrol group's fallen, a mission's waves, the raid's waves and its tithe-taker, a quest defence's waves, the War Horn's Assessor and his four, and a territory's group retaken | "others come in from a dropship": each of these would otherwise stand from nowhere, out of the player's sight or not |
| Who comes under a crown | The Fissure's waves and its Warden; the Sunken Gate's Waking and the Bulwarks it sends to claim a mooring, a crown over each mouth and perch; the Drowned Vale's waves, a crown over each mouth | Where the Lattice come; one crown over each place they come out of |
| A Boss's called foes | Built where they stand in 0.6 s, as a player arriving is: a Grey Tithe foe white then ember, a Lattice foe in its crystal; no ship or crown | Destiny's bosses' adds appear by an effect beside them |
| A foe arriving | Thinks, steps and hears not at all, breaks with no fallen leader and holds its group's beat until it lands, is in no foe's sight, and no blow, shot, spell or telegraphed blow touches it, nor does the page's aim stop on it; counted among the room's living foes | Destiny's foes are not struck while they appear; a player cannot fight what is not yet whole |
| The airship | The Brig's own hull, built by the ships' builder (`tools/modules/shipkit.py`), its masts and sails left off; two outrigger beams through its bulwarks braced with iron, a drum thruster under each end (an intake cowl, banded and capped, a flared bell aft, its throat alight), two lift bells framed under its keel, an iron furnace house amidships with a glowing firebox door and two stacks, pipes from the furnace house along the beams, iron stays, iron plate laid on its lower sides under two wales, an iron spur at its stem, its drop hatch's doors hung open, lanterns and lit stern windows, a grey banner from its foremast's stub, crates, barrels and coal on deck: 17,992 triangles | "Large modified vessels retrofitted with combustible thrusters"; the Brig is the Grey Tithe's own ship; the Blender pipeline, the owner's |
| Its flight | In over 8 s from 340 m off and 70 m up, swinging 28 m to one side, slowing, its nose flaring up to brake; hangs 16 m over its ground, bobbing; its first foe let down 1 s after it hangs, the next every 0.5 s, each built over 1 s; lingers 1.2 s; out over 9 s, climbing to 110 m and turning 0.9 rad; kept 12 m over the land on its way. Six foes a ship; a ship coming to within 40 m of another's ground sets out 1.5 s later and hangs 24 m abeam of the first, either side in turn: one over another met its hull and its climb out | Seen coming from afar and seen leaving; the player has some 10 s to see a ship before its foes stand |
| Which way a ship comes | Toward the nearest living player, swung up to 0.6 rad off that line | "seen flying in" |
| The Tithe's spawn effect | The player's own build, from the feet up in 2 by 2 cells, each white as it lands and then a moment ember; let down in a beam from the hatch (two warm white edges and a core), a ring of light at the feet widening as it is built; dust thrown up as it lands | "a spawn effect similar to the player"; the ember is its airship's fire |
| The crown | Built from the Lattice's own shards (`latticekit`): a heart of its light, seven great spars reaching down from it like a claw, seven shorter ones splayed nearly level between them, a cluster pointing down and points standing up, a halo round its waist and a long shard dripping from each spar's end: 1,544 triangles | "a crystallized structure that appears, as the enemies spawn below" |
| Its life | Its heart hangs 6.4 m over the ground. It grows out of its heart over 1.2 s, overshooting as crystal setting, its crystal drawn in out of the air; each foe begins forming 0.25 s after the one before and forms over 1.3 s, from the head down, its cells landing in its crystal, a thread of its light down to it and a ring round its feet; it holds 0.6 s after the last, cracks a third of a second flickering, and breaks in the Lattice's wave, 2.4 times a man's size, seven shards flung out to fall on the land under each, lie and sink; one first seen long past forming or breaking is not heard doing it | The Lattice's own death wave is its sign; "spawn below" |
| A patrol's groups | Its bands, the largest (three or four of two to four), and beside them two or three smaller groups: a lone Crossbowman sentry and a lone Runner scout, a Collector and his Runner, a Collector and a Crossbowman, two Crossbowmen on watch, a Collector and two Runners; the sentry and the watch hold their posts facing the landing, the rest walk beats; its square stands 16 at most as it opens | "The largest groups being what we have now, and then smaller varied groups" |
