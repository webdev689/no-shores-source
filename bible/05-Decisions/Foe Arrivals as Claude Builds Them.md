# Foe Arrivals as Claude Builds Them

> "I really want to be as detailed as possible and do this right. Take some autonomy. Be as detailed as possible and
> make this your own"

## What it settles

Claude's choices in building [[Some Foes Stand Already, Others Arrive by the Tithe's Airships and the Lattice's Crystal]].
The owner can change any of them. How it works is in [[Enemies]] ("Arrivals"), [[Lattice]] and [[Modules]].

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who stands already | A garrison as its room opens; a site's or a quest's group as a player comes within 150 m; an escort's ambush and a step's watchers; a dungeon's bands; the prologue's boarders; companions; a foe a page asks for | Each is there before the player sees it, as Destiny's foes on the ground are; an ambush is sprung, not flown in |
| Who comes by airship | Under the open sky (a node's land or an island's): a garrison group standing again, a half-fallen patrol group's fallen, a mission's waves, the raid's waves and its tithe-taker, a quest defence's waves, the War Horn's Assessor and his four, and the patrol's own airship groups | "others come in from a dropship": each of these would otherwise stand from nowhere, out of the player's sight or not |
| Who comes under a crown | The Fissure's waves and its Warden; the Sunken Gate's Waking and the Bulwarks it sends to claim a mooring, a crown over each mouth and perch; the Drowned Vale's waves, a crown over each mouth | Where the Lattice come; one crown over each place they come out of |
| A Boss's called foes | Built where they stand in 0.6 s, as a player arriving is: a Grey Tithe foe white then ember, a Lattice foe in its crystal; no ship or crown | Destiny's bosses' adds appear by an effect beside them |
| A foe arriving | Thinks, steps and hears not at all, breaks with no fallen leader and holds its group's beat until it lands, is in no foe's sight, and no blow, shot, spell or telegraphed blow touches it, nor does the page's aim stop on it; counted among the room's living foes | Destiny's foes are not struck while they appear; a player cannot fight what is not yet whole |
| The airship | The Brig's own hull, built by the ships' builder (`tools/modules/shipkit.py`), its masts and sails left off; two outrigger beams through its bulwarks braced with iron, a drum thruster under each end (an intake cowl, banded and capped, a flared bell aft, its throat alight), two lift bells under its keel, an iron furnace house amidships with a glowing firebox door and two stacks, pipes along the beams, iron stays, iron plate over its lower sides under two wales, an iron spur at its stem, its drop hatch's doors hung open, lanterns and lit stern windows, a grey banner from its foremast's stub, crates, barrels and coal on deck: 16,588 triangles | "Large modified vessels retrofitted with combustible thrusters"; the Brig is the Grey Tithe's own ship; the Blender pipeline, the owner's |
| Its flight | In over 8 s from 340 m off and 70 m up, swinging 28 m to one side, slowing, its nose flaring up to brake; hangs 16 m over its ground, bobbing; its first foe let down 1 s after it hangs, the next every 0.5 s, each built over 1 s; lingers 1.2 s; out over 9 s, climbing to 110 m and turning 0.9 rad; kept 12 m over the land on its way. Six foes a ship; a ship coming to within 40 m of another's ground sets out 1.5 s later and hangs 8 m higher | Seen coming from afar and seen leaving; the player has some 10 s to see a ship before its foes stand |
| Which way a ship comes | Toward the nearest living player, swung up to 0.6 rad off that line | "seen flying in" |
| The Tithe's spawn effect | The player's own build, from the feet up in 2 by 2 cells, each white as it lands and then a moment ember; let down in a beam from the hatch (two warm white edges and a core), a ring of light at the feet widening as it is built; dust thrown up as it lands | "a spawn effect similar to the player"; the ember is its airship's fire |
| The crown | Built from the Lattice's own shards (`latticekit`): a heart of its light, seven great spars reaching down from it like a claw, seven shorter ones splayed nearly level between them, a cluster pointing down and points standing up, a halo round its waist and a long shard dripping from each spar's end: 1,544 triangles | "a crystallized structure that appears, as the enemies spawn below" |
| Its life | Its heart hangs 6.4 m over the ground. It grows out of its heart over 1.2 s, overshooting as crystal setting, its crystal drawn in out of the air; each foe begins forming 0.25 s after the one before and forms over 1.3 s, from the head down, its cells landing in its crystal, a thread of its light down to it and a ring round its feet; it holds 0.6 s after the last, cracks a third of a second flickering, and breaks in the Lattice's wave, 2.4 times a man's size, seven shards flung out to fall on the land under each, lie and sink; one first seen long past forming or breaking is not heard doing it | The Lattice's own death wave is its sign; "spawn below" |
| A patrol's groups | Its bands, the largest (three or four of two to four), and beside them two or three smaller groups: a lone Crossbowman sentry and a lone Runner scout, a Collector and his Runner, a Collector and a Crossbowman, two Crossbowmen on watch, a Collector and two Runners; the sentry and the watch hold their posts facing the landing, the rest walk beats; its square stands 16 at most as it opens | "The largest groups being what we have now, and then smaller varied groups" |
| A site's group | A band | Its caverns and runs are laid and walked with bands; a smaller group up a ledge, facing away, is not met by a player walking a run (the zones' own test, `sites_held`) |
| The airships' groups | Half the time a band, else one of the smaller groups | Variety where the player meets most new foes |
| How many | A patrol's room holds 32 foes (a node's 24 and 8 for the airships), a zone with events 48 | Room for what the airships bring; the square's 16 leave room for its sites and quests |
| When an airship comes | Once a player roams (60 m or more from every spawn), the first 20 s after, then every 45 to 75 s, to each roaming player in turn; at most three of its groups standing | "present moreso": foes met across a zone 1.4 by 1.7 km whose garrison stands only by its landing. Measured offline on Saltmere's three zones, a player walking each zone's resources for 300 s is brought four or five ships, meets 24 to 27 foes and has 2.1 to 2.7 within 80 m on average |
| Where it sets down | On ground the room has, 35 to 80 m from the player (as near 50 m as it can), 25 m from every player, 20 m from every foe and post, 60 m from every foes' site and quest place and from the edge of each public event's ground, running or not; where no ground reaches, a ground baked round a 60 m cell 50 m from the player, ahead of them where it can be (a player by the zone's edge facing out is brought one beside them), ten at most, whichever place's turn finished each, never while another place's ground is baking nor the tick after another place waited for its turn | Between the zone's laid encounters, never crowding one; the room bakes one ground at a time, some 45 ticks each, so a site's or a quest's waits at most one of the airships' |
| What a brought group does | Walks a beat where it was set down, or holds its post there if a sentry or a watch; never stands again; once no player, standing or fallen, has come within 220 m of it for 90 s, its foes all idle, it is gone | The next is brought where the players are; gone far out of sight |
| A group standing again | By airship, under the same 120 s (10 minutes for a great foe) and 40 m rules | Nothing appears from nowhere |
| The raid's tithe-taker | Brought by airship with the second wave, his 50 s round counted from when he stands | His round is the whole 50 s |
| The scripted players (the room door's test and the page's bots) | A foe standing is one not dead, as its glance says, not one with health left (a sliver crosses as none); a foe still arriving is waited for 8 m off, guarded, and struck once it lands | As a player plays: no blow at what is not whole, no standing in the drop. Measured: the pair seals the Fissure heroic (at tick 16,378); the raid's last wave outlasts them (Marrow, alone, fells them one at a time while he heals walking home), its stores at 732; the room's own raid test (`raid_tests.rs`) and the page's two bots beside its player win the heroic raid |
| Its sounds | Every one a recording the owner picked, as the events' are: a ship setting out, `amb.thunder.roll` heard from afar; its fire, `story.fire`, every 2.6 s; hanging, `amb.sea.hull.creak`; leaving, `ship.blast`; each foe let down, `story.whoosh`; a crown forming, `amb.ice.ping` and `element.cosmic`; each foe forming, `amb.ice.crack`; breaking, `shield.break` and `finish.cosmic` | No new recording without the owner's pick; these are near what each is |
| Its light | Each thruster a fire's lamp reaching 16 m as hard as it burns; the crown its crystal's red reaching 14 m | At night a ship is seen by its fire, a crown by its red |
| On the wire | Each foe's packed numbers four more (what brings it, its number, the tick it is let down and the tick it lands, 0 once landed); every airship and crown whole in each snapshot while it lasts (`Arrival`) | The page draws a ship's whole flight from its ticks alone |
