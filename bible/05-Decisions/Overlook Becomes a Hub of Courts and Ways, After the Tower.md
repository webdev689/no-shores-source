# Overlook Becomes a Hub of Courts and Ways, After the Tower

> "With this update. I think its time to give overlook itself a detailed pass. Specifically, the large road that
> leads to the ocean has no ramps. Additionally, I want the social space itself to be more detailed. Utilizing
> blender, I want to really turn it from a giant open space to an actual social space with a plaza with corridors,
> passageways, etc that lead to other areas, while also giving each vendor its own handcrafted designed stalls and
> areas. Research the tower from destiny to get an idea. Heres a map image from destiny. Along with this, smaller
> changes like 1. Jumping off the ledge and falling auto kills you in a funny fashion as the camera stays put and
> shows your dead body falling 2. NPCs with proper pathfinding around the new social space. Some NPCs performing
> tasks like working, playing instruments, etc"

> With it, a map of Destiny 1's Tower: Tower Watch, the plaza, between Tower North and the Tower Hangar, the Hall of
> Guardians and Traveler's Walk behind it.

> "Build this to perfection, no holds barred, beyond the limits of detail. Take your time and make this something
> special. Return when this is 100%"

> Shown how it was coming: "This is insane. Keep pushing this. I want this to knock people's socks off. Dont forget
> about wall adornments. Banners and other small things to spruce up ambience. You got this"

## What it settles

- **Overlook gets a detailed pass.** The large road that leads to the ocean gets ramps.
- **The social space stops being one giant open space**: a plaza with corridors and passageways leading to other
  areas, after Destiny's Tower, built with Blender.
- **Each vendor has a handcrafted stall and an area of their own.**
- **Jumping off the ledge and falling kills the player at once, in a funny way**: the camera stays put and shows the
  dead body falling.
- **The NPCs find their way round the new social space**, and some work or play instruments.
- **Walls are adorned**: banners and other small things that lift the place's ambience.
- The rest is Claude's: [[Overlook's Hub as Claude Shapes It]].
