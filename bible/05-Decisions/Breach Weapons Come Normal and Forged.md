# Breach Weapons Come Normal and Forged

> "A mew season is working on the new breach. The breach will recieve 2 weapon sets. One for normal and one for hard.
> The hard version should have (Forged) in its name (similar to how destiny puts (Adept) in a weapons name)"

## What it settles

- **A Breach gives two weapon sets**: one won at Normal, one at Hard.
- **A weapon of the Hard set has "(Forged)" in its name**, as Destiny's raid weapons carry "(Adept)"
  ([[Everything Carries a Set Name, and Perks Roll by Pool]]).
- **The Breach's own session builds them**, with the Breach's tiers (#182).
