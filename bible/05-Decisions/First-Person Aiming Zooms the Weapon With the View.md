# First-Person Aiming Zooms the Weapon With the View

> "So when the camera zooms in for ads, it shouldnt keep the weapon the same position on screen if that makes sense.
> Like the camera should zoom but not with the weapon"

Asked which view, the owner answered "First person". Shown pictures of first-person aiming with a crossbow, the owner
chose "3: zooms too".

It replaces the first-person point of [[Aiming Sits Behind the Shoulder and Zooms the View]]; the rest of that
decision stands.

## What it settles

- **First person.** Aiming, the weapon zooms with the view, about the middle of the screen: it grows and slides out
  toward the screen's edge. A tracer still starts at its muzzle as drawn ([[First Person]], [[Test Ground]]).
