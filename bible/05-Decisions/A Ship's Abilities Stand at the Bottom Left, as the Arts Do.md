# A Ship's Abilities Stand at the Bottom Left, as the Arts Do

> "Ship reload, ship boost, and any other current/future active abilities to be positioned in the bottom left of the
> screen with proper UI. (See how the arts UI works)"

## What it settles

- In a sea fight the ship's reload, its burst and its other abilities stand at the bottom left of the screen, built
  as the Arts bar is ([[Interface]], [[Arts]]); an ability added later takes its place there.
- Replaces in part [[Ships Sail Faster, Turn Twice as Fast and Burst on Shift in Sea Fights]]: the burst's ring is
  no longer on the ship's plate.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where and how large | The Arts bar's corner, 28 px in and 22 up, its rings (44 px, 66 from 1500 by 900, doubled from 2400 by 1300), gaps and keycaps, labelled "Your Ship" | "See how the arts UI works": the same corner and build, so the two read as one kit |
| The slots | Port broadside, Starboard broadside, Burst, Brace, Load, from a list of slots | Reload is a side's; a later ability is one more entry |
| A broadside | A dark wedge sweeping away as it reloads, whole seconds under 10, the icon dimmed; brass once loaded, a wider gold while aimed at and loaded; greyed and hatched while braced or not afloat; a flash as it fires | As a Tenet's ring shows its recharge |
| The burst | A near-white light round its ring draining over its 4 s, then the wedge and seconds of its 12 s recharge | One ring for both its clocks |
| Brace and the load | Brace lit while held; the load shows the shot loaded and flashes as it changes | Both were only words on the plate or the aim line |
| The icons | Our own: a cannon on its carriage facing its side, a sail bellied under its yard with speed lines, a shield with a brass boss, and each shot (a ball, a cluster, two balls on a chain, a glowing ball) | Drawn as the Arts' pictures are |
| The keycaps | The bound keys and pad buttons; the aim's right button drawn as the key strip's mouse; the load's wheel | What the player presses |
| The plate | Keeps Hull, Sails, Crew and On fire; its boost ring, Braced chip and battery rose go to the bar | Each shown once |
| A narrow view | The key strip moves up over the bar where it would come within 8 px of it | The bar has no weapons to stack over |
