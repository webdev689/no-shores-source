# The Game Opens With a Story Mission at Sea

> "Today we will be working on the game integration flow and the first story mission that leads into the game, which
> will also properly integrate waiting features."

> "1. On game startup, for the browser the player taps to start, which begins the new handcrafted intro. The camera
> fades in, flying around the map to each island, through the clouds, different weather patterns, ships sailing,
> people fighting, the different towns, etc introducing the world of overlook. The intro ends panned over the island
> of saltmere, with ambience, birds flying about, and the living breathing world in view with the title screen
> showing and a begin prompt pulsing."

> "2. On game startup, the camera then pans away and goes deep into the ocean, a stormfront, rain coming down hard,
> thunder and lighting, as a brig comes into view, the camera pans across the deck, where youre crew is working hard,
> until finally the camera rests at the helm, which begins character creation showing your full character."

> "3. You are then prompted to create your account, select your skin tone/race. Your character begins wearing armor
> with a sword and shield."

> "4. After this is done. Your men cry out through the storm, vessels inbound. NPC's will utilize a emoji like
> notification above their head indicating their feelings. Like an exclamation point, etc. We will also utilize the
> voice blurb system from my fight-life-source repo for voice dialogue and text based dialogue."

> "5. The camera pans out showing multiple ships emerging from the shadowy depths, as the camera pans back to third
> person on your ship. You then are prompted to engage in combat, destroying the ships trying to take you down."

> "6. After ships are defeated, the air grows still as the camera pans to the deck for dialogue, as the storm rages
> on."

> "7. One crew member interrupts, yelling a larger ship is inbound headed straight for your vessel. The camera pans out
> sharply, as the vessel moves closer and crashes into your ship as you brace for impact. The screen fades to black."

> "8. The camera comes back in first person, you're flat on your back, your eyes blinking, trying to adjust, as you
> raise yourself to your feet in first person, looking around at the devistation. Fiire and brim, 2 ships clashed into
> one. The camera pans back, zooming through the linear wreckage, enemies fighting one another, until finally the
> camera comes back letting you take control in third person."

> "9. You are then tasked with fighting through the linear wreckage, killing foes, until you reach the final stage.
> The camera pans forward sharply to the main boss, your foe with a stricking themathic nameplate as it war cries."

> "10. You then engage in an arena style boss fight 1 on 1, which you cannot win."

> "11. The screen fades out and back in showing you being thrown overboard, the enemy celebrating."

> "12. You are shown sinking into the deep, fire above shown through the water, littered by the dead of your crew. You
> sink, until a bright flash, a snippet of mysterious dialouge, and a final fade to black."

> "13. You awake on the shores of saltmere, eventually approached by a bystander willing to help you. They take you
> back to the social space, where you are then let free to continue your journey. More to come after"

> "This is the ultimate vision of what we are building, a story-driven cinematic experience. Other sessions are
> working on the music system and ship models you may be able to use to beef this up."

> "I want you to go beyond with this, be as detailed as possible, finish to 100% and only come back when this is
> finished and ready in full. You are at the helm. Ensure dialouge is not overbearing, bloated, has em dashes, and
> develop a rule standard for current and future dialouge."

## What it settles

- The opening voyage that [[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]] left to be talked through is this
  mission, the [[Prologue]]. Overlook is a story-driven cinematic experience.
- In the browser, a tap starts the game. A handcrafted intro flies the camera over the islands, through clouds and
  weather, past ships, fights and towns, and ends over Saltmere with its ambience and birds, the title, and a
  pulsing prompt to begin.
  The flight to each island is replaced by [[The Intro Goes Straight to Saltmere]].
- Beginning takes the camera out to a storm at sea and a brig, across its working crew to the helm, where character
  creation shows the player's whole figure. The player creates their account and chooses their skin tone and race.
  They start in armour with a sword and shield.
- The mission runs in the owner's thirteen beats: vessels sighted, a fight at sea, a still moment on deck, a larger
  ship ramming it, waking in first person in the wreck of both, fighting through it to a boss with a nameplate of
  its own, a fight that cannot be won, thrown overboard, sinking past the fallen crew to a flash and a strange voice,
  and waking on Saltmere's shore, where a bystander takes the player to the social space and play is theirs.
- Characters show their feelings with a mark over their heads, as an emote, and speak through fight-life's voice
  blurb system, in voice and in text ([[Speech]]).
- Dialogue follows a rule of its own, for this mission and every one after it ([[Dialogue]]). It is never
  overbearing or bloated, and never holds an em dash.
- More missions follow; what they hold is the owner's.
- Claude's calls inside the mission are in [[The Prologue as Claude Shapes It]].
