# The Broadside Warning Is Written Over the Sea, Not a Panel

> "Also rework the brace warning to be something less of a panel and more of a detailed warning"

## What it settles

- The warning that a captain's guns are run out at the player is no longer a boxed panel, and it says more than
  "Broadside from the Pirate Brig - C: brace" ([[Interface]], "The broadside warning").

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Its look | No panel: outlined words over the sea, "BROADSIDE" in blood red capitals at twice the words' size between two blood triangles round a "!", pulsing to pale | As the game's other warning, "Return to the area", stands |
| When it shows | While a hostile captain afloat has a side run out with the Sloop within 45° of its beam (`runOutAt`) | An ally's guns, or a side run out at another ship, are no broadside at the player |
| Its detail | Whose battery, how far and where from the bow; its shot and what it does to the Sloop; the brace over its keycap and what it spares; how many other batteries stand run out at the player | Each says what to do: where to look, whether to brace, and that grape still kills men braced |
| Where from the bow | Dead ahead, off the bow, on the beam, off the quarter or astern, port or starboard, each a quarter of a side | As a sailor says it, quicker read than degrees |
| The tell | A fuse under the title burning in from both ends over the captain's 1.3 s tell, then flashing every quarter second with the title as the battery may fire | A captain fires no sooner than 1.3 s after running out, and runs in 2.5 s after that; the fuse says when to brace |
| Braced | The brace line in brass, "Braced", with no keycap | The press is done; what it spares stays said |
| The compass | That captain's mark lit pale red | The warning names the ship; the compass points to it |
| Reduced motion | No pulse and no flash; the fuse still burns | The fuse is information, the pulse is not |
