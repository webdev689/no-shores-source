# Every Light Is Redesigned and Animated, and Each Casts Light

> "All lighting sources in the game need a proper animated redesign"

Asked how flames and lamps should look and move: "your decision". Asked whether the lights that cast no light today
(the sea fight's muzzle flashes and burning ships, ships' stern and cabin lanterns, islands' camp fires, the passages'
lamps above ground, outposts' fire pits, the carried torch under ground) should start casting it, the owner chose that
all of them should.

## What it settles

- **Every light source in the game is redesigned and animated**: torches, braziers, fires, hearths, candles,
  lanterns, lamps and every other thing that glows ([[Sky]], [[Dungeons]], [[Cities]], [[Cinema]]).
- **How they look and move is Claude's to choose** ([[Lights as Claude Builds Them]]).
- **Every glowing thing casts light**, those that cast none today among them, within the lights the renderers draw at
  once.
