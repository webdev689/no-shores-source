# The Breach Opens in the Patrol Zone, and the Rest Is Its Own Instance

> "So just want to make clear, only the beginning portion takes place in the patrol zone itself, the rest is its own
> instance"

> Asked whether the opening encounter, holding three mooring stones while Lattice waves come to wake the Spire and
> open the way in, is fought out in the patrol zone in view of other players, or inside the instance: "Opening fought
> outside"

## What it settles

- **Only the beginning is in the patrol zone**: the section built for the Breach, where the company starts
  ([[The First Breach Follows the Vault of Glass, at Normal and Hard]]).
- **The opening encounter is fought there**, in the shared patrol zone, in view of the other players in it.
- **The rest of the Breach is its own instance**, which the company goes into once the opening is won.

How it works: [[Breaches]]; Claude's calls in [[The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It]].
