# Locked On, the Figure Faces Its Foe

> "When locked on, your character shouldnt face away when walking away from the locked on enemy"

## What it settles

- While the soft lock holds a foe, the player's figure on foot faces it however it walks, so walking away backs off
  from it ([[Test Ground]]).
