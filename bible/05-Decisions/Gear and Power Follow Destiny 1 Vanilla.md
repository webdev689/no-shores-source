## What it settles

- **Destiny 1 at launch is the reference** for how gear and power work, "pretty much 1:1". Where the owner named
  something of Overlook's own, that holds. Everything the owner left open is Claude's to shape
  ([[Gear as Claude Shapes It]]).
- **The materials:**
  - **Aurem**, the world's currency.
  - **Weapon Fragments** and **Armor Fragments**, from dismantling weapons and armour.
  - **Resources**, each island's own, gathered in its patrol areas with a simple interaction.
  - **Weapon Essence** and **Armor Essence**, the rare materials the rarest upgrades need.
  - **Fruit of Dagon**, as Destiny 1's Mote of Light: clicked and held to consume it, granting XP to a weapon or an
    armour piece.
- **The rarities:** Normal (white), Magic (blue), Rare (yellow), Legendary (gold) and Atheric (cyan blue). Atheric
  and Aurem are spelled as written: DFO's names put to new uses.

  Replaced in part by [[Vestiges Are project-final's Crystals, and Physical Pictures Are Baked]]: Magic is green and
  Legendary red.
- **What a player wears:**
  - **Weapons:** one set, a main hand and an off hand. The off hand holds a second weapon, which carries Attack, or a
    shield, which carries Defense but no Prestige.
  - **One type per weapon.** Each weapon kind is a single type, with no archetypes within it.
  - **Armour:** head, torso, arms and legs.
  - **The cloak** is cosmetic and adds to its faction's reputation while worn.
  - **Clothing** is settled by [[Clothing Is Armour, With a Wardrobe Later]], which replaced what this note first said
    of it.
- **Nodes.** Each item has nodes that are levelled up to raise its power, at a cost, and that carry its perks. An item
  unlocks its nodes as it earns XP. Armour earns XP too: this replaces "Armor does not have XP" in
  [[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]].
- **Prestige and level.** As at Destiny 1's launch, levels 1 to 20 come from XP. Past 20 the player's level comes
  from the Prestige their armour carries. Weapons carry Attack; armour carries Defense.
- **Drops are sealed, as a Vestige.** A Vestige is identified in an island's social space, and it can open below its
  own colour.
- **Where loot is earned**, not built in full yet:
  - **Patrol**: contained but explorable areas, for farming materials, foes, timed public events, bounties and quests
    that lead there, procedural dungeons and more.
  - **Missions**: contained, linear missions.
  - **Elite Dungeons**: instanced, linear, for four players matched together, as Destiny's strikes.
  - **Breaches**: the hardest content, raids for eight players: puzzles, minibosses with complex mechanics and a final
    boss, as Destiny's raids. They give the best loot and the highest Prestige.
- **The screens** are DFO's Enhanced Plus UI adapted to Overlook, with Destiny 1 as the reference: a character tab, an
  inventory tab and the rest ([[Pick - Enhanced Plus UI]]).
- **Later:** Marks, factions and their reputation are still to be discussed; the vendors are
  [[Voyages Choose a Difficulty, Overlook Lives Like the Tower]]'s.

How the loop around it is planned: [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]]. Prestige gains an
infusion in [[Prestige Stays as Built, With a Costly Infusion]]; the Elite Dungeons and Breaches are placed in
[[Dungeons Live in Patrols, on the Sea's Islands and as Elite Dungeons]] and
[[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]].
