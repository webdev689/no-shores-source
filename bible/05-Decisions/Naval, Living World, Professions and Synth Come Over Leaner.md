# Naval, Living World, Professions and Synth Come Over Leaner

> "Naval, living world, professions, synth all ported with better organization and less bloat."

## What it settles

- Four of DFO's own systems come over: the naval combat core, the living-world simulation, the profession and
  gathering acts, and the synthesiser with its written scores.
- Each is reorganised and trimmed as it comes, not copied ([[More Organisation, Less Bloat]]).
- What each decides (combat, rolls, grants) is rebuilt in Rust for the server; what each shows is ported to
  TypeScript ([[Porting from DFO]]).
