# The Lattice Has Props of Its Own, Made in Blender

> "I think with this integration. We should go ahead and use blender to design props for corrupted areas, future
> world events, etc
>
> Like a set that the faction can utilize"

> Asked which props the set starts with, offered crystal growths, overtaken remains, Lattice structures and event
> pieces: all four.

## What it settles

- The Lattice gets a set of props, designed in Blender, for the places it has corrupted and for world events to come
  ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]).
- The set starts with four kinds: crystal growths; the ruined ports' own things overtaken by the crystal; structures
  the faction grows itself; and what a world event leaves behind.

How they are drawn and where they stand: [[Lattice]]. Where corrupted areas are and what world events bring are not
settled ([[Open Questions]]).
