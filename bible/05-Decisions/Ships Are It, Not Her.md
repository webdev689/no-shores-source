# Ships Are It, Not Her

> "Please stop using "her" terminology"

## What it settles

- A ship is "it", never "her" or "she": in every word the player reads, in the code's comments, names and tests, in
  the bible's notes, and in what Claude says to the owner ([[Naval Combat]], [[Interface]]).
- The owner's own words are quoted as said.
