# Saltmere Keeps Only the Saltgrass Downs, and Everything Folds Into It

> "I want to discuss patrol zones. Currently I like the idea but 3 different patrol zones is causing me a lot of
> stress and I really think I want to focus on saltgrass before we even think about moving onto others, but I know a
> lot of quests. The breach, depend on the others"

> "Lets do this
>
> Remove both patrol zones and keep saltgrass
>
> Completely give saltgrass a detailed design pass, new proper enemy placements, fold all functions into saltgrass and
> polish the existing map and legends, along with a patrol zone design pass"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked what becomes of the Breach, the Drowned Vale, written as the Gullwing Vale as it was and opened at the Sunken
> Gate in the Vale: its gate moves into Saltgrass and its name and run stay, the Vale living on in its lore; or its gate
> moves and it is renamed for Saltgrass's own past: "Gate moves, name stays (Recommended)"
>
> Asked how the five instances of the two regions that lose their patrol zone are reached (the Shrine of Tides,
> Kraken's Grotto and the Maw of the Shallows in the Weirlands, Tidewall and the Pearl Tomb in the Grey Reach): from
> the sea, or as gates from Saltgrass: "Remove the gates to other zones"
>
> Asked how the houses' twelve tasks at the Vale's and the Moors' places come into Saltgrass: Saltgrass grows with
> the best few of those places and the rest are reworded onto its own; every task reworded onto its own eight; or most
> of the places come in: "Grow with the best few (Recommended)"
>
> Asked which map "the existing map and legends" means: the held map on land in Saltgrass and its legend, with the
> three landing zones, their map and fast travel; the held map and its legend alone; or the sea map too: "Held map +
> landing zones (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **Saltmere keeps one patrol zone, the Saltgrass Downs.** The Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood Moors are removed. The
  island keeps its three regions: the Weirlands and the Grey Reach hold their nodes and no patrol zone, as every other
  island's second and third regions do.
- **Saltgrass's gates to the other zones go.** The instances of the Weirlands and the Grey Reach are then reached as
  any instance in a region with no patrol zone is ([[Layouts]], "How a layout is planned"): landed at from the sea.
- **Every function of the two zones folds into Saltgrass**: what the story, the activities and the systems ran there
  runs in Saltgrass.
- **The Breach's Sunken Gate moves into Saltgrass.** The Drowned Vale keeps its name and its run; the Gullwing Vale
  lives on in its lore.
- **Saltgrass grows with the best few of the Vale's and the Moors' places**, so the houses' tasks set there keep their
  scenes; the other tasks are reworded onto Saltgrass's own places. Which places come is Claude's call.
- **Saltgrass gets a detailed design pass and a patrol zone design pass, with new enemy placements.**
- **The held map on land in Saltgrass and its legend are polished**, and Saltgrass's three landing zones, the zone's
  map to choose one from and their fast travel are built
  ([[The Island's Tiles Are Painted Scenes, and Each Patrol Zone Has Three Landing Zones]]).
- **Saltgrass is the one zone worked on** before any other is thought about.

It replaces in part [[Each Island Has Three Regions, and Saltmere's Three Patrol Zones Stay, Linked]] (Saltmere's three
patrol zones and their links), [[Zones Rise and Fall, Above and Below Ground]] and
[[Handcrafted Playspaces Are Scripted in Blender]] (the Gullwing Vale built first),
[[Patrol Zones and Missions Open as Their Pass Is Done]] (the zones open while their pass is done: the Saltgrass Downs
alone) and [[The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It]] (where it opens).

It builds on [[Each Island Keeps One Patrol Zone, the One by Its Port City]].

Its design pass, enemies and landing zones are settled in
[[The Saltgrass Downs Plays as Destiny 2's EDZ, Its Three Landing Zones Spread Across It]].
