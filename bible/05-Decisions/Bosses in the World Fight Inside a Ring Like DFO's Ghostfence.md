# Bosses in the World Fight Inside a Ring Like DFO's Ghostfence

> "I want to look into improving boss fights in the world, mainly the boss in the questlines."

> "For bosses that take place in the world, I want to utilize the rings we use around red mountain in dfo to
> entrapment the player within bounds where the boss doesnt reset to its position"

Asked whether the ring is DFO's Ghostfence (stone pylons with glowing orbs and a pale glowing curtain between them that
nothing walks through), and whether a boss's ring should look like it, the owner chose "Yes, look like DFO's". Asked
when it rises, "When the fight starts"; who can cross it once it is up, "No one, in or out"; and which bosses get it:
"Quest bosses and future bosses. Only saltmire for noe".

Asked how big it is, where a player who falls inside it respawns, and what happens when every player inside is down:
"Stop asking me questions and come back when this is 100%".

## What it settles

- **A boss fought in the world fights inside a ring**: the quest bosses, and bosses made later. For now only
  Saltmere's: High Assessor Corvane in the Maw of the Shallows, where the Stormcallers' road ends.
- **The ring looks like DFO's Ghostfence**: its pylons, their orbs and the glowing curtain between them. Its look and
  its wall come over from DFO ([[Pick - Boss Rings]]); its name and Red Mountain do not.
- **It rises when the fight starts.**
- **No one crosses it while it stands, in or out**: the players inside stay inside, and anyone arriving late waits
  outside.
- **The boss does not reset to its post** while players fight it inside the ring.

Its size, a fall inside it, a whole company down, and the rest are Claude's calls in
[[Boss Rings as Claude Builds Them]]. How it works: [[Boss Rings]].
