# DFO's Own Sounds Join the Sound Sheet

The owner, on the world's sounds: "We can take some sounds from DFO we already did". On the footsteps, marked "DFO"
on the sheet, and asked whether DFO's were another author's: "They're in an unpushed branch".

## What it settles

- DFO's own sounds come to the sound sheet as candidates for the world's cues (its synthesised ones no longer, by
  [[Every Sound Comes From Freesound, and the Music Rests Between Pieces]]), judged there like Freesound's
  ([[The World Sounds From the Sound Picker]]). These are the sounds DFO's synthesiser made (its climbing, gathering,
  lock, kill-effect, portal and naval sounds), drawn again from DFO's tools at 44.1 kHz rather than DFO's small baked
  copies, and the CC0 Freesound recordings DFO's guns play, offered by their Freesound numbers.
- The footsteps come from the recordings the owner chose for DFO's own steps, by ear over five rounds: CC0 or public
  domain on their own pages at OpenGameArt and archive.org (DFO's `vendor/cc0-sounds`, on its unmerged branches
  after #1655). They owe no credit. Each surface has several, and the game plays one in turn.
- What DFO took from others stays there ([[Own Work]]): Daggerfall's own sounds, other authors' mods, and the
  OpenGameArt recordings that are not the steps. DFO's heal (`02_Heal_02`) cannot ship until its source and licence
  are known.
