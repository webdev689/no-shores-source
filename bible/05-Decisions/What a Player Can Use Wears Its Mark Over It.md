# What a Player Can Use Wears Its Mark Over It

Shown the brass rings on the ground at the hub's counters, the owner wrote:

> "Instead of the circle on the ground. Can we do a a glyph above interactables/vendors? Kind of like what we use for
> morrowind models in DFO."

Asked which glyph should bob over each keeper and what a player can use, the owner chose "Each keeper's own mark
(Recommended)": each keeper's mark from their screen and sign, in the style of DFO's marks over its people.

## What it settles

- What a player can use on foot wears a mark over it in place of a ring on the ground ([[Game]], "Over what a player
  can use").
- Each keeper's mark is their own, as their screen and sign show it.
- The marks are drawn in the style of DFO's marks over the people who do something ([[Pick - NPC Marks]]).
