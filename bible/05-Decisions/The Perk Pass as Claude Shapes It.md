# The Perk Pass as Claude Shapes It

> "I want you to take this on in full with autonomy. Be extremely detailed, no half assed work or band aids, and take
> your time. Treat this as your baby and go above the limits to achieve something amazing."

> "Take autonomy, build everything to 100 and let's make this something truely impressive."

## What it settles

Claude's choices for what [[Atheric Gear Plays Like Destiny's Exotics, and Every Model Is Worn]] leaves open, under
the autonomy the owner gave the gear ([[Gear as Claude Shapes It]]) and the ship
([[The Ship's Power, Crew and Islands as Claude Shapes Them]]). The owner can change any of them. How the rules carry
them out is [[Gear]] and [[Ship Power]].

### How an Atheric is made

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Its two perks | An **intrinsic**, always on while it is worn, shown at the head of its page; and **its own** node, bought in the column the Atheric already has, for 1,000 Aurem and 1 Essence | Destiny 1's exotics: an exotic perk that is the weapon, and a second one earned in its tree. The grid keeps its shape, so no kept item's nodes move |
| What they do | Something a number cannot: lightning that leaps, a kill that bursts, a guard that bites back, a count that builds; never only a share more | The owner: "more like exotic weapons from destiny" |
| Their element | An Atheric's leaps and bursts carry the perk's own element, whatever the weapon rolled | Lightning is Volt on any bow; whether named weapons keep an element of their own is still the owner's |
| A kept Atheric | Follows its catalogue entry: a node bought moves with its perk where its column changed, else to the perk it merged into, else stays on the plain perk in its place, a reworked Atheric's own among them; one left only a perk that gives and takes, or a place another took, is given back | Destiny's reworked exotics changed every copy, and nobody bought them again. A node costs the same whatever its perk, so mostly nothing is given back; nor is a cost pressed on a player who never chose it |
| Its look | Its own metal or set, and its own make where the forge has more than one (a shield's kind, a bow's), with its rarity's glow | Destiny's exotics each had a model of their own |

Replaced in part by [[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]]: an Atheric's own node costs 2,500 Aurem, 4
fragments, 10 of its resource and 1 Essence. Replaced in part by [[The Inspect Panel as Claude Shapes It]]: the
intrinsic heads the page's perks, under "Perks", after its node grid, not the page itself.

### The Atheric weapons

Five reworked, five new: one or more for each family (blades, blunt, pairs, shields, bows, guns, the staff).

| Atheric | Kind | Look | Intrinsic | Its own |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gullwing | Bow | short, mithril | **Storm Gull**: an aimed shot that lands leaps as lightning to up to 3 more foes within 8 m of the last, each struck for half its harm in Volt | **Squall Line**: every foe a leap strikes is Charged |
| The Drowned Oath | Sword | ebony | **Undertow**: blows on a foe below half its health strike 30% harder | **Pulled Under**: a kill drags the foes within 4 m of the slain under: each struck for a third of the blow, in Cosmic, and Exposed |
| Lanternfall | Thunderlock | gilded | **Last Light**: a kill reloads it at once and gives back 25 stamina | **Lantern's Wake**: a kill bursts in Blaze: foes within 4 m of the slain take half the shot's harm |
| The Last Bell | Warhammer | orcish | **Tolling**: a foe it staggers by poise stays staggered half again as long | **Knell**: a blow that staggers by poise rings out: foes within 5 m of the struck take a quarter of it, in Volt |
| The Reefwall | Sword & Shield | adamantium, kite | **Coral Guard**: a blow it blocks gives back 15 stamina, and its striker takes a fifth of it back | **Riptide**: a riposte strikes 50% harder and Exposes its foe |
| Saltfang | Double Daggers | daedric | **Thousand Cuts**: each swing in a row landed on one foe strikes 10% harder than the last, to 50%; the row ends as Exposing Strikes' does | **Bleed the Tide**: a kill gives Haste for 4 s |
| Widow's Walk | Crossbow | mithril | **Vigil**: a shot at a foe past 20 m is a crit | **Long Wait**: a kill gives Veiled for 3 s |
| Brass Chorus | Double Pistolet | gilded | **Call and Answer**: every second shot that lands strikes 40% harder | **Encore**: a kill gives back two shots |
| Deepwatch | Staff | ebony | **Conduit**: each blow that lands takes 2% off both Tenets' cooldowns | **Undercurrent**: a kill gives Linked for 3 s |
| The Ninth Wave | Greatsword | daedric | **Swell**: each kill makes its blows strike 10% harder for 10 s, to 50%; a kill refreshes the time | **The Ninth**: every ninth swing that lands bursts: foes within 4 m of the struck take half the blow, in Volt |

A leap, a burst or a guard's bite is a blow of the player's, made with the weapon's Attack as it is struck, judged
against each foe as a spell's blow is: it is never parried or guarded, a dodge passes it, and a foe's shield takes it
by the element it carries. It sets off no perk of its own, so nothing chains from a chain. The room tells each one,
so every page draws it: a leap as a bolt from the foe before to the foe struck, a burst as a ring from the foe it
came from, a bite as a flash on its striker.

### Calls in the rules

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| An aimed shot | A shot loosed from the draw, read by its harm as it is loosed | A bow, crossbow or gun's aimed shot is the one the draw strengthens |
| A burst | Strikes the foes round its centre, not the centre itself, which the blow or kill already took | One foe is never struck twice by one blow |
| A leap a foe dodges | Leaps on from it | A dodge passes a spell's blow whole; the lightning still reaches the next |
| A bite | Carries the weapon's own element; a leap and a burst their perk's | A bite is the guard's own answer, a leap or burst the perk's |
| Thousand Cuts and Exposing Strikes | Count the same row of swings | One row of swings on one foe |
| Swell run out | Begins again at one kill | Its time is its life |
| The Ninth | Counts a sweep that strikes several foes once | A swing is one count |
| Call and Answer | Counts shots that land | A miss answers nothing |
| The new Atherics' resources | Saltfang Brineglass, Widow's Walk Heartwood Resin, Brass Chorus Goldgrass Seed, Deepwatch Mistbloom, The Ninth Wave Cairn Iron | Each from an island its words fit |
| Who sinks a ship (Feeding) | The shooter of the ball that takes its last hull; a ram never sinks one, and a struck captain scuttled is no one's | The ball that does it is the shooter's |
| Hard to Kill | Leaves exactly a tenth of its hull, raised to it if lower, once a fight | A last stand, not a heal |
| From the Deep | Doubles a ball's hull harm in the waterline band, on top of the band's own | The band already holes harder; the Atheric makes it worse |
| Snare | Slows ships of another flag within 30 m of its centre; the strongest Snare counts, never two | Balls strike only another flag; a Snare is a ship's reach |
| Squall and Web | Squall counts every ball that strikes it; Web only a ball in the rigging, each new one starting its 6 s again | What each Atheric is made of |
| A blow that does nothing | Sets off no leap, burst or bite: one on a foe four or more levels above its player, or an arrow on a foe slain this tick | A chain is a share of its blow |
| A punch with a ranged Atheric drawn | Sets off none of its perks | Its perks are its shots' |
| Conduit's cut | Tells the page as a Tenet comes ready, not at every blow | The gear message carries every item |
| Snare | Slows a ship at its oars too | A ship rowing is no freer of the web |
| On the item page | Both an Atheric's perks under "Atheric", its own dimmed until chosen; a trade-off's cost on its own line in the colour of a loss; its intrinsic first on its card | Destiny's exotic pages show both perks; a cost should read as a cost |
| A shot's perks | The item it was loosed from gives them, wherever it is as the shot lands, with the Attack and trim it was loosed with | A weapon changed while a shot flies changes nothing of it |
| A ball in the rigging | Takes the canvas and crew perks, never the hull's | Its harm there is all canvas and men |
| A player back in a sea fight | Keeps a spent Hard to Kill; no ship number is given twice | "Once a fight" is the player's, and a ball in flight is no newcomer's |
| A purchase from Shape | Names the piece shown, refused as not sold here when his week now draws another there | As a smith's ware of another day |
| A Legendary's sails and rigging | Six perks each, Backed Sails, Boarding Ladders and Preventer Stays among them, so no module offers a perk twice | The merged pools had too few for a Legendary's six nodes |

Replaced in part by [[The Inspect Panel as Claude Shapes It]]: on the item page both an Atheric's perks head its perks,
under "Perks", not "Atheric".

### The ship's perks, merged

Each thing a module's perk does is done by one perk; the officers' perks are their own and stay
([[Crew]]). A kept rolled module offering a perk that is gone offers in its place the
first perk of its column's pool it does not offer already, bought and chosen as the old one was.

| Module | Column | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| Guns | Handling | Quick Tackle (reload 10% faster), Steady Lay (scatter 15% less), Heavy Charge, Hot Guns |
| Guns | Finish (what is loaded) | Double Shotted (hole a hull 10% more), Chain Locker (tear canvas 20% more), Grape Locker (cut down men 20% more) |
| Guns | Perk | Raking Fire (20% more on a ship below a third of its hull), Firing Drill (reload 5% faster and scatter 5% less), Run Out, Overcharged |
| Hull | Perk | Oak Ribs (hull takes 10% less), Copper Sheathing (a ball below the waterline harms it 20% less), Iron Ram, Last Timbers, Carpenter's Stores, Stripped Ballast |
| Sails | Perk | Storm Canvas (canvas tears 12% less), Flying Jib (sails 6% faster), Lookout Yard, Crowd of Sail, Reefed Sails, Backed Sails |
| Rigging | Perk | Quick Braces (turns 10% faster), Grapnels, Boarding Nets (balls cut down 12% fewer of your men), Taut Rigging, Boarding Ladders, Preventer Stays |

Gone into another: Topgallants, Running Blocks and Fair Winds into Flying Jib; Seasoned Timbers into Oak Ribs;
Gunlayer's Eye and Bored True into Steady Lay; Powder Monkeys into Quick Tackle; Grapeshot Drill, Swivel Crews and
Fighting Tops into Grape Locker; Patched Sails into Carpenter's Stores; Doubled Shrouds into Storm Canvas; Trim Crew
into Quick Braces; Sealed Magazine into Boarding Nets.

### Every model worn

Every weapon and armour model the forge draws is worn by some piece a player can hold; a test lists the forge's layers
and fails on any no piece wears, but a class's own (the archer's dark helm, the woman's horned one, the class sword and axe) and the laced
boots, a garment.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The metals' ladder | Iron, steel, silver, elven, dwarven, orcish, mithril, adamantium, ebony, daedric | The forge's metals in order of their worth |
| A rolled weapon's metal | Climbs the ladder with its rarity and its level, and spreads by its name: Normal iron to level 10, steel past it; Magic steel to elven at level 1, elven to orcish at 20; Rare elven to orcish at level 1, adamantium to daedric at 20 | Only Normal, Magic and Rare pieces are rolled, so the rolled ones must reach every metal for every model to be worn. | A Normal shield | Plain or iron to level 10, by its number; its weapon's metal past it | The plain and iron shields are carried too |
| A gun | Plain when rolled; a Legendary or Atheric gilded unless it names its own | The gilded piece marks the finer gun |
