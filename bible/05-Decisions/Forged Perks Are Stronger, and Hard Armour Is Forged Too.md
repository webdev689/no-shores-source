# Forged Perks Are Stronger, and Hard Armour Is Forged Too

> "For hard mode weapons/armor. They should recieve improved forged perks"

Asked which shape that meant, stronger versions of the same perks (as Destiny's Adept raid weapons roll enhanced
perks), those and one perk node more, or perks only Hard rolls, the owner chose "Stronger same perks". Asked whether
Hard armour should carry "(Forged)" in its name as Hard weapons do: "Armour too".

## What it settles

- **A Breach's piece won at Hard rolls the same Breach perks as one won at Normal, each at a stronger grade, its
  Forged one**: weapons and armour alike.
- **A Breach's armour won at Hard is named Forged too** ("Lattice Hauberk (Forged)"), as its weapons are
  ([[Breach Weapons Come Normal and Forged]]).

How it is built: [[Gear]] ("The Breaches' perks"), [[Breaches]] ("Rewards"); each Forged number is Claude's, in
[[Gear as Claude Shapes It]] ("The Breaches' perks").
