# Lights as Claude Builds Them

Asked how the game's flames and lamps should look and move: "your decision".

## What it settles

Claude's choices in building [[Every Light Is Redesigned and Animated, and Each Casts Light]]. The owner can change any
of them. How it works is in [[Sky]] ("Lamps"), [[Dungeons]], [[Cities]], [[Layouts]], [[Island Drawing]], [[Passages]],
[[Modules]] and [[Cinema]] ("Flames").

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| An open flame | Every torch, brazier, camp fire, beacon, watchtower fire, hearth and candle burns with the prologue's fire ([[Cinema]], "Flames"): tongues that lick and sway, wisps and embers rising, its own glow on what stands round it, stepped at its 10 frames a second, sized to its source | It is the game's one fire that moves as fire does, and it already reads in the game's pixel look |
| A far flame | Past the distance its tongues read, a flame is its glow alone, flickering with its light | Hundreds of flames stand in a city; a far one costs nothing that is not seen |
| A lantern or a lamp | A small flame of the same fire behind its glass, the glass glowing as the flame moves, a soft halo round it | A lantern is a fire seen through glass, not a lit box |
| The light a flame casts | Flickers with the flame drawn: one flicker, the same on land, at sea and under ground, seeded by its source | Today the drawn flame and its light, and the land's lamps and a dungeon's lights, each flicker their own way |
| Crystal, fungus, runes and a portal | A slow breath, not a flicker, shared by all of them | They are not fire |
| Lights that cast none | Cast light: a ship's stern and cabin lanterns, the sea fight's muzzle flashes and burning ships, islands' camp fires and adit lanterns, the passages' lamps above ground, outposts' fire pits and braziers, the torch carried under ground | The owner's choice |
| How many at once | As the renderers draw them: the nearest sixteen on land and at sea, the nearest thirty-two under ground | A light past them fades as it does today |
| A lamp post | Its light at its lanterns, not the middle of its post | It lit the post's middle |
| The flicker's shape | Held through the fire's ten frames a second, a few slow waves a second, never below 80% at full depth; a kind burns deeper or shallower (a brazier's 1.2, a lantern's 0.35) | A flame drawn in held frames and a light flickering smoothly would not move together |
| The breath's pace | One slow swell in about seven seconds, never below 60% at full depth; a crystal, fungus and the portal at three quarters of it, water at a quarter | Slow enough to read as alive and not as a flame |
| A small flame | A fire under half a metre (a torch's, a candle's) takes fewer smoke puffs, tongues and embers, by its height, at least one of each | A dungeon burns dozens of them at once |
| Where tongues stop reading | 120 times the fire's height, never nearer than 12 m | A torch's tongues are a few pixels wide past some 30 m |
| A fire's light on land | Eight times its height, at least four metres | Its light reaches past its own glow, which is three times its height |
| A lantern's glass | Its flame shows through what stands up to 0.14 m nearer, its glass still glowing and flickering with it | Glass is drawn solid in the dungeon's mesh |
| A glow drawn in the mesh (an ember, a lantern's glass, a kit piece's or a landmark's flame, crystal, fungus, the portal) | Moves with the light nearest it, by that light's seed | One source's flame and light then move together without the mesh knowing which light it belongs to |
| A cluster of candles | Each candle its own small fire, the centre one seeded as the cluster's light | One light, several flames: they should not burn in step |
| A kit piece's or a landmark's flame | Stays the piece's own glowing part, flickering with its light, not a fire | Where in the piece its flame burns is not known to the game |
| The passages' lamp under ground | A fire in its cage and its light at the cage's middle (3.2 m), a dead lamp's in its glass off its crook | Its light stood at the middle of its post |
| An open mouth under ground | Its way on drawn in daylight's pale colour, not stone | The owner: every glowing thing is redesigned; it was drawn as the cell's stone |
| The light carried under ground | The dungeon's own torch or lantern kind at the hand, as on land, seeded apart from every place | The owner: the carried torch casts light under ground |
| A glowing solid's kind on land | Read from its shape and size: a dome is a stone (a sunstone) and breathes; a column 8 m wide or more is the Beacon; a pyramid, a column 0.65 m wide or more, or a block from 0.65 m to 2 m is coals a fire burns on; a block 2 m wide or more is a lantern room (the lighthouse's); a narrower one a lantern | The plan names no kind; its shapes and sizes tell its braziers, hoops, camp fires, watchtower fire and lanterns apart |
| A fire on coals | As tall as the coals are wide and a fifth more, half a metre to four, sunk a fifth of the coals' height into them, casting its own light | Its tongues rise out of the coals, not off their top |
| A lantern's flame on land | 0.6 of its glass's narrowest side, 0.1 m to 0.35 m, its base a little under the glass's middle; its light the lantern's as it stood (some ten metres) and a lantern's flicker | A lantern's light reached that far; a fire's eight times a small flame's height would leave a city's lanterns lighting nothing |
| A module's lantern (a lamp post's, a sconce's, a candle) | A lantern at each of its flames, found where its mesh glows, its glass taken as 0.3 m; before its mesh has come, at its middle | The module's own flames say where its lanterns are |
| The Beacon | Eight fires 4 m tall, each along a side of a ring round the foot of its 22 m glass, one great lamp at its middle seeded as the first | Its glass is too deep for a flame inside to show, and 4 m is the tallest fire drawn |
| A lantern room wider than 2 m | Its glass alone, glowing and flickering with its lamp | A flame inside cannot show through it |
| A glow on land moving with its light | Each lamp carries its kind and seed to the sky's uniform; a glowing surface moves with the nearest lamp within a third of its reach, a crystal only with a stone's; with none near, by where it stands | As under ground, one source's glow and light then move together without the mesh knowing which lamp it belongs to |
| A flame seen through what stands nearer, on land | 0.3 m | A lantern's glass is drawn solid, its flame some 0.25 m behind it |
| An island's ember | 0.5 m wide or more, coals (a camp fire's) with a fire on them; narrower, a lantern's glass (an adit's, a passage's lamp and a dead lamp lit) with a small flame inside; an open mouth's way on, none | The kit names one part for both; its size tells them apart |
| An outpost's fire pit and braziers | Each flame of the module stays its own glowing part, flickering with a fire's lamp reaching 9 m at it | They are a kit piece's flames, as a dungeon's kit pieces' |
| A fire's smoke on land | Leans with the sky's wind | The wind is known; smoke standing straight in a gale reads wrong |
| Where a flame's glow stops reading | 600 times the fire's height (a lantern's 0.2 m flame some 120 m, the Beacon's fires 2.4 km): past it the fire is not drawn, its glowing glass or coals still glowing | A city burns hundreds of small flames; their glows past that are under two pixels and cost a frame's time |
