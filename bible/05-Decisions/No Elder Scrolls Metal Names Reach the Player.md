# No Elder Scrolls Metal Names Reach the Player

> "Also remove metal names, thats old from elder scrolls"

Asked how far the names should go, the owner chose:

> "Everything players read (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **No player reads elven, dwarven, orcish, mithril, adamantium, ebony or daedric**: not on a screen, a card or an
  item's words. Gear names its content instead ("Drops Elite Dungeon gear").
- **The forge's own names for its art stay** inside the code for now, as renaming them means rendering and publishing
  all weapon and armour art again.
