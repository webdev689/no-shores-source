# The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken

> "Lets talk about the faction itself now"

> Offered: the crystal faction keeps project-final's name, the Lattice; its people were the people of the ruined
> ports, overtaken where they stood, which is why some ruins are changed and not burned; on land only for now, in and
> around the changed ruins, the sea left open: "Approved"

> Offered the roster: the Husk a Brute (Minor, Major); the Flicker a Skirmisher (Minor, Major); the Ravager an Archer
> (Major, Elite), hurling shards grown from its arms; the Idol an Archer standing off farther (Elite), its beam dodged
> by a step aside or by cover; the Bulwark a Captain (Elite, Miniboss) behind a crystal shield; the Heart the Boss; no
> firearms, the crystal growing its own weapons, the Idol's beam kept as the one thing out of place; the red strain
> walking out of the ruins, the purple "displaced" alone blinking and assembling out of nothing, all of them shattering
> when killed; no Swordsman: "Lets prototype and develop each using the same blender pipeline"

## What it settles

- **The Lattice** is the crystal faction ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]). Its name shows over its
  foes' bars ([[Enemies]]).
- **They were the people of the ruined ports**, overtaken by the crystal where they stood. That is why some ruins are
  changed and not burned ([[The Mystery Shows in Its Effects, Never Its Cause]]).
- **On land only, for now**, in and around the changed ruins. Whether the Lattice comes to sea is open.
- **The roster**, each type fighting as one of the five roles ([[Foes]]):

| Type | Role | Ranks | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Husk | Brute | Minor, Major | A port's person, crystal through the skull; closes and mauls, never parries or dodges |
| Flicker | Skirmisher | Minor, Major | Gaunt and fast; its blink is its dodge, and it strikes from beside |
| Ravager | Archer | Major, Elite | A Husk at range, hurling crystal shards grown from its arms |
| Idol | Archer, standing off farther | Elite | Arms fused into a lens that charges a beam, dodged by a step aside or by cover |
| Bulwark | Captain | Elite, Miniboss | Fused behind a crystal shield and leading its group; its shield's core broken, or flanked |
| The Heart | Boss | Boss | The Lattice's boss on land |

- **No firearms.** The crystal grows its own weapons: blades, mauls and shards. The Idol's beam is the one thing in
  the world out of place in sails and steel.
- **Two strains.** Red are the overtaken and walk out of the ruins. Purple are the displaced: only they blink and
  assemble out of nothing. Every one of them shatters when killed.
- **No Swordsman**: the crystal does not guard or parry as a trained fighter does.
- **Each is prototyped and developed in the same Blender pipeline** as the forge's figures ([[Sprite Forge]]).

The Husk's look, walk, blows and clothes: [[The Husk Shambles, Strikes Its Own Way and Wears the Townsfolk's Clothes]].
