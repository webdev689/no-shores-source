# The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken

> "Lets talk about the faction itself now"

> Offered: the crystal faction keeps project-final's name, the Lattice; its people were the people of the ruined
> ports, overtaken where they stood, which is why some ruins are changed and not burned up close; on land only for
> now, in and around the changed ruins, the sea left open: "Approved"

> Offered the roster: the Husk a Brute (Minor, Major), "a port's person, crystal through the skull. Closes and mauls,
> and never parries or dodges"; the Flicker a Skirmisher (Minor, Major), "gaunt and fast. Its blink is its dodge, and
> it strikes from beside you"; the Ravager an Archer (Major, Elite), "a Husk at range, hurling crystal shards grown
> from its arms"; the Idol an Archer standing off farther (Elite), its beam dodged by a step aside or by cover; the
> Bulwark a Captain (Elite, Miniboss), "fused behind a crystal shield that leads its group. Break the shield's core or
> flank it"; the Heart the Boss. No firearms: "the crystal grows its own weapons", the Idol's beam kept as "the one
> thing in the world that doesn't belong in sails and steel"; the red strain walking out of the ruins, the purple
> "displaced" alone blinking and assembling out of nothing, all of them shattering when killed; no Swordsman, "the
> crystal doesn't guard or parry like a trained fighter": "Lets prototype and develop each using the same blender
> pipeline"

## What it settles

- **The Lattice** is the crystal faction ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]). Its name is to show over its
  foes' bars, as every faction's does ([[Enemies]]).
- **They were the people of the ruined ports**, overtaken by the crystal where they stood. That is why some ruins are
  changed up close and not burned ([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]).
- **On land only, for now**, in and around the changed ruins.
- **The roster** as offered, which the owner took on to prototype, each type fighting as one of the five roles
  ([[Foes]]):

| Type | Role | Ranks |
|---|---|---|
| Husk | Brute | Minor, Major |
| Flicker | Skirmisher | Minor, Major |
| Ravager | Archer | Major, Elite |
| Idol | Archer, standing off farther | Elite |
| Bulwark | Captain | Elite, Miniboss |
| The Heart | Boss | Boss |

- **No firearms**: the crystal grows its own weapons. The Idol's beam is the one weapon out of place in sails and
  steel.
- **Two strains.** Red are the overtaken and walk out of the ruins. Purple are the displaced: only they blink and
  assemble out of nothing. Every one of them shatters when killed.
- **No Swordsman.**
- **Each is prototyped and developed in the same Blender pipeline** as the forge's figures ([[Lattice]]).
