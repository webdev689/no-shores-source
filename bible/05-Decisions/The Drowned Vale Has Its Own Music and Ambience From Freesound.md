# The Drowned Vale Has Its Own Music and Ambience From Freesound

> "A detailed design pass on everything. Proper thematic music, ambience using freesound, and just an overall push to
> make the fidelity of this insanely impressive"

Asked whether the Drowned Vale's music should be played on recorded instruments, the synthesiser or Freesound's own
pieces, the owner answered:

> "Plenty of sounds come from freesound"

## What it settles

- **The Drowned Vale has music of its own**, thematic to it, as the island families have theirs ([[Music]]).
- **Its ambience is Freesound's recordings**, as every sound's is
  ([[Every Sound Comes From Freesound, and the Music Rests Between Pieces]]).
- **Its music's sounds come from Freesound too.**

Claude read the answer as scores written for the Drowned Vale, played on instruments and textures recorded on
Freesound, each picked by the owner on the sound sheet; that reading is Claude's, put to the owner to confirm, and
each musical call made under it is in [[The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It]] ("Its music").

How it is built: [[Music]], [[Ambience]], [[Breaches]].
