# The Held Map Takes the Ship to Other Waters, Seen Sailing Away

> "Using the held map at sea, hovering over other regions slightly hightlights the region, plotting a course prompts
> you if you want to travel, which then thd camera should pan in a cinematic view showing your ship dailing away as
> the game fades, loads, and shows you arriving in the new waters."

## What it settles

- **At sea, the held map lightly highlights another water** while the pointer is over it.
- **A course plotted into another water asks whether to travel there.**
- **Travelling**, the camera pans in a cinematic view of the ship sailing away as the game fades and loads, then shows
  it arriving in the new waters.
