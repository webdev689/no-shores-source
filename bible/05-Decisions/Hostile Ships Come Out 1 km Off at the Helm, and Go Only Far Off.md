# Hostile Ships Come Out 1 km Off at the Helm, and Go Only Far Off

> "Enemies spawn way too far away in ship enemy encounters"

> Asked how near a hostile ship should come out at the helm: "1 km"

> Asked whether, under fast travel, one should still come out far enough that a ship met head-on stays in sight half a
> minute: "Keep the 30 s rule (Recommended)"

> Asked, of a ship sailed away from, when it should disappear: "Far off, about 5 km (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- A hostile ship a roll sends comes out 1 km off a player at the helm and at rest, nearer than the 5 km it came out at
  before, and farther the faster they sail: half a minute of the two closing head-on, as before
  ([[Naval Combat]], "Where").
- It is still sighted from 5 km at the helm and goes only once no player is within 1.1 times that, as before
  ([[Naval Combat]], "Sight"). This replaces where a ship comes out in [[The Sea as Claude Shapes It]].
