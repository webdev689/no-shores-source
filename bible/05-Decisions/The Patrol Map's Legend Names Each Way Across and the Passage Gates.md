# The Patrol Map's Legend Names Each Way Across and the Passage Gates

> "1. On the patrol map of each region, the pathway to the other patrol zone should be shown in the legend
> 2. Gates to passages also need their own legend"

> Asked whether the legend's rows read "Way to the Gullwing Vale" and "Passage gate": "Shorter: 'To <zone>'"

## What it settles

- **The land map's table** ([[Held Map]], "On land") in a patrol zone names each way across to the island's other
  patrol zones ([[Each Island Has Three Regions, and Saltmere's Three Patrol Zones Stay, Linked]]) in its own row, by
  its zone: "To the Gullwing Vale".
- **The passages' gates**, the sealed doors whose mouths lead into [[Passages]], have their own row in it, apart from
  a sealed door that leads to no passage.
