# The Key Strip Is a Compact Panel, a Held Key Leaves, and Settings Has Tabs

> "Today im going to have you focusing on a few things
>
> 1. Condensing and overhauling the control ui on the screen, its way too large and needs to be in a detailed compact
> format with a better AAA grade design
>
> 2. When in a social space, patrol area, dungeon, etc. Basically anywhere you can actively leave or abandon. I want to
> remove the M key menu in replace of a keybind you can hold to leave areas (kind of like how Destiny does this with
> its ghost). Exit to Menu and Exit to Desktop move to the settings menu
>
> 3. Overhaul the settings menu to be.organized like traditional games. Tabs for audio. Graphics .keybinds, etc and
> move.all the sound credits into a button you must press.
>
> Please take some autonomy, be as detailed as possible and treat this like your baby. Come back when this is 100%"

## What it settles

- The key strip is condensed into a compact, detailed format ([[Interface]], "The key strip").
- Wherever the player stands on foot and can leave (a social space, a patrol area, a dungeon), the menu M opened is
  gone: a key held leaves, as Destiny's Ghost does ([[Interface]], "The leave held").
- Exit to Menu and Exit to Desktop are in Settings.
- Settings is set out in tabs, as other games set theirs out: Graphics, Audio, the keybinds and the rest; the sound
  credits are behind a press of their own ([[Interface]], "Settings").
- The details are Claude's to call, below.
