# Elder Maren Hale Walks at Three Quarters of a Player's Pace

> "In your introduction to the city, when walking with the NPC, the npc should walk faster."

Asked "Elder Maren Hale walks at 1.5 m/s on the welcome; a player walks at 4.43 m/s (runs 7.59). How fast should she
walk?", the owner chose "Three quarters of a walk": about 3.3 m/s, the foes' pace, so a walking player gains on her
slowly.

## What it settles

- On her welcome, Elder Maren Hale walks at three quarters of a player's walk, about 3.3 m/s
  ([[Prologue]], "The elder's welcome").
- Replaces the pace in [[Factions and Vendors as Claude Shapes Them]] (1.5 m/s).
