# The Inventory Keeps Vestiges, Holds Charge, Gains Show and the Menu Sounds

With a picture of Destiny 2's inventory (its currencies, "Engrams 7/10" over ten places in two rows of five, the empty
ones dark engrams), the owner asked:

> "1. Remove gear from the inventory. It already has a place in on the character tab.
> 2. Remove vestiges tab. At the top of the inventory, below currencies. Vestiges get a capped inventory like the
> reference image.
> 3. Ensure consumables (like the heart) and dismantling gear is a hold to dismantle/consume function. For example in
> destiny, when you hold to dismantle an item, the icon does an animation for a short time until the item is
> dismantled, and then on the right of the screen, the items aquired shows. (I'd like to do sounds for all UI
> interactions also) This is how loot and other items look when aquired. Resrarch thoroughly"

The heart is the Fruit of Dagon's glyph.

## What it settles

- **The inventory holds no gear**: the gear is on the Character page alone.
- **The Vestiges stand under the currencies**, capped at ten, as Destiny 2's engrams are, not on a shelf of their own.
- **Dismantling and feeding a Fruit of Dagon are held**, and the item's icon animates while held, until it acts.
- **What is gained shows at the right of the screen**, for loot and every other gain, as Destiny shows it.
- **Every interaction of the interface makes a sound.**

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The empty Vestige places | Each a grey Vestige, faded | The picture's empty engrams are dark engrams |
| The animation while held | A wash of the item's rarity rising through its tile, the picture brightening and quivering toward the end; then shards of its rarity for a dismantle, a ring for a feed | Bungie shows the hold as a filling timer; the owner asked for the icon to animate until it acts |
| How long a dismantle is held | By rarity: 0.6 s Normal, 0.7 Magic, 0.9 Rare, 1.2 Legendary, 1.8 Atheric | Destiny holds an Exotic much longer than a Legendary, so the rarest is never lost to a slip |
| Dismantling from a tile | F held over a carried piece, or with it focused | Destiny's own key on a computer; the item's page keeps its held press |
| Feeding the Fruit | Still held on an item's page, the item charging | The Fruit grows one item, so it needs the item chosen; its card says so |
| The feed | Right of the screen, a row each: picture, name, how many; slides in, 4.5 s, fades; like gains merge; six at most | Destiny's pickups stand at the right; Bungie names no timings, so these keep a batch readable |
| The sounds | Made by the browser's synthesiser: tick, press, tab, open, close, charge, crunch, chime by rarity, refusal; ticks and presses stray a little in pitch | No sound files to fetch or license; best practice keeps ticks short and varied |
| The volume | One "Interface sounds" slider in Settings, kept in this browser, 60% at first | Accessibility guidelines ask for a separate interface volume |
