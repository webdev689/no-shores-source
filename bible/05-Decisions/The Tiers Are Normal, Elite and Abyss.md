# The Tiers Are Normal, Elite and Abyss

> "So difficulty tiers..
>
> Officially they should be
>
> Normal (Calm)
>
> Elite (Rough)
>
> Abyss (Storm)
>
> Ditch the visual storm idea"

Asked whether one should be renamed, since "Elite" also names the 18 Elite Dungeons and a foe's rank:

> "Keep elite"

## What it settles

- **The three tiers are named Normal, Elite and Abyss**: Normal was Calm, Elite was Rough and Abyss was Storm. What
  each does stays as [[Where You Go and the Seas You Choose Set the Difficulty]] says.
- **The visual storm idea is dropped**: it replaces
  [[The Seas Chosen Bring Their Weather, and a Storm Is the Mystery's]].
- **Elite keeps all three meanings**: the tier, the Elite Dungeons and the foe's rank.
- **Not in the game yet**: the voyage's card still offers Calm, Rough and Storm.

The days and the weeks over them: [[Dailies and Weeklies Follow Destiny 1, With One Abyss Dungeon a Week]].

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
