# Matchmade Dungeons Gain Encounters, Puzzles, Climbs and Raid Mechanics

> "Additionally, the matchmaking dungeon content specifically should be enhanced to include unique encounters,
> puzzles, platforming or climbing sections, and more "raid like" mechanics to enhance the experience. Feel to review
> dagons breach, the elite.breaches and other world content in DFO along with the missions we implemented in the
> faction questline."

> "Please go above and beyond with this, take autonomy, and return when everything is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- **The matchmade dungeons are more than a run of fights**: they gain encounters of their own, puzzles, sections
  climbed or jumped, and mechanics a company works together, as a raid's are.
- **The references** are DFO's Oblivion Gate and Elite Breach, DFO's other world content, and the step kinds of the
  houses' roads ([[Missions Are Varied, Not Only Simple Tasks]], [[Quests]]).
- **How it is shaped is Claude's call**, on the owner's autonomy, recorded in
  [[The Playlist and Its Dungeons as Claude Shapes Them]] and open to the owner's change.
