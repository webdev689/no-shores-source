# Foes Spread Across the Players They Fight

Told that the last audit of #68 found foes never change target while they still see theirs, so a player near a crowd
fighting someone else is left alone, and asked whether foes should spread across several players, the owner
answered:

> "2. Yes"

## What it settles

- Foes in numbers spread across the players they fight rather than all keeping to one, as part of fighting with
  intelligence ([[Foes Fight With Intelligence, Not by Rushing]], [[Foes]]).
- How they share themselves out is Claude's, recorded in [[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]].
