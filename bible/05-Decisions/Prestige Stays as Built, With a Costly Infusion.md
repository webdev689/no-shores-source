# Prestige Stays as Built, With a Costly Infusion

> "Prestige comes from armor and weapons"

Told that today only armour carries Prestige, as the owner chose with "Vanilla D1":

> "You're telling me at this point in time weapons dont count torwards prestige?"
>
> "Research destiny 1 vanilla please"

Told that at Destiny 1's launch only armour carried Light and weapons carried Attack, and that The Taken King made
Light the average of everything worn, weapons included, with infusion to keep a favourite item:

> "What do you think? Honestly"
>
> "What if we just stayed with what we have? Added some sort of costly infusion method?"

## What it settles

- **Prestige stays as built** ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]): levels 1 to 20 come from XP; past 20,
  the Prestige of the armour worn sets the level, to 30. A weapon carries Attack, not Prestige.
- **A costly infusion** is added for armour and weapons: a stronger item fed into a kept one raises it, so a
  favourite need not be left behind. What it costs is decided later.

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
