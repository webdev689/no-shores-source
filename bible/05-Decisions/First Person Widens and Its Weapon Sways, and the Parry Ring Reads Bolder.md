# First Person Widens and Its Weapon Sways, and the Parry Ring Reads Bolder

> "I want you to look into improving the first person view as the 3rd person outshines it. A better POV, rework the
> parry indicator for both views to be more legible and detailed, enhancing readability."

> Shown the Test Ground's fight in both views (the resting sword over the right third of the view, foes drawn a metre
> off, the view 50° tall) and asked which first-person changes to make: "Foes held further off", "Wider view
> (Recommended)", "Weapon sway"

> Asked where the parry indicator should sit, as it stood 2.5 m over the foe's feet: "On the foe, kept on screen
> (Recommended)"

> Asked what the reworked ring should show: "Bold, outlined rings (Recommended)", "Parry window band (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- First person's view is wider than third's, about 65° tall.
- In first person a foe is drawn at least 1.5 m from the eye, not 1 m.
- The first-person weapon lags behind turns and settles, and sways with steps.
- The held weapon keeps its size: "Smaller, lower weapon" was offered and not chosen.
- The parry ring stands round the attacker's head, pulled onto the screen's edge where it would leave it, in both
  views.
- Its rings are bold, with a dark edge, larger, and a gold band on the fixed ring shows the parry window.
- The blow's direction on the ring and a new iron mark were offered and not chosen.
