# The Meadow Art Is the Owner's, Its Tables Stay in Git, and the Grass Takes the Trees' Rain

Asked, on the pull request for [[What a Body Meets Keeps to What Is Drawn]] and
[[The Owner's Handcrafted Meadow Grows Over the Grass]], whether the meadow's five sprites are the owner's own work,
whether the two tools' outputs committed to git may stay there, whether Claude's budget for the boxes stands, and
whether the meadow should take less of the plants' wet sheen in rain:

> "1. Yes its mine
> 2. Approved
> 3. Approved. Do whatever is best
> 4. The new grass needs the same fix trees recieved"

## What it settles

- The meadow's five sprites are the owner's own work ([[Own Work]], [[Meadow]]).
- `content/forged/parts.json` (the props' parts, written by `node tools/forgedParts.mjs`) and
  `client/src/nature/meadowArt.ts` (the sprites, written by `tools/meadow/write.py`) stay in git, an exception to
  [[Generated Files Stay Out of Git]].
- The boxes' budget stands as Claude set it ([[Layouts]], "Boxes"): at most 64 of a piece's boxes over a 2 m square
  of ground and a box a square metre of its footprint, never fewer than 256, a prop twice what its loosest strips
  cost, looser a quarter at a time past that.
- The meadow takes the fix the trees took: rain darkens it and lays no sky's glare on it, and it sways as slowly as
  the plants ([[Nature]], [[Sky]]).
