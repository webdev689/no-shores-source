# The Sea and the Abyss Run Again Are Retuned Into the Band

> "Retune both into band"

Asked what should happen to two of the game's activities that fell outside the rewards band, the sea at level 5 (0.65
of a patrol zone's XP an hour, under the band's 0.7) and the week's Abyss Dungeon run again at level 30 (2.15 of its XP
and 6.03 of its loot, over the band's 2 and 5), the owner chose to retune both into it, over widening the band, holding
only the passages to it, or leaving them for later.

## What it settles

- **The band holds every activity**, the sea and the week's Abyss Dungeon among them, at the bounds it had
  ([[Passages Web Out From the Patrol Zone Across Its Island]]: "We need to keep all our content within this band").
- **The sea at low levels is retuned up into it**, and **the week's Abyss Dungeon run again is trimmed down into it**,
  rather than either being excused from the band's test ([[Activities]], "The band"; [[Naval Combat]]).
