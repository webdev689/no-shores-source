# The Lore Books' Covers Are Drawn in Detail

The owner wrote, of the Compendium's books ([[The Compendium Holds the Lore Books, After Destiny's]]):

> "Let's also completely redesign the book look. I want it to be as detailed as possible"

Asked what the redesign should cover, the owner chose "Covers only": the covers, with the reading panel as it is.
Asked how the houses' books should differ, the owner chose "One design, house colours": one binding for every book,
differing by its cloth's colour and its motif, as before.

## What it settles

- Every lore book's cover is redrawn, as detailed as it can be ([[Lore]], [[Interface]], "Compendium").
- All books share one design; each differs only by its cloth and its motif.
- The reading panel is unchanged.

## Claude's calls

The owner's to change:

- **Twice the pixels each way**: 48 by 60, drawn at whole multiples of them (48 px and 96 px in the list, 144 px and
  192 px chosen), so every pixel stands square.
- **The binding**:
  - A rounded spine with brass caps, four raised bands, a gilt fleuron in each panel and a dark title label.
  - A board of woven cloth, bevelled and lit toward the top left.
  - A blind-tooled double line, then a gilt frame with a fleuron in each corner.
  - Brass corner pieces, each with a rivet.
  - A brass title plate with an engraved line, left blank, as the book's name shows under it.
  - The motif raised in brass, with a shadow, in an octagonal medallion on a sunk field.
  - A gilt flourish under the medallion.
  - A dark stitched strap and a brass clasp on the fore-edge.
- **A book not found** keeps the same binding in plain stone, with no motif and no brass.
