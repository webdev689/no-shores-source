# The Lore Books' Covers Are Drawn in Detail

The owner wrote, of the Compendium's books ([[The Compendium Holds the Lore Books, After Destiny's]]):

> "Let's also completely redesign the book look. I want it to be as detailed as possible"

Asked what the redesign should cover, the owner chose "Covers only": the covers, with the reading panel as it is.
Asked how the houses' books should differ, the owner chose "One design, house colours": one design for every book,
differing by its cloth's colour and its motif.

Then, sending pictures of Destiny's lore book covers (graphic, printed as posters, each a bold emblem on a patterned
ground, the pages' edge showing under each) and of its shelf of them (a finished book with a gold corner turned down,
each book not yet found an empty slot outlined in dots), the owner wrote:

> "More like these"

## What it settles

- Every lore book's cover is redrawn, as detailed as it can be, after those pictures ([[Lore]], [[Interface]],
  "Compendium").
- All books share one design; each differs only by its cloth and its motif.
- The reading panel is unchanged.

## Claude's calls

The owner's to change:

- **The game's own art.** The covers are drawn fresh, nothing taken from Destiny's, and in the game's own pixel art
  ([[Arts and Loadouts as Claude Shapes Them]] keeps everything pixel art), drawn from shapes at the size each stands:
  a pixel a CSS pixel under 96, two from there (48 by 60 in a narrow list, 72 by 90 and 96 by 120 chosen).
- **The design**, printed as a poster and bound:
  - Its spine at the left, and the pages' edge under the board.
  - A face banded from light down to dark, a sunburst of 24 rays across it and its print's grain.
  - A thin brass frame with brackets at its corners, a diamond at its top and its foot, a chevron and a row of studs.
  - Rings about the middle: one broken, one of ticks as a dial's, one dotted, one plain.
  - A dark disc rimmed in brass, lit at its upper left, the book's motif raised large in brass in its middle, three
    times its size at the largest.
- **A book whose every chapter is found** turns down a gold corner, as the owner's picture shows.
- **A book not yet found** is an empty slot outlined in dots, as the owner's picture shows, in place of a plain stone
  cover; and so are the three that rest on the Compendium's front while the file holds no lore.
