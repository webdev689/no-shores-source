# The Sloop Is About 18 m Long

> Seeing the Sloop at its true size beside its crew: "This cant be right. Why is yhe ship so small?"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> How long the Sloop should be, its whole model scaled evenly (its deck, guns, sails, hit boxes and draught): "About
> 18 m (3×) (Recommended)"
>
> How big it is drawn on the Overworld once resized: "Keep 75 m drawn (Recommended)"
>
> How a player who leaves the helm gets down from its quarterdeck, fenced at its new size by a 0.7 m rail on its
> front and sides: "Gap and steps (Recommended)", the ship tools opening the rail at the quarterdeck's two front
> corners, either side of the hatch, and adding a few steps down to the main deck, in its published model.

## What it settles

- **Its size.** The Sloop stands three times its model as the owner made it: about 18.7 m long at its waterline and
  5.6 m across it, its keel 1.8 m below it ([[Sailing]]). Everything on it scales with it: its deck, its guns,
  its sails, the boxes a ball strikes and its draught. Its trim stays where [[The Sloop's Trim]] put it.
- **On the Overworld** it is still drawn 75 m long ([[Game]]).
- **Its quarterdeck** is opened at its two front corners, either side of its hatch, by gaps in its rail with steps
  down to its main deck, in its published model ([[Sailing]], "Its deck").
