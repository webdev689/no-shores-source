# The Sloop Is About 18 m Long

> Seeing the Sloop at her true size beside her crew: "This cant be right. Why is yhe ship so small?"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> How long the Sloop should be, her whole model scaled evenly (her deck, guns, sails, hit boxes and draught): "About
> 18 m (3×) (Recommended)"
>
> How big she is drawn on the Overworld once resized: "Keep 75 m drawn (Recommended)"
>
> How a player who leaves the helm gets down from her quarterdeck, fenced at her new size by a 0.7 m rail on its
> front and sides: "Gap and steps (Recommended)", the ship tools opening the rail at the quarterdeck's two front
> corners, either side of the hatch, and adding a few steps down to the main deck, in her published model.

## What it settles

- **Her size.** The Sloop stands three times her model as the owner made it: about 18.7 m long at her waterline and
  5.6 m across it, her keel 1.8 m below it ([[Sailing]]). Everything on her scales with her: her deck, her guns,
  her sails, the boxes a ball strikes and her draught. Her trim stays where [[The Sloop's Trim]] put it on her.
- **On the Overworld** she is still drawn 75 m long ([[Game]]).
- **Her quarterdeck** is opened at its two front corners, either side of her hatch, by gaps in its rail with steps
  down to her main deck, in her published model ([[Sailing]], "Her deck").
