# A Player Logs Back In Where They Left the Overworld or a Social Space

> "When logging out and logging back on. You should relog from wherever you last left in the overworld or social
> space."

## What it settles

- **Logging back in takes the player to where they last left the overworld or a social space**: aboard their ship at
  sea, or on foot in the social space ([[Accounts]], "Where a player logs back in").
- Claude's calls in building it are in [[Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them]].
