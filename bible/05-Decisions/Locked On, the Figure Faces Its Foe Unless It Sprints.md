# Locked On, the Figure Faces Its Foe Unless It Sprints

> "Also remove the change we did to being locked on and not being able to see your character sprint away. Instead,
> after sprinting, your character should face the locked on person. Basically, if you are locked on that should always
> be your facing, but remove it from sprinting"

## What it settles

- While the soft lock holds a foe, the player's figure on foot faces it, walking or still, so walking away backs off
  from it ([[Test Ground]]).
- Sprinting, it faces where it runs; once the sprint ends, it faces its foe again.
- It replaces [[Locked On, the Figure Faces Its Foe]].
