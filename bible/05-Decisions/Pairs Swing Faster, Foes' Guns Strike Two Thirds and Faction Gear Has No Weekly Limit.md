# Pairs Swing Faster, Foes' Guns Strike Two Thirds and Faction Gear Has No Weekly Limit

The owner's answer to the proposals Claude made on the open points
[[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]] left. Claude proposed:

- that a swing or a dodge need its whole stamina cost, where some stamina left is enough today;
- that a pair swing in 0.8 of its lone weapon's time at 0.9 of its damage, about an eighth more a second, since a
  pair struck as its lone weapon and an "& Shield" or a lone weapon with a torch was always as good or better;
- that a foe's guns strike two thirds of their damage, so an Elite Marksman's aimed shot fells a player of its level
  in three, not two, its accuracy unchanged;
- no weekly limit on faction gear for now, the rank, the Aurem and the Essence gating it.

> "Go ahead with your proposals except the stamina change"

## What it settles

- A player's pair swings faster, each blow a little lighter.
- A foe's guns strike two thirds of their damage.
- Faction gear has no weekly limit.
- A swing and a dodge keep needing only some stamina left.

How each is built is Claude's, in [[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]].
