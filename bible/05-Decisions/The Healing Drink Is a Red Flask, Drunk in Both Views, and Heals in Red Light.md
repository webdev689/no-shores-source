# The Healing Drink Is a Red Flask, Drunk in Both Views, and Heals in Red Light

The healing drink put the weapon away and showed nothing in its place, as the forge had no drinking pose
([[Three Healing Charges, Refilled by Blows and Kills]]). The owner wrote:

> "The healing interaction needs its own animation plus some sort of detailed healing effect"

Then the owner chose from Claude's options:

| Asked | The owner's pick | The option as offered |
|---|---|---|
| What the player drinks from | "Small round flask (Recommended)" | A stoppered glass flask with a glowing red draught, taken from the belt, tipped to the lips, put back. Standing, and a walking version since a drink moves at walk pace |
| The healing effect | "Red light rising (Recommended)" | In the healing charges' own red: the flask glows while drunk, then a soft ring flares at the feet, motes and small crosses rise off the body and a brief glow wraps the figure. Pixel-cell style like Mend's |
| First person | "Both views (Recommended)" | First-person hands raise and tip the flask, and the effect shows round the screen's edge |

## What it settles

- The drink has its own move in the forge: a small round flask of glowing red draught taken from the belt, tipped to
  the lips and lowered, standing and on the move at a walk ([[Sprite Forge]], [[Combat]]).
- The first person shows the hands raising and tipping the flask, and the effect round the screen's edge
  ([[First Person]]).
- The heal is shown in the healing charges' red: the flask glowing while it is drunk, then a ring at the feet, motes
  and small crosses rising off the body and a brief glow round the figure, drawn in cells as Mend's light is.
  Replaced by [[The Heal Is a Subtle Swirl]]: the heal's light is now a subtle swirl.
