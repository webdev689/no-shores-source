# The Player's Swings Chain Side Cut, Overhead and Thrust

After playing the fight:

> "Melee attacks should tranistion between each animation instead of random. So every attack should be different"

Asked what should replace the random stroke, the owner chose "Fixed chain, restarts after a pause": side cut, then
overhead, then thrust, then the side cut again, starting over at the side cut after about a second without swinging.
Asked whether foes swing in the same order, the owner chose "Player only".

## What it settles

- Each of the player's melee swings takes the next stroke of the chain: the side cut, the overhead, the thrust, and
  round. A second without a swing starts the chain over at the side cut.
- It replaces the click's random stroke, which came from Daggerfall Unity's click to attack.
- Foes keep choosing their strokes by chance.

Claude's calls:

- A stroke that does not reach the locked foe is passed over for the next one in the chain that does, as the random
  stroke kept to those that reached: at the edge of a weapon's reach, where only the thrust reaches, every swing
  thrusts.
- In the first person the hands draw each swing the way the chain strikes it, rather than choosing a way of their own.
