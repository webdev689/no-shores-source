## What it settles

- **Every island is cut into three regions**, each holding its own nodes. At sea, the held map lets the player
  highlight and pick a region of the island whose waters they are in, and the ship sails there at the sea's own pace.
- **On Saltmere the three patrol zones stay as they are**, the Saltgrass Downs, the Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood
  Moors, one in each region, each linked to both others by passages of their own, apart from the passages of
  [[Passages Web Out From the Patrol Zone Across Its Island]]; crossing one plays the dive's rise over the island.
- **On every other island the regions are built now**, and the patrol zones past the one by its port city are
  removed. A region need not hold a patrol zone: it holds its nodes.
- **Claude names the regions.**

It replaces in part [[Each Island Keeps One Patrol Zone, the One by Its Port City]]: Saltmere keeps its three patrol
zones, and the houses' tasks stay where they were written. Claude's calls on it are in
[[Passages as Claude Builds Them]].
