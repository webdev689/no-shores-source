# Patrol Zones and Missions Open as Their Pass Is Done

> "Let's also lock all patrol zones except the one we are building. The others will become unlocked as we do passes
> on them"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked which patrol zones stay open while the detail pass works on Saltmere's three: "Saltmere's three
> (Recommended)"
>
> Asked how a locked patrol zone shows where the player picks a node: its mark greyed with a lock, and a click says it
> is locked and lands nowhere; or hidden: "Shown as locked (Recommended)"
>
> Asked whether the missions beside a locked zone lock with it: "Lock missions also until we do a pass on them"

## What it settles

- **Patrol zones are locked** except the Saltgrass Downs, the Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood Moors. A patrol zone
  opens when its pass is done.
- **Missions are locked** until a pass is done on them.
- **A locked node still shows** where the player picks a node, greyed with a lock; choosing it says it is locked and
  does not take the player there.

It builds on [[Patrol Zones Stay as Built and Gain Linear Combat Areas]] and [[Saltmere's Detail Pass Goes Deep]].
