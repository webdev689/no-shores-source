# Procedural Islands Are Coves, With Rarer Guarded Isles

> "I say we go ahead and build out the procedural island system. Using the blender pipeline we can moduilize to make
> the islands varied and distinct, along with wiring the dungeon sessions work.
>
> Ship wreckage also"

> Offered three kinds of visit to an island (a quick stop: a cove with the wreckage and the dungeon's door, no foes
> outside; a small place to explore, with its own foes; or both, by the island's size): "C"

> Asked where a player who loses a fight washes up: "Nearest port city or nearest procedural island"

## What it settles

- The sea's procedural islands are built now ([[The World Map]], "discoveries"), with the sea's dungeons on them as
  the owner's plan for the loop places them, varied and distinct, from modules made in the Blender pipeline.
- Most are coves: the wreckage, the dungeon's door, and no foes outside. Rarer, larger isles have guards and a
  landmark to explore.
- Each island's dungeon is laid by the dungeons' generator. Ship wreckage lies on them to scavenge
  ([[Ships Have Their Own Upgrade Materials, From Ships and Island Wreckage]]).
- A player who loses a fight washes up at the nearest port city or the nearest procedural island.
- How it is built is Claude's ([[The Ship's Power, Crew and Islands as Claude Shapes Them]]).
