# DFO's Dungeons 2.0 Comes Over, Within the Patrol Areas

> "We have a bunch of sessions running and I want to talk about importing dungeon 2.0 from a dfo branch"

Told that no branch of DFO's is named so, and offered DELVE II, the Abyss Dungeons and the Delve Arc, the owner
wrote:

> "I think it's delve 2. It's basically our own propritarty dungeon generation"

Told that DFO's own dungeon generation is Dungeons 2.0 (`DUNGEONS_2.0.md` on DFO's branch `ccr-cab30c1a-oddrwb`: the
Forge and its dungeon engine, with DELVE II's encounters inside), then asked whether it is the one, where forged
dungeons stand in Overlook, and in what order it comes over:

> "1. yes
> 2. Placed within patrol areas
> 3. 3. Take your time and do everything properly"

As the parts were being weighed:

> "Dont adapt things just to adapt them. It needs to fit into what we are building"

Once the work was under way:

> "I want you to build out everything and return when this is 100% finished"

> "So with our dungeons. I dont want them to be this extravgant labyrinth that is hard to navigate. They need to be a
> slighty linear thing with a clear beginning, middle, end and an easy navigation path out of the dungeon, like a
> return portal"

> "Do a comprehensive and detailed pass on this. Ensure everything recieves our texture system, props are laid, and
> everything is perfection"

## What it settles

- **Dungeons 2.0 comes over** as far as it fits what Overlook is building: the Forge, DFO's own dungeon generator, and
  what of its dungeon engine and DELVE II serves Overlook as it stands ([[Pick - Dungeons 2.0]]). A part that would
  have to be reshaped around a system Overlook does not have stays behind. The owner counts the Forge as DFO's own:
  "our own propritarty dungeon generation".
- **A dungeon is not a labyrinth.** It runs slightly linear, with a clear beginning, middle and end, and an easy way
  out, like a return portal.
- **Props are laid in the dungeons, and everything in them wears the procedural textures** ([[Procedural
  Textures]]): the owner asked for both.
- **Forged dungeons stand within the patrol areas**, the "procedural dungeons" the owner's Patrol already holds
  ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]).
- **Done properly, with time taken, and built out in full** before it comes back to the owner. How it works in
  Overlook is Claude's to build, as the world is
  ([[The World Is Claude's to Mold]]); Claude's choices are in [[Dungeons as Claude Builds Them]], and the owner can
  change any of them.
- The [[Rules]] hold. The Forge's pictures are the procedural textures' dungeon sets and its entrances are forged
  models, both of which come over under [[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]; the rest of what
  Daggerfall made stays behind ([[Own Work]]).
- Its first build done, the owner asked for a detailed pass on everything, perfect and insanely varied
  ([[The Dungeons Are Perfect and Insanely Varied]]).
