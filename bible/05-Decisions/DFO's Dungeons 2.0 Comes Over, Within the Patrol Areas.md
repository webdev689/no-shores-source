# DFO's Dungeons 2.0 Comes Over, Within the Patrol Areas

> "We have a bunch of sessions running and I want to talk about importing dungeon 2.0 from a dfo branch"

> Told that no branch of DFO's is named so, and offered DELVE II, the Abyss Dungeons and the Delve Arc: "I think it's
> delve 2. It's basically our own propritarty dungeon generation"

> Told that DFO's own dungeon generation is Dungeons 2.0 (`DUNGEONS_2.0.md` on DFO's branch `ccr-cab30c1a-oddrwb`: the
> Forge and its dungeon engine, with DELVE II's encounters inside), then asked whether it is the one, where forged
> dungeons stand in Overlook, and in what order it comes over: "1. yes
> 2. Placed within patrol areas
> 3. 3. Take your time and do everything properly"

> As the parts were being weighed: "Dont adapt things just to adapt them. It needs to fit into what we are building"

## What it settles

- **Dungeons 2.0 comes over** as far as it fits what Overlook is building: the Forge, DFO's own dungeon generator, and
  what of its dungeon engine and DELVE II serves Overlook as it stands ([[Pick - Dungeons 2.0]]). A part that would
  have to be reshaped around a system Overlook does not have stays behind. The owner counts the Forge as DFO's own:
  "our own propritarty dungeon generation".
- **Forged dungeons stand within the patrol areas**, the "procedural dungeons" the owner's Patrol already holds
  ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]).
- **Done properly, with time taken.** How it works in Overlook is Claude's to build, as the world is
  ([[The World Is Claude's to Mold]]); Claude's choices are in [[Dungeons as Claude Builds Them]], and the owner can
  change any of them.
- The [[Rules]] hold. The Forge's pictures are the procedural textures' dungeon sets and its entrances are forged
  models, both of which come over under [[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]; the rest of what
  Daggerfall made stays behind ([[Own Work]]).
