# The Player's Display Takes DFO's Compass and Bar Layout

> Shown the Plus kit's vitals panel at the bottom left: "Note: implement the compass and player bar positioning
> elements from DFO instead of our current design for the player"

> Asked whether DFO's movable display (unlock, drag any piece, snap, lock and reset) comes over too: "Placement only
> for now (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- The player's display takes DFO's compass and the placing of its bars from DFO's Enhanced HUD
  ([[Pick - Enhanced Plus UI]], [[Interface]]), in place of the vitals panel at the bottom left.
- DFO's movable display does not come over for now: the pieces stand where DFO places them.
