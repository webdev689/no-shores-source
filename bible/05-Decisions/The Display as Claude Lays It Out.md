# The Display as Claude Lays It Out

The owner asked for the vitals at the top left and the arts and weapons at the bottom left, "as detailed as possible"
([[The Vitals Go Top Left and the Arts and Weapons Bottom Left]]). These are Claude's choices in building it; the
owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The vitals' look | Three long thin bars, health over stamina over poise, each in a fine stone frame whose ends come to a point at a brass tip, drawn pixel for pixel and never scaled | The first picture's bars, in the kit's stone and brass ([[Interface]]) |
| The vitals' sizes | Health 12 px tall, stamina 10, poise 8, 4 px apart, 28 px in from the left and 14 down; 16, 14 and 10, 5 apart, at the display's larger step; twice the first past 1440p | Health first and largest, as in the picture |
| The bars' lengths | Health's is 26% of the view's width, 150 to 600 px, kept 16 px clear of the compass; stamina's and poise's are as long as what they hold beside health's most | A souls-like display draws a bar as long as what it holds |
| A narrow view | Where health's bar cannot stand 150 px long beside the compass, the compass's column stands under the vitals, which take half the view | The Test Ground's view between its panels; the two never meet |
| Words on the vitals | None on health and stamina; poise says Iron, Staggered or Open beside its bar. A screen reader reads each as a meter | The picture's bars carry no words |
| The vitals' warnings | Health at a quarter or less flashes its frame to blood, stamina's frame lights white for a press with none, and poise's frame turns brass while open | DFO's Enhanced HUD's warnings, kept from the window |
| The buffs | In a row under the vitals, 24 px each (36 at the larger step) | They stood over the window's health before |
| The Anima | Its icon in a diamond 68 px across (102 at the larger step), lit from the bottom a row at a time as it charges, glowing once full | The super's diamond in the second picture |
| The Tenets | Each icon in a ring 44 px across (66), a dark wedge over it sweeping away clockwise as it recharges, its last 9 seconds counted in it, the ring lit once ready | The abilities' circles in the second picture |
| The Arts' keys | R, Q and G on keycaps under their icons | The second picture's buttons under each |
| A cast, and no cast | A spell just cast flashes its frame white for 0.5 s; while the player cannot cast (staggered, down or dead) every icon greys under a hatch | A refused press reads at a glance, not by colour alone |
| The weapons | Right of the Arts under a thin brass rule: the weapon in hand as its forge picture in a 116 by 36 px box (164 by 52), a Ranged one's shots large over its capacity ("18 / 24", or "No ammo"), a Melee one's name in its rarity's colour; under it the other loadout's weapon in an 80 by 22 px box (112 by 32), with its key, 1 or 2, and its own shots | The second picture's weapon in hand and the next below it, smaller |
| A weapon's picture | Laid level with its point or muzzle to the right once six times longer than wide; a quarter turn keeps its pixels square, a slant is turned and smoothed once; never more than 4 screen pixels to one of its own | Crisp, as the gear screens lay a weapon on its tile |
| The weapons' moves | A swap trades the two rows over the swap's 0.4 s, a reload fills a line under the count, a draw flashes the weapon for 0.3 s, and one put away dims and greys | Each act the rules allow shows where the eye already is |
| What moved for them | On foot the place's name and the status line stand at the top right; the company under the vitals; the prompt for E and the hints over the Arts and weapons; the keys' line wraps short of the Acquired feed; an arrow to a threat off the screen stands below the vitals or above the Arts and weapons | Nothing covers another ([[Interface]]) |
| The compass | 520 px wide, or 60% of the view the display stands over (not the whole window), so it fits between the Test Ground's panels | It must fit beside the vitals there too |
