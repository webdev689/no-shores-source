# The Player Plays as in DFO

> "So just making sure but all functions should be enabled. First/third person, same scroll to switch views, same
> attack animations, same control scheme, crouching, climbing, jumping, running, etc All the forge has to offer."

> Told that DFO's movement and camera numbers come from Daggerfall Unity, and asked whether to use the same numbers
> as Overlook's own tuning: "Same numbers"

> On how the player swings a weapon: "Click, hold to repeat"

> On which of the forge's moves DFO gave only to enemies the player should have too: "Thrust, chop, swing2h"

> On the colours of cloth and metal: "Daggerfall's exact colours"

## What it settles

- The player has every move the [[Sprite Forge]] draws for it, in first and third person: walking, running,
  crouching, jumping, climbing, hanging, shimmying, mantling, vaulting, riding, casting and fighting.
- The scroll wheel moves between first and third person, and the controls are DFO's.
- Speeds, the jump and the camera use DFO's numbers as Overlook's own tuning. No Daggerfall Unity code comes over.
- Right click swings; holding it keeps swinging.
- The player also thrusts, chops and swings two-handed, which DFO gave only to enemies.
- Cloth and metal take Daggerfall's exact colours, read once from Daggerfall's palette and dye tables into a table.
  This is a further exception to [[Own Work]], beside [[DFO's Sprite Forge Comes Over Exactly]].
