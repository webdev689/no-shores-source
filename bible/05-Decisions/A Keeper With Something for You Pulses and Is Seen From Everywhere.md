# A Keeper With Something for You Pulses and Is Seen From Everywhere

The owner wrote:

> "1. In social spaces, the glyph above the vendor should be pulsing and seen from everywhere if there is a
> notification. For example, a quest step that requires you to talk to someone, or your mailbox having items in it.
> Its how destiny handles it."

> "2. The hand held map should also recieve the same system."

Told how the marks stand now and what this would touch, the owner wrote:

> "Youre on point. Build this to 100%, be eztremely detailed and only come back when this is finished. It needs to be
> perfect"

## What it settles

- In a social space, a keeper's mark over them pulses and is seen from everywhere while the keeper has something for
  the player: a quest step that asks the player to talk to them, or letters waiting at the Postmaster ([[Game]], "Over
  what a player can use"; [[What a Player Can Use Wears Its Mark Over It]]).
- The held map shows the same: that keeper's coin pulses on it ([[Held Map]], "On land").
- It follows how Destiny marks a vendor with something waiting.
