# The Playlist's Nodes Are Drawn in More Detail, and Move

> "I want you to redesign the Playlist nodes to be more detailed."

Asked which nodes, the Playlist's mark on the map or the crests on its screen, the owner chose "Both". Asked what more
detail should add, among richer art, more information on the node and motion, the owner chose "Richer art" and
"Motion".

## What it settles

- **Both the map's Playlist mark and the Playlist screen's crests are redrawn** in more detail.
- **The detail is richer art and motion.** A node shows no more information than it did.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The crests' metal | Each shield of worked metal, lit from its top left: a dark outer edge with a bright line inside it, the field recessed and lit toward its head, an engraved line round it, five studs on the rim and a gem at its head in its tier's colour | "Richer art" on the shield every crest shares |
| The devices | Raised over the field in their shadow, lit at their head: the Elite Playlist's chevrons under a rank's pip; the Abyss Playlist's eye with rays over it, an iris round its slit and three ripples; the week's Elite Dungeon's two swords, each with a fuller, a guard, a grip and a pommel; the week's Abyss Dungeon's crown with three jewels in its band and a ball on each point; a Breach's arch of laid stones with a keystone, its crystal's far facet in shade | Each says the entry it said before, in more detail |
| The dial | On the chart each crest stands on a dial of its own, in place of the chart's two rings round its spot: an inner ring, and an outer one ticked every 5 degrees, longer every 30, with a diamond at each quarter; dim at rest, brighter lit, brightest chosen; none where the crests stand two by two, as the rings were not | Destiny's nodes stand on rings; a ring that is the crest's own can turn and light with it |
| Opening | The crests rise into place one after another, 0.05 s apart, each over 0.3 s from 12 px below | The screen arrives rather than appears |
| The dials turning | Every open entry's dial turns 3 degrees a second, and the chosen one's three arcs 10 against it; a shut entry's stands | Slow enough to read as life, not as a wait; a shut entry is not live |
| The chosen crest's glow | Breathes over 3 s, between four fifths and full | The choice reads at a glance |
| The sweep | A light sweeps a crest's shield once from its top left, over 0.55 s, as it is lit or chosen | The crest answers the pointer, the focus and the choice |
| A reward waiting | A gold ring grows from the dot to three times its size and fades, every 2 s | Draws the eye to what is still to earn, as the dot already marks it |
| The map's mark | The Playlist's shield in small, worked as the crests are, bearing a list's three lines each led by a square point, in its amber ring, in place of the ☰ | One shield for the Playlist on the map and on its screen; its list keeps it read as a playlist |
| The map's mark moving | Its ring ticked as a dial, turning 8 degrees a second, brighter under the pointer or chosen; an amber ring spreading from it to nearly twice its size every 2.4 s | A beacon over the sea, told apart from the port beside it |
| Reduced motion | Nothing moves: the crests stand in place, the dials and the ticks still, the glow full, no sweep and no spreading ring | The player's ask, as everywhere in the interface |
| Drawn once | Each part is a picture drawn once, turned, faded or grown as it moves, never drawn again a frame | Motion costs no drawing a frame |
