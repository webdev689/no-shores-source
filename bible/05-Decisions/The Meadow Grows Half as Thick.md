# The Meadow Grows Half as Thick

> "Also reduce the amount of grass"

## What it settles

- The meadow grows less grass ([[Meadow]]), replacing the thickness of
  [[The Owner's Handcrafted Meadow Grows Over the Grass]]; where it grows, how far and how it looks are unchanged.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How much less | Half: 288 tufts a cell, about one and an eighth a square metre, a far cell thinning as before | A cut that shows at a glance, and half the cards for the GPU to draw |
| Which grass | The meadow's tufts only; the baked grass the plants grow stays | Whether the baked grass goes is the owner's, as the meadow's first decision left it |
