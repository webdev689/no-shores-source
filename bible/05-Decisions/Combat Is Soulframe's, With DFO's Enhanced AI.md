# Combat Is Soulframe's, With DFO's Enhanced AI

> "Lets go ahead and develop how combat will work in Overlook. Combat will utilize directional dodging (we can figure
> out what animation could be suit this) and an auto lock on mechanic/parry system. The mobile version of dfo has a
> lock on system we can port to achieve this and I think we do have parry animations"

> Asked what the auto lock-on should be: "However Soulframe does it"

> Told how Soulframe's parry and dodge work: "Yep, make the best decisions with autonomy and develop this out to be
> extremely detailed. Also port our enhanced ai from dfo as it handles enemies and build upon it to utilize the new
> features. Dont come back until everything is 100% developed. This is your baby. Extremely detailed with no half
> assed work or band aids"

> "I figured with dodging we could reuse current animations somehow"

## What it settles

- Overlook's combat follows Soulframe: a soft lock with no button that steers a fighter's blows at the foe it fights; a
  guard held, and a parry pressed just as a blow lands, staggering the striker open to a riposte; a directional dodge
  that blows pass through, a perfect one earning a counter; stamina and poise.
- DFO's Enhanced AI comes over for the foes, built upon so they guard, parry, dodge and riposte too: its navmesh, its
  tactics and its telegraphed blows ([[Pick - Enhanced AI]]).
- The design's details are Claude's calls under the owner's autonomy, recorded in [[Combat]] and [[Foes]].
- The dodge reuses the forge's moves: no new renders for it.
- The rules are Rust, decided by the server and predicted by each player's browser ([[Agree to the Bit]]).
