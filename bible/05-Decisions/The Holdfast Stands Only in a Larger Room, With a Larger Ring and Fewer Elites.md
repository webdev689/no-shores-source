# The Holdfast Stands Only in a Larger Room, With a Larger Ring and Fewer Elites

> "In dungeons, the new hold zone activity spawns too many elite enemies, and takes place in a small room. This event
> should only take place in a larger room with a larger ring."

> "Also do an audit on other dungeon activities looking for similar issues"

## What it settles

- **The Holdfast stands only in a larger room**, not a small one.
- **Its ring is larger.**
- **It spawns fewer elite enemies.**
- **The other dungeon activities are audited for similar issues.**
