# Passages as Claude Builds Them

The owner handed the passages over in full: "Alright, this is where I give you autonomy and make this your baby. I
want you.to be extremely detailed and provide a lot of variation, utilize blender and develop this.system in full."
What the owner decided is in [[Passages Web Out From the Patrol Zone Across Its Island]] and
[[Each Island Keeps One Patrol Zone, the One by Its Port City]]. Everything below is Claude's, made under that word,
and the owner may change any of it. How it works as built is in [[Passages]].

## Why there are passages

Claude's reading of the lore already written, shown and never told
([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]):

- **Saltmere is longer than its chart.** The passages are the island's uncharted ground, reached through doors that
  are found and never found again: the Pearl Tomb "longer than the headland it is cut into", the Tithe's Grey Point
  "Longer than the chart. Under review.", the lens carried "out of a tomb in the cliffs whose door she never found
  again", the Tithe's book with "pages for places that are not on any chart". Their ways change every week, as the
  island goes on being rewritten; nothing says why.
- **The Grey Tithe already walk and count them**, as the reckoner works in the Chalk Adit: their foes hold the
  passages. The Tithe did not make them, and no one says who did.
- **The return structure is a lamp that burns on nothing**, as the Pearl Tomb's lamps do ("Somebody is still keeping
  them"), set high on a plinth of the grey-green stone no quarry on Saltmere gives (the Old Stones' and the Old
  Beacon's), in a ring of five standing stones. Cold, it is grey; lit, it shows gold, and "gold is for home". Touching
  it, the player is home, unexplained, as their arrival is ([[Players Arrive as Destiny's Transmat Does, Unexplained]]).
- **Nothing new is named**: a zone is called by what it is ("A chalk hollow", "A stone-lined way"), or, where it lies
  under a place already named, by that place.

## The passage between zones

Through a mouth, the character turns to it and walks into it, the camera following as on foot; the camera then rises
off them over 1.5 s into the island seen whole, as over its side; the way taken so far shows as dots, and the way
being taken is drawn a dot at a time over 2 s from one zone's place to the next; the room changes behind it, and the
way holds over the island until the new zone is drawn whole. Above ground the camera comes down over 1.5 s from a low
framing over the new zone into the foot's own camera; under ground, where rock and ceilings stand over it, it cuts to
the foot's own. The character walks in over 1.5 s from where the room stands them, the foot's camera behind them kept
out of the walls. A lamp's Home plays the same, the dots running back to the patrol zone. A way that fails comes down
to the veil and takes the player back to the patrol zone, or to the hub, told why.

## The band, measured

Measured by `gear::band` against an hour of the patrol zone at the same level, its times from one scripted player in
the rooms, a dungeon walked as a passage zone under ground is: a patrol garrison's foe 12 s, a patrol dungeon's 28 s
and an Elite Dungeon's 27 s, their Minibosses and Bosses apart (`room::band_measure_tests`); a passage zone's deal
played to done about 202 s over about six foes, 38 s a foe, in 43 of 45 zones (`room::deal_measure_tests`); the day's
and the week's rewards spread over 14 hours a week; the haul's losses to death from 5 in a hundred at depth 1 to 25
at depth 20. The two zones not done are each a foe that climbed a hollow's cliff ([[Passages]], "Limits").

Each zone rolls only on the character's first cache of it in a week, so it stays rare at the top level. It is in the haul until a lamp is lit, so a death loses it |
| Its level | The least that harms every foe of depth 20 (foes 27 to 30, `power::gap` giving nothing from four below): below it the next lamp cannot be reached, so a lantern found would always be lost |
| Its words | "Cold, and heavier than it looks. Something in it still wants to burn." |
| Its steps | After Destiny 1's exotic bounties, in the Journal's bounties: bring it to a lamp; kindle five lamps gone out (Kindle missions on Saltmere); bring down 100 foes with a thunderlock in Saltmere's passages; kindle Saltmere's fourth lamp, which stands cold for the holder: once its guard is down the Tithe come in waves every 30 s while they hold E 3 s at it, as at a Kindle's dead lamp. The last step done, Lanternfall is kept as found |
