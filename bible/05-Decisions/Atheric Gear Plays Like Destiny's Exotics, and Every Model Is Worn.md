# Atheric Gear Plays Like Destiny's Exotics, and Every Model Is Worn

> "So weve been making a lot of headway in our game. I wanna do a detailed pass on weapon perks, ship perks, atheric
> weapons (to make them more like exotic weapons from destiny) and just ensure all weapon and armor models are being
> utilized"

> Asked how far Atheric weapons go toward Destiny's exotics (two perks of their own and a look, as Destiny 1's; one
> perk that changes play; or catalysts as Destiny 2's): "#1 For example a bow could have a perk that makes lighting
> travel between foes"

> Asked how many Atheric weapons there should be: "Rework the 5, add ~5 more"

> Asked whether named pieces each wear their own metal or set, with rolled pieces spread across every model of their
> rarity's tier: "Own look each, tiers spread"

> Asked which the perk pass includes: "Merge ship duplicates, Trade-off perks, Atheric ship modules as exotics"

## What it settles

- **Atheric weapons follow Destiny 1's exotics**: each has two perks of its own that change how it plays, not a share
  more of a number, and a look of its own. A bow whose lightning leaps between foes is the owner's example.
- **About ten Atheric weapons**: the five there are, reworked, and about five more.
- **Every weapon and armour model the forge draws is worn**: each named piece has its own look, and rolled pieces are
  spread across every model of their rarity's tier.
- **The perk pass**: the ship's near-duplicate perks merged; perks that give and take, for weapons and ships; and the
  Atheric ship modules made exotics.
- Everything else of the pass is Claude's, under [[Gear as Claude Shapes It]] and
  [[The Ship's Power, Crew and Islands as Claude Shapes Them]]: [[The Perk Pass as Claude Shapes It]].
