# Voyages Choose a Difficulty, Overlook Lives Like the Tower

> Told Claude's calls for the gear loop (foe levels by island; where the stall and vault stand in Overlook; where
> resource nodes stand in each patrol area; the perks' numbers): "Your calls, my changes:
>
> 1. Foes level by island, but the player can select a difficulty to partake in when setting on a voyage which
> influences drops and XP, but affects scaling.
>
> 1. The overlook should be extremely detailed where things are placed, proper vendor stations, npcs, lively. It
> really should reflect the destiny tower
> 3. Resource nodes should have multiple spots to spawn in, that way players can plan amd farm routes. Patrol areas
> in general should be extremely detailed.
> 4. Agreed. This is your baby"

## What it settles

- **Foes are levelled by island.** Setting out on a voyage, the player chooses a difficulty to take part in: a harder
  one gives better drops and more XP, and scales the foes up.
- **Overlook's social space is extremely detailed**, with things placed with care, proper vendor stations, people
  and life about it. It reflects Destiny's Tower.
- **Resource nodes spawn at several spots each**, so players can plan farming routes.
- **Patrol areas in general are extremely detailed.**
- **The perks' numbers are Claude's** ([[Gear as Claude Shapes It]]).
