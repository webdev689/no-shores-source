# Armour, Cloaks and Clothes Are Forged Large for the Gear Screens

> "Can we also update the armor and cloak to utilize proper icons for the inventory, like we did with weapons"

Shown steel armour and both cloaks drawn large and alone in four views (from the front, three quarters, three quarters
from behind and from behind), each beside how it reads shrunk into a gear tile, the owner chose:

- Asked which angle armour is seen from: "Front", square from the front, as the weapons are seen.
- Asked which way a cloak's icon faces: "Front", its collar, clasp and lining toward the eye, the cloak falling behind.
- Asked how a pair of gauntlets, worn at the hips and so far apart, is shown: "Side by side", both gauntlets moved
  close together.
- Asked whether clothes (the shirts, tunics, breeches and shoes a Normal and some Magic pieces are worn as) get large
  icons too, or only the armour sets and cloaks: "Clothes too".

## What it settles

- Armour, cloaks and clothes are drawn as the weapons are
  ([[Weapons Are Forged Large for the Weapon Rows and Gear Screens]]): rendered again by the [[Sprite Forge]] alone and
  larger than the figure's layers, for the gear screens and the item pages ([[Interface]], "Pictures").
- Armour is seen from the front, and so is a cloak, its collar, clasp and lining toward the eye.
- A pair of gauntlets is both gauntlets side by side.
- Every armour set's pieces, both cloaks and the clothes the gear wears get large pictures.

## Claude's calls

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How it is seen | Worn on the body standing (`idle`), the body left out, looking down 8 degrees as the figure's strips do | As the previews the owner chose from were drawn, so each piece hangs and is lit as it is worn |
| Size | The weapons' four times the body's pixels a metre | One scale for every large picture |
| A slot's pieces | One picture, joined by depth: a torso's cuirass with its pauldrons, legs' greaves and boots, a garment with its drape, a shirt with what goes over it | A slot is worn as one item |
| The padding under armour | Left out | It is the body's suit, not the piece |
| Gauntlets side by side | 6 texels apart, as worn in height | As in the preview's "Arms, together" row |
| Which bodies | The man's and the woman's, each drawn on the player's own | The forge fits each set and garment to each body |
| Which clothes | Each garment look an item can wear alone: 18, and the two cloaks | An item's number picks each garment, so only those pairs are worn |
| Colours | The figure's own grades and dye greys; the page lays the same metal, dye, faction and worn-dye ramps on them and snaps the result to the palette, as for weapons | So a picture matches the piece worn |
| Before they are published | The figure's cut-out, as before | Nothing breaks before the forge publishes |
