# The Intro Goes Straight to Saltmere

After playing the [[Prologue]] live:

> "The beginning intro between cities is too slow, I wanna try something different"

Asked what the intro should become (quick cuts between the four islands, one flight over the sea, or straight to
Saltmere), the owner chose "Straight to Saltmere": first light over the cloud, then down through it to Saltmere's
harbour and the title, about 25 s.

## What it settles

- The intro no longer flies to the other islands. It opens at first light over the cloud, goes down through it to
  Saltmere, and ends on the title and the prompt to begin, as before.
- It replaces the flight "to each island" in [[The Game Opens With a Story Mission at Sea]]; the rest of that
  decision stands.
