# A Ship's Level Raises Her Strength, and the Tiers Reach the Sea

> Told that a hostile ship's level (her water's band, 1 to 30) only changed her drops, so a level-27 Minor fought
> exactly as a level-4 Minor, and asked whether her level should raise her strength as land foes are raised by
> level: "Yes, like land foes"

> Told that the difficulty tiers (Normal, Elite, Abyss) applied to nodes on land but never reached the sea, and asked
> whether they should: "Raise levels and drops"

## What it settles

- A hostile ship's level raises her strength at sea (her hull, crew and gunnery) the way a foe's level raises it on
  land ([[Naval Combat]], [[Foes]]).
- At sea, the Elite and Abyss tiers raise the ships' levels and their drops as they do on land; they do not change
  which ranks come ([[Gear]]).
