# Melee Swings a Quarter Faster, Strikes All in Reach, and the Lock Lets Go Past 70°

> "3. When looking away from a locked on foe, you shouldnt stay locked on
> 4. Melee weapons base attack speed needs to be increased. Plus melee weapons should be able to hit multiple enemies
> if others are close enough"

Asked how much faster, the owner chose "A quarter faster": swings take 75% as long. Asked how melee should hit foes
standing close, the owner chose "Every stroke, all in reach": no cap, each stroke strikes every foe within its reach
and arc, at full damage. Asked when the lock should let go, the owner chose "Past 70° at once".

## What it settles

- Every melee weapon swings in three quarters of the time it took, each keeping its place against the others
  ([[Combat]]).
- Each stroke strikes every fighter of another side within its reach and arc, each taking the full blow; a stroke's
  arc still sets how many it can reach.
- The soft lock lets go the moment the fighter's look leaves the 70 degrees within which it takes a foe.
