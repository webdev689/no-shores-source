# Garrisons Take the Gear Loop's Levels and Shields

> "Also ensure yhe other PR's work fits into this. They implemented an enemy shield mechanic"

> Told that the gear loop's pull request stands six camps of levelled foes in each patrol node, one in five
> shielded, while this one stands Grey Tithe garrisons by rank in every node, and asked how they should combine:
> "Garrisons, levelled and shielded (Recommended)"

> Asked how a foe's rank and its island's level should work together: "Level sets power, rank multiplies
> (Recommended)"

> Asked whether the gear loop merges first, with the enemy system carrying it on top: "#60 first, #61 on top
> (Recommended)"

> Asked whether a Boss or a Miniboss may carry a shield too: "Not Bosses or Minibosses"; and whether rank should lift
> a foe's level: "Drawn evenly, as built (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- The Grey Tithe's garrisons stand in every node, as [[Enemies]] says. They take the gear loop's island levels, raised
  by the seas; one foe in five carries an elemental shield
  ([[Weapons Carry Blaze, Volt or Cosmic, and Foes Carry Shields]]); and a kill pays XP, Aurem and Vestiges as the gear
  loop's camps did ([[Gear]]). The camps give way.
- A foe's island level sets its power against a player's gear; its rank still scales its health, poise, might,
  defence and telegraphs.
- The gear loop's pull request merges first; the enemy system's carries it and merges after.
- A Boss or a Miniboss never carries a shield; a Minor, Major or Elite garrison foe may, one in five.
- A garrison foe's level is drawn evenly from its island's band, whatever its rank.
