# The Swing's Arc Never Covers Its Striker

> "I notice the seperate attack visual overlaps the entire player even though it shouldnt"

## What it settles

- The swing's arc (the attack feel's, in the [[Game]] and the [[Test Ground]]) stands where its blade goes. Its own
  striker's figure hides what passes behind it, as walls and other figures do, so from behind an overhead, a slant or
  a thrust shows only round its striker.
- It replaces Claude's earlier choice of drawing the arc over its own striker, in
  [[Arts and Loadouts as Claude Shapes Them]].
- The smear a strike leaves behind its striker stays as it is.
