# Where You Go and the Seas You Choose Set the Difficulty

> "Legendary materials should come from harder content. In destiny I believe these were really hard strikes, so these
> could be really hard dungeons here. So difficulty levels that correspond to prestige"

Asked whether a dungeon's difficulty comes from where it stands or from a tier the player chooses:

> "I like chosen tier, but with the new sea changes, certain map areas are tied to difficulty"

Offered the area as the base (each island and its waters keeps its level band) and the seas chosen when setting out
(Calm, Rough, Storm) as the tier; the Prestige tiers in the areas past level 20; Legendary materials from Rough and
Storm dungeons there, on land and on the sea's islands; and Storm with Nightfall's rules, limited revives and a wipe
ending the run:

> "I actually love this. Plus foes being 2 levels above should be a thing, no?"

## What it settles

- **The area sets the base level**: each island and its waters keeps its band, 1 to 4 up to 26 to 30
  ([[Gear]]).
- **The seas chosen set the tier**: Calm as the band, Rough two levels over it, Storm three, each paying more.
- **Past level 20**, in islands 7 to 9 and their waters, Rough and Storm dungeons drop the Legendary materials.
- **Storm has Nightfall's rules**: limited revives, and a wipe ends the run.
- **Foes stand up to two levels over the highest player**, to 32, as Destiny 1's hardest raid did ([[Gear]]).

The weather each brings: [[The Seas Chosen Bring Their Weather, and a Storm Is the Mystery's]].

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
