# Friendly Ships Fly the Three Houses' Colours, and Travel Keeps the Hostile Clock

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on the sea.

Asked which port faction each friendly ship belongs to, the owner answered:

> "The three houses' colours"

Asked whether logging back in at sea should keep the voyage's tier (Normal now), the owner answered:

> "remove the elite and abyss tiers for now"

Asked whether that meant the seas alone or every place with tiers, the owner answered:

> "The seas only"

Asked whether a sea island and its dungeon, which took their tier from their sea, should lose Elite and Abyss with it,
or whether sea dungeons should keep them, the owner answered:

> "Keep their tiers"

Asked whether a shipless player may join a companion where only the sea leads, the owner answered:

> "Players can choose to travel with company members aboard their ship"

Asked about a Boss closing on a lone Sloop, which wins every such fight, and given Claude's opinion that a Boss should
stay unbeatable alone but a Sloop should be able to outsail it and escape, the owner answered:

> "Agree"

Asked whether the battery rose's red outline on the side a blow came from should come back on the ship's console, the
owner answered:

> "No"

Asked whether to limit travel, since each travel resets the hostile roll to 30 s and one who keeps travelling meets no
hostile ship, and given Claude's opinion that travel should stop resetting the roll rather than gain a cooldown, the
owner answered:

> "Agree"

Asked whether travel's small faults should be fixed (control a moment early for a run that opens in the wake's last
instant, a ship the room took off its route still drawn on it from land, a ruin's crystal kept clear in its 110 m
window), the owner answered:

> "Yes, fix them"

Asked whether the week's run should still be entered from anywhere at sea, the owner answered:

> "Requires you to be docked at port"

Asked whether a parked ship's plate should follow a username or guild tag changed while its player is ashore, the
owner answered:

> "Update them too"

Asked whether each hull's eye frames well in the sea fight's aim, the owner answered:

> "Aiming is in a better place, but outside the aim, it could still use some tweaking along with better design"

## What it settles

- **Each friendly ship sails for one of the three houses** and flies its colours ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]],
  [[Factions]]).
- **The seas lose their Elite and Abyss tiers for now**: every sea sails at Normal. Places on land with tiers keep
  them ([[Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them]]), and so do a sea island and its dungeon: each stands at
  the tier the voyage set out at, as a node landed at does, and asks the one sea, at Normal, of its island
  ([[Islands]]).
- **A player may travel aboard a company member's ship** ([[Shipyard]], "Limits").
- **A Boss still wins against a lone Sloop, but a Sloop can outsail it and escape** ([[Naval Combat]]).
- **The hit side's red outline stays off the ship's console.**
- **Travel no longer resets the hostile roll**: its clock keeps counting through a travel, so one who keeps travelling
  meets hostile ships as often as one who sails. Travel has no cooldown.
- **Travel's three small faults are fixed.**
- **The week's run is entered only with the ship docked at a port.**
- **A parked ship's plate follows its player's username and guild tag** as they change while ashore, as it follows
  their level ([[A Ship Docks in Sight, Undocks as Its Player Leaves, and Lies Parked Where Others See It]]).
- **The sea fight's view outside the aim wants tweaking and a better design**: a pass for later, its specifics the
  owner's ([[Open Questions]]).
