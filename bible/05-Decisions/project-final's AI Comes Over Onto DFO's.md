# project-final's AI Comes Over Onto DFO's

> "We ported enhanced ai from dfo, but that system was developed on project-finals navmesh, which im wondering would
> be better to import since it was also developed with destiny in mind, and propwrly adapting to our combat system."

Told that DFO's navmesh is project-final's, ported whole and fixed since, and that project-final's AI above it is what
DFO did not take, then asked to keep DFO's AI and add project-final's sensing, cover and firing spots, flanking and
director, plus the navmesh's terrain input: "Yes"

## What it settles

- DFO's Enhanced AI stays the brain of the [[Foes]], and the [[Navmesh]] stays as it is.
- project-final's AI comes over on top of it ([[Pick - project-final AI]]): its perception (sight and hearing, the
  last place a target was seen and the search for it, waking allies), its cover and firing spots worked out from the
  navmesh, its flanking, and its combat director.
- The navmesh takes project-final's ground heightfield, so it can be baked over the [[Land]].
