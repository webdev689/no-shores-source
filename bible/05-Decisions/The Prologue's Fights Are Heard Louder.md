# The Prologue's Fights Are Heard Louder

> "Combat audio is way too low in the intro mission"

## What it settles

- The prologue's fights are heard louder than they were: the sea fight's guns, hits, splashes and ships breaking up,
  and the wreck's blows on foot.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How much | Three times as loud, about 10 dB | The storm's gale, rain and thunder share the sea fight's sounds' bus and ran about 13 dB over fair weather; the wreck's fires share its blows' bus and sat at their level |
| Where | The story's two fights only; the open world's mix, the storm and the music as they were | The owner named the intro mission |
