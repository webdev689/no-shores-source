# Each Region Has Its Own Dungeons, and the Dungeons Go Deeper and Darker

> "1. Each of the region panels should recieve their own procedural dungeons with procedural names accounting for the
> player's progress either being normal, elite or abyss also randomly.
> 2. The dungeons within saltgrass should also do the same for the player wanting to enter. Give dungeons more of an
> outside design. Use blender if needed
> 3. The icons on panels + the hovering is good but there must be a better way to handle it along with giving the
> panels themselves a better border design. Remove the placeholder missions, outposts, and world bosses until we
> integrate them in a meaningful way
> 4. One of the dungeons designs doorways are disconnected from the roof leaving a large gap
> 5. Give the dungeon mission types a visual overhaul
> 6. I want to go deeper with dungeon designs, implementing even more variation, deeper dungeons, layouts you could get
> lost in, larger rooms, varied geometry, more horror elements, and so much more.
>
> I want you to take autonomy, and treat this like your baby. Go deep and be as detailed as possible and come back when
> this is 100% to completion"

Asked which part of the game "the dungeon mission types" meant, the owner chose "Both of those": the puzzle rooms
inside an Elite or Abyss dungeon (the rune order, the pressure plates, the flooded climb and the held ring), and how
each kind of dungeon is shown on the island's tiles and its card. Asked what the puzzle rooms' overhaul should look
like, the owner chose "Your call". Asked how a dungeon's tier is shown on its tile and its card, the owner chose
"Skulls by tier": one, two or three skulls beside the dungeon's name.

## What it settles

- **Each region of an island has dungeons of its own**, each named procedurally, met at Normal, Elite or Abyss drawn
  at random among the tiers the player's progress opens.
- **A patrol zone's dungeons do the same**: each is met at a tier drawn for the player wanting to enter.
- **A dungeon's entrance has a design outside**, above ground; Blender may build it.
- **The island's tiles keep their icons and hover, handled better**, in a better border; the missions, outposts and
  world bosses are taken off the tiles until they are integrated in a meaningful way.
- **No doorway stands apart from the roof over it.**
- **The puzzle rooms are overhauled in looks**, the look Claude's call; **a dungeon's tier shows as skulls** beside its
  name, one Normal, two Elite, three Abyss.
- **The dungeons go deeper**: more variation, more storeys, layouts a player can get lost in, larger rooms, varied
  geometry, more horror.
- **Claude builds it on its own autonomy**, its choices recorded in [[The Dungeon Overhaul as Claude Builds It]], each
  the owner's to change.
