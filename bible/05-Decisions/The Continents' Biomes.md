# The Continents' Biomes

> Asked which biome each continent takes: "You choose the biomes"

## What it settles

Claude chose one biome for each continent ([[Each Continent Is One Biome]]); the owner can change any of them. Each
starts from one of World 2.0's ten climate profiles ([[The World Map]]: "Start from DFO's climates"), named in our
own words. The colder biomes lie in the north of the frame, the hotter in the south, and Saltmere takes the mildest,
as the most welcoming place in the region.

| Continent | Where | Area (km²) | Biome | Starts from (World 2.0 profile: mean temperature, rain) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, Saltmere | Centre | 418,536 | `temperate-coast` | Iliac Bay: 13 °C, 900 mm |
| 2 | North-west | 607,108 | `highland` | High Rock: 11 °C, 1,100 mm |
| 3 | North, east of centre | 189,176 | `woodland` | Imperial: 15 °C, 950 mm |
| 4 | South-east | 413,480 | `desert` | Hammerfell: 22 °C, 280 mm |
| 5 | South | 204,253 | `savanna` | Elsweyr: 21 °C, 420 mm, wetter to the south |
| 6 | South-west | 188,277 | `rainforest` | Valenwood: 22 °C, 1,900 mm |
| 7 | North-east | 350,682 | `steppe` | Morrowind: 14 °C, 640 mm, rugged |
| 8 | East | 771,600 | `swamp` | Black Marsh: 21 °C, 2,100 mm |
| 9 | Far north | 358,984 | `tundra` | Skyrim: 2 °C, 650 mm |

Summerset's profile (19 °C, 1,000 mm) is the one not used. Each continent's biome is its `climate` in
`content/world/layout.json`. The profiles' names stay in DFO; only their numbers come over.
