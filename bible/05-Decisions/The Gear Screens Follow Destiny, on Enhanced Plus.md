# The Gear Screens Follow Destiny, on Enhanced Plus

> "I know its early but I do have a vision for where I want to ui to be."

> "For example, Destiny is still the reference and the enhanced plus UI in DFO already offers a fully fleshed out UI.
> Like icons and their colors, different sub UIs. We dont need to invent much from scratch."

> "On the character page. You should still in your sprite in the middle with equipment and weapons"

> "The slots should show the visible icons and when you hover over a slot you should identified gear. 9 slots per
> slot. On the inventory page, it should be grid based with currency as a header, with filter tabs to switch between
> consumables (like the fruit), materials, vestiges, etc"

> "The weapon page should overlay when inspecting a slot and should have an enlarged sprite visual."

> "Like it doesnt need to be 1:1. We use it to achieve our vision, but you get the idea. I just really want us to be
> as detailed as possible and really utilize the enhanced plus ui to its potentiol"

The owner sent five pictures with it: Destiny 2's consumables grid with an item's card over it, its character screen
with a helmet's card, a redrawn character screen, an armour piece's card with Equip, Details and Dismantle, and
Destiny 1's weapon page with the weapon drawn large over its stats and node grid.

## What it settles

- **Destiny is the reference, and DFO's Enhanced Plus is what it is built from**: its icons and their colours and
  its sub-screens, not invented anew. It need not be one to one.
- **The character page** shows the player's own figure in the middle, their weapons and equipment in slots round it.
- **Each slot shows its item's icon.** Hovering over a slot shows that item, identified. Each slot holds nine.
- **The inventory page is a grid**, the currencies as its header, with tabs to switch between consumables (the Fruit
  of Dagon among them), materials, Vestiges and the rest.
- **Inspecting a slot's item** opens its page over the screen, with the item drawn large.
- As detailed as it can be, using Enhanced Plus to the full ([[Interface]], [[Pick - Enhanced Plus UI]]).
