## What it settles

- **A zone's land varies in height**, instead of flat floors joined by canyon lanes round circular basins: areas on
  platforms joined by ramps, stairs and bridges, and ways that wind and lead deeper.
- **Places above and below ground**: enclosed tunnels and caverns under rock, and open sunken pits, ravines and courts.
- **Props such as gateways connect** to what they stand in, instead of standing on their own.
- **The generator is built to serve other systems**, future raids among them.
- **A zone's areas, ways and gates may be re-planned**; each zone keeps its brief. This replaces the limit in
  [[Patrol Zones Stay as Built and Gain Linear Combat Areas]] and [[Saltmere's Detail Pass Goes Deep]] that kept a
  zone's areas, paths and gates as built.
- **Saltmere's three zones first**, built and pictured deeply for the owner to look at; the other 24 zones take the
  generator without per-zone tuning until then.
- **The design is approved as asked**: areas on heights shaped by their kind, ways that switch back, climb, bridge
  and tunnel between them, gates set in walls or clefts, and a map of each zone's spaces and connections.
- **Foes fight in tunnels and caverns too**: the [[Navmesh]] learns floors under roofs in this pass.
- **The Gullwing Vale is built first, end to end**, as the showcase, with pictures and a fly-through for the owner to
  judge, before the Saltgrass Downs and the Driftwood Moors.
- **The land gains real 3D rock**: cave ceilings, overhanging cliffs, natural arches and tunnel mouths, lit and
  collided like the rest, where the ground's one height a point cannot reach.
- **A hamlet's blocks step up on terraces** where the brief asks for heights: its blocks are laid with lanes wide enough
  for a ramp, and each block uphill stands on its own terrace with a ramp up from the plaza.
