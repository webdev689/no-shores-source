# The Houses' Roads as Claude Shapes Them

The owner handed the roads over: "Go all out for this and dont come back until this is 100%". What the owner decided
is in [[Each House Has Its Own Road to Its Art]]. Everything below is Claude's, and the owner may change any of it. The
roads live in `content/quests/quests.json` and their lines in `content/story/houses.json`; how the rules judge them is
in [[Quests]].

## The opening

| Call | Choice |
|---|---|
| When | Once Elder Maren Hale's welcome is played or forgone, a player pledged to no house is on "A House of Your Own" |
| Its tasks | Hear the three houses (speak with each keeper in the Hall of the Arts, each saying what their house is in two lines), then choose a house (pledge at a chapel) |
| Its first line | Arlo Kestrel calls across the Hall: "You'll hear all three of us. Start with the loudest. Me." |
| The keepers | The Stormcallers' keeper is **Arlo Kestrel**; the Veiled's gives only **Sable**; the Lamplighters' is Elder Maren Hale ([[Elder Maren Hale Welcomes the Player to Overlook]]). This answers the open question of their names |
| A pledge begins the road | Pledging starts that house's road at once, its first lines spoken at the chapel |

## Every road

| Call | Choice |
|---|---|
| Shape | Ten tasks in three acts. Act one in one zone gives the Art; act two crosses a second zone, the sea and an island, and gives the first Tenet and the first fragment; act three crosses the third zone and an instance, and gives the second Tenet and two fragments, the last back at the chapel |
| The Art | Given at the end of the third task, with its Anima, so most of the road is walked with it: Tempest, Sunwell or Duskraven |
| The Tenets | One at the end of the fourth task, one at the end of the eighth, each taught by the keeper as the story needs it |
| Fragments | Three, each chosen by the player from the Art's six: after the island, after the instance, and at the road's end. The rest are earned by raising the Art, or bought, as before ([[Factions]]). A choice the Art has no fragment left for pays the fragment's price at the counter, 7,500 Aurem, instead |
| Until it is earned | No Art is worn before the road gives one; an Art, a Tenet or a Core slot's fragment not yet earned shows locked, saying which road earns it |
| Characters made before | Keep every Art and Tenet they wear today; their road gives its fragments all the same |
| Switching | A switch makes the new house's road the one walked. Every road keeps where it was, and what a road gave is kept |
| In a group | Each player's road is their own. A foe counts for every player who struck it, as a kill's reward does |
| The way round | Each road goes round the island once: the Saltgrass Downs first, then the Moors and the Vale in the order its instance asks, the sea and the island in the Cairn Straits between them; about 2,370 km of sailing |
| Lines in a row | A task's closing lines and the next task's opening ones are heard as one, so they keep the rule of two lines a speaker in a row across the join ([[Dialogue]]) |
| Speaking from afar | Each house speaks to its own across the island: the Stormcallers on the wind, the Lamplighters by their lamps, the Veiled in a whisper. So a keeper's lines are heard in the field |
| Foes for the road | A task that asks for foes at a place stands them there for the player as they come near, the island's Tithe or pirates at its levels; others nearby may fight them too |
| The sea and the islands | The sea's tasks ask for a ship beaten, or boarded, in hostile water (the Cairn Straits is the nearest); the island's for an isle or a cove landed on and searched |
| The instances | Each road ends in a different one: the Stormcallers' at the Maw of the Shallows against High Assessor Corvane, the Lamplighters' in the Pearl Tomb, the Veiled's in Kraken's Grotto |

## The Stormcallers: The Stolen Storm

The Tithe took the Stormcallers' storm-glass from the Old Beacon on the Saltgrass Downs; Pell, a young Stormcaller, went
after it. The player searches the Tithe Barns while Tithe Camp fights, gathers Brineglass, and
holds the glass on the High Downs until the storm finds them: **Tempest**. Pell's voice comes on the wind, and Grey
Point Light on the Driftwood Moors burns with lightning; his tracks run beside Tithe boots (**Quicken**) to their camp,
whose tally names a ship carrying the lightning rods. A Tithe ship at sea, and the isle where half the rods lie (a
fragment). The rest rode up the Gullwing Vale's river road with Pell: the player runs it from Weir Mill under Arlo's
storm and catches the riders at the Water Meadows, where the twist comes: Pell lit Grey Point for the Tithe, who
promised to forgive his family's tenth, and High Assessor Corvane means to sell the storm back (**Arc Lash**). The rods
round the Old Stones are struck as the storm walks; the road ends against Corvane in the Maw of the Shallows, and in the
Hall with Pell mending the racks.

## The Lamplighters: The Keeper's Lamp

Someone stole the lens from Overlook's Beacon, and false lights draw ships onto the rocks. At Shingle Landing the player
finds Tobin, a ship's boy who lived, and brings him to Ewe Fold past the Barrow Field, wreckers springing on them
halfway, then douses the false lights there: **Sunwell**. The wreckers' purses hold Tithe silver paid out at Gullwing in
the Vale, and the Tithe beat Gullwing for seeing it (**Mend**); the player holds the hamlet. The wreckers' ship at sea,
their hoard in a cove but no lens (a fragment), and at the Saltbreaker Hulks on the Moors the twist: a Reeve says the
Tithe bought the lens fair (**Dawnflare**). The player sets Grey Point Light true, and its light finds the Pearl Tomb's
door; the road ends there with the Reeve's master, and the lens lit again on the Beacon.

## The Veiled: What the Tithe Writes

The Tithe wrote the player's name in its book before they washed ashore. Sable wants the page. The player tails a
collector through Saltgrass to Chalkpit Ruin, lifts his tallies (each with their name and a sum), and reaches the Chalk
Adit unseen by its watch: **Duskraven**. The reckoner who keeps the book has gone to Moorhold on the Moors (**Dusk
Daggers**), then to sea; a Tithe ship boarded, and his isle holds only his ledger in a cipher (a fragment). Wren, a
Veiled runner, meets the player at Vale Mouth and walks them through Orchard Farm into the Hollow Coven, where she reads
the twist: half the debt is paid already, by someone in Overlook (**Vanish**). The reckoner's chest at Valeguard opens
by his marks and points to Kraken's Grotto, where the road ends with the book, and Sable burning the page unread. Who
paid half stays a mystery.

## The varied steps

The owner asked for missions that are more than simple tasks ([[Missions Are Varied, Not Only Simple Tasks]]). Six
kinds came, each a goal any later mission may use ([[Quests]]), and each road took them in its house's way:

| Kind | What it asks | Where the roads use it |
|---|---|---|
| Escort (replaced in part: the figure rides at a gallop and the player keeps within 30 m, [[Keepers Give Gifts, the House Arts Come With a Horde, Escorts Ride, and the Horse and Ship Are Taught]]) | Meet a figure, who greets the player, and keep within 15 m as it walks; it waits while the player lags and while an ambush stands | The Lamplighters bring Tobin from Shingle Landing to Ewe Fold past the Barrow Field, wreckers springing halfway; the Veiled meet Wren at Vale Mouth and follow her through Orchard Farm into the Hollow Coven (the second Tenet comes there) |
| Tail | Follow a figure between 10 and 40 m; nearer, it notices and the tail starts again | The Veiled follow a Tithe collector from Saltgrass to Chalkpit Ruin, their first step |
| Defend | Keep a ward standing through two waves; if it falls, try again | The Stormcallers hold the storm-glass on the High Downs (the Art comes as the storm finds it); the Lamplighters hold Gullwing's wounded against the Tithe, mending their own wounds. The second waves differ |
| Puzzle | Use points round a place in the order a line gives (a wrong one's line says the whole order again), or with no order, each once | In order: the Stormcallers strike the Old Stones' rods (west, north, east, south: Pell's lines); the Lamplighters set Grey Point's lamps (red, white, green, gold: Maren's); the Veiled open the reckoner's chest at Valeguard (tithe, debt, interest, fee: Wren's). In any order: the Stormcallers search the Tithe Barns, the Lamplighters douse the Barrow Field's false lights (the Art comes there), the Veiled lift the tallies in Chalkpit Ruin |
| Unseen | Come within 8 m of a place's middle with no foe within 60 m of it alert on the player; its watch stands round the middle facing outward, so the player slips between them | The Veiled reach the Chalk Adit past its watch (the Art comes there) |
| Race | Pass checkpoints in order within a time, run over the ground as the player likes, not along laid paths | The Stormcallers run the river road from Weir Mill by Gullwing to the Water Meadows in 270 s under Arlo's storm |

Each road is twelve tasks, a horde after its Art's ([[The Welcome's Gifts, the Hordes, the Ride and the Lessons as Claude Builds Them]]): the puzzle in order comes before its instance. A failed step says its own line (the glass broken,
the riders gone, seen, noticed, a wrong rod, stone or mark) and is tried again: a defence or an escort after 10 s, an
unseen step once no foe has been on the player for 10 s, a race from its first checkpoint, a puzzle from its first
point. Every task has an `id`, and the save keeps each road and task by id, so a mission added anywhere keeps every
player's place. A figure, a ward and a puzzle's points are the player's own: other players in the room do not see them.

Replaced in part by [[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]: the foes a road stands are the island's Grey Tithe.
