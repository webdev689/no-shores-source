# Each Account Keeps Four Characters, Chosen on a Screen After Destiny 1's

> "Implement 4 character slots a person can save to, along with a switch character option in the ingame settings
> menu. Please be really detailed with the character UI"

> Asked how the character screen is laid out, the owner chose "Destiny 1 style": "The chosen character's figure stands
> large in the centre; four emblem banners stacked on the right; Play under them."

> Asked what one account's four characters share beyond the account itself, the owner chose "Vault and Aurem, as D1":
> "Destiny 1's way: the vault and Aurem are shared by all four; everything else is the character's own."

## What it settles

- **An account keeps four characters**, each in a slot of its own.
- **A character is chosen on a screen after Destiny 1's**: the chosen character standing large in the centre, the four
  slots as emblem banners down the right, Play under them.
- **Settings has Switch character**, which leaves the game for that screen.
- **The vault and Aurem are the account's**, shared by all four; the rest of a character (its gear, materials, ships,
  house, story and quests) is its own.
