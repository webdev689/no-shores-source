# Exposed, Charged and Haste Make No Sound

The owner, on the sound sheet's fourth pass, for Exposed, Charged and Haste: "No sound needed"

## What it settles

- A foe Exposed or Charged, and Haste given, make no sound in the world; their looks and icons stay as they are
  ([[Arts]]). Linked and Veiled keep a sound each ([[The World Sounds From the Sound Picker]]).
