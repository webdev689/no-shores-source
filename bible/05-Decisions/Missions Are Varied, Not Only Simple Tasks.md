# Missions Are Varied, Not Only Simple Tasks

> "Lets do a comprehensive audit on this and push it further. This is the foundations for how future missions will be
> laid and needs to be perfect and varied.
>
> Along with simple tasks, we need yo implement varied functionality for mission types. Maybe a faction member is
> somebody you need to meet up with and follow, maybe you have to defend something, or maybe you have to take on a
> easy puzzle. The possibilities are endless"

## What it settles

- **The houses' roads are the foundation every later mission is laid on** ([[Each House Has Its Own Road to Its Art]],
  [[Quests]]), so the system is audited throughout and made to last.
- **Missions are varied.** Beside simple tasks, a step may be meeting a house's member and following them, defending
  something, or an easy puzzle, and the system takes more kinds as they come.
- The kinds, and how each road uses them, are Claude's, the owner's to change:
  [[The Houses' Roads as Claude Shapes Them]].
