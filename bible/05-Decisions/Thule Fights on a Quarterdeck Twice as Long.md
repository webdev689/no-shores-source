# Thule Fights on a Quarterdeck Twice as Long

> "3. In the prolouge, the main boss should spawn in a larger arena. Currently it happens on a small helm deck. Using
> blender, expand the helm area"

Asked how big, the owner chose "About 10 × 20 m": the quarterdeck runs forward toward the mainmast, twice the room,
still inside the Frigate's hull.

## What it settles

- The wreck's arena, the Frigate's quarterdeck where Captain Brannoc Thule is fought, is built in Blender about 10 m
  across and 20 m long ([[Prologue]]).
