# Pixelify Sans Is Overlook's Font

> "Lets go ahead and decide on our official font"

Shown the gear screens set in four ways, the owner chose: "A: Pixelify Sans everywhere", which was offered as
"Enhanced Plus's own face on every word, as DFO does, with DFO's fixed 5, 2 and Z". Choosing it granted the face a
named exception to [[Own Work]].

## What it settles

- **Pixelify Sans is the game's one face**, for every word on every screen, as DFO's Enhanced Plus sets it.
- **DFO's fixed digits come with it**: Silkscreen's 5 in place of Pixelify's, which reads as an 8, and DFO's own 2
  and Z, which no longer read as each other.
- **Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen's 5 are exceptions to [[Own Work]]**. Both are under the SIL Open Font Licence,
  and both are served with the page, not fetched from another site.
