# Pixelify Sans Is Overlook's Font

> "Lets go ahead and decide on our official font"

Shown the gear screens set in four ways and asked "Which typeface should be Overlook's official font? (The two
comparison sheets I just sent show each option on the real gear screens.) Choosing A, B or C also grants that font a
named exception to the Own Work rule. It would ship with the page under its free licence (OFL), with no request to
Google.", the owner chose: "A: Pixelify Sans everywhere", which was offered as "Enhanced Plus's own face on every
word, as DFO does, with DFO's fixed 5, 2 and Z."

## What it settles

- **Pixelify Sans is the game's one face**, for every word.
- **DFO's fixed digits come with it**: Silkscreen's 5 in place of Pixelify's, which reads as an 8, and DFO's own 2
  and Z, so neither is taken for the other.
- **Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen's 5 are exceptions to [[Own Work]]**, shipped with the page under their free
  licence (the SIL Open Font Licence), with no request to Google.
