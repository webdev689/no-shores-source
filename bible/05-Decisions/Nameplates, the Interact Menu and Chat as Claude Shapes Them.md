# Nameplates, the Interact Menu and Chat as Claude Shapes Them

> "So for other players, above their head I want like the reference shows."

The owner asked for nameplates over other players ([[Other Players Wear a Nameplate, Their Level or Prestige in a
Square]]), a menu holding Use opens on a player ([[Holding Use on a Player Opens Their Interact Menu]]) and DFO's chat
cut to three channels ([[Chat Comes From DFO With Local, World and Whisper]]). Where Overlook must choose, the choice is
Claude's, with its reason. The owner can change any.

## What it settles

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Which plates show | Every other player within 60 m whose head the blocks and land do not hide, shrinking with distance, laid apart | DFO's law for names over players; the owner's pictures show several at once |
| The plate's colours | The square filled blue or green, the name in the same colour: the players' blue, the company's green | The owner's second picture colours the square and the name alike |
| A guest's plate | The square alone | A guest has no username to show |
| The three dots | A speech bubble with a tail, in the plate's colour, in the square's place | The owner's second picture |
| What counts as a menu | The pause menu, the menu's window (Character, Inventory, its tabs), and a player's menu or Inspect; not the held map or the chat | "If in a menu" |
| The interact mark | A thin diamond round the body, a chevron in each corner, and Use's key with "Interact" beside it, only where Use does nothing else | The owner's picture |
| Reach | 6.4 m from the player's feet, the crosshair on the body; a guest is never offered | DFO's reach for a person; the Roster asks by username |
| The hold | 0.45 s, with the menus' hold sound | DFO's hold for its player menu |
| The menu's rows | The five the owner named; one that cannot be done stands greyed with why; the friend row asks, accepts or cancels as the Roster would | The Roster's own actions ([[The Roster Follows Destiny 1, With Blocking]]) |
| Inspect | The Roster's Details page over the view | It is Overlook's Inspect: their nameplate, guild, power and worn pieces |
| Where the chat lives | One Durable Object in the accounts | The accounts know who a player is, where they play and whom they block |
| Who may speak | A player with a username; a guest only hears | A line names its speaker; the Roster's asks need a username too |
| Local's reach | Those in the same room, kept by the page within 60 m | DFO's: if you can read a name over a head, you hear them |
| A Whisper | `/w Name`, `/r` to answer; to a player who blocks the speaker, answered as sent | DFO had none; the Roster's block law |
| The chat's key | Enter, on foot | DFO's key; Enter does nothing else on foot |
| The panel | Bottom left, over the Arts; Tab turns Local and World, the arrows read back | Destiny's chat stands low at the left; DFO's tabs, cut to two |
| A Whisper's colour | `#ffaad4` | A hue of DFO's chat no channel kept here uses |
| Region's reach | Every ship in the same water of the same sea room, each line marked with the water's name; Local refused at sea, Region on land | The sea's waters are its regions ([[World Map]]); no figures stand at sea to be heard near |
| Region's colour | `#8fe28f` | DFO's Region green |
| How a mute works | One way, hiding only their chat lines and Whispers, a Whisper to a muter answered as sent; from the menu's Mute row, `/mute` and `/unmute`, and Settings' list of the muted; 200 at most | DFO's `/mute` was a moderator's, so a player's own mute is new: it hides less than a block, which stays the way to keep a player away; what is hidden follows the Roster's block law |
| Inspect's banner | The menu's own banner, the emblem's pattern carried across the window, their name and guild's tag, level, Prestige and Defense over it | The Character tab stands under that banner for the player's own character |
| Level and Prestige, side by side | Two framed plaques over the figure, each its name over a rule and its number large: the level in brass, Prestige in a pale steel; the meter to the next level under both | Past level 20 Prestige sets the level ([[Gear]]), so the two read as a pair |
| The Armour heading | Level with the Weapons heading, under room as tall as the Art's row, so each armour row stands level with a weapon's | [[The Weapons Heading Stands Under the Art]] moved the Weapons heading down a row |
| The gold of levels past 20 | The plates' own brass (`TONES.brassHi`), the Character tab's level colour | One gold for the level wherever it shows |
| What Inspect leaves out | Hints, the XP meter and Appearance; Back returns to their menu | What only the player's own character can do |
