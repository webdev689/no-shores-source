# Ships Have Their Own Power, Modules and Crew Who Fight Beside You

> "So this feeds into our unplanned ship maintence and upgrade system.
>
> I want ships to have their own prestige, attack and defense seperate from the character system. Ship modules can be
> found, attached, and leveled, just like the character elements, along with nodes and perks.
>
> Ships should all recieve crew management. Named figures that can be used as companions in the field with their own
> nodes, perks and xp gain. These can be recruited in port cities through your affiliated faction or plundered rarely
> from vessels."

> Asked what Claude thought of it, after Claude's research and proposals: "Take autonomy, build everything to 100 and
> let's make this something truely impressive."

## What it settles

- A ship has its own Prestige, Attack and Defense, apart from its captain's ([[Gear]], [[Naval Combat]]).
- Ship modules are found, attached and levelled, with nodes and perks, as the character's gear is.
- Every ship has crew to manage. Named crew fight beside the player as companions, with their own nodes, perks and
  experience. They are recruited in port cities through the player's faction, or taken rarely from vessels.
- It builds the ship as gear and crew as companions of [[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]], and brings
  back the ship's parts the owner had set aside. How it is built is Claude's
  ([[The Ship's Power, Crew and Islands as Claude Shapes Them]]).
