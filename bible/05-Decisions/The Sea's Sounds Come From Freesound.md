# The Sea's Sounds Come From Freesound

After playing the [[Prologue]] live:

> "I want to rework all the sea sound effects and such to be freesound instead of synth"

Asked which sea sounds should become Freesound recordings, picked by ear on the sound sheet, the owner chose all
four groups offered: "The open sea's sounds", "The story's sea sounds", "Sounds for the sea fight" and "The shore's
surf".

## What it settles

- **The open sea**: its swell, wash, wind, rigging, timbers, rain on the sea and on the deck, hull creaks and slaps,
  rope creaks, the bow's thump, flapping canvas and knocking blocks are made again from Freesound recordings. The
  rain at sea is among them, though [[The Weather's Gutters and Thunder Come From Freesound]] kept the synthesiser's
  rain; on the islands it stays as kept.
- **The story's sea sounds**: the prologue's waves, wind, timbers, the ram's crash, splashes, the deep, the wreck's
  fire and the rush through cloud.
- **The sea fight** gains sounds of its own from Freesound: cannon fire, shot striking a hull, shot falling in the sea,
  and a ship breaking up as she sinks.
- **The shore**: the surf at every island's edge and the water lapping in Saltmere's harbour.
- Each is picked by the owner on the sound sheet ([[Sounds Come From Freesound, Judged on a Test Sheet]]). Until a
  pick comes, the synthesiser's sound plays, and the sea fight's new sounds are silent.
