# A Lesson's Steps Tick in the Tracker Alone, Not at the Foot

> "Let's also improve the bottom quest notification popup (I love it), but reduce how much it spams."

Told that the welcome sends some 37 notices to the foot of the screen, one at a time and 4.5 to 6.5 s each, the riding
and sailing steps seconds apart so the notices fall a minute or more behind, and asked which changes would cut them,
the owner chose one: "Lesson steps tracker-only", the option that read: "Riding, sailing and gift-use steps (16
popups) only tick in the tracker. Their task's 'Step done' still pops, along with gifts, Arts and quest
acquired/done."

## What it settles

- A riding, sailing or gift-use step the room ticks is shown in the tracker alone, with no notice at the foot
  ([[Quests]], "The lessons").
- Every other notice stands as before: a task's "Step done", a keeper's gift, an Art, a Tenet or a fragment, a quest
  acquired or done, and the finest loot ([[Interface]], "Notices of what is obtained").
- This replaces the "Announcing each step" row of
  [[The Welcome's Gifts, the Hordes, the Ride and the Lessons as Claude Builds Them]].
