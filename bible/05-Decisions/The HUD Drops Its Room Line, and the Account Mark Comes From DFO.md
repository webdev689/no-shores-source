# The HUD Drops Its Room Line, and the Account Mark Comes From DFO

After the fourth audit the owner was asked:

1. "Should strangers see whether a player is "Playing" or "Away"?"
2. "Should blocking someone also remove them from a company or guild you share?"
3. "Should anyone be able to check whether a username exists?"
4. "Does a guild motto need any rules beyond one plain line?"
5. "Should Rare and Legendary colours be further apart? They look nearly the same."
6. "Should the HUD's "In the room as player N" and the key list fade once you've settled in?"
7. "Should joining be allowed from a fight at sea, or while you're dead?"
8. "Should the font's letter spacing at 11 px move to whole pixels? It lands on half pixels today."

The owner answered:

> "1. Yes
> 2. No
> 3. Yes
> 4. Definitely need guidelines/a system for account names, guild names, motto, etc
> 5. No
> 6. You can remove the in the room label. The social tab should show you any players in the room (game)
> 7. Best decision. Whatever other games do
> 8. Best decision. I think some text is too small. Especially on the character tab
>
> Also:
> 1. Take care of your left for later items
> 2. Implement the account glyph from DFO that will sit in the top right of the screen when the menu is up"

The left-for-later items were a scale for screens past 1440p, an item page's picture that keeps a weapon's grip, a glyph
of its own for each material, and Destiny 1's three-by-three gear shelf.

## What it settles

- **Strangers see whether a player is playing or away.**
- **A block leaves a shared company or guild as it is.**
- **Anyone may check whether a username exists.**
- **Names, guild names and mottos follow guidelines and a system**:
  [[Names Follow Guidelines, Checked by the Accounts]].
- **Rare and Legendary keep their colours.**
- **The HUD drops its "In the room" line**; the Social tab's Game column shows the players in the room.
- **Joining and the text sizes are Claude's to decide**, joining as other games do it.
- **The left-for-later items are built**, and **DFO's account mark stands at the top right while the menu is up.**

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Joining | Not from a fight at sea, nor while fallen; the player is told to go to them once it is over, or once they respawn | Most games keep travel out of a fight, and the fallen respawn before they go anywhere |
| Letter spacing | Each letter's and word's spacing rounded up to a whole pixel | At 11 px the 1/22 of a letter was half a pixel, which greys the strokes |
| Text sizes | On a wide screen, the Character page's labels, the banner's numbers and the footer's keys at 22 px | 22 px is the face's next sharp step; a narrow screen has no room for it |
| The scale past 1440p | The menu, its cards and the HUD's words at twice their size from 2400 by 1300 | The 1280 by 720 layout doubled keeps every size on the face's grid |
| The account mark | DFO's profile mark: a round well rimmed in brass, the player's face or a hooded silhouette, a gem filled for an account kept anywhere; pressed, it opens Settings | DFO's own mark, in the menu's top line beside the tabs or a station's name |
