# Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe

> "Enemy Factions:
>
> Each Enemy faction in the world has a presence. Sometimes both on land and sea, or just on sea, or just on land.
> This needs discussion"

> Asked how many factions to start with: "Grey Tithe is the first and as such we start with them. Others come later"

> Asked whether a faction's presence is fixed or shifts over time: "To be decided. Fixed for now"

> Asked what each kind of presence means: "I meant a faction could have a presence at sea/land or one of both. So the
> Grey Tithe enemy types on sea would be different from those on land"

> Asked whether factions fight each other where they meet: "They will"

> Asked whether enemy factions are separate from the factions a player joins in Overlook: "Yes"

> Asked whether project-final's crystal faction comes over as one of them: "Undecided. Possibly"

## What it settles

- Each enemy faction has a presence in the world: at sea, on land, or both.
- A faction's enemy types at sea are not its types on land.
- The Grey Tithe ([[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]]) is the first enemy faction, and the work starts with
  it; the others come later. It is present at sea and on land, with different enemy types in each.
- A faction's presence is fixed for now.
- Enemy factions fight each other.
- Enemy factions are separate from the factions a player joins in Overlook.
- Every enemy has a rank ([[Enemies Come in Five Ranks, After Destiny]]), and its name within its faction shows over
  its bar.

## Still open

- Whether a faction's presence shifts over time.
- Whether project-final's crystal faction comes over ([[Pick List - project-final]]).
- Where each faction is present, and what sets one faction apart from another.
