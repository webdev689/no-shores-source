# More Organisation, Less Bloat

> "We utilize it as a perfect reference and build a better, more modern quest system. More organization with less
> bloat. Thats the goal of this project"

## What it settles

- The goal of the whole project: everything that comes from DFO Online comes over better organised and smaller.
- DFO is a reference to improve on, not a shape to copy. A port that carries DFO's size or tangles over has missed.
- The [[Rules]] and checks exist to hold this line.
