# The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear

> Told that sailing still plays as DFO's fast travel, its hostile ships rolled close by and outrun in about two
> seconds at a voyage's pace, and asked what the sea is for: "Why don't you give me your suggestions and ill build on
> them. Sailing is the one thing that doesn't feel like it has a place in the new system"

> Offered waters with owners and levels (home waters round Overlook kept safe; the Grey Tithe's, the pirates', and
> contested waters where they meet; each shown on the held map): "Approved. I love this idea"

> Offered two ways to sail (the voyage as now, and free sailing at true scale at the helm in any waters), with ships
> sighted on the horizon several kilometres out, to chase, evade or let pass: "Approved"

> Offered things to do at sea (bounties from Overlook, public events, wrecks and flotsam to gather, and sea strikes
> against a faction's flagship): "Approved"

> Offered a fight that leads somewhere (sink it and gather what floats, or make it strike and board it for a fight
> on its deck; a player who loses washes up at the nearest port, and the damage stays until repaired): "Approved"

> Offered the ship as gear (its hull, guns, sails, and crew hired in Overlook, each with perks; ship power at sea;
> paint, sails and figurehead as cosmetics): "Approved. Crew also can be utilized as the new companion system which
> can be ported from DFO"

> Offered companies sailing as fleets, with no fighting between players at sea: "Approved"

> Offered being the pirate (attacking merchantmen for their cargo, at the cost of notoriety with Overlook's factions
> and hunters sent after the player): "Unsure"

> On what comes first: "I want to first have you decide on all ship types and we build them out in blender one by
> one. UV map them, and texture them."

> On the opening voyage: "The opening intro mission requires love and care which we will discuss further"

## What it settles

- The sea takes its place in the loop that [[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]] sets: it is a patrol zone
  of its own, and the ship is a second set of gear.
- The sea is divided into waters, each with a level and an owner. Overlook's home waters hold no hostile ship
  ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]]).
- The voyage stays; free sailing at the helm joins it, at true scale, in any waters.
- Ships are sighted on the horizon and chosen: chased, evaded or let pass. A bold ship that comes for the player
  still pans to the fight at once ([[The Sloop Is Painted, Sails the Overworld and Meets Pirates]]).
- Bounties, public events, wrecks and sea strikes are the sea's activities.
- A fight ends in a sinking or a boarding; losing washes the player up at the nearest port, and the ship's damage
  stays until repaired.
- The ship is gear: hull, guns, sails and crew, each with perks. Crew is the companion system, ported from DFO.
- A company sails as a fleet. Players do not fight each other at sea.
- Merchantmen come to sea, for escorts and events, beside the hostile ships that were the only ones sailing.
- Being the pirate is open ([[Open Questions]]), and so is the opening voyage.
- First come the ships themselves: every type, chosen by Claude, built in Blender one at a time, UV-mapped and
  painted ([[The Ships as Claude Shapes Them]], [[Hulls]]).
