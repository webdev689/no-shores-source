# Fewer Plates Show, Sized by Rank, After Destiny

> "1. I want to adjust how enemy health bars work. I want to research how destiny handles enemy health bara visually
> that reduces clutter of too many health bars"

> Shown the research, and six ways to cut the clutter: "Your opinion?"

> Shown Claude's recommendation (show an unfought foe's plate only for the one nearest the crosshair, within about 5
> degrees, or the one the soft lock holds; keep a fought foe's plate 6 s after the last exchange, the target plate's own
> time, then fade it; a Minor's bar short and thin, an Elite's and up longer and cut in thirds; the land hiding plates;
> the level square kept; the target's own plate over its head left until the owner has played with which plates show): "Yep do all that
> you recommended"

## What it settles

- A foe not fought shows its plate only when it is the one nearest the crosshair, within about 5 degrees of it, or
  the one the soft lock holds ([[Enemies]], [[Interface]], [[Test Ground]]).
- A fought foe keeps its plate for 6 s after the last exchange, then fades. That's the target plate's own time, so the
  two agree.
- A plate over a figure goes with its foe's rank: a Minor's bar short and thin, an Elite's and up longer and cut in
  thirds ([[Enemies Come in Five Ranks, After Destiny]]).
- The land hides a plate as a block does.
- The level square stays on every plate.
- The target's own plate over its head stays, to be looked at again once the owner has played with which plates show.
