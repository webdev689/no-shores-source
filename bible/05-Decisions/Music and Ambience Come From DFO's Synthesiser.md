# Music and Ambience Come From DFO's Synthesiser

The owner, asked whether the music should be DFO's written scores, project-final's recorded tracks with its music
director, or both: "1. It's a consistent system"

Then: "World ambience for the sea and each of the 9 islands. Social space theme and ambience. Music that leads into
combat. I'm really looking to flesh this out and be as detailed as possible. While I want to retain the feel of
Daggerfall, I also want to put our own modern twist on it"

Asked whether music plays while roaming an island (each island its own theme, ambience only until danger nears, or a
sparse phrase now and then): "B"

Asked how to build it (synthesised while the game runs, rendered ahead into files, or both): "1 but also probably
using freesound for some ambience if you cant get it right."

Then: "I want you to build this end to end with precision, no half assed work, no bad sounds and be as detailed as
you possibly can. Go beyond the limits and make this something truly special. Come back when this is 100%"

## What it settles

- The music is DFO's own: its FM voice bank, its song player and its written scores, in the one system DFO built
  ([[Naval, Living World, Professions and Synth Come Over Leaner]]). Nothing of Daggerfall's songs comes.
- Roaming an island is ambience only. Music comes as danger nears and leads into combat.
- The sea, and each of the nine islands, has an ambience of its own. The social space has a theme and an ambience.
- It keeps Daggerfall's feel, with a modern twist of Overlook's own.
- It is synthesised while the game runs. Freesound fills in ambience the synthesiser cannot make convincing, picked
  on the sound sheet ([[Sounds Come From Freesound, Judged on a Test Sheet]]).
- Every piece is judged on the sound sheet.
