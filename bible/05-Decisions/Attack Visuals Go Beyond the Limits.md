# Attack Visuals Go Beyond the Limits

The owner, on the Arts round ([[Arts Are Overlook's Subclasses]]): "Do a deep comprehensive audit and design pass on
this. This needs to be as detailed as possible. Attack visuals should go beyond the limits. Take your time and come
back when everything is 100%"

## What it settles

- The Arts round gets a full audit and design pass, as detailed as it can be, before it comes back to the owner.
- Attack visuals (blows, shots, spells and what they do to what they strike) are pushed past what they are now, not
  kept within them. How is Claude's, recorded in [[Arts and Loadouts as Claude Shapes Them]], "How attacks look".
- Nothing else: no new rules, numbers or spells come of it.
