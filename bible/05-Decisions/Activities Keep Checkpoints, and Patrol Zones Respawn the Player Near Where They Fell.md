# Activities Keep Checkpoints, and Patrol Zones Respawn the Player Near Where They Fell

> "I want to adjust respawning because currently it respawns you at the start of whatever the location youre
> currently in. Dungeons and other activities should have checkpoint progress where you can respawn, patrol zones
> should respawn you close to where you died."

## What it settles

- **A respawn stands the player at their progress or near their fall**, not at the start of the place.
- **Dungeons and other activities keep checkpoint progress**, and a player respawns at it ([[Revives]]).
- **In a patrol zone a player respawns close to where they died.**
- It replaces the respawn at "the section's nearest spawn" in [[The World as Claude Shapes It]]. Claude's calls in
  building it are in [[Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them]].
