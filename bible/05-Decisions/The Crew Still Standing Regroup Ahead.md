# The Crew Still Standing Regroup Ahead

> "Crew members that stay alive should follow you as you progress"

Asked how they should follow, the owner chose the option that read: "Regroup ahead: Survivors gather at each new
zone's checkpoint as you reach it, and join the fight there."

## What it settles

- On the wreck, the Brig's crew still standing gather at each new zone's checkpoint as the player reaches it, and
  join the fight there.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who regroups | Every crewman still standing from the zones before the one reached; a zone's own crew stand by its boarders as before | They "stay alive" and "follow you as you progress" |
| Where | Round the checkpoint, 1.8 m out and a step apart on its deck, facing the zone's wave | At the checkpoint, and turned to the fight they join |
| How they get there | They walk, by the room's routes, as a garrison walks to a new place | Seen coming, never appearing |
| The arena | None comes into it | Thule's fight is one on one ([[The Game Opens With a Story Mission at Sea]], beat 10) |
