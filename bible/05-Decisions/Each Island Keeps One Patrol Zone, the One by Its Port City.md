# Each Island Keeps One Patrol Zone, the One by Its Port City

> "So lets instead talk about the world. I want to dial back scope a bit and only do one patrol zone per island
> instead of 3"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked which zone stays on each island: the one by its port city, as on 18 November (on Saltmere the Saltgrass
> Downs, the most built of its three); another on Saltmere; or one named for each island: "Each patrol is by their
> port city"
>
> Asked what happens to the twelve tasks of the houses' roads set in Saltmere's two zones that go, two for each house
> in the Gullwing Vale and two in the Driftwood Moors: moved into the kept zone, their lines reworded for its places;
> cut; or talked through first: "Move them into the kept zone (Recommended)"
>
> Asked what happens to what is built in the zones that go (on Saltmere their runs, dungeons, landing sites, Fissure
> and raid; on the other islands their laid-out zones): removed; the best folded into the kept zone, which grows; or
> the zones closed with their files kept: "Fold the best into the kept zone"

> "So saltgrass, in my opinion, is the best built, but not finished. Building 9 fully fleshed out patrol zones is a
> lofty goal on top of an already lofty game"

> "You gave great advice. Only develop a zone when one is needed."
>
> "One patrol zone per island,adjust whatever is needed in the storylines to adjust."

Asked whether the story stays in the patrol zone, with the passages of
[[Passages Web Out From the Patrol Zone Across Its Island]] where the procedural missions happen: "4. Correct"

Asked again what comes of the Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood Moors once the passages are planned: "5. Fold whatever
you.think would work into saltgrass and/or fold their items into passageways"

## What it settles

- **One patrol zone on each island**, the one by its port city: on Saltmere the Saltgrass Downs. The other two on
  each island go. It replaces the 27 zones of [[Patrol Zones Stay as Built and Gain Linear Combat Areas]].
- **A zone is developed only when one is needed**: the other islands' zones wait until play reaches them.
- **The story bends to fit**, and stays in the patrol zone. The houses' roads keep their tasks: those set in the
  zones that go move into the kept zone, their lines reworded for its places
  ([[Each House Has Its Own Road to Its Art]]).
- **What works from the zones that go is folded in**, into the Saltgrass Downs, into the passages, or both, as Claude
  judges.

Replaced in part by [[Each Island Has Three Regions, and Saltmere's Three Patrol Zones Stay, Linked]]: on Saltmere the
three patrol zones stay, linked, and the houses' tasks stay where they were; the other islands' excess zones go.

It builds on [[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]] and
[[Patrol Areas Are Laid Out With RMB, and Missions Run Through Them]].
