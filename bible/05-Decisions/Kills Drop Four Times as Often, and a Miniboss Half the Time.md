# Kills Drop Four Times as Often, and a Miniboss Half the Time

> "I think we need to up the drops for loot a bit. A streamer has been playing for 2 hours and has found 1 common
> item"

> Told that a kill dropped something 2% of the time from a Minor, 4% from a Major and 10% from an Elite, about three
> in four of them commons, and asked how much to raise it: "Destiny 1's pace (×4)", the option that read: "Minor 8%,
> Major 16%, Elite 40%. About one Rare in 200 kills, as Destiny 1 players counted. Per 100 Minor kills: about 6
> commons and 2 Vestiges."

> Told that ×4 put an Elite's 40% above a Miniboss's 35%, against the order by rank, and that a dungeon's clear took
> its Vestige chance from its Miniboss: "Miniboss 50% (Recommended)", the option that read: "Elite keeps your ×4
> (40%). A Miniboss's Vestige goes 35% → 50%. Its separate item chance stays at 35%. A dungeon clear's Vestige stays
> at 35%."

## What it settles

- A kill leaves a Vestige by the foe's rank: Minor 8 in 100, Major 16, Elite 40, Miniboss 50, a Boss always. A
  Normal one still comes as its common item ([[Commons Drop as Themselves, Never as Vestiges]]).
- A Miniboss's item as itself stays 35 in 100, and a dungeon's clear still leaves a Vestige 35 times in 100.
- The colours, what a Vestige opens to, a Boss's drops and the tiers' raise stay as they were.
- It replaces the rates by rank in [[The Balance Pass Goes Halfway Back, and Loot Follows Destiny 1's Order]] and the
  Miniboss's Vestige in [[Bosses and Dungeons Drop Items, Clears Pay, and Shapes Come From the Week's Abyss]].

How it works: [[Gear]], "Vestiges".
