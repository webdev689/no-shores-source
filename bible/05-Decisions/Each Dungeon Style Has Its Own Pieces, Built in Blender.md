# Each Dungeon Style Has Its Own Pieces, Built in Blender

> "Lets do a comprehensive design pass on dungeons, building multiple modules in the blender pipeline allowing for
> dungeons to feel unique and distinct."

Told that every wall, doorway, pillar and ceiling of a dungeon was made in code and nothing in it came from Blender
yet, and offered three kinds of module (landmark rooms, style kits, or both, with both as Claude's pick):

> "I want you to do everything and be as detailed as possible. Return when everything is 100%"

## What it settles

- **A comprehensive design pass on the dungeons**, so each feels unique and distinct
  ([[The Dungeons Are Perfect and Insanely Varied]]).
- **Many modules, built in the Blender pipeline**, as the ships are ([[Ship Models Live on R2]]).
- **Everything, in as much detail as can be made**: both kinds, a kit of pieces for each style and landmark rooms.
  How they are made is Claude's to build ([[Dungeons as Claude Builds Them]]); the owner can change any of it.
- The earlier decisions hold: a dungeon runs slightly linear, with a clear beginning, middle and end and a return
  portal, its props laid and everything in the procedural textures.
