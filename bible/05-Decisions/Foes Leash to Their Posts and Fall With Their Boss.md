# Foes Leash to Their Posts and Fall With Their Boss

> Told that a Miniboss of the Captain role threw the same four telegraphed blows as its Elite, at the same rate,
> against "even more telegraphs", and asked how a Miniboss should show more: "Faster: more often (Recommended)"

> Told that a Boss's called foes never leave, so over repeated fights they fill a boss node's room and its later
> calls are refused, and asked what should happen to them: "They fall with their Boss (Recommended)"

> Told that foes chase a player who runs without end, off their navmesh and out of the node, and never walk back to
> their posts, and asked whether garrison foes should give up and return: "Leash to their post (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- A Miniboss throws its telegraphed blows more often than an Elite: its cooldowns ×0.8 in its first phase
  ([[Enemies Come in Five Ranks, After Destiny]], [[Enemies]]).
- When a Boss falls, the foes it called fall too, so each fight starts clean and every phase's call comes.
- A garrison foe gives up once its target is 50 m from its post or off the room's navmesh. It walks back to its post,
  healing as it goes, and stands idle there again ([[Foes]]).
