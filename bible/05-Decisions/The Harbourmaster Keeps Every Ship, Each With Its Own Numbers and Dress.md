# The Harbourmaster Keeps Every Ship, Each With Its Own Numbers and Dress

> "Today I want you to take autonomy and flesh out the ship system by integrating all our current models into the
> harbormaster. The player in the tutorial shouldnt have a ship until they reach the harbor master in the tutorial.
>
> Each model should have their own standard defined statistics. Along with us implementing ship customization
> options with a cost. Please be extremely detailed amd make this your own. Come back when everything is 100%
> completed"

## What it settles

- **Every ship model built comes to the Harbourmaster**: the Sloop, the Schooner, the Brig and the Frigate
  ([[Hulls]], [[The Ships as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- **A new player has no ship in the tutorial until they reach the Harbourmaster** ([[Quests]],
  [[The Stranger Welcomes the Player, and a Quest Walks Them Round Overlook]]).
- **Each model has standard numbers of its own.** This replaces, for the ships players sail, a hull changing "never
  its strength" ([[Hulls]], [[Naval Combat]]).
- **Ships can be customised**, each option at a cost.
- **Claude decides what the owner has not**, on its autonomy, and records each call in
  [[The Ships at the Harbourmaster as Claude Shapes Them]]; how it works is in [[Shipyard]].
- **The bar is very high**: extremely detailed, and not done until it is 100%.
