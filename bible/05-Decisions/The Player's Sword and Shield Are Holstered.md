# The Player's Sword and Shield Are Holstered

> "The player character should have their weapon and shield holstered"

## What it settles

- In the [[Prologue]] the player wears their sword and shield put away: the sword sheathed at the hip, the shield
  slung on the back.
- Their weapon comes out as the game's always does: a swing draws it ([[Player]]).
