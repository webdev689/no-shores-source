# Friendly Ships Take Several Courses, About Five in View, Sailing Visibly

> "When I first loaded onto the sea, there were just so many ships in a consistent straight line for as far as the
> eye can see. Like what is going on here?"

> Told they were the friendly ships on the lanes, nothing misplaced but far too many: "Nothing is misplaced, but yes
> there are way too many, plus ships shouldn't just have one straight travel path, which is my point."

> Offered several courses per route, each ship shifted sideways, or each ship weaving: "Several courses
> (Recommended)"

> Asked how many friendly ships should be in view at once near a port, about 40 then: "About 5"

> Asked whether they should move at a pace that visibly sails rather than their true 5 m/s: "Visibly sail"

> Asked which one pace, seen from the sea, from land and from the helm alike: "60 m/s (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **Several courses.** Each route between two port cities is planned more than one way through open sea, and each
  friendly ship sails one of them, so ships leaving a port head different ways ([[World Map]], "Traffic").
- **About five in view** near a port at once.
- **They sail at 60 m/s**, the pace dial's slowest step.
- Replaces the lanes' one course and the schedule in [[The Sea as Claude Shapes It]], "Friendly ships".

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The courses | Three a pair: the shortest, and two leaving each port 30 degrees either side of its heading to the clear water 100 km out, each stretch planned and checked round land as the shortest is; one no clear water makes, or more than 1.3 times the shortest's length, is left out | Ships out of a port fan out (Overlook's Rimeholt courses about 30 degrees apart), every course keeps the lanes' clearance of coasts and isles, and none detours far: a course turned aside only at the route's middle ran 2,985 km against the shortest's 1,402 |
| The schedule | Each course has its own departures every 3,000 s, the courses of a pair starting a third of that apart | 5.2 ships within 60 km of Overlook's roadstead on a day's mean, counting those lying there: the owner's five |
| Lying at a roadstead | 600 s, as before | Unchanged by the decision |
