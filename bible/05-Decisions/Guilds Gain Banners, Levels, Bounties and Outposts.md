# Guilds Gain Banners, Levels, Bounties and Outposts

The owner showed Destiny 2's clan screen (a banner hung from a T-shaped staff, the clan's name and tag, its members,
a line of its words, its level on a rule, the weekly personal contribution, the week's clan engrams and the roster)
and wrote:

> "So I want to talk about the games guild system
>
> The reference image is what im imaging for design wise.
>
> 1. Guild name and banner creation with the guild tag being added to player's nameplate/the social menu.
>
> 2. Guild description, Guild bounties, and Guild leveling.
>
> 3. The ability for guilds to establish what im coining as outposts on any island for a cost.
>
> The outpost system:
>
> The outpost system allows players their own plot of land, where they can place structures. These structures give
> guild specific buffs, passive income earning, guild dungeons, guild bosses, housing decoration, and much more.
>
> I want you to research properly, see what can be adapted from DFO, and take your time developing this.
>
> This is your baby to curate and I want it to be AAA grade. Come back when this is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- **The Guild tab is built now**, after Destiny 2's clan screen. It is no longer marked Later
  ([[The Menu Follows Destiny, the Vault and Vendors in the Social Space]]).
- **A guild has a name and a banner its players make**, and its **tag shows on its players' nameplates** and in the
  social menu.
- **A guild has a description, guild bounties and levels.**
- **A guild may establish outposts on any island, for a cost**: a plot of land of its own, where it places
  structures that give guild buffs, passive income, guild dungeons, guild bosses, housing decoration and more. A guild
  dungeon is a place for dungeons beside the three in
  [[Dungeons Live in Patrols, on the Sea's Islands and as Elite Dungeons]].
- **DFO is researched**, and what can be adapted from it comes over ([[Pick - Guilds and Heraldry]],
  [[Pick - Halls, Works and Decor]]).
- **Claude curates the system**, recording each call in [[Guilds and Outposts as Claude Shapes Them]], and comes back
  when it is complete. The [[Rules]] and [[Ask Before Deploying]] still hold.
