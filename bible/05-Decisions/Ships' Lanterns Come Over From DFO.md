# Ships' Lanterns Come Over From DFO

> "I want to import the ship lantern function from DFO also so you can see ships at night with their lanterns on"

> Told that DFO's near ship lanterns are Come Sail Away's code (another author's mod) on Daggerfall Unity, which
> [[Own Work]] keeps out, and asked how they should come over: "Nope its ours"

Asked which of DFO's rules on lit ships to keep, the owner chose "Tithe run dark" (Grey Tithe ships sail with their
lanterns out; merchants and friendly ships are lit), "Fleeing douses" (a merchant running from an attack puts hers
out) and "Night sight" (at night a lit ship is spotted to 650 m, a dark one only to 220 m, by lookouts and by the
player).

Asked which ships carry lanterns, the owner chose "Traffic & moored", "Player ships" and "Hostile ships".

## What it settles

- DFO's ship lanterns come over, its port of Come Sail Away's lanterns with them, as
  [[Come Sail Away's Sailing Comes Over]] brought its sailing: an exception to [[Own Work]]. What DFO read from
  Daggerfall's own files at run time (the lantern's picture) is not in No Shores, so the lanterns are drawn on our
  hulls ([[Hulls]]).
- At night ships show their lanterns: the friendly traffic between the ports, the Sloops moored at Overlook's pier,
  the players' ships and hostile ships ([[Sailing]], [[Naval Combat]]).
- The Grey Tithe's ships run dark, and a merchant running from an attack puts her lanterns out
  ([[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]).
- At night a lit ship is spotted to 650 m and a dark one only to 220 m, by the ships' lookouts and by the player.
- Night sight, the stern windows and the fight's effects go further in
  [[Night Sight Reaches Every Sighting at Sea, Stern Windows Burn, and Fight Effects Improve]].
