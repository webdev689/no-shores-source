# A Held Aim Swings Between the Sides, the Oversea View Keeps Its Height, and No Ship Is Sailed Through

> "Lets talk about sea polish.
>
> 1. When aiming with your ship, I want the player to be able to rotate their camera from one side to another instead
> of having to leave the aim and switch sides
>
> 2. Lamps jitter during movement
>
> 3. The ship during high waves sinks beneath the water instead of staying afloat
>
> 4. In the held map, clicking on a node or region area to travel doesnt move your ship, only a random click at sea
> works
>
> 5. Remove camera scroll in and out for oceanview, player can still rotate the camera
>
> 6. Enemy ships cannot be interacted with sometimes and you can just sail through them
>
> Please take autonomy be as detailed as possible and come back when this is 100%"

## What it settles

- **A held aim swings to the other side.** Turning the camera while aiming takes the aim round to the ship's other
  battery, still aiming; the aim need not be let go to change sides. It replaces in part
  [[A Held Aim Keeps the Camera on Its Side]].
- **The lamps hold still on a moving ship**: a lantern's light moves with the ship, never trembling behind it.
- **A ship stays afloat in high waves**, never sinking beneath the water.
- **A click on a node or a region on the held map travels the ship there**, as a click on the open sea does.
- **The Oversea view has no zoom.** The wheel no longer brings its camera in or out; the player still turns it.
- **An enemy ship can always be dealt with, and no ship is sailed through it.**

How each is done is Claude's: [[The Sea's Polish as Claude Builds It]].
