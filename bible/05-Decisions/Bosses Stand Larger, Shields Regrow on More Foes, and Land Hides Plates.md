# Bosses Stand Larger, Shields Regrow on More Foes, and Land Hides Plates

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on foes, events and plates.

Asked whether the Tithe-Taker should keep its fight without a ledger, the owner answered:

> "Stay as it is. One note is that miniboss and boss enemies should scale in size."

Asked whether an Elite Dungeon's Court should be a darkness zone below Abyss, the owner asked Claude's opinion; Claude
recommended it at Elite and Abyss, not Normal, and the owner answered:

> "13e. Approved"

Asked which areas of a patrol zone hold territories, the owner answered:

> "Some places could hold only civilians, so you can populate them with overlook guards and civilians doing their own
> thing"

Asked which places, the owner answered:

> "You choose"

Asked whether a sheet of the airship's own engines and hatch and the crown's forming should let the owner pick their
sounds, the owner answered:

> "Yes, a sound sheet"

Asked whether a foe's elemental shield, once broken, should come back after a while, the owner answered:

> "Yes, regrows"

Asked which foes beyond the patrols' own carry shields, the owner chose:

> "Dungeon foes,Patrols only,Breach foes"

Asked whether "no blurbs" over a group's break also rules out a sound as it breaks and the "!" alert mark on a plate,
the owner chose:

> "Allow "!" mark"

Asked which proposed plate changes to make, the owner chose:

> "Land hides plates,Fix issue where other players damage numbers and such can be seen far away"

Asked whether a death by a fall and a death in a fight should look the same, the owner answered:

> "One look"

## What it settles

- **Minibosses and Bosses are drawn larger** than other foes ([[Enemies]]). The Tithe-Taker stays as it is
  ([[Public Events as Claude Builds Them]]).
- **An Elite Dungeon's Court is a darkness zone at Elite as at Abyss**; not at Normal
  ([[Revives, Respawns and Relogs as Claude Shapes Them]]).
- **Some places in a patrol zone hold only civilians**: Overlook's guards and townsfolk going about their own business,
  and no Tithe group. Which places is Claude's call, listed in [[The Open Questions' Answers as Claude Builds Them]].
- **The arrivals' own sounds come from a sound sheet** the owner picks from ([[Foe Arrivals as Claude Builds Them]]).
- **A broken shield regrows** after a while ([[Weapons Carry Blaze, Volt or Cosmic, and Foes Carry Shields]]).
- **Patrol, dungeon and Breach foes carry shields.**
- **A plate's "!" alert mark stays; a group's break makes no sound** of its own.
- **Land hides plates**, as blocks do ([[Fewer Plates Show, Sized by Rank, After Destiny]]), as the page already
  draws them (`plateSight.ts`). The other proposals (a soft-locked Boss shown once, a mark for a Major, the display
  scaled with the screen) were not chosen.
- **Other players' damage numbers and the like are not seen from far away**, a fault to fix ([[Interface]]).
- **Both deaths share one look** ([[Game]]).

Built ([[Enemies]], [[Gear]], [[Game]], [[Interface]]; Claude's calls in
[[The Open Questions' Answers as Claude Builds Them]]). Already so: the "!" mark, the break's silence, land hiding
plates, and every dungeon's foes carrying shields. A Miniboss or Boss is drawn larger, but the rules' body it is struck
by is not; the foes of a public event still carry none ([[Open Questions]]).
