# Weapons Carry Blaze, Volt or Cosmic, and Foes Carry Shields

> "I also want to introduce weapon elements. Blaze (Fire), Volt (Electric), Cosmic (Purple energy)
>
> Enemies will sometimes utilize shields where weapons with these elements are very effective at taking down their
> shields, and less effective against other shields depending on their type. Weapons shouldnt guarantee to roll with
> an element. If the weapon does have an element. It shows as a glyph in weapon view and on the icon itself in the
> corner"

Asked how a weapon with no element does against a shield, the owner chose: "Normal damage", which was offered as "No
element is the safe middle: never great, never bad. The matching element is the big win; a wrong element is worse
than none."

## What it settles

- **Three elements**: Blaze, which is fire; Volt, which is electric; Cosmic, which is purple energy.
- **A weapon may roll one; it is not guaranteed.** Some weapons have no element.
- **Foes sometimes carry a shield**, and each shield has a type.
- **Against a shield**, a weapon whose element matches its type is very effective; one with another element is
  less effective; one with no element does normal damage.
- **A weapon's element shows as a glyph** on its page and in the corner of its icon.

Asked later whether each pair should have its own value, the owner kept one half for every other element, as
[[The Face Is Mended Whole, and the Gauge Counts Upgrades]] records.
