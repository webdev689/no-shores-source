## What it settles

- **A main hand and an off hand give way to two weapon slots: Melee and Ranged.**
- **Melee** holds one of: Sword & Shield, Dagger & Shield, Double Daggers, Sword, Double Swords, Staff, Mace, Double
  Mace, Mace & Shield, Great axe, Greatsword, and the flails and warhammers the game already had.
- **Ranged** holds one of: bow, crossbow, pistolet, double pistolet, thunderlock.
- **The Greatsword** is drawn, for now, with the sword's pictures held in both hands, with a greatsword's numbers.
- **No Relic.** The Relic the owner first proposed is dropped.

How each loadout fights, what becomes of the shields and off-hand pieces already carried, and the rest are Claude's,
