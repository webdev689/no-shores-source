# Hostile Ships and Islands Show on the Sea Map, Travel Goes Anywhere at Sea, and Home Waters End 10 km Out

The owner:

> "1. I want to add hostile ships viewable as icons on the waters you are currently occupying along with any spawned
> procedural islands. The waters of the lantern sea should get hostile ships beyond its borders, out in the open sea
>
> 2. Allow click to travel anywhere on your occupied sea map for instant travel,  like we do for traversing to other
> zones.
>
> 3. Increase enemy ship spawn rates and procedural island spawn rates"

Asked where the Lantern Sea's safe home waters should end, past which it would play like other waters (Grey Tithe
ships at its band, levels 1 to 4, procedural islands rolling there, and the border drawn on the sea map), the owner
chose "10 km", the option that read: "Safe within 10 km of the coast; about 97% of the Lantern Sea becomes hostile."

Asked how much more often hostile ships should come, told each player at sea rolled every 60 to 140 s with a 0.18 to
0.36 chance by the water's band, no roll while 3 sail in sight, and that cap staying, the owner chose "×2", the option
that read: "Rolls every 30–70 s."

Asked how much more often procedural islands should spawn, told the sea rolled every 10 s at 0.3 chance while a ship
had fewer than 2 islands within 8 km, at most 96 standing, the owner chose "×1.5", the option that read: "Up to 3
within 8 km, 0.45 chance per roll, 144 at most."

Asked what an in-water travel should do to the hostile roll, told each travel held the next roll back to at least
30 s, the owner chose "Same as other travel", the option that read: "An in-water travel also holds the next roll back
to at least 30 s."

## What it settles

- Home waters reach 10 km from Overlook's island's coast. Past that, the Lantern Sea's open sea is the Grey Tithe's:
  hostile ships at the Lantern Sea's band (levels 1 to 4), procedural islands, and none of home waters' shelter
  ([[World Map]], "Waters"; [[Naval Combat]]; [[Islands]]). This replaces in part
  [[The Waters Come From the Islands]], where the first island's water was home throughout.
- Each player at sea rolls for a hostile ship every 30 to 70 s after the first, twice as often as before; a roll's
  chance by band and the cap of three in sight stay ([[Naval Combat]], "At sea").
- The sea rolls an island round a ship while it has fewer than three within 8 km, at 0.45 a roll, with at most 144
  standing ([[Islands]], "At sea").
- A course chosen anywhere at sea on the held map, in the ship's own water or another, asks to travel there, and a yes
  travels there as travel to other waters does ([[Held Map]], [[Sailing]], "Travel by the held map"). Every travel
  holds the next hostile roll back to at least 30 s. This replaces in part
  [[The Held Map Takes the Ship to Other Waters, Seen Sailing Away]] and
  [[The Grey Tithe's Sea, Travel and the Overtaken Ruins as Claude Builds Them]], where only another water asked.
- The held map at sea marks every hostile ship and every standing procedural island in the water the ship lies in
  ([[Held Map]]).
