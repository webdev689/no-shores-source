# Night Sight Reaches Every Sighting at Sea, Stern Windows Burn, and Fight Effects Improve

> "1. Night sight? 2. Light the stern windows 3. Improve ship effect visuals"

Told that night sight (a lit ship spotted to 650 m, a dark one to 220 m) covered the captains in a fight and the
player's compass, marks and map, but not a player joining a clash of a Tithe ship and a merchant (750 m) nor a hostile
at sea choosing whom to chase (5 km), the owner chose "Apply to both".

Told that the hulls' published meshes do not tell the stern's windows apart, and asked how to light them, the owner
chose "Re-publish the hulls": every hull exported again with its windows marked as glass and published to R2.

Asked which ship effect visuals to improve, the owner chose "Fight effects" (the sea fight's puffs: a gun's flash and
smoke, spray, splashes, fire, splinters, after DFO's `navalEffects.js`) and "Flashes light ships" (a gun's flash, fire
and a blast light the ships and the water near them).

## What it settles

- At night night sight holds wherever a ship is seen at sea: also a player joining a clash, and a hostile at sea
  choosing its quarry ([[Ships' Lanterns Come Over From DFO]], [[Naval Combat]]).
- A ship's stern windows burn while her lanterns do. Every hull is exported again with its windows marked, and
  published to R2: the owner's yes for that publish ([[Hulls]]).
- The sea fight's effects are improved, and a gun's flash, a fire and a blast light the ships and the water near them
  ([[Naval Combat]]).
