# A Locked-On Dodge Rolls the Way It Goes

> "5. When locked on and dodging, you should dodge in the direction you're facing animation wise. Currently you
> always dodge facing the enemy."

## What it settles

- Locked on, a dodge's figure faces the way the dodge goes, as the roll is drawn, rather than turning to face the
  locked foe through it ([[Combat]], [[Test Ground]]). It is how the dodge is shown; the soft lock itself holds as
  before.
