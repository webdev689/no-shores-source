# The Discord Is Claude's to Build, Organised and Unbloated

> "I want you to suggest everything to me. How you would set this up and show me your layout. Im looking for
> something extremely organized, proper embeds, code of conduct, patch notes, announcements, and everything in between
> without being overbloated"

Then, shown Claude's proposal for the server:

> "With all this in mind. Do a comprehensive detailed pass and push the discord to its limit, finding it organized,
> non-bloated but making it a AAA grade experience in any way you can. Take your time and come back when this is 100%"
>
> "This is your baby"

## What it settles

- The No Shores Discord server's layout is Claude's to choose: its channels, roles, permissions, the messages it
  starts with, its code of conduct, how patch notes and announcements are posted, and how the bot's modules are set.
- It is organised and kept small: every channel has one job, and what a busier server would need is left out until it
  is needed.
- Its messages are proper embeds, in the same shape each time.
- Claude's calls are in [[The Discord Server as Claude Builds It]]. The owner can change any of them.
