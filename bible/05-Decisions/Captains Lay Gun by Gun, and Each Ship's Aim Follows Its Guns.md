# Captains Lay Gun by Gun, and Each Ship's Aim Follows Its Guns

> "Please have the camera depending on each ship correctly have the right aim UI depending on their cannon amount. I
> also need to see images once done,Captains aim gun by gun"

Asked whether captains should lay each gun as a player's guns are laid, or keep one elevation for their battery's
middle, the owner chose gun by gun, and asked for the aim to follow each ship's guns, with pictures.

## What it settles

- A captain's guns are each elevated for the range from their own port and deck, as a player's are, still square
  ([[Ship Guns Keep Square Broadsides]]), so a big captain's second gun deck throws as far as its first
  ([[Naval Combat]], "How it works").
- The aim camera stands at the station of the hull sailed, and the aim draws one arc for each gun of the side laid:
  the Sloop's 3, the Schooner's 5, the Brig's 7, the Frigate's 10 ([[Naval Combat]], "The aim, as DFO's"; [[Hulls]]).
