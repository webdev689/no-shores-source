# Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe

> "I definitely want enemies to be present on the outskirts of home waters. And pirates should no longer exist. It
> should only be Grey Tithe"

> Told where the pirates are woven in (three of the waters, the clashes at sea, the island camps, the prologue's first
> attackers, the deck crews): "Wait.. so pirates are integral?"

> "Replace pirates with Grey Tithe."

> Asked what happens to the sea's clashes, two enemy ships fighting each other with no player, always a pirate ship
> against a Tithe ship: "Tithe fight friendly ships"

> Asked how close enemies on the outskirts of Overlook's home waters may come to a ship still inside them: "Skip"

## What it settles

- **There are no pirates.** The Grey Tithe takes their place everywhere they were: at sea, on the islands, on the
  decks a boarding meets, in the prologue, and in every word the game shows
  ([[The Grey Tithe Take a Tenth, and Overlook Does Not Pay]], [[Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe]]).
- **A clash at sea is a Grey Tithe ship fighting a friendly ship**, one of the traffic between the port cities
  ([[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]]).
- **How close enemies come to home waters is left as it is**: the question was skipped, so home waters still hold no
  hostile ship ([[The Waters Come From the Islands]]).

Which waters the Tithe holds now: [[The Ruined Cities' Waters Are the Lattice's, and Their Ruins Are Overtaken]].
How it is built: [[Naval Combat]], [[Sailing]], [[Enemies]]; Claude's choices: [[The Grey Tithe's Sea, Travel and the Overtaken Ruins as Claude Builds Them]].
