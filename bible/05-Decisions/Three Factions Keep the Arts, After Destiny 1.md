# Three Factions Keep the Arts, After Destiny 1

> "Factions:
> 3 factions each dealing with the 3 arts.
>
> They allow you to upgrade your art, plus purchase art cores. Arts gain XP 1 to 5 levels like gear. Once reached, you
> can visit the approriate faction to level up for a cost, allowing you to choose a core each level. Arts/Tenants grow
> in power/duration with each level up. The level 5 power scale is the current default.
>
> Players can pledge to one and switch pledges at any time for a cost.
>
> Being pledged allows you to buy their cape, which earns you faction XP in the world, which isnt capped.
>
> Research how factions work in destiny 1. This is the goal."

> Asked to name the three factions, one for each Art, from three sets offered: "Stormcallers / Lamplighters / The
> Veiled"

> "Please take this on with autonomy, be as detailed as possible, and come back when this is 100% finished"

## What it settles

- **Three factions, one for each Art**: the Stormcallers keep Storm, the Lamplighters Divine and the Veiled Shadow
  ([[Arts Are Overlook's Subclasses]]).
- **A faction raises its Art and sells its Core fragments.**
- **An Art has levels 1 to 5**, and gains XP as gear does. Once the XP is reached, its faction raises it a level for a
  cost, and the player chooses a Core fragment at each level.
- **Each level makes the Art and its Tenets stronger and last longer.** Level 5 is the Arts' power as it was.
- **A player pledges to one faction**, and may switch at any time, for a cost.
- **Pledged, a player may buy the faction's cape.** Worn, it earns faction XP in the world, with no cap
  ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]] said a cloak adds to its faction's reputation while worn).
- **Destiny 1's factions are the reference.**
- The numbers and everything else left open are Claude's, the owner's to change:
  [[Factions and Vendors as Claude Shapes Them]].
