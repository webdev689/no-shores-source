# A Tall Wall Is Climbed Within 45° of Square

> "4. A pre exhisting issue where the test geometry in the test room can only be mantled, not climbed"

A wall too high to grab was climbed only from exactly square: a look the least off it slid the body along the wall,
and a body moving at all never took hold. Asked how far off square should still start a climb, the owner chose
"Within 45° (Recommended)".

## What it settles

- Held forward into a wall too high to grab, a body stopped across the wall's face, however it slides along it,
  takes hold when its look is within 45 degrees of square to the wall, and climbs square to it ([[Movement]]).
