# Keepers Give Gifts, the House Arts Come With a Horde, Escorts Ride, and the Horse and Ship Are Taught

> "1. Instead of a sword and shield.in the tutorial. Give the player a choice of melee weapon and ranged weapon
>
> 2. Each visit to a vendor during the tutorial should grant some sort of free item for tutorial purposes
>
> 3. The quest steps involving aquisition of arts should include scenarios where abilities are infinite and fully
>    powered where you have to defeat hordes of enemies. This gives the players a power fantasy and shows off the arts
>
> 4. The escort quest is way too slow and the figure needs to ride a horse.
>
> 5. There needs to be proper integrated ship tutorials and horse tutorials"

Asked where the player chooses a melee and a ranged weapon, the owner chose "Weaponsmith only", the option that read:
"Only Overlook's tour changes: the player picks one melee and one ranged weapon there. The wreck keeps its sword,
shield and bow."

Asked what each keeper's visit gives, the owner answered: "Remove the aurem grant from the stranger. Each NPC now gives
a gift, you choose and ensure it makes sense. Also remove the camera pan to every stall. The quest already achieves
this."

Asked which steps get a horde fight with abilities on no cooldown and fully charged, the owner chose "#3", the option
that read: "House Art steps only: only the three roads' Art steps; the wreck's Storm stays as it is." The owner added:
"Plus theres a step of the tutorial sometimes cant complete because your dodge is sometimes not a perfect dodge. Theres
also an issue where if you skip through things, the screen in yhe tutorial stays blurry. Also the current tutorial with
the arts is too rapid fire and doesnt pace for you to experience each art. The wreckage in the first tutorial area is
also floating (the wreckage blocking the path)"

Asked how an escort's figure moves on a horse, the owner chose "Gallop, player rides", the option that read: "The
figure gallops at 12 m/s, the player's own gallop. The step asks the player to mount, and the keep-up distance widens
to about 30 m."

Asked where the ship and horse lessons go, the owner chose "In the Overlook quest", the option that read: "After the
Tack's horse: a riding task (mount, walk, gallop, boost, dismount, ride to a marked point). After the Harbourmaster's
ship: a sailing task at the harbour (board, set sail, steer, trim, fire at a target, open the held map, return).
Taught as the wreck teaches: tracker steps ticked by doing them."

After an audit of the work, asked whether the three house leaders the opening visits give a gift too, the owner
answered: "Gift only from pledged house." Asked whether the stranger should name Overlook's places again, face to face
with no camera pan, the owner answered: "Keep removed. Rework dialouge to be better. Quest already handles the
tutorial". Asked whether the opening should teach the gifts the round does not use (the Mage's Vestige, the Vault
Keeper's cloak, the Tailor's dye, the Postmaster's letter), the owner chose "Add steps", the option that read: "Add a
step for each: open the Vestige at the Mage, take the cloak from the vault, wear the dye, take the letter. Claude
writes them." Offered the house's cape as the pledged leader's gift, the owner answered: "the cape shouldn't be
given", then: "Give a piece of uncommon faction gear".

## What it settles

- **The Weaponsmith gives the player a choice of one melee weapon and one ranged weapon** on the opening's round of
  Overlook. The wreck keeps its sword and shield and its bow ([[Prologue]]).
- **Every keeper visited on the opening's round gives a gift** that fits what the keeper does, chosen by Claude
  ([[Quests]], [[Gear]]).
- **The stranger gives no Aurem**, and her welcome no longer flies the camera to each stall: the round of the keepers
  shows the way ([[Prologue]]). She names no place; her words are reworked to read better ([[Prologue]]).
- **Of the house leaders, only the leader of the house the player pledges to gives a gift**: a piece of uncommon
  faction gear, not the cape ([[Quests]], [[Factions]]).
- **The opening teaches each gift**: a step each to open the Mage's Vestige, take the cloak from the vault, wear the
  dye and take the letter, written by Claude ([[Quests]]).
- **Each house road's step that gives its Art has a horde to fight**, the Art's abilities on no cooldown and fully
  charged. The wreck's Storm stays as it is ([[Quests]], [[Arts]]).
- **The wreck's teaching is fixed**: the dodge lesson completes with any dodge that meets the blow, not only a perfect
  one; skipping leaves no blur on the screen; the Storm's lesson is paced so each ability can be felt; the heap of
  wreckage blocking the path stands on the deck, not floating ([[Prologue]]).
- **An escort's figure rides a horse at a gallop**, 12 m/s, the player's own; the step asks the player to mount, and
  they keep up within about 30 m ([[Quests]]).
- **The opening teaches riding and sailing**: a riding task after the Tack's horse (mount, walk, gallop, boost,
  dismount, ride to a marked point) and a sailing task at the harbour after the Harbourmaster's ship (board, set sail,
  steer, trim, fire at a target, open the held map, return), each step ticked by doing it ([[Quests]], [[Sailing]]).

Replaces in part [[The Stranger Welcomes the Player, and a Quest Walks Them Round Overlook]] (her Aurem, the camera
going to each place she names, and buying the starting gear) and
[[The Tack Gives the First Horse, the Prologue Ends in Plain Clothes, and Each Speaker Is Named in a Colour]] (the
stranger's 400 Aurem). Claude's calls: [[The Welcome's Gifts, the Hordes, the Ride and the Lessons as Claude Builds Them]].
