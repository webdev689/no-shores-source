# Public Events as Claude Builds Them

> "Take autonomy, just available props. Develop new using the blender pipeline and make yhis insanely detailed"

## What it settles

Claude's choices for the public events ([[Public Events Run on a Clock, After Destiny 1]],
[[Public Events Are Built in Full, on Claude's Autonomy]]). The owner can change any of them.

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What it stands on | The Lattice's work (#77) and `main` | The Fissure pours out the Lattice, which #77 draws |
| The Lattice's skin | The ashen grey-green of the dead: each share of a man's skin light on the weights 0.80 red, 0.90 green, 0.78 blue, every human tone alike | Left to Claude; no living tone, and it reads apart from the red crystal |
| How the Lattice is rendered | Its own workflow, run by a push that changes `tools/lattice/render-request.txt` (or by hand once it is on `main`): a job a move (all five views, since the forge sets a move's cell from all of them) and one for the wave, then one job lays them in and publishes | A move is the finest a job can take; a hand-run workflow starts only from the default branch |
| The first render | The five foes the events use; the Heart waits | Every render spends the repository's Actions minutes, and the Heart, the dearest, is in no event |
| The Bulwark's change | In its second phase, or at half its health; the change plays once, then it sweeps and slams its maul | The rules carry no change of their own |
| The Flicker's and the Idol's blink | The rules' dodge: out through its landing backwards and in through it, dissolved in the purple at the middle | Both "never walk, they blink" |
| A Lattice death | Its wave at once (no fall first), at 15 frames a second, tinted in its crystal and scaled to it; the figure fades in 0.3 s | The wave is its death, for all |
| The seal's wave | The same wave, four times a man's size, in the red | The owner's death wave closes the fissure |
| Before the pictures are published | A plain man in grey and brown, empty-handed, at the foe's size | The page must not break, and a foe must still be seen |
| The Idol's beam and the Ravager's shards | Aimed at the chest of the fighter it targets, else 14 m ahead; a shard flies four frames from alternate hands | What the pictures cannot draw |

| The countdown, the result | 8 minutes to win; the result shows 12 seconds | Long enough for a heroic run, short enough to keep the clock |
| A room opened mid-event | Starts it with what its start minute leaves; keeps none across its closing | A room holds no state between players |

| The room's foes | 32 at most in a room that holds events, any other node's still 24 | A garrison and an event's foes at once |
| The Fissure's waves | 3 foes and 1 per extra player (6 at most), when 2 or fewer stand but 30 s after the last at the soonest, or every 40 s, under 10 standing | The shards' pace sets the event's: scripted players at Saltmere's levels seal it in 2.5 to 3 minutes, heroic in 3 to 4.5 |
| Shards | 16 needed, 6 more for each player beyond the first, 40 at most; 1 from a foe, 2 from an Elite or greater; taken within 1.5 m, set within 6 m; the needed never falls when players leave | Leaving never makes it easier |
| The growth | An Idol's Elite at its spot, never moving or striking, 45 s with 2 s to rise and sink | Struck like a foe, as the spec asks |
| The raid's waves | Five foes each, Collectors in every wave, Elites in the third, one Runner per extra player (3 at most), a rank lower for a party of two or fewer; the next 30 s after one is broken | The stores need guarding all the while; at level 4, scripted players hold it in 2 to 3 minutes, heroic in 3.5 to 5.5 |
| Plundering | A Collector within 2.5 m of its store plunders it in 20 s alone | A store held is a store kept |
| The tithe-taker | Never strikes; his round paced to take 50 s; gone wherever he stands when his time is up; the raid waits for him | He counts and leaves, as the owner put it |
| Withdrawing | Foes walk to the crack or the approach at 4 m/s and are gone within 8 s | Seen to leave, not vanish |
| Taking part | 30 s on the ground, or a blow on one of its foes, or a shard set | As the spec |
| The chests' pay | XP 1,500 to 4,500 by tier at level 5, and as much faction reputation (the pledged faction's, its cape worn, as a kill's), Aurem 120 to 360, Fragments 2 to 7, Essence in the heroic tiers, 3 to 8 of the island's resource, a Vestige always | A share of what eight minutes of fighting pays, more with each tier |
