# The Owner's Handcrafted Meadow Grows Over the Grass

> "2. Implement the handcrafted grass texture we have from DFO
>
> Take autonomy. Research properly. Come back when everything is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- DFO's meadow comes over ([[Pick - Meadow Grass]]): the five handcrafted sprites DFO keeps as the owner's (a tall
  tuft, the same cut short, a tuft of flowers, a dry tuft and a bush), stood up as crossed cards over the grass
  ([[Meadow]]). Who made them is for the owner to confirm ([[Own Work]]).
- That the texture brings DFO's whole meadow with it (where it grows, its pick, sizes, colour and wind), not its
  blades or its pixel grass, is Claude's reading of the ask.
- The details are Claude's to call, below.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Over the grass of every landmass whose low ground is grass (temperate coast, highland, woodland, savanna, steppe, swamp, rainforest): the patrol zones, the nodes and the land round the cities, off water, roads, towns, what is kept clear, the beach, stone and snow, as the land draws its grass. Not the desert's sand or the tundra; not an island's ground, which has no height to read yet; not the Test Ground or a dungeon | Where DFO grew its meadow: on the grass it drew |
| How | DFO's meadow as it was: three crossed cards a tuft near and two far, each cut to its sprite, turned its own way and fixed in the world; DFO's pick, sizes, lean and colour law, the owner's green taking the ground's hue and the petals and dry stalks keeping theirs | "We have from DFO": the meadow as the owner had it there |
| How far, how thick | To 160 m, as the plants' grass, fading from 88 m; about two and a quarter tufts a square metre, as DFO's | DFO drew 300 m; the plants' grass sets No Shores' reach, and a real GPU's cost is unmeasured |
| Beside the plants' grass | The baked grass the plants grow stays, the meadow over the ground under it | The meadow is a ground cover; whether the baked grass goes is the owner's |
| A setting | None: the meadow is always drawn | Graphics holds the resolution alone, and a grass row would be a setting invented |
| The pictures | Written into the client as rows of letters by `tools/meadow/write.py` from the owner's PNGs, which stay out of git, as the logo is | The assets check keeps pictures out of git; the owner's own art comes in as pixel pictures |
