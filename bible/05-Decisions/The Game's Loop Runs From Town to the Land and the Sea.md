## What it settles

- **A social hub** holds the vendors, a bounty board and other functions.
- **The bounty board** in town sends players to the island's land and to the sea.
- **A patrol area** is for exploring, world events, gathering materials, bounties and some missions' setups.
- **Missions are curated**, and come much later.
- **The sea** has procedural small islands with dungeons, and wreckage that gives ship materials.
- **Prestige is crucial**, and the sea plays a larger part in it, through its Breaches
  ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]]).
- **Where dungeons stand**: [[Dungeons Live in Patrols, on the Sea's Islands and as Elite Dungeons]]. **How hard
  things are**: [[Where You Go and the Seas You Choose Set the Difficulty]]. **Prestige**:
  [[Prestige Stays as Built, With a Costly Infusion]]. **The days and the weeks**:
  [[Dailies and Weeklies Follow Destiny 1, With One Abyss Dungeon a Week]]. **What opens when**:
  [[The Story Opens Places, Levels Open Tiers, and the Campaign's End Opens the Week]]. **The story**:
  [[The Story Is Nine Missions, With Side Quests Between Them]].
- **Nothing of it is built yet**: these decisions put the game's plan straight first.

It builds on [[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]], [[The World Map]] and
[[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]].
