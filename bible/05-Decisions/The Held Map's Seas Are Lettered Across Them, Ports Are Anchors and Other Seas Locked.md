# The Held Map's Seas Are Lettered Across Them, Ports Are Anchors and Other Seas Locked

> "Remove the tiny nodes on the held map. Island region names should show when hovering in a detailed parchment
> format. Ports should.be signified by anchors now and show the port name on hover.
>
> The lantern sea name should stretch and round across the sea and blend in. The reguon level should be attached to
> the main island name."

Told that only Saltmere has an island name, and asked where an unnamed island's level should go: "For the other sea
regions. Darken their areas and place a lock glyph in the center of their region that stands out".

Asked whether every sea's name curves as the Lantern Sea's, or only the Lantern Sea's: "Every sea". Asked what the
ruined port cities show: "Anchor, faded, name on hover". Asked whether every island's regions show their name on hover
or only the ship's island's: "Only the ship's island".

## What it settles

- **No node is marked** on the held map at sea ([[Held Map]]).
- **A port is an anchor**, its name shown on hover; **a ruined port a faded anchor**, its name shown on hover.
- **The regions of the ship's island** show their names on hover, on a detailed card of parchment.
- **Every sea's name** stretches and curves across its sea, blending into the map.
- **The level band** stands with the island's own name (Saltmere's), not the sea's.
- **The other seas**, whose islands have no name, are darkened, each with a lock glyph at its middle that stands out.

Replaces, for the waters' names, [[The Held Map Colours Each Water's Name by Its Difficulty]].
