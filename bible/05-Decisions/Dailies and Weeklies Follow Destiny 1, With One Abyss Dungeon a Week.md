# Dailies and Weeklies Follow Destiny 1, With One Abyss Dungeon a Week

> "Destiny 1 has always been the reference and we have a solid plan built. I envision a stark and consistant daily
> and weekly activity system, along with the planned difficulty tiers. Abyss Dungeons being the hardest content outside
> of Breaches (raids)"

Offered Destiny 1's first year as the shape: a daily reset at 9:00 UTC and a weekly one on Tuesday; a patrol dungeon
of the day in its three tiers and the board's bounties; an Elite Dungeon of the week with a modifier; an Abyss Dungeon
of the week with rotating modifiers, limited revives, a wipe ending the run, its best drop once a week and a boon for
the week; and Breaches locked weekly. Asked whether Abyss is one featured dungeon a week, the same for everyone and
its rewards locked weekly, as Destiny 1's Nightfall, or any dungeon at Abyss at any time with weekly challenges for
the best drops, as Destiny 2's Ordeal:

> "Yep I approve your recommendation. I want to build everything out properly."

## What it settles

- **Daily and weekly activities follow Destiny 1**: stark and consistent, over the tiers
  ([[The Tiers Are Normal, Elite and Abyss]]).
- **Abyss Dungeons are the hardest content after Breaches** ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of
  Eight]]).
- **Abyss is one featured dungeon a week**, the same for everyone, its rewards locked weekly, as Destiny 1's
  Nightfall.
- **It is built in full.** The rest of the shape offered is Claude's to make, and the owner's to change.

What each opens with: [[The Story Opens Places, Levels Open Tiers, and the Campaign's End Opens the Week]].

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
