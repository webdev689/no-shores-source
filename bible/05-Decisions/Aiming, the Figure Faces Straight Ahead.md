# Aiming, the Figure Faces Straight Ahead

> "I wanna handle some bugs with animations. So when youre locked onto an enemy and aiming down sights, your
> character angles instead of staying straight forward"

## What it settles

- While a ranged weapon is aimed, the figure faces straight ahead, where its player looks, not toward the foe or the
  point under the crosshair ([[Combat]], "Shots").
- It stays facing ahead through its reload while the aim is held.
- The aimed shot still flies onto the point under the crosshair ([[Ranged Weapons Aim and Fire]]).
