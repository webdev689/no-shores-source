# Some Foes Stand Already, Others Arrive by the Tithe's Airships and the Lattice's Crystal

> "I want to talk about enemy spawns and how they appear.
>
> In destiny some enemies are already on the ground, while others come in from a dropship or spawn effect.
>
> For the lattice and Grey tithe id like to do the same
>
> In lore, the grey tithe should have access to airships (we develop). Large modified vessels retrofitted with
> combustible thrusters.
>
> In patrol, these should be seen flying in, with enemies having a spawn effect similar to the player.
>
> The lattice should use a crystallized structure that appears, as the enemies spawn below.
>
> I really want to be as detailed as possible and do this right. Take some autonomy. Be as detailed as possible and
> make this your own
>
> Come back when this is 100% complete.
>
> Along with this change, enemies should come in varied groups in patrol, amd be present moreso than they are now. The
> largest groups being what we have now, and then smaller varied groups."

> "I definitely want to use the blender pipeline for this"

## What it settles

- **Two ways a foe is there**, as in Destiny: some stand on the ground already; others come in. So it is for the Grey
  Tithe and the Lattice ([[Enemies]]).
- **The Grey Tithe have airships**, which No Shores develops: large modified vessels, retrofitted with combustible
  thrusters. In patrol they are seen flying in, and the foes they bring arrive with a spawn effect like the player's
  ([[Players Arrive as Destiny's Transmat Does, Unexplained]]).
- **The Lattice arrive under crystal**: a crystallized structure appears, and its foes spawn below it ([[Lattice]]).
- **Patrol groups are varied, and there are more foes**: the largest groups are the ones patrols have now, and smaller
  groups of varied kinds stand beside them.
- **The airships and the crystal are made with the Blender pipeline** ([[Modules]]).
- **Claude decides the rest**, in detail, recording each call in [[Foe Arrivals as Claude Builds Them]]; it is done
  when it is complete.
