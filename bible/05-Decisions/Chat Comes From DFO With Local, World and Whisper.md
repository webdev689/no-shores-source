# Chat Comes From DFO With Local, World and Whisper

With the nameplates and the interact menu, showing Destiny's list of names in brackets on a dark band between two
rules, the owner wrote:

> "Alongside all of these changes. I want to port over the chat system from DFO, but simplify and redesign it around
> the next reference shown. With only local, world, and whisper. No lobby player listing"

## What it settles

- **Overlook has text chat**, ported from DFO's ([[Pick - Chat]]), simplified.
- **Three channels only**: Local, World and Whisper.
- **No list of the players in a channel**, as DFO's chat kept beside its lines.
- **Its look is redrawn after the picture**: names in brackets on a dark band between two rules.
- It answers the Roster's open point on messages ([[The Roster and Emblems as Claude Shapes Them]]).
