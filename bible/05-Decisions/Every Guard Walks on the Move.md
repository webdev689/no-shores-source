# Every Guard Walks on the Move

> "Walking while blocking needs to be a thing"

Told that guarding already lets a fighter move at under half its pace while the figure slides in a still pose, and
asked which guards should walk, the owner chose "Every guard (Recommended)".

## What it settles

- A fighter walking with its guard up walks in it: a shield's raised block over the shield's march, and the guard of
  a blade, an axe, a mace or a staff with no shield over the walk.
- Standing, the guard is held still as before.

Claude's calls:

- The forge draws each guard held through a stride, its first frame over the walk's four (`blockwalk`, `parrywalk`).
- A figure whose layers are not all drawn walking keeps the still guard, as an action on the move does.
