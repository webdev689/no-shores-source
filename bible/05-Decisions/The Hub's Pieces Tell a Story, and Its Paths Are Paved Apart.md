# The Hub's Pieces Tell a Story, and Its Paths Are Paved Apart

The owner, shown the hub's courts filled ([[The Hub Feels Lively, Its Ground Populated]]): "I feel like the pathway
needs to be a different texture", then "Its way too crowded. Like it needs to make sense with object placement. It
needs to tell a story, and the pathway needs to be a different texture from the ground"

## What it settles

- **The courts are too crowded** as filled: fewer pieces.
- **Where a piece stands makes sense**, and the placing **tells a story**.
- **The paths are a different texture from the ground.**

How it is done is Claude's: [[Overlook's Hub as Claude Shapes It]].
