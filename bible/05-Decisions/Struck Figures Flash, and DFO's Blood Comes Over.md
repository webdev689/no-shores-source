# Struck Figures Flash, and DFO's Blood Comes Over

> "Hit feedback needs to be better"

Offered a flash on the struck, an on-screen hit-stop, a camera shake, and sparks and blood, the owner chose
"Flash on the struck" and "Sparks and blood", then wrote:

> "Btw just import the blood system from dfo"

Told that DFO's blood numbers were read off DaggerBlood's decompiled mod and its red taken from Daggerfall's blood
texture, which [[Own Work]] bars, the owner chose "Exactly as DFO has it".

## What it settles

- A figure a blow harms flashes, white, or red for the player's own ([[Test Ground]]).
- DFO's blood comes over whole, its numbers, orderings and colours unchanged: an exception to [[Own Work]] for
  DaggerBlood's numbers and Daggerfall's blood red.
- Pictures of Daggerfall's stay behind: DFO's splash billboard from TEXTURE.380 and the frame its gibs wore.
- Blows also throw sparks.
