# Patrol Foes Hold Territories Across the Zone, and None Comes on a Timer

> "I dont think enemies should passively come every 35 to 45 seconds. Thats a band aid"

> Asked what should make patrols present instead of the timed airships, the zone's garrison standing only on its
> landing's square and the rest of the zone empty but its caverns and runs: "Territories across the zone
> (Recommended)": "Lay groups across the whole zone's open ground from its layout's areas, each standing on ground
> baked round it as a player comes near, as sites do. A cleared territory is retaken later, its group brought back by
> airship while no player is near. No timer."

## What it settles

- **No foe comes on a timer.**
- **A patrol's foes hold territories across the zone**: a group stands at each of the areas its layout lays foes in,
  on ground baked round it as a player comes near, as the caverns' and runs' sites already did.
- **A cleared territory is retaken later**, its group brought back by airship while no player is near.
- It answers how patrols are "present moreso" in
  [[Some Foes Stand Already, Others Arrive by the Tithe's Airships and the Lattice's Crystal]]; Claude's calls in
  building it are in [[Foe Arrivals as Claude Builds Them]].
