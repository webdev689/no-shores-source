# Players Arrive as Destiny's Transmat Does, Unexplained

> Told that nothing in the bible says why the player comes back from death, and offered three ways it could fit: no
> in-world reason; echo the wake, a short version of the prologue's eyes blinking open, so coming back always looks
> like waking on the shore again; or a new force the owner would name: "#2 is a really good mystery to be on. Theres
> really no explanation for it"

> Asked how much of the prologue's wake a respawn should replay: "When I said respawn effect. I meant for like a
> translator from destiny"

> Asked whether arriving in a node for the first time should play it too: "Everything you respawn or spawn into a
> location"

> Asked what other players see where someone respawns: "Your effect. On top of this instead of eyelids for the
> prolouge. Just use screen darkening, screen blinking."

## What it settles

- Every spawn into a place and every respawn plays an arrival, after Destiny's, and other players see it.
- The arriving player's own view darkens and blinks open: no eyelids, as the prologue's wake has.
- Why the player comes back is a mystery. The game gives no explanation for it.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "A translator from destiny" | Destiny's transmat, its arrival in light | The owner's word for the effect a Guardian spawns and respawns with |
| The figure | Built from the feet up in 2 by 2 cells over 0.6 s, each white as it lands; no shadow, and its weapon's glow shows, once it is whole | A plain death's crumble run the other way, in the figures' own pixels and the same shader |
| The player's own figure | Begins once their view is first open, 0.5 s in | Begun at once, the blink and the veil hid it from the one arriving |
| The view | Dark for 0.3 s, open to 15% dark by 0.5 s, shut to 90% by 0.6 s, open by 1 s; the interface over it stays lit | Two blinks, over before the figure has stood a second |
| Who and where | Players only, never foes: on foot, on a boarded deck and at the prologue's checkpoints | "Everything you respawn or spawn"; a ship arriving at sea is not a player spawning |
| When | As the veil lifts on a place, as the player boards a deck, on each respawn, and for another player first seen after the player arrived | The veil hides anything sooner; boarding stands the player on another ship's deck; players already there when one arrives were not spawning |
