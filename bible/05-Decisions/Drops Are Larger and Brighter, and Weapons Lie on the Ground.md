# Drops Are Larger and Brighter, and Weapons Lie on the Ground

The drops lying for a player were small, a thin line of light over each, and a weapon that dropped as itself went
straight to its player's gear, seen only in the loot feed ([[Loot Drops Are Seen, and Ranged Weapons Carry Ammo]],
[[Commons Drop as Themselves, Never as Vestiges]]). The owner wrote:

> "1. The vestige drops and weapon drops need to be much larger, showing on the ground
> 2. The ammo drop needs to be smaller
> 3. The drops themselves need to have more oomph, more player visibility"

The owner then chose from Claude's options:

| Asked | The owner's pick | The option as offered |
|---|---|---|
| How a weapon drop shows on the ground | "Real pickup (Recommended)" | The weapon's own picture lies hovering on the ground like a Vestige, in its rarity's light, until you walk over it |
| The sizes | "Vestige 2×, ammo 0.6× (Recommended)" | A Vestige about knee to waist high; an ammo box clearly a small pickup beside it |
| What gives drops more oomph | "Rarity light beams", "Landing burst", "Glow and sparkle" | A tall, bright pillar of the rarity's colour over each drop, seen from far off (Destiny-style), Legendary and Atheric tallest; a flash and a ring on the ground as each drop lands, with a sound in its rarity's weight; each drop glowing in its rarity's colour, with motes drifting up and a pulse |

Asked whether armour that drops as itself should lie on the ground as weapons do, the owner answered:

> "Yes armor also"

## What it settles

- **A weapon or a piece of armour that drops as itself lies on the ground** for its player, its own picture hovering
  in its rarity's light, and is taken by walking over it, as a Vestige is ([[Gear]], "Drops").
- **A Vestige, a weapon and a piece of armour on the ground are twice their size**; an Ammo box or crate is 0.6 of its size.
- **Each drop has a tall pillar of light in its rarity's colour**, seen from far off, a Legendary's and an Atheric's
  the tallest.
- **Each drop bursts as it lands**: a flash and a ring on the ground, with a sound in its rarity's weight.
- **Each drop glows in its rarity's colour**, motes drifting up from it and its glow pulsing.
