# Three Races

> "My vision is 3 races.
>
> 1. Human (All skin colors)
> 2. Felid (Khajit)
> 3. Anole (Argonian)
>
> And eventually renaming the armor sets and weapons to fit our vision"

## What it settles

- Overlook has three races: Human, Felid and Anole.
- A Human may have any skin colour. The [[Sprite Forge]]'s skin ramps give the range.
- Felid wear the forge's Khajiit body, and Anole its Argonian body.
- The armour sets and weapons will be renamed to fit Overlook. Until the owner names them, they keep the forge's names.
