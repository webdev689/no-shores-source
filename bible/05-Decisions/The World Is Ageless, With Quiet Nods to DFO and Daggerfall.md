# The World Is Ageless, With Quiet Nods to DFO and Daggerfall

> "Lets start from the beginning and take everything slowly.
>
> From the start ageless, mystery is something im going for in the world, with callbacks to DFO, to daggerfall in
> some inconspicuous way."

> Offered, as one such nod: Daggerfall ends with the Warp in the West, where every ending came true at once and the
> Iliac Bay's map was redrawn; "islands being rewritten, history garabeled" echoes it, the idea only, its names left
> out as [[Own Work]] requires: "YES"

## What it settles

- Overlook's world is ageless, and mystery is at its heart. It fits the opening's "time where time and history has
  yet to be written consistantly" ([[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]]).
- The world calls back to DFO and to Daggerfall, never openly.
- A callback is an idea, made of our own words and shapes. No name, word or picture of Bethesda's comes with it
  ([[Own Work]]).
- The first callback is the Warp in the West's idea: a world where more than one history came true and the map was
  redrawn. Overlook's islands being rewritten and its history garbled are that idea
  ([[Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery]]).
