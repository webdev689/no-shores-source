# A Foe's Plain Swing Takes Seven Eighths of Its Time

> "We recently adjusted parry timing and actually made it harder where I wanted to make it slighty easier"

> Told that the longer parry window (15 ticks, 0.25 s) forgives only a press made early, that the hard part is
> reacting in time, and that a foe's plain sword swing lands 0.3 s after it begins because the swings made a quarter
> faster ([[Melee Swings a Quarter Faster, Strikes All in Reach, and the Lock Lets Go Past 70°]]) sped foes' swings too;
> asked how much more time a foe's plain swing should give, a player's own swings keeping their speed: "Slightly slower
> (Recommended)", the option that read: "A foe's plain swing takes 7/8 of its old time instead of 3/4: a sword's blow
> lands 0.35 s after it starts (was 0.3). Foes also hit a little less often, so they do a bit less damage per second."

## What it settles

- A foe's plain swing takes seven eighths of DFO's swing time, where a player's takes three quarters: a sword's blow
  lands 0.35 s after a foe's swing begins.
- A player's swings, and their companions', keep their speed. A foe's telegraphed blows keep their own wind-ups.
- The parry window stays 15 ticks.
