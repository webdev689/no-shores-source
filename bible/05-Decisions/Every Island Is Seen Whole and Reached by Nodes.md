# Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes

> "Inside our vision for the project theres certain aspects Destiny 1 will be used as a reference for, and one of
> those is how it's world works.
>
> For example:
> 1. When you're on the open sea Islands, their cities, their detail is detailed, but not fully rendered compared to
> when youre on the island itself. The city of overlook, being the main social space is massive, compared to the
> imperial city in DFO but definitely not as large. When you're in the city, youre only in one main section, while the
> other parts of the city appear detailed but they should be unreachable. The same goes for other cities we place,
> mission nodes, etc
>
> Every ounce of land can be utilized for future proofing, but its never accesable. This is how we move away from DFO
> into something different"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> How big Overlook and its main section are: "Probably half the size of the imperial city. All major port cities on
> every island will share this size. 1 per island. The social space itself should be the size of the tower from
> destiny"
>
> How the cities are built: "Utilize from this branch ccr-cab30c1a-oddrwb all our new proprietary models, and our
> procedural texture system. These will become a staple for our project. We will also utilize world 2.0/RMB for the
> world building to layout towns and such. Each port city should have a unique design and motif. Like the imperial
> cities enormous tower."
>
> What stops the player at a section's edge: "Warning to go back"
>
> Whether the sections of one island join on foot: "Utilize our proprietary overworld from DFO. You can utilize it for
> overworld sea travel, plus the birds eye island viewing. Nodes are seperated into missions, dungeons, social spaces,
> patrol areas, world bosses, etc"

## What it settles

- **Destiny 1 is the reference** for how the world works.
- **Seen, not reached.** From the open sea, an island, its cities and their detail are drawn in detail, but not as
  fully as when the player is on the island. On an island the player is in one section at a time. The parts of a city
  and the land beyond that section are drawn in detail and cannot be reached. Every city, mission node and other place
  works the same way.
- **All the land is kept.** Every part of every island can be used later, but is never reached until then. This is
  where Overlook moves away from DFO.
- **Port cities.** One major port city on each island, each about half the size of DFO's Imperial City, all of them
  the same size. Each has its own design and motif, as DFO's Imperial City has its enormous tower. Overlook is
  Saltmere's.
- **The social space**, the part of Overlook the player walks, is the size of Destiny 1's Tower.
- **Building.** The models and the procedural texture system on DFO's branch `ccr-cab30c1a-oddrwb` come over and
  become a staple of the project. World 2.0 and RMB lay out the towns.
- **A section's edge** warns the player to go back.
- **The Overworld** comes over from DFO, for travel across the sea and for the bird's-eye view of an island. The places
  the player is taken to are nodes, by kind: missions, dungeons, social spaces, patrol areas, world bosses and more.

It builds on [[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]] and [[The World Map]]: islands are landed on, not walked as
an open world, and ships go faster in the Overworld. How it is built: [[World Map]].
