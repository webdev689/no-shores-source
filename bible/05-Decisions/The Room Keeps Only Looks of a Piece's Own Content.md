# The Room Keeps Only Looks of a Piece's Own Content

Asked whether the room should refuse an armour set or weapon metal that is not of the worn piece's own content, since
a modified page could show a daedric helm on world gear to everyone, the owner chose:

> "Yes, server checks looks (Recommended)"

Asked whether to render the Abyss Dungeons' crossbows, which the forge does not draw yet, for this round:

> "Not now"

## What it settles

- **What others see of a piece's look is the room's word**: a worn armour set or weapon metal is kept only if it is
  of that piece's content, written on it, or its faction's
  ([[Armour and Weapon Looks Belong to the Content That Gives Them]]).
- **The Abyss Dungeons' crossbows keep the nearest metal the forge draws** for now, which the room lets stand, until
  their own art is made.

How it works: [[Server]] and [[Gear]], "Looks".
