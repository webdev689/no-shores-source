# The Player Has No Art in the Prologue

> "You shouldn't have an art in the prolouge. This is obtained after the prolouge with the new faction system"

## What it settles

- Through the [[Prologue]] the player has no Art: no Anima, no Tenets, nothing an Art gives.
- Arts are obtained after the prologue, with "the new faction system"; no decision sets that system out yet.

[[Arts Are Overlook's Subclasses]].

Replaced in part by [[The Prologue Is Reworked With Real Armour, a Bow, the Storm and No Elites]]: a wisp gives the
player the Storm Art, Volt, for the rest of the prologue once the first boarder falls.
