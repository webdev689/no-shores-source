# The Third-Person Aim Stands 0.8 m Out and 3 m Back

> "Here are the new third person aim values."

The owner sent the Test Ground's aim panel, its numbers reading "shoulder: 0.8, back: 3, up: 0.25, thirdZoom: 1.3".

## What it settles

- **Third person, aiming.** The camera stands 0.8 m right of the head, 3 m behind it and 0.25 m above it, and zooms
  in 1.3 times. These are the game's own numbers (`AIM`), the place the panel's "Back to the game's" returns to
  ([[Test Ground]], [[Aiming Sits Behind the Shoulder and Zooms the View]]).
