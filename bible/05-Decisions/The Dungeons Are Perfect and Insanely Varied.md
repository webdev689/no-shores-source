# The Dungeons Are Perfect and Insanely Varied

> "Make sure youre on overlook. Do a detailed pass on everything. I want this to be perfect and insanely varied"

Said of the patrol areas' dungeons ([[DFO's Dungeons 2.0 Comes Over, Within the Patrol Areas]]), once their first
build was finished and its pictures and review were reported.

## What it settles

- **A detailed pass on everything** in the dungeons: how they are laid, how they stand in the rooms, and how the Game
  draws them.
- **They are perfect and insanely varied.** How the variety is made is Claude's to build, as the dungeons are
  ([[Dungeons as Claude Builds Them]]); the owner can change any of it.
- The earlier decisions hold: a dungeon runs slightly linear, with a clear beginning, middle and end and a return
  portal, its props laid and everything in the procedural textures.
