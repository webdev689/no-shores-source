# The Website Is at noshores.online, After DFO's

> "So im buying a new domain (noshores.online) and setting up a discord. Basically I want to design our new website
> based on our new website for DFO. along with some of the same type of artwork of our own design that reflects our
> world and the lore. I also dont want to include the bells and whistles like the timers or admin panel"

> Asked where the website lives: "site/ in no-shores-source (Recommended)"

> Asked which pages its first version has: "Home + The World (lore),The Game,Discord bot panel"

## What it settles

- No Shores has a website at `noshores.online`, made after DFO Online's (`website.df.source`) and kept in this
  repository under `site/` ([[Website]], [[Pick - Website]]).
- Its pictures are the same kind as DFO's site, drawn by our own code, and show No Shores' world and lore
  ([[No Shores]]).
- Its first pages are Home, The World, The Game and the Discord bot's panel
  ([[DFO's Discord Bot Comes Over, Its Panel on the Website]]).
- DFO's live timers and its staff panel do not come over.
- Nothing is deployed without the owner's yes ([[Ask Before Deploying]]). Where it is hosted, and whether the game is
  played from it, are settled by [[noshores.online Is Served From Cloudflare, and the Game Is Played There]].
