# A Run on the Rock's Top Is Held by Out of Bounds

> Told that a way sunk 13 m into the rock's top still let 3 of 208 test walks climb out (a body climbs a face on a
> slant), and asked whether to give the rampart steeper faces, cut it deeper, change the movement rules, or accept the
> rare escapes: "Accept rare escapes", then "Wait can we revert this. We already have out of bounds"

## What it settles

- **A run along the rock's top is not sunk into it.** No trench is cut for it, and its walls and battlements are not
  what keeps a body in.
- **Out of bounds holds it**, as it holds a body anywhere outside its zone's section ([[Reach]]): a body that climbs off
  the run onto the rock is warned, and dies if it stays out.
- The movement rules stay as they are.

It replaces the sunken way in [[Patrol Zones Stay as Built and Gain Linear Combat Areas]].
