# The Sea Fight's Ship Plate Wears the Vitals' Thin Bars

> Asked whether the sea fight's ship plate should switch to the vitals' new thin bars or keep the carved window:
> "Switch"

## What it settles

- The ship's plate in a sea fight is drawn as the vitals' long thin bars on foot, not as the carved window, so the
  sea's plate and the vitals on foot read alike ([[Naval Combat]], [[Interface]],
  [[The Vitals Go Top Left and the Arts and Weapons Bottom Left]]).
- It replaces the carved window in [[The Sea's Screens Wear Enhanced Plus]]; the rest of that decision stands.
- Where the bars stand, and what goes with them, are Claude's calls ([[The Sea as Claude Shapes It]]).
