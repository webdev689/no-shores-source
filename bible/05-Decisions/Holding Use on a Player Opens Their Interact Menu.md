# Holding Use on a Player Opens Their Interact Menu

Showing Destiny's prompt to interact with a player stood before the camera, the owner wrote:

> "Also I want to implement a player interaction like the reference shows, allow you to bring up an interact menu
> where you can add as a friend, invite to company, invite to guild, whisper, or inspect the player's character
> (bringing up their character menu."

Asked how the menu should open when aiming at another player up close, the owner chose "Hold Use (E / X)", offered as
"As DFO's player menu did (your DFO pick: 'default it to hold E'). A tap does nothing on a player."

## What it settles

- **A player aimed at up close can be interacted with**, as the picture shows.
- **Holding Use opens the interact menu** (E on the keys, X on the pad, as Use is bound); a tap does nothing on a
  player.
- **The menu offers** Add Friend, Invite to Company, Invite to Guild, Whisper and Inspect.
- **Inspect brings up the player's character page.**
