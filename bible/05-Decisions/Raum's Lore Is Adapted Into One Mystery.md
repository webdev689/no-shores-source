# Raum's Lore Is Adapted Into One Mystery

> "I wanted to adapt my previous attempts at storytelling in my past repo projects. The crystalline enemy faction,
> the mysterious crystal entity torn in the sky.
>
> Maybe the area at which we play has somehow been affected by everything? Islands being rewritten, history
> garabeled"

> Asked how much of Raum, the lore in project-final's bible, comes over: "Adapt it, mold it to our art, our setting"

> Asked whether the entity torn in the sky stays behind: "No entity"

> Offered the Cube brought over reshaped, a perfect black shape that should not exist, turning on axes it should not
> have, changing whatever it touches, without the Covenant's fleets, Earth or Raum's dates: "Approved"

> Told that the world already has islands that come and go: "Islands dont come and go. These were planned smaller
> islands that havent been added"

> Offered the call in the deep kept apart from the Cube, its "flicker of hope" the opposite of the Cube's wrongness:
> "Ohp good job"

> Asked whether one mystery stands, the Cube behind the crystal faction, the rewritten islands and the garbled
> history: "Im unsure", then "One mystery"

> "Whatever is happening effects the qorld. Crystalline growth and overtake, flesh gore horror"

> Asked whether that replaces Raum's tone rule, "cosmic horror, not grimdark. The wrongness is subtle", or joins it,
> subtle from afar and flesh up close, and whether the Grey Tithe's suggestion stands: "We can go with that. Although
> that sounds a little over explanatory"; offered it all shorter, "unease from afar, flesh up close": "Actually, i
> approve"

## What it settles

- **Raum is raw material.** The lore of project-final's bible (`bible/01-Overview/Lore.md`) is molded to Overlook's
  art and its setting of sails and steel, not brought over as written ([[Pick List - project-final]]).
- **The crystal faction comes over**, adapted: project-final's Lattice, the crystal that overtakes the living
  ([[Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe]]).
- **The entity torn in the sky does not.**
- **The Cube comes over, reshaped:** a perfect black shape that should not exist, turning on axes it should not have,
  changing whatever it touches. The Covenant's fleets, Earth and Raum's dates stay behind.
- **One mystery.** The Cube is behind the crystal faction, the islands being rewritten and history garbled.
- **The islands do not come and go**: the smaller isles are planned and not yet added. How this sits with the
  discoveries that fade ([[The World Map]]) is open.
- **It touches the world.** Crystal grows and overtakes, and its horror is flesh and gore. From afar it is subtle; up
  close it is flesh.
- **The call in the deep is not the Cube** ([[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]]).
- How the mystery is shown: [[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]. The crystal faction:
  [[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]. The Grey Tithe:
  [[The Grey Tithe Take a Tenth, and Overlook Does Not Pay]].
