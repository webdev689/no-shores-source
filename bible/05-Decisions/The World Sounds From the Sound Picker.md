# The World Sounds From the Sound Picker

The owner: "I'd also like to utilize our sound picker to grab freesound sounds to build out all sounds for weapons,
walking, subclasses, and whatever needs sounds further to fully build it out."

## What it settles

- The world gets its sounds the way the interface did ([[Sounds Come From Freesound, Judged on a Test Sheet]]): cues
  listed in `content/audio/freesound.json`, candidates found by the `freesound` workflow, every one judged on the sound
  sheet.
- The cues cover the weapons (swings, hits, blocks, parries, guards broken, crits, kills, drawing and sheathing), the
  ranged weapons (draws, shots, reloads, an empty gun, what a shot strikes), moving (footsteps by ground, jumps,
  landings, the roll), the Arts (each spell, each Anima, the cast), the elements' bursts and finishers, the statuses,
  foes' shields, drops, ammo, a foe's alert and the respawn.
