# An Island's Places Stand as Even Tiles in a Grid, and the Playlist's Opens Its Screen

> "I was thinking for the island panels to be more like evenly designed rectangles on the screen. In an almost grid
> like format with space between each.
>
> The playlist would open the current playlist ui when clicked"

## What it settles

- **The island's panels are evenly designed rectangles on the screen**, laid in an almost grid-like format with space
  between each.
- **The Playlist's, clicked, opens the Playlist's screen as it is.**

Its tiles' look and text, and what a patrol zone's tile does, are replaced by [[The Island's Tiles Are Painted Scenes, and Each Patrol Zone Has Three Landing Zones]].

It replaces, in part, [[The Sea Has Two Views, Docking Asks First, and an Island's Nodes Stand in a Panel]]: the
island's nodes stand as these rectangles, not as one panel down the left of the view.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The grid's place | Across the view under the island's header, 24 px in from each side and 104 px down; tiles 280 to 340 px wide, as many columns as fit up to five, centred, 16 px between them; their height one for all, fitted to three rows in the view (176 to 220 px), scrolled when there are more | Even rectangles with space between, the island seen between and through them |
| The ground | Each tile a little transparent (74%), edged in brass; the one under the pointer or focus lit brass | The earlier ask's "slightly transparent", kept |
| What a tile holds | Its name in brass over its line (its kind and levels, or why it is shut), its details as label and value, and at its foot what a click does; the Playlist's with its four crests | Every tile alike, each as detailed as its place allows |
| A click | Does the tile's press: lands at its place (choosing it first, as the flow lands only at the node chosen), opens an Elite Dungeon's card to go in from the sea, opens the Playlist's screen; a shut tile is dim, says why and does nothing | The owner's "when clicked"; each tile shows its details whole, so nothing is left to open first |
| Enter | Does the press of the tile under the pointer or focus, the first's while none is | As the panel answered Enter |
| The island's own tile | First in the grid: the island's name in brass, where the ship lies, its levels at Normal, a chart of it with its harbour and the ship, and Back to the sea (Esc) | The panel's header as one of the rectangles, so the grid starts where the panel did and three rows fit a 720-pixel view |
| The order | The island's own, the port, the Playlist, then the patrol zones, Elite Dungeons, missions, world boss and outpost | As the panel listed them |
| The details | Shortened so each holds its tile's width: a patrol zone's dungeons ("Three; today's is second"), the harder seas, today's modifier; an Elite Dungeon's blocks, seals, company, pay and its week; the Playlist's week and rewards; a mission's, world boss's and outpost's foes' sites and named places where it has them, an outpost what it is for | A tile of one size holds less than a list that grows |
