# The Prologue Shows the Chart That Disagrees, and the Grey Tithe Sail Under Their Colours

Walking the [[Open Questions]] one at a time, the owner answered those on the opening voyage and the Grey Tithe.

Asked whether the chart that disagrees with the coast should come into the opening voyage, the owner answered:

> "Add the chart clue"

Asked whether the Grey Tithe's sails are unmarked, as the lore has them, or fly its colours and flag, as its ships at
sea do, the owner answered:

> "Change the lore"

Asked whether the Grey Tithe's roster, its boss on land and its garrisons stand, and how deep the Covenant under them
goes, the owner answered:

> "Keep the roster; Covenant later"

## What it settles

- **The opening voyage carries the chart that disagrees with the coast**
  ([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]).
- **The Grey Tithe's ships fly their colours and flag**, and the lore changes to say so: its sails are no longer
  unmarked ([[The Grey Tithe Take a Tenth, and Overlook Does Not Pay]], [[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]).
- **The Grey Tithe's roster, its boss on land and its garrisons stand** as built
  ([[The Enemies as Claude Builds Them]]).
- **The Covenant waits**: how deep it goes, and when the player learns it, is for later ([[Open Questions]]).

Built ([[Prologue]], [[Lore]]; Claude's calls in [[The Open Questions' Answers as Claude Builds Them]]). The owner's
premise ([[No Shores]]) and the website's copy of it still call the Tithe's ships unmarked: those words are the owner's
own to change.
