## What it settles

- **After the intro, the player's first goal is to pledge to a house** ([[Factions]]).
- **Each house has its own road**, a quest line of many steps with a story of its own, that takes the player round
  the game as it is: about 45 to 60 minutes, in three acts.
- **The pledge is the Art.** A house's road gives its Art for good. The other two Arts are earned by switching the
  pledge and walking those houses' roads.
- **The Art comes early in the road**, and as the player presses on, its **two Tenets** are earned, then a selection
  of its **fragments**.
- **The rules keep the road**: the server is the authority on where a player stands in it and what it has given.
- Everything else is Claude's, the owner's to change: [[The Houses' Roads as Claude Shapes Them]].
