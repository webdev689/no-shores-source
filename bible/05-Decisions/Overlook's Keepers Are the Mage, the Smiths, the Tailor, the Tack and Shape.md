# Overlook's Keepers Are the Mage, the Smiths, the Tailor, the Tack and Shape

> "Overlook should offer the following."

> "Vendors:
>
> The Mage: Indentifies your vestiges
> The weaponsmith: Provides a daily assortment of weapons to buy
> The armorsmith: provides a daily assortment of armor to buy
> The tailor: Provides daily dyes for your armor set, emblems.
> The Tack: Provides a daily assortment of horse cosmetics to buy
> Shape: A mysterious figure who arrives on Friday to Sunday evening. Currently soawning inly in overlook and in a
> static random place each week. Offers random legendary and atheric gear for purchase with a new currency (Shapes).
> Shapes are only found in Elite/Abyss content
>
> The UI should be consistant across NPCs, with NPCs having their own appropriate, yet lore accurate dialouge. (See
> the session working on our first story mission)"

The owner's references were three of Destiny 1's vendor screens (the Hunter Vanguard, the Speaker and the Gunsmith):
the vendor's figure at the left in a pose, their title with a mark at the top left, a line of words and the
currencies at the top right, "Reputation details", rows of wares under engraved headings, "Stock refresh" with the
time left, and the footer's keys.

## What it settles

- **The Mage** identifies Vestiges ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]: a Vestige is identified in a social
  space).
- **The Weaponsmith** and **the Armorsmith** each sell a new assortment every day, of weapons and of armour.
- **The Tailor** sells dyes for the armour and emblems, new every day.
- **The Tack** sells horse cosmetics, new every day.
- **Shape**, a mysterious figure, comes on Friday and stays until Sunday evening, for now only in Overlook, at one
  place each week, chosen at random and kept all that week. Shape sells random Legendary and Atheric gear for
  **Shapes**, a new currency found only in Elite and Abyss content: the tiers the owner named for the seas a voyage
  chooses ("The Tiers Are Normal, Elite and Abyss", on pull request #82), today Rough and Storm.
- **One screen for every keeper**, after the references, and every keeper speaks lines of their own, true to the lore,
  under [[Dialogue]].
- The numbers and the rest left open are Claude's: [[Factions and Vendors as Claude Shapes Them]].
