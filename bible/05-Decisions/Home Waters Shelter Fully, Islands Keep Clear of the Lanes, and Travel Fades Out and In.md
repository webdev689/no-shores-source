# Home Waters Shelter Fully, Islands Keep Clear of the Lanes, and Travel Fades Out and In

Asked after the audit of
[[Hostile Ships and Islands Show on the Sea Map, Travel Goes Anywhere at Sea, and Home Waters End 10 km Out]].

Told that a Tithe ship could wait at the 10 km line and keep a player safe at Overlook as its target, refusing their
travel as chased and stirring the compass and the fight music at the hub, and asked whether home waters should fully
shelter a player, the owner chose "Shelter fully", the option that read: "No hostile ship targets a player inside home
waters or stays at sea for one; travel from Overlook is never refused as chased."

Told that every travel holds the next hostile roll back to 30 s, so a player who keeps travelling in their own water
never gets a hostile roll, about one travel every 12 s with the cinematic, and asked whether to keep it, the owner
answered:

> "#1 replace the cinematic with a screen fade out and fade in"

the first option reading "Keep as is": "Every travel holds the roll; the cinematic's time is the cost of avoiding ships
this way."

Told that procedural islands can appear across the friendly merchants' lanes, merchant ships seen sailing through them,
and asked whether to fix it in #221, the owner chose "Fix in #221", the option that read: "Islands keep clear of every
lane by their size and a margin, as they already keep clear of players' courses."

Told that a new level-1 player in the starting Sloop can meet a level-4 Elite Tithe Brig 10 km off Overlook, and asked
whether to keep the Lantern Sea's mix, the owner chose "Keep as chosen", the option that read: "Levels 1 to 4 with the
usual mix, Elites included, as the 10 km answer chose."

Asked after a second audit, told that travel now takes about 2.5 s and every travel holds the next hostile roll back
30 s, and that the room accepted a travel that does not move the ship at all, which a modified client could send every
29 s to meet no roll, the owner chose "Refuse no-move travels", the option that read: "The room refuses a travel that
would leave the ship where it lies; every real travel still holds the roll, as you chose."

Told that a sea fight begun just past the 10 km line goes on when the player sails back inside home waters, the captain
following them toward Overlook, and asked whether home waters should end a fight, the owner chose "Captain breaks
off", the option that read: "A captain whose foe crosses into home waters breaks off and leaves the fight, as one that
sails off does; home waters shelter fully."

## What it settles

- No hostile ship takes a player inside home waters for its quarry, and none stays at sea for one; travel from
  Overlook is never refused as chased ([[Naval Combat]], "At sea").
- Every travel still holds the next hostile roll back to at least 30 s, and the room refuses a travel that would
  leave the ship where it lies ([[Sailing]], "Travel by the held map").
- A captain whose foe crosses into home waters breaks off and leaves the fight, as one that sails off does
  ([[Naval Combat]]).
- A travel by the held map is a screen fade out and fade in, with no cinematic: the picture fades to black, waits
  while the room moves the ship and the new waters load, and fades in ([[Sailing]], "Travel by the held map"). This
  replaces in part [[The Held Map Takes the Ship to Other Waters, Seen Sailing Away]], where the camera showed the ship
  sailing away and arriving, and Claude's cinematic in
  [[The Grey Tithe's Sea, Travel and the Overtaken Ruins as Claude Builds Them]].
- Islands are rolled clear of every friendly lane by their reach and a margin, as of players' courses ([[Islands]]).
- The Lantern Sea's open sea keeps levels 1 to 4 and the usual mix of ranks, Elites included.
