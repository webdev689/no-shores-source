# Every Drop Wears Its Content's Set, With Random Perks

> "So back on this topic
>
> All drops
>
> World drops, elite, and abyss drops should all have their set look. Keeping their random rolls."

Asked what a Legendary or Atheric drop should be, the owner chose:

> "Set look, random perks (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **Every drop is a piece in its content's own set look**, its perks rolled at random, at every rarity, Legendary and
  Atheric too ([[Armour and Weapon Looks Belong to the Content That Gives Them]]).
- **Named pieces never drop**: they come from the vendors, the factions and Shape alone, so no drop wears another
  content's look.
