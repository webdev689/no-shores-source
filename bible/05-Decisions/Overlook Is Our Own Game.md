# Overlook Is Our Own Game

> "I want to build a proprietary game in our own engine. A proper pirate sandbox experience with goals of venturing
> into MMO territory. Everything built by us with 0 bethesda assets."

## What it settles

- The game, the engine and every asset are ours. Nothing of Bethesda's, and no other author's work, enters the repo.
- The code is private and proprietary.
- The game's direction: a pirate sandbox, aiming at MMO scale. Where [[Overlook Is a Shared World After Destiny 1]]
  differs, it holds: a shared world rather than an MMO.
