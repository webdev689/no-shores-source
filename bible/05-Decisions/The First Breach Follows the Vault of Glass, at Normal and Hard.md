# The First Breach Follows the Vault of Glass, at Normal and Hard

> "Today my task for you is to focus on the breach which is the hardest and most intense content in the game. My
> reference for our first breach is the Vault of Glass from Destiny 1, so please research this throughly."

> "1. This content should have a normal and hard difficulty. Hard has limited revives, which players not being able
> to respawn. This is also where we implement the player follow function where players can observe teammates in death
> while their dead or waiting to respond (only for company)"

> "2. Both difficulties recieve their own loot with the hard difficulty only being the way to obtain max prestige."

> "Like vault of glass, this breach should start in dedicated designed section in the patrol zone (started from the
> Playlist menu), where other players in the zone can see that your team is taking on the raid."

> "I want you to really utilize vault of glass as reference on building the traversal, encounters, bosses with our
> own unique mechanics and following our own lore. This raid also recieves its own horse model, banner, shader, and
> lore book."

## What it settles

- **The first Breach is built now**, on land: the hardest content in the game, a raid
  ([[Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight]], [[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]). Sea
  Breaches still wait.
- **Destiny 1's Vault of Glass is the reference** for its traversal, its encounters and its bosses. The mechanics are
  our own and so is the lore.
- **Two difficulties, Normal and Hard.** On Hard the revives are limited and a fallen player does not stand again on
  their own.
- **A fallen player follows their company.** While down, or waiting to stand again, a player watches a living member
  of their own company, and only of their company. It is built with the Breach.
- **Each difficulty has its own loot.** Only Hard reaches the most Prestige.
- **It starts in the patrol zone**, in a section built for it, and is launched from the Playlist. Other players in
  the zone can see that a company is taking it on.
- **Its own rewards:** a horse, a banner, a shader and a lore book.
- **Its spaces are modelled with the Blender pipeline** ([[Handcrafted Playspaces Are Scripted in Blender]]).

How it is built, and every call left open: [[The First Breach Is Built in Full, on Claude's Autonomy]] and
[[The Drowned Vale as Claude Builds It]]. How it works: [[Breaches]].
