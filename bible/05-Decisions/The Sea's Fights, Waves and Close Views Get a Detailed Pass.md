# The Sea's Fights, Waves and Close Views Get a Detailed Pass

> "I also want to fold into this a proper detailed pass on ship combat, water enhancement/ship interaction, and proper
> waves."

> "I also want to fix current issues like the ship growing when zooming in oddly. When zoomed in and sailing fast, the
> ship ZOOMs and moves insanely fast. Not sure how we would handle this"

> Offered three ways to handle the speed up close (the voyage's pace following the view, easing to the world's own
> time close in, at the helm, in Manage and on the deck; close views at the slowest step; or the pace kept with a
> steadier eye): "Whatever is the best way to handle this"

> Asked which parts the combat pass covers, and chose all four: "Fights end as approved" (sink it and gather what
> floats, or make it strike and board it for a fight on its deck against its faction's deck crew), "Losing and
> damage" (a player who loses washes up at the nearest port, and their ship's damage stays until repaired there),
> "Fight feel" (muzzle flash and smoke, splashes, splinters, holed and torn sails, masts that fall, a ship that settles
> and goes down when sunk) and "Fight limits" (more than one player in a fight at sea, a ship joining a fight under
> way, sails coming down as they are shot away, wary captains falling on ships they outgun)

> Asked which parts of the waves and the water round ships, and chose all four: "Sea by weather" (the swell's height
> and length follow the weather and the open water, calm in a harbour and the lee of land, high in a storm), "Ships ride
> the waves" (each ship pitches, rolls and heaves on the waves under its whole hull, its bow slamming into crests and
> throwing spray), "Wakes and bow waves" (a V-shaped wake behind every ship, a bow wave and quarter waves, foam along
> its own hull line, and spray) and "Readable from above" (the swell, wakes and foam drawn so they read from the raised
> Overworld view)

## What it settles

- A ship no longer grows oddly as the view comes in, and it no longer races past up close: how the voyage's pace
  meets the close views is Claude's call.
- The sea's fights end as [[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]] approved: a sinking and what floats, or
  a boarding and a fight on its deck. A player who loses washes up at the nearest port, and their ship's damage stays
  until it is repaired there ([[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]]).
- A fight shows its blows: flash and smoke, splashes, splinters, torn sails, falling masts and a sinking.
- A fight at sea holds more than one player, takes in a ship that comes to it under way, brings sails down as they are
  shot away, and wary captains fall on ships they outgun.
- The sea follows the weather and the open water; every ship rides the waves under its whole hull, leaves a wake, and
  throws a bow wave and spray; all of it reads from the raised view ([[Naval Combat]], [[Water]], [[Sailing]]).
