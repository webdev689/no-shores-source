# Breaches Drop the Best, at Sea Too, for a Fleet of Eight

> "So.. the best drops, like destiny.. should come from the planned breaches"

Asked what the sea gives to Prestige, if the best drops come from Breaches, and what a good evening at sea is:

> "Sea breaches, like the serpent from DFO"

Asked whether a sea Breach is open to any ship that sails in, as DFO's serpent was, or taken on by a set fleet of up
to eight players, as a raid is:

> "Set fleet of 8. But I dont want to build this yet. Just getting our stuff straight."

## What it settles

- **The best drops come from Breaches**, the raids ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]).
- **Some Breaches are at sea**: a great foe fought from ships, as DFO's Sea Serpent was. That is the sea's larger part
  in Prestige.
- **A sea Breach is taken on by a set fleet of up to eight players**, as a Breach on land is.
- **Not built yet.**

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
