# The Heart Is the Port's People Fused Into One Giant

> Shown the Heart drawn after project-final's floating gem: "This design aint it chief", then "The heart design. Do
> something different from the reference in project-final"

> Offered three. The first: "the people of a ruined port, fused by the crystal into one towering giant, about three
> times a man. Bodies are half-swallowed in its flesh, some still moving, faces pressed into the crystal"; its chest a
> cage of crystal ribs, a heart grown huge glowing through the bars, "its weak point, and where its name comes from"; a
> ship's anchor "on a length of chain, torn from the ruined harbour"; it drags the anchor, stomps and sweeps, and when
> hurt "tears a Husk out of its own body and throws it into the fight". The others: a ruined port's bell tower
> overtaken and walking, and the port's last keeper grown huge: "Try #1"

> Shown it standing: "I love it"

## What it settles

- **The Heart, the Lattice's boss, is the Heart of the Port**: the people of a ruined port fused into one giant about
  three times a man, bodies half swallowed in its flesh, faces pressed out of it
  ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]).
- **Its heart** grows huge in a cage of crystal ribs in its chest, glowing through the bars: its weak point.
- **Its weapon** is a ship's anchor torn from the ruined harbour.
- **It fights** dragging the anchor, sweeping and stomping, and when hurt it tears a Husk out of its own body and
  throws it into the fight.

How it is drawn and moves: [[The Lattice as Claude Shapes It]], [[Lattice]].
