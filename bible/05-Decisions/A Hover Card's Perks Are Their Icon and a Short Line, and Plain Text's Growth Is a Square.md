# A Hover Card's Perks Are Their Icon and a Short Line, and Plain Text's Growth Is a Square

The owner's answers to Claude's two questions on #202: whether [[Plain Text]] should stop naming the growth "a ring in
its nodes", and whether the hover card should keep every perk's words.

> "2. Yes change it
> 2. No just their perk icon and a small perk description"

Asked what "a small perk description" meant, the owner chose "New short lines": a new short line written for each
perk, shown beside its icon, the full words staying on the item's page, the lines Claude's for the owner to check.

## What it settles

- **[[Plain Text]]'s example is the square**: an item's growth shows as a square filling round its locked nodes and a
  gauge on its icon, as [[The Inspect Panel Follows Destiny's Weapon Page, Its Stats Shown and Its Nodes Square]] has
  it.
- **On an item's hover card, each perk is its icon and a short line.** Its name and its whole words are on the item's
  page alone.
- **Every perk has a short line** (`short` in `content/gear/traits.json`), and a perk that gives and takes one for its
  cost (`short_takes`). Claude wrote them for the owner to check.
