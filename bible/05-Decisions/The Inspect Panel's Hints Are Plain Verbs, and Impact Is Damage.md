# The Inspect Panel's Hints Are Plain Verbs, and Impact Is Damage

> "1. Text hints should simply be choose, buy, etc. Remove the text that says frees a hand
> 2. Rename impact to damage"

## What it settles

- **A hint is a plain verb**: Buy, Choose, and the like, beside its key.
- **The page no longer says a weapon leaves a hand free.**
- **The stat named Impact is named Damage.**
