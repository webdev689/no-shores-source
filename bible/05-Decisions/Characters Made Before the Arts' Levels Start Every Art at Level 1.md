# Characters Made Before the Arts' Levels Start Every Art at Level 1

> Asked "Characters made before #92 have no Art levels. As built, merging puts every one of them at level 1 in every
> Art, so their spells hit, heal and last at 0.6 of what they do today, and fragments not set in a Core are locked.
> Which should old characters start at?", from level 5, level 1 as built, or level 5 with every fragment: "Level 1,
> as built"

## What it settles

- **A character kept before the Arts had levels starts every Art at level 1**, with no XP toward the next, as a new
  character does, and rises at the factions as anyone does ([[Arts]], [[Factions]]).
- **It owns the fragments its Cores held**, so nothing set is taken away; the rest are earned or bought.
