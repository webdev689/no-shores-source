# A Passage Kill's Share Scales the XP That Follows It, and the Passages Read Gear's Week

> "Whatever is best"

Said in answer to whether the `passage` crate may use the `gear` crate, for the week's modifiers a zone's currents keep
clear of, a widened check (`CRATES`, [[Checks]]), or whether every caller of the web passes the week's modifiers in.

> "Approved"

Said in answer to whether a passage foe's share of a kill, three quarters (`lamps::KILL_SHARE`), also scales the
officer and boon XP that follow its kill, Bountiful's more Vestiges not shared.

## What it settles

- A passage foe's kill pays its share of the officer and boon XP that follow it as of its own; Bountiful's more
  Vestiges are paid whole ([[Passages]], [[Passages as Claude Builds Them]]).
- The first is Claude's call, for the owner to change: `passage` may use `gear`, which uses no other workspace crate, so
  the week's modifiers have one source and no caller of `Web::of` passes them ([[Code Map]]).
