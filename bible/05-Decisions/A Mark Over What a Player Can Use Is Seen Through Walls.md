# A Mark Over What a Player Can Use Is Seen Through Walls

> "Some of the glyphs above vendors are inside the buildings with the NPC, and cannot be viewed. Not sure the solution
> to this"

Told that the keepers stand inside their stall buildings and the three faction keepers inside the Hall of the Arts, so
a mark 0.2 m over their heads sits under the roof, behind walls, and asked which fix, the owner chose "Seen through
walls": "The mark stays over the keeper's head but is drawn through anything in front of it, faded while hidden, like a
waypoint."

## What it settles

- A mark over what a player can use stays where it stood, and where something stands before it, it is drawn through
  it, faded ([[Game]], "Over what a player can use").
- Changes in part [[What a Player Can Use Wears Its Mark Over It]], whose marks were hidden by what stood before them.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How faded | 0.45 of the mark's own opacity, laid flat, so no layer of it shows through another | Reads as behind a wall, yet still picks a keeper out across a court |
| Which marks | Every mark over what a player can use: the keepers', the board's "!", Shape's and the dungeons' doors | One rule for all of them; each is something to go to |
| Its reach | Unchanged, 30 m, fading over its last fifth | The nearest twelve are still the ones laid |
