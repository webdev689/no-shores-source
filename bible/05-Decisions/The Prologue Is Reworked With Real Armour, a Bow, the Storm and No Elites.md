## What it settles

- **The armour the player wakes in is real gear**, equipped in their slots and shown on the Character page as items.
- **The parry window is a little longer**, for every fight in the game.
- **The prologue's foes are Minors and Majors**, but for its boss, Captain Brannoc Thule.
- **After the first boarder** (the parry and dodge lesson), the foe brought down drops a bow, and the game asks the
  player to equip it and to switch between it and their sword.
- **Then a mysterious wisp**, detailed and tied to the lore, flies about, encircles the player and engulfs them in
  its energy: they hold the Volt Art, Storm ([[Arts]]), for the rest of the prologue. The next fight teaches each of
  its abilities.
- **The rest of the prologue plays as before**, to Thule, the deep and the shore, and the stranger leads the player
  to Overlook.
