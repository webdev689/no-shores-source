# Overlook's Hub as Claude Shapes It

The owner asked for a detailed pass on Overlook after Destiny's Tower, then handed the how over: "Build this to
perfection, no holds barred, beyond the limits of detail. Take your time and make this something special. Return when
this is 100%" ([[Overlook Becomes a Hub of Courts and Ways, After the Tower]]). Everything below is Claude's, made under
that word, and the owner may change any of it.

## Destiny 1's Tower, and what came over

The Tower's landing plaza, Tower Watch, held the quick vendors (the Gunsmith, the Cryptarch, the Postmaster, the
Vaults) round open ground looking over the Last City. Tower North opened to the left, up stairs (the Outfitter, a
faction, the Speaker); the Hangar to the right, down a passage (a faction, the Shipwright, the Quartermaster), where
ships landed; the Hall of Guardians down a stair at the back (the Vanguard round a long table, the Crucible). A sealed
door hinted at more. Frames swept, people stood at their work, players sat under the tree and kicked a ball about.
Sources: Destinypedia's Tower page, Push Square's beta diary, Game Informer's interview with the art director, guides
to Xûr's places.

| The Tower | Overlook |
|---|---|
| Tower Watch, the landing over the Last City | The Watch Court, landing on the Seaward Walk over the city and the sea: the obelisk, the Mage's study, the two vault houses, the notice board |
| Tower North, through a passage to the left | The West Court, through the west range: the Tailor's atelier, a fountain, the band |
| The Hangar, to the right, where ships land | The Forge Yard through the east range (the Weaponsmith's forge, the Armorsmith's armoury), the East Walk (the Tack's stable), then the Harbour Stair down to the harbour way and the quay (the Harbourmaster, the chandler) |
| The Hall of Guardians, down a stair at the back | The Hall of the Arts inside the Beacon's base, through the portico's great door: a chapel for each faction round the war table, Elder Maren Hale at the Lamplighters' |
| The sealed door to the Food District | Sealed gates at the Beacon Walk's ends, where the platform runs on round the Beacon |

## The road to the ocean

The road is the harbour avenue, from the platform's east wall down to the quay. Every avenue in every city was laid as
flat terraces with walls between; the ramps are laid by the city's plan, so every avenue gets them:

| Call | Choice |
|---|---|
| Between terraces | A ramp the road's width, along the road, across the shared edge, six times as long as it rises, at most 64 m, never more than 32 m into a cell, so a cell's middle stays flat; under 0.5 m the ground's blend walks it |
| At the head | A switchback up the platform's wall in the avenue's head cell: legs 10 m wide rising at most 12 m each, flat landings at the turns, its top at the end nearest the social space |
| Walls | The terrace wall is cut where a ramp crosses; a ramp has side walls where it runs below or above the lawns, and a parapet wherever it stands over them |
| Walked | Overlook's harbour way, from the Harbour Stair to the quay and its pier, is part of Overlook's social space; the other avenues are seen |

## The fall

| Call | Choice |
|---|---|
| What kills | In a social space, a fall of 6 m or more kills at once, inside the area or out; straying out on the flat keeps its warning |
| The body | Every dead body keeps falling under its own weight, tumbling, and lies where it lands; others see it fall |
| The camera | Holds where it was when the player died, turning to follow the body down; the grey and "You fell." come once the fall has played |

## The townsfolk

| Call | Choice |
|---|---|
| Their ways | The hub carries its own graph of ways through every court, passage and stair; townsfolk find their way along it, keeping right |
| Shared | Each townsperson's day loops on the world clock, so every player sees the same person in the same place |
| At work and play | Smiths at the anvil, sweepers, porters, a band of lute, flute, recorder and tambourine with dancers, a juggler, a snake charmer, talkers, drinkers on the quay, a pilgrim at prayer, browsers at the stalls |
| The elder | Elder Maren Hale walks the same ways in her welcome |
