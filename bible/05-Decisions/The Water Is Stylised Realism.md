# The Water Is Stylised Realism

> "So from a baseline, I think it's important that we develop the water system next. You can use dfo as reference,
> but I want ours to be more detailed. A proper water shader, crashing waves, ripples, actual water, depth. Literally
> one of the most impressive things we can develop and interact with landmasses"

> Asked how the water should look: realistic; stylised realism, in the spirit of Sea of Thieves, with bold colours
> and shaped foam; or matched to the sprites' palette: "2"

> Asked whether the water is for the surface only (wading and swimming) or underwater too (diving, the seabed, light
> on the sand, visibility fading with depth): "Everything. Do not come back until this is finished 100%"

## What it settles

- The water comes next, and it is ours: DFO's water is a reference, and Overlook's is more detailed.
- It has a proper water shader, crashing waves, ripples and real depth, and it meets the land.
- Its look is stylised realism, in the spirit of Sea of Thieves: bold colours and shaped foam.
- It is built whole, the surface and underwater alike: wading, swimming and diving.
