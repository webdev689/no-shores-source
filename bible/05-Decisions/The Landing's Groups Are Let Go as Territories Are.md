# The Landing's Groups Are Let Go as Territories Are

> Asked how a patrol zone's room, which holds 32 foes at once and keeps up to 16 at its landing never let go, should
> leave room for its territories and quests: "1. Raise the limit above 32", "2. Release the landing's groups too when
> no player has been near for a while, the same way territories are released. This frees their places for the rest of
> the zone", or leave it: "2"

## What it settles

- **A patrol's landing's groups are let go as its territories are**: once no player has been near for a while, their
  foes go out of sight, and they stand again when a player comes back.
- **Their places go to the rest of the zone**: its territories, its quests' waves and its groups standing again.
- It follows [[Patrol Foes Hold Territories Across the Zone, and None Comes on a Timer]]; how it works is in
  [[Enemies]] ("Territories and runs").
