# Each House's Lore Book Tells Its Art's Origin

The owner wrote:

> "So the first lore books I want to develop are for each faction. Their pages should be obtained throughout their
> questlines and finished om quest completetion. I need you to really write these out properly, no em dashe, true
> fantasy and mystery. All also explaining the origin of their respective arts. Be as detailed as possible and come
> back when this is 100%"

## What it settles

- The first lore books are the factions', one for each house ([[Lore]], [[Factions]]).
- A house's book is found through its questline, and is finished as the quest is completed ([[Quests]]).
- The books are written in full, as fantasy and mystery, with no em dashes.
- Each book tells where its house's Art came from ([[Arts]]).

## Claude's calls

Under "Be as detailed as possible", the owner's to change:

- **A chapter for each task.** Each house's road has twelve tasks, so its book has twelve chapters (the horde's
  added in [[The Welcome's Gifts, the Hordes, the Ride and the Lessons as Claude Builds Them]]), one found as each
  task is done, the last as the road ends in the Hall of the Arts.
- **The books.** They sit in one category, "The Three Houses", whose tile wears the lighthouse, since the Hall of the
  Arts and its chapels stand in the Beacon's base. Each book is a record its house keeps:
  - The Stormcallers': "The Old Beacon Rolls", oiled linen kept under the Old Beacon's stair. Its cover is
    storm blue with the bolt.
  - The Lamplighters': "The Keeper's Hours", the Beacon's watch log, each entry opening with the hour, the wind
    and the sea. Its cover is dark amber with the lantern.
  - The Veiled's: "Unwritten", written in the margins of pages taken from the Tithe, never signed. Its cover is
    dusk purple with the moon.
- **Where each Art came from:**
  - Storm. A first caller on the high stone, told three ways on three rolls in one hand: Wenna Kestrel the
    shepherd, Merrow Vane the salt-wife, and two sisters with no names.
    - Quicken was her run to Shingle Landing; the Tempest the six hounds that came down the stair with her; Arc Lash
      a caller's stand at Weir Mill's bridge.
    - Brineglass is left by storms and holds them.
  - Divine. Aud of the Shingle, whose lamp burned on nothing on the last night of a winter. Its light hung over
    the drowned as the first Sunwell, mended her burned hands, and rose at dawn as a gull of fire that burned the
    wreckers' boats and named Gullwing.
  - Shadow. The first of the Veiled, never named, who burned her page from the Tithe's book unread. The smoke
    gathered into the first Duskraven.
    - The second of them learned to throw the dark between the stars (Dusk Daggers).
    - The first stepped into the margin, where the book never writes (Vanish).
- **Each house's six fragments are named in its book**: the six ways (Storm), the six graces (Divine) and the six
  habits (Shadow).
- **The mysteries the books add, shown and never explained**
  ([[The Game Shows the Mystery and Explains Almost None of It]]):
  - The Stormcallers' book:
    - The three tellings of the first caller are in one hand.
    - The Old Beacon and the Old Stones are of a stone no quarry gives.
    - Under red weather the Storm does not answer and the house comes down.
    - The Storm will come back to the high stone when it is called by a name no one has written down.
    - Lightning will not take the Tithe's grey sails.
  - The Lamplighters' book:
    - The Barrow Field holds forty-one barrows for thirty-eight burials.
    - The Beacon's lens was brought up, not made.
    - The Pearl Tomb holds hundreds of lamps that burn on nothing, freshly kept.
    - A page of the oldest Hours is torn out.
    - Among the four who lit the Beacon first is "the stranger who came ashore".
  - The Veiled's book:
    - The Tithe's coin is a grey disc cut with one ruled line.
    - Moorhold's chairs face the walls.
    - A tally ship's log gives counting-houses at bearings that point at open sea.
    - The Tithe's book is never written in, only copied from. Its ink is always wet, and it holds names before
      their people come.
    - The reckoner's ledger has a column about the player that Wren cannot read.
- **Every word the roads' scenes already say is kept as said** (`content/story/houses.json`); replaced by
  [[Every Line Reads as Spoken, and the Lore Is Detailed, Mysterious and Gripping]]. No book names the Cube,
  the Covenant or what calls in the deep.
