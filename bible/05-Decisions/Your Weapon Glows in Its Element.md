# Your Weapon Glows in Its Element

> "Also after we get this out. I want to port over the elemental weapon visuals from DFO and improve upon them for our
> 3 elements"

Asked where the glow should show, the owner answered: "Your first and third person weapons. Projectiles trail also
gains elemental effect". Shown the design (the player's own weapon glowing in first and third person, other players'
figures and the menu plain, their arrows' and shots' trails taking the element, Blaze and Volt from DFO's looks with
Volt in Overlook's electric blue, Cosmic a new purple swirl with star glints), the owner chose "Build it
(Recommended)"; asked whether a landed blow should also give off a small burst of its element, "Yes, add it
(Recommended)".

## What it settles

- **DFO's elemental weapon visuals come over, improved**, for Blaze, Volt and Cosmic.
- **The player's own weapon glows in its element**, in first and in third person.
- **The player's projectiles' trails take the element.**
- **A landed blow gives off a small burst of its element.**
- Blaze and Volt start from DFO's fire and shock looks, Volt in Overlook's electric blue; Cosmic is a new purple swirl
  with star glints.

DFO's weapon glow was marked Cut on the [[Pick List]]; this is the owner's approval of it ([[Pick - Weapon Glow]]).
