# Faction Blades Wear Their Faction's Colour on Hilt and Trim

Asked how a faction's blades should read, since a Stormcaller sword was drawn the same as a sea dungeon's elven one and
a Veiled dagger as an Abyss Dungeon's ebony one, the owner chose:

> "Faction-colour hilt and trim (Recommended)"

## What it settles

- **A faction's blade keeps its metal**, and its hilt, guard and trim take its faction's colour, as the faction's
  shields, bows and staves already wear it whole ([[Each Faction Sells Gear of Its Own, in Its Own Colour]]).
- **A faction's weapon reads apart from a content's** of the same metal
  ([[Armour and Weapon Looks Belong to the Content That Gives Them]]).

How it works: [[Gear]], "Looks".
