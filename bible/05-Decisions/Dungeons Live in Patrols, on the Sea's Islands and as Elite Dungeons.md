# Dungeons Live in Patrols, on the Sea's Islands and as Elite Dungeons

> "Alright so next is where these spawn. With the other sessions in mind, they need to be integrated in patrol zones
> and open sea small island spawns (player discovered)"

Offered the same generator in two places: in each patrol, always there, found while exploring and set as bounties'
targets; and on the sea's islands, appearing and fading, shared by whoever is near; both by where they stand and the
seas chosen:

> "Approved"

Asked what the 18 named dungeon places on the map become, two to an island: curated missions, the Elite Dungeons
already planned, or nothing:

> "Missions come much later. Let's do elite dungeons"

## What it settles

- **A patrol's dungeons** stand in it always, found while exploring and set as bounties' targets.
- **The sea's dungeons** stand on its small procedural islands, appearing and fading, shared by whoever is near
  ([[The World Map]], "discoveries").
- **Both are laid by the same generator**, and how hard each is follows
  [[Where You Go and the Seas You Choose Set the Difficulty]].
- **The 18 named dungeon places become the Elite Dungeons**, for four players matched together, as Destiny's
  strikes ([[Gear and Power Follow Destiny 1 Vanilla]]).

It is part of [[The Game's Loop Runs From Town to the Land and the Sea]].
