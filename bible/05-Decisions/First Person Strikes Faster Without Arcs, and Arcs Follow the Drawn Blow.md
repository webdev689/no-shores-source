# First Person Strikes Faster Without Arcs, and Arcs Follow the Drawn Blow

> "I love everything. Remove the trail visuals from first person, and speed up first person attack frames. Also ensure
> trails follow the weapon instead of being random"

Then, of the last:

> "Nvm about the weapon following. Just ensure the arc goes in the direction of wherever your atgtack swings/thrusts"

## What it settles

- **No arcs in first person.** Seen through the eyes, no swing arc is drawn ([[Test Ground]]).
- **First-person strikes run faster.** A strike's frames run faster than DFO's, and its blow still lands when the rules
  land it ([[First Person]]). The pace, the same as the third-person figure's
  ([[Third-Person Strikes Run Faster Than DFO's]]), is Claude's pick.
- **An arc goes the way its attack is drawn.** It sweeps, comes down or drives out as the striker's figure is drawn
  swinging or thrusting. It does not trace the weapon.
