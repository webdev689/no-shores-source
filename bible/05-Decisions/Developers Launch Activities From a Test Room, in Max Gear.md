# Developers Launch Activities From a Test Room, in Max Gear

> "For developers (discord signed in) on the character menu should be a test room option to launch activities. The
> Playlist activities along with the new breach."

> "The test character should spawn with max gear and should be able to easily solo content for testing."

> Asked who is a developer, the owner chose "A Discord server role": "Anyone holding a role (Founder, or a new
> Developer role) in the No Shores Discord; the accounts ask the bot. Adding a dev is giving the role."

> Asked how a test character gets through what one player cannot do alone, the owner chose "Boost + solo rules":
> "Test runs only: the tester deals 10× harm and cannot fall, one player counts as the whole company (a mooring or the
> hall holds while any one is held), and Hard opens without Normal won."

## What it settles

- **A developer is one whose Discord account holds the Founder role or a Developer role** in the No Shores Discord;
  the accounts ask the bot.
- **The character screen offers developers a Test Room**, from which they launch the Playlist's activities and the
  Breach.
- **A test character plays them**, spawned in max gear.
- **In test runs alone**, the tester deals ten times the harm and cannot fall, one player counts as the whole company,
  and Hard opens without Normal won.
