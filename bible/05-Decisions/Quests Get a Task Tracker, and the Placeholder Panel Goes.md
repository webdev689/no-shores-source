# Quests Get a Task Tracker, and the Placeholder Panel Goes

> "we need to implement a proper UI task element for the prolouge and future quests"

> "Players need to know that dont need to destroy the ship, only element the crew"

> "Remove the placeholder details in the bottom left corner of the screen. Adapt some of the elements to a proper UI
> element"

## What it settles

- A proper task element shows the player's current task, for the [[Prologue]] and every quest after it.
- In a sea fight the player is told they need not sink a ship: defeating her crew is enough.
- The placeholder lines in the bottom left corner go. What of them a player needs moves into proper interface
  elements ([[Interface]]).

## Claude's calls

- The tracker stands at the right under the place's name, clear of the boss bar, the plates and the dialogue box.
  The ship panel stands at the bottom left, the toasts over the dialogue box, and the key strip at the bottom right.
- A sea fight's task is "Defeat their crews", with "Ships left" (the ships fighting her still afloat), "Their men"
  and "No need to sink them". Each captain still has her own plate.
- Waking on the wreck the task is "Learn the controls": Move, Look round, Attack, and Block or dodge, each ticked as
  the player does it. It gives way to "Fight your way through the wreck" once a foe comes at the player, or a moment
  after all four are ticked; foes fighting the crew further on leave it up. The boss fight's is "Face Captain Brannoc
  Thule".
- The key strip drops walking on foot, which the lessons teach, and keeps the click that takes the mouse to look.
