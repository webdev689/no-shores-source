# Aiming Sits Behind the Shoulder and Zooms the View

> "So I want to go back to the recent combat integration we did. It's really good, but a few things.
>
> 1. When aiming by right click holding, the zoom in is too intense and should sit behind the shoulder offset from
>    center
> 2. Aiming down in third person should utilize the shoot frame if that makes sense. Like the aim pose
> 3. In first person, the sprite on screen shouldnt move. The screen should zoom itself. This avoids sprite
>    misplacement/tracer misplacement"

It replaces the aim's camera and first-person hands in [[Ranged Weapons Aim and Fire]]; the rest of that decision
stands.

## What it settles

- **Third person.** Aiming, the camera stands behind the right shoulder, off the middle, and zooms in less
  ([[Test Ground]]).
- **The aim pose.** A figure aiming a gun holds its shot's aimed frame, before it fires ([[Test Ground]]).
- **First person.** The weapon on screen stays where it is drawn; the view zooms instead, so a tracer leaves its
  muzzle ([[First Person]]). Replaced by [[First-Person Aiming Zooms the Weapon With the View]].
