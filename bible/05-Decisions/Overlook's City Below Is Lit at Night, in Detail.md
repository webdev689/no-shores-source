# Overlook's City Below Is Lit at Night, in Detail

> "For the city in the social space, I want to add lighting for the city below at night and make it extremely
> detailed."

Asked which lights, the owner chose "Lit windows", "Street lamps" and "Harbour & quay", and not the same for
Sunspire and Rimeholt.

## What it settles

- At night Overlook's city below the hub is lit, in detail ([[Cities]], [[Sky]]):
  - **Lit windows**: the houses' windows glow warm at night, a share of them lit, some going dark through the night,
    varied by district.
  - **Street lamps**: lamp posts along the avenues and streets, with their glow and pools of light on the paving.
  - **The harbour and quay**: lanterns along the quay, the piers and the harbour mouth, their light on the water.
- This is Overlook's city; Sunspire and Rimeholt are not asked for here. The ruins stay dark.
