# Come Sail Away's Sailing Comes Over

> Told that DFO's player sailing could not come over, as DFO ran it on another author's mod: "the player sailing can
> come over. it's ours"

> Told that DFO's own notes call it a port of RedRoryOTheGlen's Come Sail Away 2.1, read off its compiled assembly
> (DFO's mod registry: permission granted, its record open), and asked whether to keep [[Own Work]] or make an
> exception: "Bring it over and adapt to our infrastructure"

## What it settles

- The player's sailing as DFO had it, its port of Come Sail Away, comes over.
- It is adapted to Overlook: what it decides is rebuilt in Rust for the server ([[TypeScript and Rust]],
  [[Server-Authoritative Multiplayer]]), and it sails on Overlook's water and land ([[Water]], [[Land]]).
- This is an exception to [[Own Work]].
- It joins DFO's naval combat core, which already comes over
  ([[Naval, Living World, Professions and Synth Come Over Leaner]]).
