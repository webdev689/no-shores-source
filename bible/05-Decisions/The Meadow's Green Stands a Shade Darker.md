# The Meadow's Green Stands a Shade Darker

> "Also the color of the grass is too bright"

## What it settles

- The meadow's green is darker ([[Meadow]]), replacing the shade lighter than its ground that came with DFO's colour
  law ([[The Owner's Handcrafted Meadow Grows Over the Grass]]); its hue, its petals and dry stalks, its patches' tints
  and its light are unchanged.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How much darker | 28% darker than before: its green stands a shade darker than its ground (0.85, 0.89 and 0.78 of it, a channel each), its hue kept | Pictured in daylight at the Saltgrass Downs as it was, 15% darker and 28% darker: at 15% the tufts still stood out lighter than the land, at 28% they read as grass growing on it |
