# Foes Show a Closing Parry Ring

> "Enemies need a parry indicator above their head for timing"

Offered a ring closing as the blow lands, a flash in the parry window, or a filling bar, the owner chose
"Closing ring".

## What it settles

- Over a foe at a blow meant for the player that can be parried, a ring closes onto a fixed one exactly as the blow
  lands; an iron blow, which must be dodged, shows red with no ring ([[Test Ground]]).
