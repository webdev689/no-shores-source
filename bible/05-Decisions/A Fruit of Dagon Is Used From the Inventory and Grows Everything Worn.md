# A Fruit of Dagon Is Used From the Inventory and Grows Everything Worn

> "Also remove the hold to feed prompt for all gear. It simply needs to be consumed from your inventory and applies to
> all gear"

Asked which pieces one Fruit grows (everything worn; worn and carried; or everything owned) and how much each gets
(5,000 XP each, or 5,000 shared), the owner chose:

> "Everything worn"

> "5,000 XP each"

## What it settles

- **No item's page offers to feed a Fruit of Dagon.**
- **A Fruit of Dagon is used from the inventory.**
- **One Fruit gives every piece worn 5,000 XP each**: the Melee and Ranged loadouts, the four armour pieces and the
  cloak, as the owner's choice named them.
