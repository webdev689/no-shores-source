# Ships at Sea Wear Their Guild's Tag

Told that the plate over another player's ship at sea showed no guild tag, as the sea was told no player's guild, the
owner wrote:

> "Yes, we do need ship nameplates for the guild."

## What it settles

- The plate over another player's ship at sea carries their guild's tag after their name, as the plate over their head
  does on land (`Ada [SALT]`), and none for one in no guild ([[Guilds]], [[Server]]).
- It extends [[Guilds Gain Banners, Levels, Bounties and Outposts]] (the tag on the player's nameplate) and
  [[Other Ships Wear Their Players' Marks, the Sea Map Has No Fog, and New Players Ride a Bay That Bursts]].
