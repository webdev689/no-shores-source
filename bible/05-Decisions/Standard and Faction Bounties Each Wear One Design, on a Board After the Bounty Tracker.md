# Standard and Faction Bounties Each Wear One Design, on a Board After the Bounty Tracker

Sending Destiny 1's Bounty Tracker again (the vendor large at the left under the screen's mark and title; at the right
a line of words, the currencies, "Available bounties" over small square tiles in a row to each kind, every tile of a
kind wearing the same icon on the same colour, a refresh bar under them, and a card floating over the tile under the
cursor with the bounty's name, its kind, its task, a line of its tale, its rewards, what it needs and its press), the
owner wrote:

> "Im going to have you take autonomy and focus on overhauling the bounty menu and bounty design. Reference is posted.
> Standard bounties should have their own standard design, faction bounties should also have their own standard icon
> design. As it stands. The icons themselves are too large, and the menu isnt what I envisioned.
>
> Please take your time. Be as detailed as possible and come back when this is 100% completed"

## What it settles

- **The bounty menu and the bounties' design are overhauled on Claude's autonomy**, each call recorded in
  [[The Bounty Board as Claude Shapes It]].
- **The menu follows the Bounty Tracker** the owner sent.
- **Standard bounties wear one standard design**, every one alike.
- **Faction bounties wear one standard icon design of their own.**
- **The icons are smaller** than they were.
- **It is detailed, and done only at 100%.**
