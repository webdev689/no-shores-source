# The Lattice Is Drawn Whole Before It Comes Into the Game

> Shown the Husk's moves in deeper detail: "Approved. I love this.
>
> Let's do this pass with all enemies before we do the full render and put this ingame"

> Shown every foe's moves: "Do a detailed pass on all models now"

> Shown them again: "Do another comprehensive audit and detailed pass on everything"

> Asked what comes next for the six foes, offered hurt and death moves, the Test Ground, their fight, or more on their
> looks: "Hurt and death moves (Recommended)"

> Shown them collapsing as they die: "Instead of each having a death animation. Have they disperse in a crystallized
> energy wave". Asked how it is drawn, offered one wave for all, each foe's body bursting, or both: "One wave for all"

## What it settles

- **Every Lattice foe gets the Husk's pass** before any is rendered in full and brought into the game: its look, and
  its moves in deep detail ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]).
- **Then a detailed pass on every model, and an audit of everything** with a further pass on what it finds.
- **Then their hurt moves**: each foe struck and staggered. None has a death of its own: a Lattice foe killed
  disperses in a wave of crystallized energy, one wave for all, as the game fades its figure: burst from where it
  stood, sized to it, in its crystal's colour.
- **Then the full render and the game**, in that order ([[Lattice]]).
