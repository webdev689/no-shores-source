**Clashes: Tithe ships and friendly ships**

| Choice | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who clashes | A bold Grey Tithe ship within engage range of a friendly ship of the traffic under way, not lying at its roadstead nor in home waters | "Tithe fight friendly ships"; home waters hold no hostile ship |
| The friendly ship | Fights for the players, in the traffic's own hull at Major and the Tithe ship's level, always bold, never boarding; named "Merchant Sloop" (Schooner, Brig, Frigate), its side "Merchants" | It is the players' side; the traffic's ship is what was met |
| A player who comes near | Joins on the friendly side; won when every Tithe ship is beaten, paid as for those | As a clash was joined before |
| The friendly ship's fate | Nothing paid or lost for it; sunk, it lays no flotsam; struck, it leaves the fight at once; afloat, it sails on along what is left of its own route to its port while a player keeps it in sight; its traffic ship is not drawn again until its voyage would have ended | So it is never drawn twice, and never lost to the player's fight |
| What a friendly ship does in a fight | Never stops a player's ship mending; its compass mark stays friendly; a Tithe ship counts as the player's foe while it fights the merchant | It is the players' side |
