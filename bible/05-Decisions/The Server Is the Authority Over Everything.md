# The Server Is the Authority Over Everything

Shown an audit's list of what the rooms took on the page's word (the prologue's end, which entrance a player stands at,
the seed of a sea island's dungeon, the sea's tiers' gates), the owner wrote:

> "We should be server authoritive 100% across the codebase"

Asked how the prologue should be judged, since its wreck fight runs in the page and the page says when it is done,
the owner first chose "Server runs it (Recommended)", then asked:

> "I mean, does it need to be SA?"

Told that the prologue's end pays nothing and only lets a player into what they reach minutes later anyway, so it
can stay the page's word until the prologue pays something, the owner wrote:

> "Yeah lets go with yohr recommendation"

## What it settles

- Nothing a room keeps, pays or opens rests on the page's word: where a player stands, where they came from and
  what they fought.
- The prologue's end stays the page's word while the prologue pays nothing: its wreck fight is played in the page,
  and a room keeps only that it was played ([[Prologue]]). Should it come to pay, it is fought on the server.
- It holds [[Server-Authoritative Multiplayer]] to every system, the sea's and the story's among them.
