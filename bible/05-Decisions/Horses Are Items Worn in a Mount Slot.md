# Horses Are Items Worn in a Mount Slot

> "(REMOVE THE HORSE MOUNT FROM INVENTORY. Implement a new mount inventory icon in the character page)"

> Asked what is seen there today, the only horse the menus draw being on the Appearance screen: "Mount icon slot in
> inventory. Remove horse from appearance tab. Horses become an item you can equip."

> Asked how a new player, who starts with no mount, gets their horse: "Later, not in the tutorial"

## What it settles

- **A horse is an item**, equipped in a slot of its own, the mount's, shown by its icon on the Character page with the
  gear.
- **The Appearance screen draws no horse.**
- **A new player has no horse** through the tutorial; one comes later.
