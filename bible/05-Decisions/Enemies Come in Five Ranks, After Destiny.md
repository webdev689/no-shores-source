# Enemies Come in Five Ranks, After Destiny

> "Enemy Ranks:
>
> Minor (Red Bar) - No telegraphs, fodder
> Major (Orange Bar)  - Beefer minors, some telegraphs
> Uniqet (Elite) (Yellow Bar) - Much beefier, more telegraphs
> Miniboss (Yellow Bar) - Even more beefy, even more telegraphs
> Boss (Boss Yellow Bar. 3 phases) - unique mechanics each phase, you get the point"

> Asked whether a rank is separate from an enemy's type: "Using destiny as reference. Enemies can be different types"

> Asked how a player tells an Elite from a Miniboss, both under a yellow bar: "Overhead bar with enemies name within a
> faction"

> Asked whether ranks also change how a foe defends (how often it parries, blocks or dodges, how fast it reacts), or
> only its health and telegraphs: "Whatever is the most detailed"

> Asked whether the third rank is named Unique: "Go with Elite instead"

> Asked whether ships at sea get ranks: "Yes"

## What it settles

- Enemies come in five ranks, with Destiny as the reference: **Minor**, **Major**, **Elite**, **Miniboss** and
  **Boss**. A rank is not a type: enemies of different types share a rank, and a Major is a beefier minor.

| Rank | Bar | Body | Telegraphed blows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minor | Red | Fodder | None |
| Major | Orange | A beefier minor | Some |
| Elite | Yellow | Much beefier | More |
| Miniboss | Yellow | Beefier again | More again |
| Boss | The boss bar | | Three phases, each with its own mechanics |

- An enemy's bar is over its head, with its name within its faction: the name is what tells an Elite from a Miniboss.
- A rank changes how a foe defends as well as its health and telegraphs, in the most detailed way: those details are
  Claude's calls, recorded in [[Foes]] as they are built.
- Ships at sea have ranks too ([[Naval Combat]]).
- The factions the names belong to: [[Enemy Factions Start With the Grey Tithe]].
