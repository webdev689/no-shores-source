# The Ways Across Stand at the Runs' Ends, Not on the Landing

> "Also the gates are on the map but dont show physically in the patrol zone. Interacting and going into a gate shows
> the travel but then just stays on the screen. You dont enter the passage. I also dont see the exits to other patrol
> zones on the map"

> Asked what should stand where the ways across to the other patrol zones stood, with nothing built there: "They
> shouldnt be in the middle of the patrol zone. Repurposes the objective locations which are currently unused to be
> used for the passasge activity and patrol paths"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked where the Saltgrass Downs' two ways across go, its three runs all ending in passage gates, the owner chose
> "Share a run's end": "The way across stands in the end arena of the run facing that zone; the run's sealed door
> stays its passage gate."
>
> Asked which run ends hold the Gullwing Vale's and the Driftwood Moors' two ways across, the owner chose "The run
> facing each zone": "Each way across stands at the end of the run lying most nearly toward its zone; the third run's
> end stays as it is."
>
> Asked what stands at a run's end that is a way across, the owner chose "The run's own sealed door": "The stone arch
> and door already built there; E at it offers 'Go to <zone>'. (On the Downs, if shared, it would stand a second arch
> in the end arena.)"

## What it settles

- **A patrol zone's ways across** to the island's other patrol zones
  ([[Each Island Has Three Regions, and Saltmere's Three Patrol Zones Stay, Linked]]) no longer stand on its landing:
  each stands at the end of the run lying most nearly toward its zone, in the run's end arena, the place a mission's
  objective was kept for.
- **On the Gullwing Vale and the Driftwood Moors** the way across is the run's own sealed door, E at it offering
  "Go to <zone>"; the third run's end stays as it is.
- **On the Saltgrass Downs** each run's sealed door stays its passage gate ([[Passages]]), and the way across stands
  a second arch in that run's end arena.
- Going through a gate must take the player in, not leave them on the travel's screen.
