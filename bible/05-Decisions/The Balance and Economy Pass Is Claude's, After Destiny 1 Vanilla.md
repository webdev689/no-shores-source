# The Balance and Economy Pass Is Claude's, After Destiny 1 Vanilla

> "I want you to do a comprehensive balance pass and economy pass on the entire game ecosystem so far. Please use
> Destiny 1 vanilla as a reference and be as detailed as possible. Im assigning you autonomy and making this your
> baby. Come back when this is 100% complete.
>
> Be as detailed and accurate as you possibly can. Touch every inch"

## What it settles

- The whole game so far gets a balance pass and an economy pass: the fight on foot and at sea, and every source of pay
  and every cost.
- Destiny 1 at launch ("vanilla") is the reference.
- The pass is Claude's to make, without asking along the way. Claude records each choice in
  [[The Balance and Economy Pass as Claude Shapes It]], and the owner can change any of them.
- The [[Rules]] still hold, and so does [[Ask Before Deploying]]: the work comes back finished, and merging and
  deploying wait for the owner's yes.
