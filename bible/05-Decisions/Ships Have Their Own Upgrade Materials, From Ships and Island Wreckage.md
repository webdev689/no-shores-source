# Ships Have Their Own Upgrade Materials, From Ships and Island Wreckage

> "Yeah, I'm thinking ships get their own upgrade materials, found by destroying and looting ships + scavaging
> wreckage on islands"

> Asked whether to build the materials now or record the plan only: "Record the plan only"

> Asked what a destroyed or looted ship gives, ship materials and gear or ship materials only: "Materials and gear"

> Asked which wreckage on islands can be scavenged, the ruined harbours' wrecks now and the small islands later, or
> the small islands only: "Small islands planned only for now"

## What it settles

- **Ships have their own upgrade materials**, apart from the gear a player wears: what the ship as gear is upgraded
  with ([[The Sea Is a Patrol Zone, and the Ship Is Gear]]).
- **They are found by destroying and looting ships**, and by scavenging wreckage on the sea's small islands.
- **A destroyed or looted ship gives both**: ship materials and gear.
- **Only the small islands' wreckage is scavenged**; the wrecks in the ruined cities' harbours stay as they lie
  ([[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]]).
- **It is the plan, not built yet.** Until it is, a sunk or boarded ship gives gear alone
  ([[The Sea's Fights, Waves and Close Views Get a Detailed Pass]]).
