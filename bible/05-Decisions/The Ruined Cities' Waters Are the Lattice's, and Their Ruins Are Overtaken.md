# The Ruined Cities' Waters Are the Lattice's, and Their Ruins Are Overtaken

> "The ruined cities regions.should show controlled by the lattice with the ruined cities recieving extremely
> detailed lattice transformation to their ruined archetecture."

> Asked what is met sailing the waters the Lattice holds, the Lattice being met on land only: "Grey tithe only for
> now. Lattice ships come later"

## What it settles

- **The waters of the six ruined cities show as the Lattice's**: Cairnwatch's, Elmhaven's, Goldreach's, Mistfall's,
  Windmere's and Fenhallow's ([[Three Port Cities Remain, and the Others Are Desolate and Destroyed]],
  [[The Waters Come From the Islands]]).
- **The Grey Tithe's ships sail them for now.** Lattice ships come later.
- **The ruined cities' architecture is overtaken by the Lattice, in extreme detail**: the crystal of the ruined ports'
  people grown through what is left of them ([[The Lattice Are the Ruined Ports' People, Overtaken]]).
- With the pirates replaced ([[Pirates Are Replaced by the Grey Tithe]]), the Grey Tithe holds the two living ports'
  waters past home: Sunspire's and Rimeholt's.
