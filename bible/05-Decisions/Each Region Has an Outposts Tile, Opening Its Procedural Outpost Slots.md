# Each Region Has an Outposts Tile, Opening Its Procedural Outpost Slots

Each island had one guild outpost site, shown as one tile in its region's view. The owner wrote:

> "Also, for the panels themselves lets list what each panel does along with level and title.
>
> We also need to ensure guild outposts get their own panel
>
> When clicking on the outposts panel, it should show a procedural assortment of slots to choose from (island nodes)"

The owner's answers to the questions that followed:

> Asked whether the slots are many outpost sites, the island's existing nodes, or something else: "Keep. Each region
> of the island gets an outpost slot panel"

> Asked how the slots are drawn and how many, and where the outposts tile shows: no preference, each.

> Asked which words each tile shows: "Title, level, kind"

## What it settles

- **Each tile shows its title, its level and its kind** ([[Interface]], "The island's tiles").
- **Guild outposts keep working as they do**: each guild plays its outpost at a site in a room of its own
  ([[Outposts]]).
- **Each region of an island has an outposts tile**, which opens a procedural assortment of that region's outpost
  slots, each a node of the island, to choose from.

## Claude's calls

| Call | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How many, how drawn | Three slots a region, drawn once from the island's seed, the same for everyone and stable; each island's existing site is one of its region's three | The owner had no preference; an outpost built on a slot must stay where it stands |
| Where the tile shows | In every region's view, opening that region's slots | Each region gets its own, as the owner said |
