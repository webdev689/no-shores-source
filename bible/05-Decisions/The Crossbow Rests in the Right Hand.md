# The Crossbow Rests in the Right Hand

> "Crossbow held in third person is on wrong hand"

> Asked how the crossbow should be held standing and walking, hanging from the right hand at the side or across the
> body in both hands like its sprint: "Right hand, at side"

## What it settles

- Drawn, a crossbow stands and walks in the body's own moves, hanging from the right hand at the side by its stock,
  pointing down; it is no longer held as a bow is, in the left fist. Its shot and its sprint are as they were.
