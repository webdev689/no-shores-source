# The Saltgrass Downs Plays as Destiny 2's EDZ, Its Three Landing Zones Spread Across It

The owner's answers to the questions on the Downs' design pass, its enemies, and its three landing zones
([[Saltmere Keeps Only the Saltgrass Downs, and Everything Folds Into It]]):

> Asked where the Downs' three landing zones should stand: spread across the Downs (one in the north, one in the
> middle, one in the south, so any part of the Downs is a short walk from one), all on the shore, or Shingle Landing
> plus two inland: "Spread across the Downs (Recommended)"
>
> Asked what a player should see when fast travelling to another of the Downs' landing zones: Destiny 2's (a short
> fade from the map, then they stand at the landing zone), their ship flying them over the Downs, or their horse
> carrying them along the ways: "Destiny 2's"
>
> Asked which game's patrol zone the Downs' design pass should play like: Destiny 2's EDZ (public events, hidden lost
> sectors, patrol tasks picked up in the field), Guild Wars 2's maps, Elden Ring's open field, or World of
> Warcraft's zones: "Destiny 2 (EDZ)"
>
> Asked how the Downs' enemies should stand: camps at places that stand again a while after they fall, patrols
> walking the ways between the places, or both: "Both"

## What it settles

- **The Downs' three landing zones stand spread across it**: one in the north, one in the middle, one in the south,
  so any part of the Downs is a short walk from one.
- **Fast travel between them is Destiny 2's**: a short fade from the map, then the player stands at the landing zone.
- **The Downs' design pass plays like Destiny 2's EDZ**: public events, hidden lost sectors, and patrol tasks picked up
  in the field.
- **The Downs' enemies stand both ways**: camps at its places that stand again a while after they fall, and patrols
  walking the ways between the places.

It builds on [[The Island's Tiles Are Painted Scenes, and Each Patrol Zone Has Three Landing Zones]] (the three landing
zones, the zone's map to choose one from, and their fast travel).
