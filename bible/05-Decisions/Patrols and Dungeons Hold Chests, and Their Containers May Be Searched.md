# Patrols and Dungeons Hold Chests, and Their Containers May Be Searched

> "I want to implement patrol chests that spawn in random set places that giver a chance of loot, aurem, and patrol
> materials.
>
> I also want to implement these same chests within all dungeon enviroments plus allow for objects to have a chance
> to be a searchable item.
>
> Take autonomy, be as detailed as possible and return when this is 100% complete"

## What it settles

- **A patrol holds chests** that stand at places drawn at random from a set of places its layout marks
  ([[Layouts]], "The sites"; [[Server]]).
- **A chest gives a chance of loot, Aurem and patrol materials** ([[Gear]], "A find").
- **The same chests stand in every dungeon**: a patrol's, an Elite Dungeon's at every tier, the playlists' and a sea
  island's ([[Dungeons]], "In the rooms").
- **Objects have a chance to be searchable.**
- **Claude decides what the owner has not**, recording each call in
  [[Chests and Searchable Containers as Claude Builds Them]]; detailed, and not done until it is whole.
