# The Duskraven Sounds With Fire Under Its Raven

The owner, on the sound sheet's first pass, picking a crow's call for the Duskraven: "Mix with a fire sound"

## What it settles

- The Duskraven plays its raven and a fire together. The fire is a cue of its own, `spell.duskraven.fire`, picked on
  the sound sheet like any other ([[The World Sounds From the Sound Picker]]).

The owner's other words on that pass guide the next searches: Tempest "Less electric sound", and the crossbow's
reload "All these dont sound like a reload".
