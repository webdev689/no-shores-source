# Own Work

Everything in the repository is made by us, or made by our tools from sources in the repository. Outside code enters
only as a Cargo or npm dependency we agree to add, its licence read when it is added. No other author's art, sound,
models, words or data, and nothing of Bethesda's.

The exceptions are the sprite forge, its renders and the CC0 motion in nine of its moves, which the owner brought
over exactly ([[DFO's Sprite Forge Comes Over Exactly]]); Daggerfall's palette and dye colours, read into a table
([[The Player Plays as in DFO]]); Come Sail Away's sailing as DFO ported it ([[Come Sail Away's Sailing Comes Over]]);
the forged models, procedural textures and RMB town blocks of DFO's branch `ccr-cab30c1a-oddrwb`, with the
Daggerfall measures, texture archives and palette they are built on
([[Every Island Is Seen Whole and Reached by Nodes]]); and DFO's blood with DaggerBlood's numbers and Daggerfall's
blood red ([[Struck Figures Flash, and DFO's Blood Comes Over]]).

**Why.** DFO Online ported other authors' mods and later spent months replacing them with its own work, and one ship
system came from a decompiled mod with no licence. Overlook starts where DFO ended up ([[Overlook Is Our Own Game]]).

**Checked by.** The assets check keeps media out of git outside `bible/Attachments/`, so art must come from our
tools. Review holds the rest. [[Lessons from DFO]] has the evidence.
