# Plain Text

Text is easily explained. A player reads a line once and knows what it means; a note says how a thing works in the
words a newcomer would use. Nothing reads as AI text: no stock phrases, no flourish, no words that sound weighty and
say little. Nothing is bloat: a line that adds nothing goes. Where a picture can carry a thing, the picture does and
the words go: an item's progress is a ring or a gauge, not a count of XP.

**Why.** The owner set it ([[Text Is Plain]]).

**Checked by.** Review.
