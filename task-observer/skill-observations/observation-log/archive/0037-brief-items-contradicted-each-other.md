The wreck2 brief carried an auditor's fix for pits ("fill a row's inside gaps at the deck's own height") beside another
item ("what stands drawn is solid"). A gap in the deck grid is a mast, a gun or a hatch, so the two asked for opposite
things; the worker chose walls and said why. Copying each finding's proposed fix into a brief lets such pairs through.
Proposal: a brief states each defect and the outcome wanted, not the auditor's fix, and the controller reads the items
side by side for contradictions before starting the worker. Workers also met the sleep rule when waiting on a
background check (they used an until-loop) and auditors' scratch scripts with absolute paths of the controller's tree.
