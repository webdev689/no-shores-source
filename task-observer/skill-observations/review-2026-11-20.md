### 2. A barrier for building past an approval (#3) — three breaks of one rule

The rule "Never invent game design" was in context all along, and was broken three times in one task: the thirds put on
the target plate too, the approved rule paraphrased into a different one in the notes, and a line the owner never said
added to the decision note. Reviewers caught two, the implementer the third. By the skill's rule, a third rewording would
not count; the fix must be a barrier. Options:

- **(A, recommended)** A scripted fidelity review: `tools/claude-toolkit/fidelity-review.sh <approval file> <range>`
  runs a headless reviewer with a fixed brief (the owner's words verbatim, the diff, "list everything built or recorded
  beyond them, and every paraphrase of them") and writes its report; plus a CI check that fails a pull request changing
  `bible/05-Decisions/` unless its description carries that report's summary under "Fidelity review". The review is then
  performed the same way every time, and a decision cannot merge without it. A new check: the owner's agreement is
  needed.
- **(B)** The script alone, its report shown with every merge question; no CI check.
- **(C)** Text only, in Recording a Decision ("copy the approved words; ask before extending an approval to anything it
  did not name"). Not a barrier: the skill's rule says this will not change the outcome.
