# Activities, Phase 6: the Journal page. Implementation plan

**Goal.** This phase builds the Journal tab the owner asked for. It has three parts:
- **The quests.** The main part shows the quests held as a grid of cards that pages. Each card has a banner drawn in code, the quest's name, one italic line of its tale, and its current step's number and name. Story missions and side quests wear different banners. No quest is written yet, so the page shows an honest empty state. It renders whatever `content/gear/quests.json` holds once a mission writer adds quests.
- **The bounties.** A right column holds six slots in a 2-by-3 grid, headed "Bounties" with "n / 6" taken. Each bounty taken is a square tile with a mark for where it counts, and its state shows on the tile. Hovering, focusing or pressing a tile shows its words and count under the grid. Bounties are still taken and turned in at the board alone.
- **The timers.** On the Journal tab only, DFO's timer hourglass stands where the account portrait does. Pressing it, or T, drops a timers panel from the Journal's top. The panel holds everything This Week shows today, grouped as DFO's timers window groups its rows. The "This Week" page and its title go.

**Architecture.**
- **The rules (`gear`).** A quest gains its kind, name, tale, banner key and named steps. Each step counts its own objective, in turn. The count kept in `Character.story.quests` runs across the steps, so nothing new is saved. `Quest::step_at(count)` says which step a count stands at, and `Character::quests_held` gives each quest held at its step.
- **The wire.** The Gear message gains `quests: [GearQuest]` (id, step, done) and `GearBounty.pool` (0 land, 1 sea, 2 dungeons). The page then judges neither a step nor where a bounty counts. It reads the quests' words from the same content file the rules read.
- **The kit (`client/src/plus/journalArt.ts`).** It draws DFO's 9×12 hourglass, the two banners and the three pool marks, pixel for pixel, as `emblems.ts` draws the faction emblems.
- **The page.**
  - `journalWords.ts` holds the pure words: the countdown and its local moment (after DFO's `timerText` and `localWhenText`), the quest cards, and the bounty column.
  - `journalTimers.ts` holds the panel, built from This Week's model (`ThisWeek`).
  - `journalPage.ts` holds `startJournal`: the hourglass, the one page kept and updated in place, the T key, Escape, and the focus.
  - `gearWindow.ts` gains 2 lines net. It asks the journal for its page and hourglass, and swaps the hourglass for the account mark on the Journal tab.
- **The data source.** The journal reads `activityScreens`: `week()` for the timers, and a new `kept()` (the last word of the character any room sent) for the quests and bounties. It therefore reads the same at sea.

**Tech stack.**
- Rust: `gear` uses only serde, serde_json and libm.
- FlatBuffers: `crates/protocol/messages.fbs`, generated by `npm run schema`.
- TypeScript strict, tested with `node --test` over `pageForTests.ts`'s fake page. The tests that need room.wasm need `npm run wasm` first.
- The CSS is plain, in the Enhanced Plus kit's roles.

## Review focus

These are the five inputs most likely to break this phase.

1. **A long name, tale line or step name.**
   - **Risk.** It can widen a card, push the bounties off the page, or be lost where it is cut short.
   - **Pins.** `every_quest_written_reads_whole_with_steps_and_words_its_card_can_hold` (Task 1) caps what content may hold. `a long name or tale stands whole in its card’s words and title…` (Task 6) checks the page keeps the whole text.
2. **A narrow window or a phone width.**
   - **Risk.** The two columns, the 2-by-3 tiles and the panel must fold into one column without spilling sideways.
   - **Pin.** Layout is CSS, and no Node test can see it; the repo has no browser runner. The pin is the harness's measurement in Task 7. At 860×600 and 390×844 it fails on any sideways overflow inside the Journal, and on any card's words outside its card. I will say this plainly in the pull request.
3. **The panel open while the menu changes tab, or shuts.**
   - **Risk.** The panel could stay down, or its T key could act on another tab.
   - **Pin.** `on the Journal the hourglass stands in the account mark’s place, and its timers go up as the menu leaves it` (Task 6, `gearWindow.test.ts`).
4. **Keyboard focus.**
   - **The pad.** The client has no gamepad code: no `getGamepads` anywhere in `client/src`, and the Plus pad has not come over. Key focus is what a pad would drive.
   - **Risk.** The page updates every 100 ms while open and is rebuilt when the gear or the minute changes. A rebuild must not drop the focus.
   - **Rules to keep.** Escape takes the panel up before the menu steps back. The page under the panel rests (`inert`).
   - **Pins.** `the hourglass or T drops the timers from the top; Escape, Close or the hourglass take them up, the focus going back` and `the timers stay down and the focus stays put as the minute, the day and the gear change under them` (Task 6).
5. **The reset passing while the panel is open.**
   - **Risk.** A countdown could go negative, or stick at 0. The day's draws could stay stale.
   - **Pins.** `as the reset passes with the timers open the count stops at nothing, then the new day’s rows count anew` (Task 5), and the second Task 6 test above.

**Also pinned.** A room on another build can send a quest id or a step the page's content lacks. `the quests held are cards, story missions first…` (Task 4) skips the id and holds the step at its last.

**Shared test constants:** `FRIDAY_NINE = 29_919_420` (day 20,778; daily reset +1,440 minutes; weekly reset +5,760), and `AT = FRIDAY_NINE * 60_000`.

```rust
/// The most characters a card shows whole: a quest's name and a step's name on one line, its tale on two.
pub const NAME_MOST: usize = 32;

pub const STEP_NAME_MOST: usize = 32;

/// What a quest is, which its card's banner shows: a story mission or a side quest. Read from the content, never kept.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, PartialEq, Eq, Deserialize)]
#[serde(rename_all = "lowercase")]
pub enum QuestKind { Story, Side }

/// A step of a quest: its name, what it asks in words, what it counts and how many.
#[derive(Clone, Debug, PartialEq, Eq, Deserialize)]
#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]
pub struct Step { pub name: String, pub words: String, pub objective: Objective, pub target: u32 }

#[derive(Clone, Debug, PartialEq, Eq, Deserialize)]
#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]
pub struct Quest {
    pub id: String,
    pub kind: QuestKind,
    pub name: String,
    /// One line of its tale, as its card shows it.
    pub tale: String,
    /// The banner its card wears, by the page's key; none wears its kind's.
    #[serde(default)]
    pub banner: Option<String>,
    pub steps: Vec<Step>,
    pub xp: u32,
    pub aurem: u32,
}
impl Quest {
    /// Every step's target together: the count that finishes it.
    #[must_use] pub fn total(&self) -> u32;
    /// The step a count so far stands at, by its place from 0, and whether every step is done (then the last).
    #[must_use] pub fn step_at(&self, count: u32) -> (usize, bool);
}
/// A quest a character holds, as its card shows it: its id, the step its count stands at, and whether all are done.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, PartialEq, Eq)]
pub struct HeldQuest<'written> { pub id: &'written str, pub step: usize, pub done: bool }
impl Character {
    /// The quests held that the content still has, in the order written, each at the step its count stands at.
    #[must_use] pub fn quests_held<'written>(&self, written: &'written [Quest]) -> Vec<HeldQuest<'written>>;
}
```
- **`step_at`.** Add the steps' targets up in order. The first step whose running total is still above the count is `(place, false)`. Past them all, it is `(len.saturating_sub(1), true)`.
- **`total`.** Sum the targets with `u32::saturating_add`.
- **`saw_with`.** For each held quest, take `let (place, done) = quest.step_at(*count);`. Then `if !done && quest.steps.get(place).is_some_and(|step| step.objective.counts(feat))`, add one.

- **`page()`.** Each call does the following, in order:
  1. If the node is not connected (just brought back, or the menu was shut), the timers go up with no focus moved.
  2. Read `source.week()` and `source.kept()`.

  4. Call `panel.show(week, now())`.
  5. Return the node.
